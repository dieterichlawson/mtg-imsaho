# Innistrad draft guide

_Conventional Innistrad (ISD) limited wisdom, written without the draft loop's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

- Innistrad is a two-colour format with deep archetypes. Pick a lane by the middle of pack 1, read what flows, and stay open for your second colour.
- WU spirits/fliers is the strongest common archetype: cheap fliers (Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Moon Heron) backed by Claustrophobia, Rebuke and Feeling of Dread. Moorland Haunt is excellent here.
- UG self-mill builds around Spider Spawning: Armored Skaab, Grasp of Phantoms, Boneyard Wurm and other self-mill enablers become playable only in that deck.
- BR vampires and WR humans are aggressive: cheap creatures, burn and Brimstone Volley.
- GW travellers wants many humans, Travel Preparations and Avacynian Priest; it attacks wide.
- RG werewolves curve out with Darkthicket Wolf, Villagers of Estwald and Kruin Outlaw.
- Black is the deepest removal colour (Dead Weight, Victim of Night, Tribute to Hunger) and pairs with anything.

## Commons that win

- White: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Rebuke, Bonds of Faith, Avacynian Priest, Slayer of the Wicked (uncommon), Travel Preparations in GW, Thraben Sentry is filler.
- Blue: Claustrophobia, Stitched Drake, Moon Heron, Silent Departure, Grasp of Phantoms, Murder of Crows (uncommon). Delver of Secrets is weak in limited.
- Black: Dead Weight, Victim of Night, Tribute to Hunger, Moan of the Unhallowed, Typhoid Rats, Vampire Interloper, Walking Corpse is filler.
- Red: Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Bloodcrazed Neonate is poor, Ashmouth Hound, Crossway Vampire.
- Green: Prey Upon, Darkthicket Wolf, Orchard Spirit, Villagers of Estwald, Travel Preparations, Ambush Viper, Festerhide Boar is filler.
- Colourless: Blazing Torch (removal any deck plays), Silver-Inlaid Dagger, Butcher's Cleaver.

## Uncommons and rares

- Bombs: Bloodline Keeper, Olivia Voldaren, Mayor of Avabruck, Geist-Honored Monk, Garruk Relentless, Angelic Overseer, Devil's Play, Army of the Damned, Sever the Bloodline.
- Premium uncommons: Fiend Hunter, Slayer of the Wicked, Morkrut Banshee, Midnight Haunting, Murder of Crows, Kruin Outlaw, Spider Spawning (in its deck), Galvanic Juggernaut.

## Pick order

1. Bombs.
2. Cheap removal: Brimstone Volley, Dead Weight, Victim of Night, Fiend Hunter, Slayer of the Wicked, Claustrophobia, Rebuke, Tribute to Hunger, Geistflame, Blazing Torch, Prey Upon.
3. Evasive and efficient creatures in your colours: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Moon Heron, Darkthicket Wolf, Orchard Spirit.
4. Good filler creatures and the best tricks (Ranger's Guile, Spare from Evil only late).
- Flashback cards (Moan of the Unhallowed, Geistflame, Travel Preparations, Silent Departure) are worth two cards; value them up.
- Skip narrow cards: Curses other than Curse of Death's Hold, mill-yourself cards outside UG, Wooden Stakes, Cobbled Wings, Ghoulcaller's Bell.

## Building

- 17 lands, 16 for a low-curve aggressive deck. 15-17 creatures.
- Two colours. Splash only for a bomb or premium removal with a land or two of support.
- Fliers win this format: play your evasive threats and your answers to fliers.
- Prefer a curve of two- and three-drops; four or five six-drops at most.

## Playing

- Tempo matters: develop early, attack in the air, use removal on blockers that stop your fliers.
- Werewolves flip when no spells are cast in a turn; cast a spell each turn to keep opposing werewolves human, and hold your own spells to flip yours when it matters.
- Count flashback cards in the graveyard as cards in hand.
- Hold Rebuke, Smite the Monstrous and Brimstone Volley for the attacker that matters.
