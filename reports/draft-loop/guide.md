# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-10-31's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Eleven nights: 88 decks, 320 games. Guided seats are 119-142 in games (46%, 47-61 in matches); unguided are 201-178 (53%, 85-71). Guided seats have done worse than unguided every night so far, so the guide has not been shown to help. Judge by the card numbers and do not follow any line here blindly.

- BG is still the best pair by results: 32-22 in matches, 56% of games (130 games, 18 decks). But tonight two guided BG decks went 0-3 and 2-1. The 0-3 deck had Somberwald Spider and Spider Spawning and still lost: it ran Darkthicket Wolf, Festerhide Boar and Brain Weevil with only two fliers. The 2-1 deck won with Somberwald Spider and Vampire Interloper and no Spider Spawning, and lost to WU fliers. Both are anecdotes, but they agree with earlier lessons: BG with only ground bodies loses, and Spider Spawning does not carry a ground deck.
- BR 55% (69 games, 10 decks), WR 54% (71 games, 10 decks), WU 53% (88 games, 12 decks). WU went 3-0 (unguided: Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation, Rebuke, Slayer of the Wicked, Spectral Rider, Stitched Drake) and 2-1 (guided: Chapel Geist, Stitched Drake, Voiceless Spirit, Murder of Crows, Claustrophobia) tonight, so WU is a good pair when it has fliers and cheap removal. A WR deck with Unruly Mob and Riot Devils went 1-2 and left two Moon Herons in the sideboard (anecdote). BR went 2-1 on cheap ground bodies, but was beaten by WU fliers.
- Middling: WG 47% (51 games, 7 decks), RG 50% (14 games, 2 decks).
- Weak: UR 45% (65 games, 9 decks; tonight a UR deck of fifteen cheap bodies went 1-2), UB 44% (70 games, 9 decks), UG 38% (16 games).
- WB is 5-10 in matches, 36% of games (36 games, 5 decks). Tonight's WB deck went 1-2. Avoid.
- Fliers are the theme: at least five of tonight's losing decks were beaten by WU or other fliers (Abbey Griffin, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Angelic Overseer, Murder of Crows). Games stall for 14 to 55 turns. Removal plus evasive finishers break stalls. Ground bodies alone do not.
- Black, green or white-blue first are all fine. Pick a main colour by about pick 5 and a pair by mid pack 1. Stay flexible until mid pack 1: tonight a WU drafter took Spider Spawning, Lumberknot and Charmbreaker Devils early, none made the deck, and WU flowed in pack 2 (anecdote). Do not leave a colour for one early removal spell unless the new colour is clearly open.
- Splashing is poor. Once the pair is clear, off-colour picks do not make decks (Cobbled Wings, Mulch, Skeletal Grimace all went unused tonight).
- Mono-colour is risky: 2 mono-red decks 3-3 in matches (16 games).
- Read what is passed to you. Two decks of the same pair often share a table, so take contested playables earlier or move. Do not pass a second removal spell to a neighbour in your pair's lane (tonight Sensory Deprivation was passed for a second Ghoulraiser and the WU deck used it to beat that BR deck).

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Brimstone Volley, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Smite the Monstrous, Bonds of Faith, Sensory Deprivation, Geistflame.
2. Strong uncommons and rares: Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee, Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders, Slayer of the Wicked, Galvanic Juggernaut, Murder of Crows, Manor Gargoyle.
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Moon Heron, Abbey Griffin, Crossway Vampire, Somberwald Spider. Fliers beat many decks tonight, so take these over a fourth removal spell, a trick or a third ground two-drop.
4. Good commons: Typhoid Rats, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Avacynian Priest, Ashmouth Hound, Silver-Inlaid Dagger, Thraben Sentry.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Walking Corpse, Armored Skaab, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Delver of Secrets, Dissipate, Think Twice, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Lost in the Mist, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Darkthicket Wolf, Festerhide Boar, Brain Weevil. Take on-colour evasion or removal over a speculative pick or another cheap ground body.

## Building

- Land count is unsettled. 17 lands went 0-3 (BG), 2-1 (BG, WU) and 1-2 (WR, WB); 16 is untested. Tonight's losses were about evasion, not land count. Try 16 only with a low curve and few ground bodies.
- Count pips and match basics to them. 9/8 splits suit even decks. Avoid double pips in both colours.
- Play 5 or more two-drops only if they are good ones. Cap ground filler at a few cards: tonight the 0-3 BG, 1-2 WR, 1-2 UR and 2-1 BR decks were all ground-heavy and lost to fliers.
- Main your fliers: a WR deck left two Moon Herons in the sideboard for Unruly Mob and Riot Devils and lost stalls to fliers (anecdote).
- Play at least four evasive or removal-backed finishers and four or more answers to fliers; three answers did not save a BG deck (Tribute to Hunger, Spidery Grasp, Corpse Lunge).
- Keep 4 or more removal in the main, paired with evasion. Include one bigger late-game threat over another two-drop.
- Keep one or two card-advantage cards (Moan of the Unhallowed, Altar's Reap) for stalls (anecdote). A thin WB deck without them lost long games.
- Do not main Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone or Mulch.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small early ground bodies. Games ran 14 to 35 turns.
- Break stalls with removal, fight spells, Spider Spawning and evasive threats, not more ground bodies.
- Spread threats, since Rebuke and Smite the Monstrous kill attackers. Expect Victim of Night to kill your best early threat.
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Angelic Overseer, Battleground Geist and Murder of Crows.
- Against WU, kill the fliers early; Claustrophobia and Sensory Deprivation will target your best creature.
- Against BR, block early and value two-drops over top-end; Brimstone Volley and Typhoid Rats punish slow hands.
- Against BG, expect Typhoid Rats and Somberwald Spider to make the ground hard. Win through the air with removal backup, and answer Somberwald Spider and Tribute to Hunger early (a WU deck lost 0-2 to BG on this, anecdote).
