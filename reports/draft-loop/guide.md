# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-18's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Twenty-nine nights: 232 decks, 858 games. Guided seats are 314-334 in games (48%, 128-136 in matches, 88 decks). The conventional guide is 72-69 (51%, 30-30 in matches, 20 decks). Unguided seats are 472-455 (51%, 190-182 in matches, 124 decks). On the ten nights with all three kinds of seat, guided went 64-80 in games (44%, 27-33 in matches), conventional 72-69 (51%) and unguided 156-143 (52%). Guided is still behind both, and this guide has not been shown to help. Treat archetype lines as hypotheses and lean on card numbers and on advice that is consistent across lessons:
- Commit to two colours early (by mid pack 1). Stranded early picks were the most repeated lesson. On 2026-11-18 a guided WR took Victim of Night second and never played it; a guided WB that committed by mid pack 1 went 3-0 with all three first picks (Falkenrath Noble, Corpse Lunge, Voiceless Spirit) in the deck, while a second Woodland Sleuth and Nightbird's Clutches were wasted picks (anecdotes). Earlier: Tribute to Hunger and Typhoid Rats in a WR, Sever the Bloodline in a UG, Curiosity and Spare from Evil in a BR (anecdotes).
- Count playables and keep a curve of cheap creatures. Games run long (15 to 38 turns on 2026-11-18), so cheap creatures need a way to finish.
- Take removal, but only in your colours.
- Have two or three real threats that win in the air or in a long game. Fliers alone do not suffice: on 2026-11-18 a guided WR with cheap creatures, Rally the Peasants and four fliers went 0-3 (0-6 in games, 17 to 38 turns), and on 2026-11-17 another guided WR with four fliers also went 0-3 (anecdotes). But decks short of fliers lost to fliers too: on 2026-11-18 a BR with only Vampire Interloper as a flier lost to UG's Spider Spawning and Murder of Crows, and a WG with Chapel Geist as its only flier stalled in 16 to 32 turn games (anecdotes).

Pairs by games won (decks, matches, game win rate), from the statistics up to 2026-11-18:
- BG: 54% (210 games, 29 decks, 48-39 in matches). Best by matches among big pairs. Take cheap removal and early blockers over a fourth fatty, and keep a flier answer. A low-curve green-based BG went 3-0 earlier (Ambush Viper, Kindercatch, Lumberknot, Moldgraf Monstrosity); a top-heavy BG went 0-3 (anecdotes).
- RG: 54% (92 games, 12 decks, 20-16 in matches). Small sample. Needs cheap blockers, ways to reach fliers, and an answer to Claustrophobia and Victim of Night. An unguided RG went 3-0 on burn and fight (Prey Upon, Brimstone Volley, Geistflame); a conventional RG with two Orchard Spirits went 1-2 (anecdotes).
- WR: 52% (206 games, 28 decks, 44-40 in matches). Slipping: all three guided WR decks of 2026-11-17 and 2026-11-18 combined went 2-7 in matches, with two 0-3s (anecdotes). The 2026-11-18 0-3 ran 10 Plains/7 Mountain with Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Into the Maw of Hell and two Tormented Pariah, and its drafter blamed too few Mountains. Core cards still test well (Ashmouth Hound, Avacynian Priest, Rebuke, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith, Smite the Monstrous, Furor of the Bitten), but a WR needs removal-backed finishers beyond ground bodies, and a heavy red load wants 8 or 9 Mountains.
- WU: 52% (207 games, 28 decks, 43-41 in matches). White removal plus real cheap fliers (Midnight Haunting, Invisible Stalker, Chapel Geist, Abbey Griffin, Stitched Drake, Moorland Haunt) works; an unguided WU went 3-0 on 9 Plains/8 Islands on 2026-11-17 (anecdote). Take evasive threats over more interaction.
- WG: 51% (164 games, 23 decks, 37-32 in matches). Swings with the build. On 2026-11-18 a conventional WG went 2-1 but its 16 to 32 turn games stalled with Chapel Geist as the only flier: draft evasion and card advantage over extra ground bodies (anecdote). Needs cheap plays and a 10-source main colour.
- BR: 50% (204 games, 28 decks, 45-39 in matches). Two BRs on 2026-11-18 each went 2-1 (one unguided, one conventional; anecdotes). Black flowed there and Dead Weight went first pick in packs 2 and 3. The losses were to a UG with Spider Spawning and Murder of Crows, and to a removal-heavy WB (Fiend Hunter, Smite the Monstrous, Rebuke) in 21 to 29 turn games. BR wants early drops, removal and evasive finishers like Falkenrath Noble, plus card advantage. Skip Ancient Grudge and Heartless Summoning.
- WB: 47% (150 games, 20 decks, 30-30 in matches). Below average overall, but on 2026-11-18 a guided white-first WB on 11 Plains/6 Swamp went 3-0 with Fiend Hunter, Rebuke, Smite the Monstrous, Voiceless Spirit, Abbey Griffin, Falkenrath Noble and Corpse Lunge; six removal spells, Doomed Traveler, Avacynian Priest and Selfless Cathar curved out, and Rally the Peasants closed long games (anecdote). It also beat BR's grind. Count BB pips; go white-first.
- UR: 46% (156 games, 21 decks, 28-35 in matches). Weak. On 2026-11-18 an unguided UR went 1-2 (2-5 in games), losing 0-2 to a fast WB and to a BR in 19 to 24 turn games; its removal-heavy build never closed (anecdote). Earlier a red-first UR with Geistflame, Rage Thrower and Sulfur Falls went 3-0. Make UR red-first, with burn and an evasive clock (Spectral Flight, Pitchburn Devils) rather than Fortress Crab-style defence.
- UB: 45% (220 games, 29 decks, 34-53 in matches). Worst big pair. On 2026-11-18 an unguided UB went 1-2 with thin self-mill (Ghoulcaller's Chant and Ghoulcaller's Bell left out) and both losses went to three games; its removal (two Tribute to Hunger, Sensory Deprivation, Corpse Lunge) did carry a 2-0 over WR (anecdote). UB needs a low curve, cheap blockers and cheap pressure (Falkenrath Noble). Do not take Delver of Secrets, Think Twice or Dream Twist.
- UG: 44% (54 games, 7 decks, 8-13 in matches). Weak overall. On 2026-11-18 an unguided UG went 1-2, losing to WB and UR fliers in 15 to 34 turn games and winning the one it sided into (Fortress Crab, Moonmist, Naturalize, Pitchburn Devils) over BR (anecdote). Mono U is 4-5 in matches (21 games) and mono R 3-3 (16 games).

General notes:
- White is the best-supported colour on individual cards (Slayer of the Wicked, Midnight Haunting, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Bonds of Faith). Red also has many high-win-rate cards (Stromkirk Noble, Furor of the Bitten, Into the Maw of Hell, Kruin Outlaw, Ashmouth Hound). Green has Moldgraf Monstrosity, Lumberknot, Gatstaf Shepherd and Villagers of Estwald. Cheap white removal (Rebuke, Fiend Hunter) is contested, so take it early.
- Black is crowded in general, but it flowed on 2026-11-18 (anecdote). Take black removal early in pack 1, but only if you will play black. Black is a safe anchor when blue and red are thin.
- Read what is flowing after pack 1. If strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8, move in. Judge by real playables.
- Commit: main colour by about pick 5, a pair by mid pack 1, your second colour by pick 5 of pack 2 at the latest. Off-colour early picks were wasted repeatedly (anecdotes): on 2026-11-18 Victim of Night in a WR; on 2026-11-17 Tribute to Hunger and Typhoid Rats in a WR, Sever the Bloodline and Trepanation Blade in a UG, Curiosity and Spare from Evil in a BR; on 2026-11-16 a first-pick Olivia Voldaren in a WU; on 2026-11-15 Into the Maw of Hell and Furor of the Bitten in a WB. Do not leave a colour for one early card, bomb or not, unless the new colour is clearly open. If you take a bomb, stay in its colour.
- Colourless playables are a reasonable early hedge, but Galvanic Juggernaut is heavily contested (a BR took it first in pack 2 on 2026-11-18 while a WG across the table also ran it; anecdote). Do not take it over removal.
- Games run 11 to 62 turns, mostly 15 to 38. Grindy removal and card advantage matter, and cheap starts only win if you can close.
- Splashing is poor. Do not run a colour on six basics unless it is a light second colour with a dual land and few cards.

## Commons that win

Deck game win rate for decks running the card (statistics to 2026-11-18).

- Green: Villagers of Estwald (243 games, 56%), Woodland Sleuth (230 games, 55%), Grave Bramble (191 games, 54%, taken at 9.0), Avacyn's Pilgrim (130 games, 54%, taken at 9.1), Festerhide Boar (287 games, 53%, filler; a third copy was a wasted pick on 2026-11-18, take a flier or cheap removal over it), Kindercatch (296 games, 53%), Prey Upon (268 games, 53%, taken at 2.8), Grizzled Outcasts (168 games, 52%). Spidery Grasp, Ambush Viper and Darkthicket Wolf were dependable (anecdotes). Somberwald Spider is a flier answer.
- Black: Typhoid Rats (317 games, 55%), Victim of Night (315 games, 53%, taken at 1.6; it cannot target Vampires, Werewolves or Zombies, so check the opponent), Dead Weight, Corpse Lunge, Bump in the Night, Ghoulcaller's Chant (wanted by self-mill decks).
- White: Voiceless Spirit (400 games, 55%), Avacynian Priest (345 games, 56%), Bonds of Faith (339 games, 55%), Thraben Sentry (260 games, 53%, taken at 7.6), Unruly Mob (291 games, 53%, taken at 8.9; one is fine, a second lost a WR on 2026-11-17), Selfless Cathar, Chapel Geist, Doomed Traveler, Silverchase Fox (ground filler).
- Red: Furor of the Bitten (160 games, 56%, taken at 9.6; a pump, not removal), Ashmouth Hound (295 games, 55%), Tormented Pariah, Pitchburn Devils, Night Revelers, Geistflame, Brimstone Volley, Curse of the Pierced Heart and Traitorous Blood (marginal playables).
- Blue: Spectral Flight (275 games, 53%), Hysterical Blindness (late pick), Frightful Delusion (anecdote).
- Colourless: Silver-Inlaid Dagger (53% earlier, uncommon). Blazing Torch was praised twice (anecdotes).
- Claustrophobia, Rebuke, Dead Weight, Tribute to Hunger, Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Sensory Deprivation and Stitched Drake are praised in lessons and go early, but only on colour.

## Uncommons and rares

- Green: Moldgraf Monstrosity (63 games, 59%, rare, taken at 4.3), Lumberknot (192 games, 56%), Gatstaf Shepherd (206 games, 55%), Spider Spawning (170 games, 54%; it helped a UG beat a BR on 2026-11-18, anecdote), Hollowhenge Scavenger (140 games, 54%), Hamlet Captain (193 games, 53%), Boneyard Wurm (dies to Smite the Monstrous; do not take a second). Spider Spawning needs self-mill enablers, and enablers need it. Garruk Relentless carried a 2-1 BG (anecdote).
- Black: Disciple of Griselbrand (140 games, 58%, taken at 9.1), Morkrut Banshee (226 games, 53%, taken at 1.7; BB on 7 Swamps went poorly), Bloodgift Demon, Sever the Bloodline (rare, 70% earlier in 46 games), Falkenrath Noble (in the 3-0 WB and a UB win), Crossway Vampire (a third is filler), Curse of Death's Hold (slow in a 1-2 UB). Abattoir Ghoul and Ghoulraiser went 1-2 in a WB (anecdote).
- Red: Stromkirk Noble (81 games, 58%, rare, taken at 4.9), Into the Maw of Hell (227 games, 56%, taken at 3.4), Falkenrath Marauders (61% earlier in 57 games, rare), Kruin Outlaw (96 games, 53%, rare), Hanweir Watchkeep (146 games, 53%), Rage Thrower, Rakish Heir, Skirsdag Cultist, Instigator Gang, Vampire Interloper.
- White: Slayer of the Wicked (208 games, 61%, taken at 1.8), Midnight Haunting (189 games, 58%), Elite Inquisitor (90 games, 58%, rare), Mentor of the Meek (87 games, 55%, rare), Rally the Peasants (186 games, 53%, taken at 6.8; closed long games in the 3-0 WB), Fiend Hunter (in the 3-0 WB), Angelic Overseer, Divine Reckoning, Smite the Monstrous (kills power-4 creatures; in the 3-0 WB and earlier a 3-0 WU, anecdotes). Cloistered Youth is only average.
- Blue: Murder of Crows (only if blue), Invisible Stalker, Stitched Drake, Mindshrieker (72 games, 53%, rare), Battleground Geist (212 games, 52%), Moon Heron and Orchard Spirit. Blue fliers beat ground decks on 2026-11-17 and 2026-11-18 (Murder of Crows with Spider Spawning beat a BR) but lost earlier to Somberwald Spider and Victim of Night.
- Colourless: Manor Gargoyle (104 games, 56%, rare, taken at 2.2). Galvanic Juggernaut (272 games, 54%, taken at 1.2) is overdrafted: do not take it over removal or a strong on-colour card, and do not rely on it alone to close. Smite the Monstrous hits it.
- Lands: Moorland Haunt (63% earlier in 51 games, comes late), Sulfur Falls (60 games, 55%, in blue-red decks, taken at 11.1), Woodland Cemetery in B/G, Gavony Township in a long-game white deck (anecdote).
- Bombs: Bloodline Keeper lost on 7 Swamps in a BG and a WB and was the only finisher in a 1-2 WB. Garruk Relentless never made a WU deck but won in a BG. Olivia Voldaren was first-picked and never played in a WU. Take bombs only if they will be on colour and castable, and back them with a second threat.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal on colour: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia (blue), Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Smite the Monstrous, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith, Fiend Hunter, Sensory Deprivation (blue), Corpse Lunge, Harvest Pyre, Spidery Grasp, Bramblecrush, Blazing Torch. Take a second if offered. About five is the target, but six worked in the 3-0 WB on 2026-11-18 when the creature base was cheap and Rally the Peasants closed (anecdote); a fourth removal spell lost to a second threat in an earlier WB. Do not take removal outside the colours you are likely to play, and do not take it second pick before you know your colours.
2. Strong uncommons and rares on colour: Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Moldgraf Monstrosity, Sever the Bloodline, Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee (if you can support BB), Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders, Instigator Gang, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw, Rally the Peasants, Midnight Haunting, Angelic Overseer, Garruk Relentless (green only).
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Invisible Stalker, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Mindshrieker, Battleground Geist, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Somberwald Spider. Many losing decks lacked fliers or answers, but several decks with three or four fliers also lost (WR twice, WG, UB), so fliers do not replace cheap plays and removal. Aim for three or four evasive threats or answers by the end of pack 3, and take a third flier over a second Unruly Mob, Silverchase Fox, Thraben Purebloods, Festerhide Boar or Gatstaf Shepherd. Do not take an off-colour flier over an on-colour one. Against green, count reach. Card advantage (Mentor of the Meek, Ghoulcaller's Chant in self-mill, Spider Spawning, Rally the Peasants) was asked for in several lessons from long games.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Avacyn's Pilgrim, Ambush Viper, Kindercatch, Darkthicket Wolf (in green), Ashmouth Hound, Feral Ridgewolf, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils and Tormented Pariah if red, Silver-Inlaid Dagger, Unruly Mob (one), Doomed Traveler, Avacynian Priest, Vampire Interloper, Bump in the Night. Take cheap threats and early evasive creatures so one removal spell cannot blank your curve, and take them over card-draw filler, counterspells and bounce. Take playable two-drops over late-pick junk such as Delver of Secrets, Cobbled Wings and Spare from Evil (all went unplayed on 2026-11-18). Against WR, prefer cheap creatures to a second four-power body (Smite the Monstrous).

Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell or card draw. Walking Corpse made a 3-0 BG and a 0-3 BR (both anecdotes), so it is only a 23rd card. Galvanic Juggernaut is an acceptable early colourless hedge but not over removal.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Stitcher's Apprentice, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Ancient Grudge, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab over a better creature (keep it only as a cheap blocker), Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Bloodcrazed Neonate, Make a Wish, Grimoire of the Dead, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Trepanation Blade, Delver of Secrets, Heartless Summoning, Curiosity, Spare from Evil, Nightbird's Clutches, Mask of Avacyn over a flier, Riot Devils, Kessig Wolf, Cloistered Youth over a flier, a second Moment of Heroism, a second Makeshift Mauler, and a third Festerhide Boar. Naturalize, Wooden Stakes and Sharpened Pitchfork are narrow filler (a BR ran the last two and went 2-1, but its drafter would cut them; anecdote). Lost in the Mist is only worth a second copy in a controlling blue deck (a UG drafter wished for it; anecdote). Armored Skaab, Skaab Goliath and Forbidden Alchemy are only for a deck with Spider Spawning and real self-mill. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default. On 2026-11-18 a WG's 16 to 32 turn games supported it, and the 16-land option is risky when boards stall (anecdotes). Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 WB, 2-1 WB), 11 Plains/6 Swamp (3-0 WB on 2026-11-18), 11 Plains/6 Islands (2-1 WU), 9 Plains/8 Islands (3-0 WU), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG), mostly red with 6 Islands plus Sulfur Falls (3-0 UR) (anecdotes). Count pips and give the main colour 9 to 10 sources. A 9 Forest/7 Plains WG went 0-3, and WRs on 6 or 7 Mountains and on 7 Mountains (10 Plains/7 Mountain, 0-3 on 2026-11-18) went badly with a heavy red load (anecdotes). Give a colour with several double-pip or removal cards eight or nine sources, not six or seven.
- Double pips: give a BB or GG colour 8+ sources or cut them. A WB with Morkrut Banshee and Abattoir Ghoul on 7 Swamps went 2-4 in games, and Bloodline Keeper on 7 Swamps lost; but a 3-0 BR with Bloodgift Demon ran 7 Swamps and a white-first WB on 6 Swamps went 3-0 (anecdotes). Avoid double pips in both colours. Three GG Somberwald Spiders want 8+ Forests. With removal in both colours of a UB, aim for 9/8.
- Aim for about five or six real removal spells (pumps like Furor of the Bitten do not count), three or four answers to fliers (which can also clear ground blockers), and two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers. Play the removal you drafted.
- Keep a curve with enough cheap plays. The 0-3 BG, 0-3 WRs and 0-3 ground BRs were top-heavy or full of ground filler, while winning decks (3-0 BG, 3-0 RG, 3-0 WU, 3-0 WB) had early drops and burn or removal. A low curve alone is not enough: UBs with cheap cards but no early pressure or evasion lost 17 to 26 turn games, and the 0-3 WR had cheap creatures and still lost every game. Count two-drops and early evasive creatures.
- Keep a flexible late threat or value source (Mentor of the Meek, Moan of the Unhallowed, Gavony Township, Moorland Haunt, Rally the Peasants, Divine Reckoning, Spider Spawning), but do not cut good cheap cards to fit one.
- Pair removal with good cheap creatures and close with fliers. Do not pad with ground filler when a flier or removal spell is available. If you have no real fliers, keep your reach.
- Self-mill needs a real plan: a UB that left Ghoulcaller's Chant and Ghoulcaller's Bell in the sideboard had thin fuel (anecdote). Either draft enablers and payoffs together or play a plain removal deck.
- Tricks have a place: an RG with one Ranger's Guile and no Moment of Heroism had its werewolves answered by Rebuke and Smite the Monstrous (anecdote). One or two cheap tricks in a ground deck is fine, after removal and creatures.
- Do not leave yourself with no answer to an opposing colour's key cards: an RG with no black had nothing for Claustrophobia and Victim of Night decks. Enchantment-based removal on your best creature is a real risk; keep multiple threats. Creatures with toughness above one matter against Geistflame, Brimstone Volley and Harvest Pyre. Creatures with power 4+ are exposed to Smite the Monstrous.
- Settle your 23 in the main deck by pack 3. Sideboard plans rarely rescue a deck, though a BR beat a WG 2-1 on 2026-11-18 after siding in Kessig Wolf Run, Trepanation Blade and Undead Alchemist for Kessig Wolf, Traitorous Blood and Typhoid Rats, and a UG won game two after swapping in Fortress Crab, Moonmist, Naturalize and Pitchburn Devils (anecdotes). Plan post-board swaps before round one, check every swap against your colours, and do not side out main removal or threats for situational cards. Keep at least one cheap play main.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core. Do not main Gnaw to the Bone, Mulch, Dream Twist, Make a Wish or self-mill cards without a self-mill deck. Cut a redundant combat trick before a playable creature, and cut filler before a proactive creature.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke, Fiend Hunter and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies. Check creature types before counting on Victim of Night (no Vampires, Werewolves or Zombies).
- Expect long games, often 15 to 40 turns, and up to 62. Break stalls with removal, Spider Spawning, Rally the Peasants, Moan of the Unhallowed and evasive threats. A UG lost a 34-turn ground grind; favour Murder of Crows-style evasion over extra ground blockers when the board stalls. Apply pressure early rather than waiting, especially with UB, UR and WU.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR. Fast starts beat slow hands in 9 to 17 turns on some nights (anecdotes), but WR games ran 13 to 38 turns, so pair cheap creatures with late fliers or Rally the Peasants to close.
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Angelic Overseer, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Murder of Crows, Midnight Haunting, Falkenrath Noble, Screeching Bat, Galvanic Juggernaut, Boneyard Wurm and Scourge of Geier Reach.
- Against WU, UB, UR, UG and mono-blue, kill fliers early. Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation and Lost in the Mist will target your best creature, so keep a second threat ready. Blue decks win long grinds, so do not durdle and keep a flier answer.
- Against WB, expect real removal (Fiend Hunter, Smite the Monstrous, Rebuke, Victim of Night) held for your fliers and best creatures, plus Angelic Overseer, Chapel Geist and Rally the Peasants closing long games; a WB beat a BR in a 21 to 29 turn grind on 2026-11-18. Do not rely on one big threat, and bring card advantage.
- Against BG, WG and RG, expect Typhoid Rats, Somberwald Spider, Spidery Grasp, Ghostly Possession, Fiend Hunter, Prey Upon, Garruk Relentless and burn to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup, expect reach, and do not flood the board with ground bodies.
- Against WR, BR and UR, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Furor of the Bitten and Pitchburn Devils to punish small creatures. Do not overcommit and keep your own fliers alive. WR also runs Rebuke (sometimes two), Smite the Monstrous, Midnight Haunting, Fiend Hunter, Slayer of the Wicked, Rally the Peasants, Ghostly Possession and Bonds of Faith, and will answer your fliers, tricked-up creatures and four-power creatures.
- Against white decks with Fiend Hunter and Avacynian Priest, expect one-for-one trades on early creatures; prefer threats that leave value behind. Expect Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning in long games.
