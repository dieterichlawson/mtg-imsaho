# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-10-29's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Nine nights: 72 decks, 263 games. Guided seats are 95-106 in games (47%, 38-46 in matches); unguided are 168-157 (52%, 70-62). The guide has not been shown to help, so judge by the card numbers and do not follow any line here blindly.

- BG is the best pair: 29-16 in matches, 59% of games (107 games, 15 decks). Tonight BG was 1-2 (a guided 17-land deck with one Somberwald Spider and ground filler, lost four games to fliers in 16 to 38 turns, one deck, anecdote). BG with ground bodies only loses. BG with evasive finishers or Spider Spawning still wins.
- WU 55% (66 games, 9 decks), BR 55% (44 games, 6 decks; tonight a BR deck went 3-0, anecdote), WR 54% (57 games, 8 decks).
- Middling: WG 47% (43 games, 6 decks; tonight a WG deck with Spider Spawning, Voiceless Spirit, Orchard Spirit and Rebuke went 2-1, so WG is not clearly bad), RG 50% (14 games).
- Weak: UR 45% (58 games, 8 decks), UB 42% (62 games, 8 decks; two more UB decks went 1-2 and 0-3 tonight), UG 38% (16 games).
- WB is 4-8 in matches, 34% of games (29 games, 4 decks). Thin, but a WB deck went 2-1 tonight on cheap removal.
- Games stall for 14 to 55 turns. Removal plus evasive finishers or Spider Spawning break stalls. Ground bodies alone and slow fliers did not.
- Green or black first, with white-blue, white-red or black-red as alternatives. Pick a main colour by about pick 5 and a pair by mid pack 1. Do not leave a colour for one early removal spell unless the new colour is clearly open. Avoid UB: two UB drafters abandoned green and wasted Prey Upon, Darkthicket Wolf and Ambush Viper.
- Splashing is poor (a UR deck with five Mountains could not cast its red). One fixer such as Avacyn's Pilgrim or Shimmering Grotto in a near two-colour base was fine (anecdote).
- Mono-colour is risky: a mono-red deck with little removal went 1-2 (anecdote). Keep playable off-colour cards until your pair is clear.
- Read what is passed to you. Two decks of the same pair often share a table, so take contested playables earlier or move.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal: Victim of Night, Prey Upon, Dead Weight, Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Sever the Bloodline, Rebuke, Claustrophobia, Smite the Monstrous, Bonds of Faith, Fiend Hunter, Tribute to Hunger.
2. Strong uncommons and rares: Spider Spawning, Morkrut Banshee, Falkenrath Noble, Slayer of the Wicked, Falkenrath Marauders, Galvanic Juggernaut, Manor Gargoyle.
3. Evasive finishers on colour: Voiceless Spirit, Chapel Geist, Midnight Haunting, Stitched Drake, Moon Heron, Battleground Geist. Take them over a fourth removal spell or a duplicate trick.
4. Good commons: Villagers of Estwald, Typhoid Rats, Kindercatch, Woodland Sleuth, Somberwald Spider, Avacynian Priest, Festerhide Boar.

Take removal or evasion over filler. Skip curses (Curse of the Bloody Tome and Curse of the Pierced Heart were cut), equipment, Cobbled Wings, Rooftop Storm, Armored Skaab, Manor Skeleton, Fortress Crab, Walking Corpse and Rotting Fensnake. Take on-colour two-drops over speculation. Once your pair is set, do not take off-colour cards. Early picks in a colour you abandon are wasted.

## Building

- Land count is unsettled. 17 lands on a low curve went 1-2 (UB and BG tonight), but 17 also went 2-1 (WG) and 16 is untested. Play 16 with a low curve (try this), 17 with several 5-drops. Land count did not explain tonight's BG loss; lack of evasion did.
- Count pips first. Split lands by pips and card quality, and avoid double pips in both colours. 9 Mountain and 8 Swamp worked for a 3-0 BR deck, and a 10 Plains white-heavy WU deck went 2-1 (anecdotes).
- Play 5 or more two-drops, at least three evasive or removal-backed finishers (two evasive finishers were not enough for UB), and at least two answers to fliers. Somberwald Spider and Spider Spawning answer fliers in green.
- Cap ground filler at a few cards. Ground-body decks lost long stalls repeatedly.
- Keep 4 or more removal in the main, but removal alone did not save a BG deck facing fliers. Pair it with evasion.
- Keep one or two card-advantage cards (Moan of the Unhallowed, Altar's Reap) in decks that expect stalls (anecdote).
- Do not main Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone or Mulch.

## Playing

- Trade early and hold removal for fliers and big blockers. Do not spend Rebuke or Smite the Monstrous on small ground bodies in a stall.
- Break stalls with removal, fight spells, Spider Spawning and evasive threats, not more ground bodies. Fast fliers beat slow evasion.
- Spread threats, since Rebuke and Smite the Monstrous kill attackers. Expect Victim of Night to kill your best early threat.
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Abbey Griffin and Murder of Crows. Slow fliers like Moon Heron lost to BG earlier (anecdote), so pressure early or hold the ground.
- Against BG, expect Typhoid Rats and Somberwald Spider to make the ground hard. Win through the air with removal backup; fliers plus removal beat BG 2-0 in 16 turns (anecdote).
- Against BR aggro (Brimstone Volley, Rakish Heir, Typhoid Rats), block early and value two-drops over top-end.
