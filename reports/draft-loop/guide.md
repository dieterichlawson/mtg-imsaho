# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-16's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Twenty-seven nights: 216 decks, 798 games. The guided seats are 302-317 in games (49%, 123-129 in matches, 84 decks). The conventional guide is 54-57 (49%, 23-25 in matches, 16 decks). Unguided seats are 442-424 (51%, 178-170 in matches, 116 decks). On the eight nights with all three kinds of seat, guided went 52-63 in games (45%, 22-26 in matches), conventional 54-57 (49%) and unguided 126-112 (53%). Guided is still behind both, and this guide has not been shown to help. Treat archetype lines as hypotheses and lean on card numbers and on advice that is consistent across lessons:
- Commit to two colours early (by mid pack 1). Early picks stranded in a colour you do not play were the most repeated lesson, and it happened again on 2026-11-16 with a first-pick Olivia Voldaren that never made a WU deck.
- Count playables and keep a curve of cheap creatures. Games on 2026-11-16 ran 11 to 27 turns even for low-curve decks, so cheap creatures need a way to finish.
- Take removal, but only in your colours.
- Have two or three real threats that win in the air or in a long game. Fliers alone do not suffice, and fliers lost to reach (Somberwald Spider) and removal on 2026-11-16.

Pairs by games won (decks, matches, game win rate), from the statistics up to 2026-11-16:
- WR: 55% (184 games, 25 decks, 42-33 in matches). Best pair by games, tied with RG. On 2026-11-16 two guided WR decks both went 2-1 and both lost to RG in 21 to 27 turns. One had played Riot Devils and Kessig Wolf, the other Silverchase Fox and Unruly Mob over a third flier (anecdotes). Earlier, a guided WR with three fliers and ground filler went 0-3 on 6 Mountains (2026-11-14). Core: cheap creatures (Ashmouth Hound, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Avacynian Priest), removal (Rebuke, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith), Rally the Peasants, Furor of the Bitten and real fliers (Voiceless Spirit, Midnight Haunting). Take a third or fourth flier or removal over ground filler, and give red enough Mountains. Expect RG opponents.
- RG: 55% (84 games, 11 decks, 19-14 in matches). On 2026-11-16 an unguided RG went 3-0 on burn and fight (Prey Upon, Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Into the Maw of Hell) with green 2-3 drops (anecdote). It beat both WR decks above. Earlier needs: cheap blockers, ways to reach fliers, and an answer to Claustrophobia and Victim of Night.
- BG: 54% (203 games, 28 decks, 46-38 in matches). On 2026-11-16 an unguided BG went 2-1. Garruk Relentless, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Tribute to Hunger and Morkrut Banshee beat BR and UB, and three Darkthicket Wolves and Spider Spawning were dependable, but it lost 1-2 to WR in 20 to 22 turns with a slow top end (anecdote). Earlier a green-based BG went 3-0 on a low curve with Ambush Viper, Kindercatch, Lumberknot and Moldgraf Monstrosity. A top-heavy BG (Boneyard Wurm, Lumberknot, two Prey Upon) went 0-3. Take cheap removal and early blockers over a fourth fatty.
- WR/BG aside, WU: 51% (201 games, 27 decks, 40-41 in matches). Average and often too slow. On 2026-11-16 an unguided WU with Rebuke and Lost in the Mist ground through 19 to 25 turn games and went 1-2 (anecdote). White removal plus real cheap fliers works (Midnight Haunting, Invisible Stalker, Moorland Haunt). Take evasive threats over more interaction.
- WG: 51% (156 games, 22 decks, 35-31 in matches). It swings with the build. Earlier a guided WG with four fliers went 0-3 on 9 Forest/7 Plains with early picks never played. You need cheap plays and a 10-source main colour (anecdote).
- BR: 50% (181 games, 25 decks, 40-35 in matches). On 2026-11-16 a conventional BR went 0-3 (1-6 in games) with a ground-creature curve (including a third Crossway Vampire and Walking Corpse), stalling against Boneyard Wurm and Somberwald Spider in 17 to 30 turns (anecdote). Earlier a ground BR on 17 lands also went 0-3. Red removal is contested by other BR drafters (Geistflame, Brimstone Volley). BR wants early drops, removal and evasive finishers like Falkenrath Noble. Play your removal, and skip Ancient Grudge and Heartless Summoning-type speculation.
- UR: 46% (149 games, 20 decks, 27-33 in matches). Weak overall, but an unguided red-first UR with Geistflame, Rage Thrower and light blue (six Islands plus Sulfur Falls) went 3-0 on 2026-11-14 (anecdote). Make UR red-first with burn.
- UB: 45% (204 games, 27 decks, 32-49 in matches). Worst big pair. On 2026-11-16 a conventional UB went 0-3 (Stitched Drake, Moon Heron and Battleground Geist lost to Somberwald Spider and Victim of Night; Bloodcrazed Neonate, Cellar Door, Dream Twist and Memory's Journey were cut), while an unguided UB went 2-1 but with 18 to 26 turn games and no early pressure. Smite the Monstrous in a WR deck also punished its Skaab Goliath and Galvanic Juggernaut (anecdotes). UB needs a low curve, cheap blockers and cheap removal. Do not take Delver of Secrets or Dream Twist.
- WB: 45% (143 games, 19 decks, 27-30 in matches). Below average and unproven. Earlier a guided WB (Ghoulraiser, Abattoir Ghoul, Morkrut Banshee on 10 Plains/7 Swamps) and a conventional WB (Bloodline Keeper) both went 1-2 (anecdotes). Count BB pips, and take a second threat over a fourth removal spell.
- UG: 45% (38 games, 5 decks, 5-10 in matches). Weak overall. Fliers plus Claustrophobia worked once (Moon Heron, Orchard Spirit); a self-mill build without Spider Spawning did not (anecdotes). Mono U is 4-5 in matches (21 games) and mono R 3-3 (16 games).

General notes:
- White is the best-supported colour on individual cards (Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Midnight Haunting, Bonds of Faith). Red also has many high-win-rate cards (Furor of the Bitten, Ashmouth Hound, Into the Maw of Hell, Stromkirk Noble, Kruin Outlaw). Green has Moldgraf Monstrosity, Lumberknot, Gatstaf Shepherd and Villagers of Estwald. Cheap white removal (Rebuke, Fiend Hunter) is contested, so take it early.
- Black is crowded. Take black removal early in pack 1, but only if you will play black.
- Read what is flowing after pack 1. If strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8, move in. Judge by real playables.
- Commit: main colour by about pick 5, a pair by mid pack 1, your second colour by pick 5 of pack 2 at the latest. Off-colour early picks were wasted repeatedly (anecdotes):
  - On 2026-11-16: a first-pick Olivia Voldaren in a WU; two Riot Devils in a WR.
  - On 2026-11-15: Into the Maw of Hell and Furor of the Bitten in a WB; Stitched Drake, Battleground Geist and Moon Heron in a 3-0 BG; Victim of Night and Dead Weight in a UG; Heartless Summoning first-picked in pack 3 by a BR.
  - Earlier: Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation, Garruk Relentless, two Dead Weight and two Vampiric Fury, a first-pick Fiend Hunter.
  Do not leave a colour for one early card, bomb or not, unless the new colour is clearly open. If you take a bomb, stay in its colour.
- Games run 11 to 62 turns, mostly 15 to 37, and two-thirds of recent anecdotes were 18 or more turns. Grindy removal and card advantage matter, and cheap starts only win if you can close.
- Splashing is poor. Do not run a colour on six basics unless it is a light second colour with a dual land and few cards (the 3-0 UR).

## Commons that win

Deck game win rate for decks running the card (statistics to 2026-11-16).

- Green: Villagers of Estwald (212 games, 56%), Woodland Sleuth (222 games, 55%), Grave Bramble (184 games, 54%, taken at 8.8), Avacyn's Pilgrim (122 games, 53%, taken at 9.2), Festerhide Boar (264 games, 53%, filler), Kindercatch (265 games, 53%), Spidery Grasp (262 games, 53%, taken at 8.4), Ambush Viper (anecdote), Prey Upon (carried the 3-0 RG and 2-1 BG, anecdotes). Darkthicket Wolf was dependable in an unguided BG (anecdote). Somberwald Spider is a flier answer.
- Black: Typhoid Rats (303 games, 55%), Victim of Night (298 games, 53%, taken at 1.6; it cannot target Vampires, Werewolves or Zombies, so check the opponent), Bump in the Night, Dead Weight, Ghoulcaller's Chant.
- White: Bonds of Faith (310 games, 56%), Avacynian Priest (316 games, 54%), Voiceless Spirit (379 games, 54%), Thraben Sentry (260 games, 53%, taken at 7.6), Unruly Mob (275 games, 53%, taken at 9.0; one is fine), Selfless Cathar, Chapel Geist, Silverchase Fox (ground filler).
- Red: Furor of the Bitten (138 games, 60%, taken at 9.9; a pump, not removal), Ashmouth Hound (264 games, 57%), Tormented Pariah (271 games, 54%), Pitchburn Devils (323 games, 53%), Night Revelers, Geistflame, Brimstone Volley, Curse of the Pierced Heart and Traitorous Blood (marginal playables).
- Blue: Spectral Flight, Hysterical Blindness (late picks), Frightful Delusion (anecdote).
- Colourless: Silver-Inlaid Dagger (154 games, 53%, uncommon). Blazing Torch was praised twice (anecdotes).
- Claustrophobia, Rebuke, Dead Weight, Tribute to Hunger, Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Sensory Deprivation and Stitched Drake are praised in lessons and go early, but only on colour.

## Uncommons and rares

- Green: Moldgraf Monstrosity (57 games, 61%, rare, taken at 3.9), Lumberknot (176 games, 57%), Gatstaf Shepherd (184 games, 56%), Spider Spawning (154 games, 55%), Hollowhenge Scavenger (140 games, 54%), Hamlet Captain (178 games, 53%, but it was cut for early board presence in a 2-1 BG, anecdote), Boneyard Wurm (dies to Smite the Monstrous; do not take a second). Spider Spawning needs self-mill enablers, and enablers need it. Garruk Relentless carried a 2-1 BG (anecdote).
- Black: Disciple of Griselbrand (126 games, 58%, taken at 9.1), Morkrut Banshee (211 games, 53%, taken at 1.7; BB on 7 Swamps went poorly), Bloodgift Demon, Sever the Bloodline (rare, 70% earlier in 46 games), Falkenrath Noble (carried a UB win, anecdote), Crossway Vampire (a third is filler), Curse of Death's Hold. Abattoir Ghoul and Ghoulraiser went 1-2 in a WB (anecdote).
- Red: Into the Maw of Hell (213 games, 58%, taken at 3.4), Stromkirk Noble (74 games, 57%, rare), Kruin Outlaw (81 games, 56%, rare), Rage Thrower (130 games, 53%), Rakish Heir, Skirsdag Cultist, Hanweir Watchkeep, Falkenrath Marauders (67% earlier in 49 games), Instigator Gang, Vampire Interloper.
- White: Slayer of the Wicked (187 games, 59%, taken at 1.7), Midnight Haunting (169 games, 59%), Mentor of the Meek (81 games, 59%, rare), Elite Inquisitor (83 games, 55%, rare), Rally the Peasants (165 games, 55%, taken at 6.7), Angelic Overseer, Fiend Hunter, Divine Reckoning, Smite the Monstrous (WR decks ran two on 2026-11-16 and it kills power-4 creatures; anecdote). Cloistered Youth is only average.
- Blue: Murder of Crows (only if blue), Invisible Stalker, Stitched Drake, Mindshrieker (72 games, 53%, rare), Battleground Geist, Moon Heron and Orchard Spirit. Blue fliers lost to Somberwald Spider and Victim of Night in a 0-3 UB (anecdote).
- Colourless: Manor Gargoyle (95 games, 56%, rare, taken at 2.2). Galvanic Juggernaut (241 games, 53%, taken at 1.2) is overdrafted: do not take it over removal or a strong on-colour card, and do not rely on it alone to close. Smite the Monstrous hits it.
- Lands: Moorland Haunt (63% earlier in 51 games, comes late), Sulfur Falls (60 games, 55%, in blue-red decks), Woodland Cemetery in B/G, Gavony Township in a long-game white deck (anecdote).
- Bombs: Bloodline Keeper lost on 7 Swamps in a BG and a WB and was the only finisher in a 1-2 WB. Garruk Relentless never made a WU deck but won in a BG. Olivia Voldaren was first-picked and never played in a WU. Take bombs only if they will be on colour and castable, and back them with a second threat.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal on colour: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia (blue), Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Smite the Monstrous, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith, Fiend Hunter, Sensory Deprivation (blue), Corpse Lunge, Harvest Pyre, Spidery Grasp, Bramblecrush, Blazing Torch. Take a second if offered, but aim for about five; a fourth removal spell lost to a second threat in a WB (anecdote). Do not take removal outside the colours you are likely to play. Red removal is contested by other BR drafters. Cheap removal was the consistent theme in the 3-0 RG and the 2-1 BG on 2026-11-16 (anecdotes).
2. Strong uncommons and rares on colour: Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Moldgraf Monstrosity, Sever the Bloodline, Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee (if you can support BB), Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders, Instigator Gang, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw, Rally the Peasants, Midnight Haunting, Angelic Overseer, Garruk Relentless (green only).
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Invisible Stalker, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Mindshrieker, Battleground Geist, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Somberwald Spider. Many losing decks lacked fliers or answers, but several decks with three or four fliers also lost (WR, WG, UB), so fliers do not replace cheap plays and removal. Aim for three or four evasive threats or answers by the end of pack 3, and take a third flier over a second Unruly Mob, Silverchase Fox, Thraben Purebloods or Festerhide Boar. Do not take an off-colour flier over an on-colour one. Against green, count reach.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Avacyn's Pilgrim, Ambush Viper, Kindercatch, Darkthicket Wolf (in green), Ashmouth Hound, Feral Ridgewolf, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils and Tormented Pariah if red, Silver-Inlaid Dagger, Unruly Mob (one), Doomed Traveler, Avacynian Priest, Vampire Interloper, Bump in the Night. Take cheap threats and early evasive creatures so one removal spell cannot blank your curve, and take them over card-draw filler, counterspells and bounce. Against WR, prefer cheap creatures to a second four-power body (Smite the Monstrous).

Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell or card draw. Walking Corpse made a 3-0 BG and a 0-3 BR (both anecdotes), so it is only a 23rd card.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Stitcher's Apprentice, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Ancient Grudge, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Lost in the Mist, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab over a better creature (keep it only as a cheap blocker), Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Bloodcrazed Neonate, Make a Wish, Grimoire of the Dead, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Trepanation Blade, Delver of Secrets, Heartless Summoning, Mask of Avacyn over a flier, Riot Devils (two were taken and one played in a WR; take a removal spell or flier instead), Kessig Wolf (main-decked in a WR; cut it for a flier or removal), Cloistered Youth over a flier, a second Moment of Heroism, and a second Makeshift Mauler. Naturalize and Wooden Stakes are sideboard cards at best. Armored Skaab, Skaab Goliath and Forbidden Alchemy are only for a deck with Spider Spawning and real self-mill. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default; 16 is untested beyond one anecdote (a 17-land WR lost on 2026-11-16, which does not show that 16 would have won). Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 WB, 2-1 WB), 11 Plains/6 Islands (2-1 WU), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG), mostly red with 6 Islands plus Sulfur Falls (3-0 UR) (anecdotes). Count pips and give the main colour 10 sources. A 9 Forest/7 Plains WG went 0-3, and a WR on 6 or 7 Mountains went badly with a heavy red load (anecdotes). Do not give a colour with several double-pip or removal cards only six or seven sources.
- Double pips: give a BB or GG colour 8+ sources or cut them. A WB with Morkrut Banshee and Abattoir Ghoul on 7 Swamps went 2-4 in games, and Bloodline Keeper on 7 Swamps lost; but a 3-0 BR with Bloodgift Demon ran 7 Swamps (anecdotes). Avoid double pips in both colours. Three GG Somberwald Spiders want 8+ Forests. With removal in both colours of a UB, aim for 9/8.
- Aim for about five real removal spells (pumps like Furor of the Bitten do not count), three or four answers to fliers (which can also clear ground blockers), and two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers. Play the removal you drafted: a BR benched Sensory Deprivation with only three removal spells (anecdote).
- Keep a curve with enough cheap plays. The 0-3 BG, 0-3 WR and 0-3 ground BRs were top-heavy or full of ground filler, while winning decks (3-0 BG, 3-0 RG, 2-1 WR) had early drops and burn or removal. A low curve alone is not enough: an 18 to 26 turn UB had cheap cards but no early pressure or evasion. Count two-drops and early evasive creatures.
- Keep a flexible late threat or value source (Mentor of the Meek, Moan of the Unhallowed, Gavony Township, Moorland Haunt, Rally the Peasants, Divine Reckoning, Spider Spawning), but do not cut good cheap cards to fit one.
- Pair removal with good cheap creatures and close with fliers. Do not pad with ground filler when a flier or removal spell is available. If you have no real fliers, keep your reach.
- Do not leave yourself with no answer to an opposing colour's key cards: an RG with no black had nothing for Claustrophobia and Victim of Night decks. Enchantment-based removal on your best creature is a real risk; keep multiple threats. Creatures with toughness above one matter against Geistflame, Brimstone Volley and Harvest Pyre. Creatures with power 4+ are exposed to Smite the Monstrous.
- Settle your 23 in the main deck by pack 3. Sideboard plans rarely rescue a deck. Check every sideboard swap against your colours and do not side out main removal. Keep at least one cheap play main.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core. Do not main Gnaw to the Bone, Mulch, Dream Twist, Make a Wish or self-mill cards without a self-mill deck. Cut a redundant combat trick before a playable creature, and cut filler before a proactive creature.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke, Fiend Hunter and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies. Check creature types before counting on Victim of Night (no Vampires, Werewolves or Zombies).
- Expect long games, often 15 to 40 turns, and up to 62. Break stalls with removal, Spider Spawning, Rally the Peasants, Moan of the Unhallowed and evasive threats. Apply pressure early rather than waiting, especially with UB and WU. Ground-only decks stalled into 17- to 31-turn losses (anecdotes).
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR. Fast starts beat slow hands in 9 to 17 turns on some nights (anecdotes), but recent WR games ran 13 to 27 turns, so cheap creatures should be paired with late fliers or Rally the Peasants to close.
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Angelic Overseer, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Murder of Crows, Midnight Haunting, Falkenrath Noble, Screeching Bat, Galvanic Juggernaut, Boneyard Wurm and Scourge of Geier Reach.
- Against WU, UB, UR, UG and mono-blue, kill fliers early. Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation and Lost in the Mist will target your best creature, so keep a second threat ready. Blue decks win long grinds, so do not durdle and keep a flier answer.
- Against WB, expect real removal (Rebuke, Victim of Night) held for your fliers, plus Angelic Overseer, Chapel Geist and Ghoulraiser value. Do not rely on one big threat.
- Against BG, WG and RG, expect Typhoid Rats, Somberwald Spider, Spidery Grasp, Ghostly Possession, Fiend Hunter, Prey Upon, Garruk Relentless and burn to make the ground hard. RG beat two WR decks on 2026-11-16. Win in the air with removal backup, expect reach, and do not flood the board with ground bodies.
- Against WR, BR and UR, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Furor of the Bitten and Pitchburn Devils to punish small creatures. Do not overcommit and keep your own fliers alive. WR also runs Rebuke, Smite the Monstrous (sometimes two), Midnight Haunting, Fiend Hunter, Slayer of the Wicked, Rally the Peasants and Bonds of Faith, and will answer your fliers and four-power creatures.
- Against white decks with Fiend Hunter and Avacynian Priest, expect one-for-one trades on early creatures; prefer threats that leave value behind. Expect Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning in long games.
