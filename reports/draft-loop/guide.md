# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-03's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Fourteen nights: 112 decks, 411 games. Guided seats are 172-181 in games (49%, 70-74 in matches); unguided are 239-230 (51%, 98-94). Guided seats have not beaten unguided seats, so the guide has not been shown to help. Treat every line here as a hypothesis, not a rule, and lean on the card numbers.

- BR: 56% of games (84 games, 12 decks, 22-14 in matches). Best pair by matches. Tonight a guided-table BR deck beat WB 2-1 with early pressure (turns 14 and 15), while an unguided BR aggro deck went 1-2 with too few ways to close (anecdotes). Black removal plus a few real finishers is the point; filler is not.
- BG: 55% (137 games, 19 decks, 33-24 in matches). Needs evasion or removal-backed finishers; ground bodies alone have gone 1-2.
- WU: 54% (117 games, 16 decks, 27-21 in matches). Tonight WU went 2-1 with two Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider and Claustrophobia (anecdote), but another WU deck lost long grinds after cutting its 5-drops. Fliers plus cheap removal plus one late threat work.
- WR 54% (78 games, 11 decks). WG 53% (74 games, 10 decks, 17-13 in matches); tonight WG went 3-0 (6-1 in games) with Spider Spawning, Prey Upon, Bonds of Faith, Ghostly Possession and Chapel Geist (anecdote). Removal plus a few fliers is a good WG plan.
- Weak: UR 45% (103 games, 14 decks, 17-25 in matches). Tonight two UR decks went 1-2 and 0-3 and also fought each other for the same cards (anecdotes). UB 44% (85 games, 11 decks, 11-22 in matches). UG 38% (16 games). RG 43% (30 games, 4 decks); tonight's RG deck went 1-2 with no late flier (anecdote).
- WB: 40% of games (60 games, 8 decks, 9-15 in matches). Earlier it looked terrible, but tonight two guided and unguided WB decks went 2-1 each (anecdotes) on Rebuke, Dead Weight, Tribute to Hunger, Smite the Monstrous, Voiceless Spirit and Falkenrath Noble, and the 0-3 deck was Walking Corpse filler. Still below average overall: play it only with plenty of cheap removal and fliers.
- White looked strongest at tonight's table (WG 3-0, WB 2-1, WU 2-1; anecdote). White fliers and removal are worth taking early.
- Evasion plus removal breaks stalls. Games stall for 13 to 55 turns; ground bodies alone do not break them.
- Pick a main colour by about pick 5 and a pair by mid pack 1. Do not leave a colour for one early removal spell unless the new colour is clearly open: a first-pick Victim of Night in pack 2 went unused in the 3-0 WG deck, and a Brimstone Volley went unused earlier (anecdotes).
- Splashing is poor; off-colour picks rarely make decks. Mono-colour is risky (2 mono-red decks 3-3 in matches).
- Read what is passed. Two UR decks at one table split their core cards and both lost; if a neighbour is already in your colours, move or take contested playables earlier. Do not pass a second removal spell to a neighbour in your pair's lane.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Brimstone Volley, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Smite the Monstrous, Bonds of Faith, Sensory Deprivation, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Hysterical Blindness. Take removal over Curiosity or Curse of the Bloody Tome (anecdote).
2. Strong uncommons and rares: Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee, Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders, Slayer of the Wicked, Galvanic Juggernaut, Murder of Crows, Fiend Hunter, Manor Gargoyle, Stromkirk Noble, Kruin Outlaw.
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Moon Heron, Abbey Griffin, Somberwald Spider. Take a few before pack 3: decks without reach or flier answers lost to WU fliers (anecdote).
4. Good commons: Typhoid Rats, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Ashmouth Hound, Bump in the Night, Thraben Sentry, Unruly Mob, Furor of the Bitten, Silver-Inlaid Dagger.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Walking Corpse, Armored Skaab, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Lost in the Mist, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Festerhide Boar, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Riot Devils, Cellar Door, Stensia Bloodhall. Delver of Secrets and Darkthicket Wolf are marginal. Late in a pack, take a playable creature over a dead card, since pack 3 playables run thin.

## Building

- Use 17 lands unless the curve is low. Results at 17 vary from 0-3 to 3-0; a 3-0 WG deck ran 10 Forest and 7 Plains (anecdote).
- Count pips and match basics to them. Avoid double pips in both colours.
- Aim for four or more answers to fliers and four or more evasive or removal-backed finishers.
- Keep one late threat or finisher (a flier, a big body, or a sweeper like Blasphemous Act in a stalled matchup). Decks without one lost 24-to-55-turn grinds repeatedly (anecdotes tonight in RG, WU, UR).
- Ground bodies are fine when removal answers the fliers; ground-only decks without removal lose.
- Play the removal and flier answers you drafted rather than sideboarding them. Check every sideboard swap against your colours: tonight a UR deck brought in black cards.
- Main fliers over filler such as Riot Devils, Walking Corpse or a marginal fourth creature.
- Keep good two-drops, but do not play a third mediocre one over removal or an evasive threat.
- Include one or two card-advantage cards (Ghoulcaller's Chant, Unburial Rites) for stalls (anecdote).
- Do not main Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone or Mulch.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies.
- Expect long games, often 20 to 40 turns. Break stalls with removal, Spider Spawning and evasive threats.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry against BR and RG; fast BR starts beat slow WB hands in 14 to 15 turns (anecdote).
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Angelic Overseer, Battleground Geist, Invisible Stalker and Murder of Crows.
- Against WU and UR, kill fliers early; Claustrophobia and Sensory Deprivation will target your best creature.
- Against BG, expect Typhoid Rats and Somberwald Spider to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup.
- WU should keep cheap two-drops and its flier clock even when a stall looks likely (anecdote: a WU deck lost 1-2 to WG in 13 to 19 turns).
