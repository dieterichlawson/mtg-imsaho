# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-21's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Thirty-two nights: 256 decks, 950 games. Guided seats are 336-357 in games (48%, 137-145 in matches, 94 decks). The conventional guide is 99-88 (53%, 41-37 in matches, 26 decks). Unguided seats are 515-505 (50%, 206-202 in matches, 136 decks). On the thirteen nights with all three kinds of seat, guided went 86-103 (46%, 36-42 in matches), conventional 99-88 (53%) and unguided 199-193 (51%). Guided is still behind both, and this guide has not been shown to help. Treat archetype lines as hypotheses and lean on card numbers and on advice that is consistent across lessons.

- Commit to two colours early (by mid pack 1). Stranded early picks are the most repeated lesson. On 2026-11-21 a guided WR first-picked Victim of Night and never played it; a guided RG left Dead Weight, Infernal Plunge and two Altar's Reap unplayed; a conventional WR lost three picks to Traveler's Amulet (anecdotes). Earlier: Olivia Voldaren (2026-11-16 and 2026-11-20), Devil's Play (2026-11-19), Victim of Night (2026-11-18), Into the Maw of Hell and Furor of the Bitten (2026-11-15) were taken and never played (anecdotes). If you take a bomb, stay in its colour and play it. Do not take removal outside your likely colours.
- Fliers decide games. On 2026-11-21 a WU (unguided) went 3-0 with three Delver of Secrets, Snapcaster Mage, Stitched Drake, Claustrophobia, Fiend Hunter, Grasp of Phantoms and Sensory Deprivation, and beat a guided WR 2-1 and a conventional WR 1-2 (the latter had one flier, Voiceless Spirit); a guided RG lost 1-2 to WU's fliers; a conventional BR ground aggro deck lost to Stitched Drake and Screeching Bat (all anecdotes). Earlier nights said the same (2026-11-20, several lessons). Take cheap flier answers (Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Silent Departure, Brimstone Volley, Rebuke) before an extra two-drop, and a third flier over ground filler such as Silverchase Fox or Thraben Purebloods.
- Cheap curves must also close. Games ran 11 to 51 turns on 2026-11-20 and 46 and 47 turns on 2026-11-21. A guided-style WB on 17 lands lost 40- and 29-turn games for lack of late card advantage; a UB lost 41 and 36 turns to WR for lack of evasive pressure (anecdotes). Have two or three real threats that win in the air or in a long game, backed by removal and some card advantage.
- Take removal, but only in your colours, and play what you drafted.
- Green decks with Somberwald Spider beat fliers; bring removal for it. Balefire Dragon in an RG beat a guided UB 2-0 on 2026-11-20 (anecdote): keep two flier answers before pack 3.

Pairs by games won (decks, matches, game win rate), from the statistics up to 2026-11-21:
- RG: 55% (108 games, 14 decks, 24-18 in matches). Best by rate, still a small sample, and it went 1-2 (3-5 in games) on 2026-11-21 with Harvest Pyre as its only real red removal and no answer to WU fliers. A conventional RG went 3-0 on 2026-11-20 on Harvest Pyre, three Tormented Pariah, Darkthicket Wolf, Festerhide Boar and 17 lands (anecdote). Needs red removal or fliers, cheap blockers, reach, and an answer to Claustrophobia and Victim of Night. Once red is set, take red fliers or removal over black picks.
- BG: 54% (210 games, 29 decks, 48-39 in matches). Best by matches among big pairs. Take cheap removal and early blockers over a fourth fatty, and keep a flier answer. A low-curve green-based BG went 3-0 earlier; a top-heavy BG went 0-3 (anecdotes).
- WG: 52% (196 games, 27 decks, 44-37 in matches). On 2026-11-20 four WGs went 1-2 with plenty of cheap ground bodies and too few fliers or removal (anecdotes); a guided WG went 3-0 on 2026-11-19 with Bonds of Faith, Mentor of the Meek and Rally the Peasants. Needs cheap plays, a 10-source main colour, and evasion or removal.
- WU: 52% (232 games, 31 decks, 49-44 in matches). Best performing archetype on 2026-11-21 (3-0 unguided, see above). White removal plus real cheap fliers (Midnight Haunting, Invisible Stalker, Chapel Geist, Abbey Griffin, Stitched Drake, Moorland Haunt) and blue pacifism auras work. Delver of Secrets worked in a deck with three copies and Snapcaster Mage (anecdote, one deck; the earlier advice against Delver in UB/UR stands for decks without enough spells). Keep a cheap ground defender and a late threat.
- WR: 52% (221 games, 30 decks, 47-43 in matches). Slipping: guided WRs of 2026-11-17 and 2026-11-18 went 2-7 in matches combined; on 2026-11-21 a guided WR went 2-1 and a conventional WR 1-2, both losing to blue fliers (anecdotes). A heavy red load wants 8 or 9 Mountains; a WR needs fliers and removal-backed finishers beyond ground bodies. Save Rebuke and Brimstone Volley for fliers, not ground attackers.
- BR: 49% (234 games, 32 decks, 49-47 in matches). A conventional ground-bound BR aggro deck went 1-2 on 2026-11-21 with two Bloodcrazed Neonate, running 17 lands on a cheap curve (anecdote); cut a land for a two-drop in a truly low curve, and cut Neonate for any playable flier or removal. On 2026-11-20 an unguided BR went 1-2 (0-2 to WG). BR wants cheap removal, evasive finishers like Falkenrath Noble, and card advantage. Skip Ancient Grudge, Heartless Summoning, and late duplicates of Rage Thrower or Markov Patrician.
- WB: 47% (163 games, 22 decks, 32-34 in matches). An 11 Plains/6 Swamp white-first WB went 3-0 on 2026-11-18 and 1-2 on 2026-11-19; an unguided WB went 1-2 on 2026-11-21 (40- and 29-turn losses, too little card advantage, ground creatures like Unruly Mob ran out of pressure; anecdotes). Go white-first, count BB pips, and take card-advantage finishers over Walking Corpse filler.
- UG: 49% (68 games, 9 decks, 12-15 in matches). Weak overall. A conventional UG went 2-1 on 2026-11-19 with Murder of Crows, Moon Heron and Lantern Spirit; an unguided UG went 2-1 on 2026-11-21 with Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation, Galvanic Juggernaut and Moon Heron (anecdotes). Spider Spawning did nothing without self-mill.
- UR: 45% (163 games, 22 decks, 29-37 in matches). Weak. Make UR red-first with burn and an evasive clock; cut a second Frightful Delusion.
- UB: 46% (252 games, 33 decks, 39-60 in matches). Worst big pair. On 2026-11-20 three UBs went 2-1, 1-2 and 1-2; on 2026-11-21 an unguided UB went 1-2 (anecdotes). UB needs a low curve, cheap blockers and answers (Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation, Sever the Bloodline) plus cheap pressure (Falkenrath Noble, Vampire Interloper). Do not take Think Twice, Dissipate or Dream Twist; Think Twice taken at picks 10 and 6 was cut.
- Mono U is 4-5 in matches (21 games) and mono R 3-3 (16 games).

General notes:
- White is the best-supported colour on individual cards (Slayer of the Wicked, Midnight Haunting, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Bonds of Faith). Red has many high-win-rate cards (Stromkirk Noble, Furor of the Bitten, Into the Maw of Hell, Kruin Outlaw, Ashmouth Hound). Green has Moldgraf Monstrosity, Lumberknot, Gatstaf Shepherd and Villagers of Estwald. Cheap white removal (Rebuke, Fiend Hunter) is contested, so take it early.
- Black is crowded. Take black removal early in pack 1, but only if you will play black.
- Read what is flowing after pack 1. If strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8, move in.
- Commit: main colour by about pick 5, a pair by mid pack 1, your second colour by pick 5 of pack 2 at the latest. Do not hedge with a second colour's removal once you are set.
- Galvanic Juggernaut (55%, 311 games, taken at 1.2) is contested and fine as a colourless finisher, but take removal, Bonds of Faith or a flier over it. It has 5 power and dies to Smite the Monstrous; do not rely on one big threat.
- Games run 11 to 62 turns, mostly 15 to 47. Grindy removal and card advantage matter.
- Splashing is poor. Do not run a colour on six basics unless it is a light second colour with a dual land and few cards.

## Commons that win

- Green: Villagers of Estwald (297 games, 57%), Woodland Sleuth (277 games, 56%), Grave Bramble (222 games, 55%, taken at 8.9), Avacyn's Pilgrim (154 games, 54%, taken at 9.1), Festerhide Boar (325 games, 54%; filler: take a flier or cheap removal over a third copy), Prey Upon (297 games, 53%, taken at 2.8), Kindercatch (318 games, 53%), Grizzled Outcasts (168 games, 52%). - White: Voiceless Spirit (436 games, 54%), Avacynian Priest (383 games, 55%), Bonds of Faith (371 games, 55%), Thraben Sentry (260 games, 53%), Unruly Mob (327 games, 53%; one is fine), Doomed Traveler (374 games, 53%), Selfless Cathar, Chapel Geist, Silverchase Fox (ground filler).

- Blue: Spectral Flight, Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation, Hysterical Blindness (109 games, 52%, late pick).
- Colourless: Silver-Inlaid Dagger (53% earlier). Blazing Torch is praised in some lessons but missed a WU deck on 2026-11-20 (anecdote).
- Claustrophobia, Rebuke, Dead Weight, Tribute to Hunger, Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Sensory Deprivation and Stitched Drake are praised in lessons and go early, but only on colour.

## Uncommons and rares

- Black: Sever the Bloodline (69 games, 61%, rare, taken at 1.3), Disciple of Griselbrand (140 games, 58%, taken at 9.2), Morkrut Banshee (226 games, 53%, taken at 1.7; BB on 7 Swamps went poorly), Bloodgift Demon (65 games, 54%), Falkenrath Noble, Crossway Vampire (a third is filler), Curse of Death's Hold (slow).
- Green: Moldgraf Monstrosity (rare, 59% earlier), Gatstaf Shepherd (245 games, 55%), Lumberknot (215 games, 55%), Hollowhenge Scavenger (156 games, 54%), Spider Spawning (177 games, 55%; idle without self-mill enablers), Hamlet Captain, Boneyard Wurm (dies to Smite the Monstrous; do not take a second). Garruk Relentless carried a 2-1 BG (anecdote).
- Red: Falkenrath Marauders (65 games, 58%, rare, taken at 2.3), Desperate Ravings (65 games, 57%, late pick), Stromkirk Noble (96 games, 56%, rare), Into the Maw of Hell (250 games, 55%, taken at 3.4), Kruin Outlaw (104 games, 55%, rare), Hanweir Watchkeep (146 games, 53%), Rage Thrower (169 games, 53%), Instigator Gang, Vampire Interloper. Balefire Dragon beat a UB 2-0 (anecdote).
- White: Slayer of the Wicked (232 games, 60%, taken at 1.8), Midnight Haunting (215 games, 59%), Mentor of the Meek (104 games, 58%, rare), Elite Inquisitor (104 games, 57%, rare), Rally the Peasants (216 games, 53%, taken at 6.8; closes long games), Dearly Departed, Fiend Hunter, Angelic Overseer, Divine Reckoning, Smite the Monstrous. Smite only hits power 4 or more: two copies in a guided WR were dead against Delver of Secrets and Stitched Drake on 2026-11-21 (anecdote), so it is an answer to Galvanic Juggernaut, Boneyard Wurm and fat green creatures, not to small fliers. Cloistered Youth is only average.
- Blue: Murder of Crows (only if blue), Invisible Stalker, Stitched Drake, Mindshrieker, Battleground Geist, Moon Heron, Lantern Spirit, Orchard Spirit, Snapcaster Mage and Grasp of Phantoms (both in the 3-0 WU, anecdote). Geist of Saint Traft and Moon Heron beat a UB on 2026-11-20 (anecdote). Blue fliers lost to Somberwald Spider, Victim of Night and WG.
- Colourless: Manor Gargoyle (127 games, 55%, rare, taken at 2.2). Galvanic Juggernaut: see notes above.
- Lands: Moorland Haunt (comes late), Sulfur Falls (blue-red), Woodland Cemetery in B/G, Gavony Township in a long-game white deck (anecdote).
- Bombs: Bloodline Keeper lost on 7 Swamps in a BG and a WB. Olivia Voldaren and Devil's Play were first- or second-picked and never played. Take bombs only if they will be on colour and castable, and play them.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal on colour: Sever the Bloodline, Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Claustrophobia (blue), Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Harvest Pyre, Geistflame, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith, Fiend Hunter, Sensory Deprivation (blue), Silent Departure (blue), Grasp of Phantoms (blue), Corpse Lunge, Spidery Grasp, Smite the Monstrous (only against big creatures; not a flier answer), Blazing Torch. Take a second if offered. About five is the target; six worked in one 3-0 WB. Do not take removal outside your likely colours, and not second pick before you know them.
2. Strong uncommons and rares on colour: Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Moldgraf Monstrosity, Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee (if you can support BB), Falkenrath Marauders, Instigator Gang, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw, Rally the Peasants, Midnight Haunting, Dearly Departed, Angelic Overseer, Spider Spawning (only with self-mill), Garruk Relentless (green only).
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Invisible Stalker, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Lantern Spirit, Mindshrieker, Battleground Geist, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Scourge of Geier Reach, Somberwald Spider, Screeching Bat. Lessons on 2026-11-19, 2026-11-20 and 2026-11-21 asked for evasion and flier answers over ground filler about ten times; aim for three or four evasive threats or answers by the end of pack 3 and take a third flier over a second Unruly Mob, Silverchase Fox, Thraben Purebloods, Festerhide Boar, or a third Villagers of Estwald. Do not take an off-colour flier over an on-colour one, and play the fliers you draft (a BR took Abbey Griffin and cut it). Against green, count reach. Card advantage (Mentor of the Meek, Rally the Peasants) was asked for in several lessons.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Avacyn's Pilgrim, Ambush Viper, Kindercatch, Darkthicket Wolf, Ashmouth Hound, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils, Tormented Pariah, Silver-Inlaid Dagger, Unruly Mob (one), Doomed Traveler, Avacynian Priest, Vampire Interloper, Bump in the Night. Take playable two- and three-drops over late-pick junk such as Frightful Delusion, Cobbled Wings, Spare from Evil, Traveler's Amulet and Ranger's Guile. Against WR, prefer cheap creatures to a second four-power body. In UB, take the early curve before late engines.

Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell or card draw. Walking Corpse is only a 23rd card.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Stitcher's Apprentice, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Ancient Grudge, Traveler's Amulet (three dead picks in one WR), Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Butcher's Cleaver and Geistcatcher's Rig over a bomb or removal, Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab over a better creature, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Bloodcrazed Neonate, Make a Wish, Grimoire of the Dead, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Trepanation Blade, Heartless Summoning, Curiosity, Spare from Evil, Nightbird's Clutches, Frightful Delusion, Traitorous Blood, Vampiric Fury, Reckless Waif over removal, Mask of Avacyn over a flier, Riot Devils, Kessig Wolf, Cloistered Youth over a flier, Intangible Virtue, Ranger's Guile (an unguided UG took three late and cut extras), a second Moment of Heroism, a second Makeshift Mauler, and a third Festerhide Boar. Naturalize, Wooden Stakes and Sharpened Pitchfork are narrow filler. Lost in the Mist is only worth a second copy in a controlling blue deck. Armored Skaab, Skaab Goliath, Skaab Ruinator and Forbidden Alchemy are only for a deck with Spider Spawning and real self-mill. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default; long games (46 and 47 turns on 2026-11-21) support it, though a 17-land WB stalled and a 17-land low-curve BR flooded in lessons, so cut to 16 only for a truly low curve with several two-drops (anecdotes). Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 and 2-1 WB), 11 Plains/6 Swamp (3-0 and 1-2 WB), 11 Plains/6 Islands (2-1 WU), 9 Plains/8 Islands (3-0 WU), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG), mostly red with 6 Islands plus Sulfur Falls (3-0 UR), 8 Swamp/9 Mountain (1-2 BR) (anecdotes). Count pips and give the main colour 9 to 10 sources. A 9 Forest/7 Plains WG went 0-3 and WRs on 6 or 7 Mountains went badly with a heavy red load (anecdotes).
- Double pips: give a BB or GG colour 8+ sources or cut them. A WB with Morkrut Banshee and Abattoir Ghoul on 7 Swamps went 2-4 in games, and Bloodline Keeper on 7 Swamps lost; but a 3-0 BR with Bloodgift Demon ran 7 Swamps (anecdotes). Avoid double pips in both colours. Three GG Somberwald Spiders want 8+ Forests.
- Aim for about five or six real removal spells (pumps do not count), three or four answers to fliers (which can also clear ground blockers; Smite the Monstrous does not count), and two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers. Play the removal you drafted and the bomb you took.
- Keep a curve with enough cheap plays, but a low curve alone is not enough. The 0-3 BG, 0-3 WRs and 0-3 ground BRs were top-heavy or ground filler; the 0-3 BR of 15 cheap ground creatures, UBs with no pressure, 1-2 WGs of 15 to 16 cheap creatures and the 1-2 ground BR of 2026-11-21 lost to fliers and grinds. Count two-drops and early evasive creatures, keep one or two cheap ground defenders in tempo decks, and trade a marginal two-drop for a third flier or a removal spell.
- Keep a flexible late threat or value source (Mentor of the Meek, Moan of the Unhallowed, Gavony Township, Moorland Haunt, Rally the Peasants, Divine Reckoning), but do not cut good cheap cards to fit one. A WB that cut Spare from Evil lacked late card advantage (anecdote).
- Self-mill needs a real plan: Spider Spawning idled in a UG with no enablers, and UBs on Skaab Ruinator and Stitcher's Apprentice were slow (anecdotes). Do not build around graveyard recursion alone: an RG sided in Purify the Grave against a WB on 2026-11-21 (anecdote). Either draft enablers and payoffs together or leave them out.
- Tricks have a place after removal and creatures; one or two cheap tricks in a ground deck is fine, but fight tricks like Ranger's Guile, Mulch and Spidery Grasp were filler for unguided drafters. Enchantment-based removal on your best creature is a real risk; keep multiple threats. Toughness above one matters against Geistflame, Brimstone Volley, Harvest Pyre and Tormented Pariah; power 4+ is exposed to Smite the Monstrous (two copies on 2026-11-21 answered a Galvanic Juggernaut). Do not leave yourself with no answer to an opposing colour's key cards (Claustrophobia, Victim of Night, Tormented Pariah, Bonds of Faith).
- Settle your 23 in the main deck by pack 3. Sideboard plans rarely rescue a deck. Plan post-board swaps before round one, check every swap against your colours, and do not side out main removal or threats for situational cards. Keep at least one cheap play main.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core. Do not main Gnaw to the Bone, Mulch, Dream Twist, Make a Wish or self-mill cards without a self-mill deck.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke and Fiend Hunter for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies. Hold Smite the Monstrous for power 4+ creatures only; it cannot touch Delver of Secrets, Stitched Drake or most fliers. Check creature types before counting on Victim of Night.
- Expect long games, often 15 to 47 turns, and up to 62. Break stalls with removal, Rally the Peasants, Moan of the Unhallowed and evasive threats. Apply pressure early rather than waiting, especially with UB, UR and WU.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR. Fast starts win some 9 to 17 turn games, but pair cheap creatures with late fliers or Rally the Peasants to close.
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Lantern Spirit, Geist of Saint Traft, Angelic Overseer, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Murder of Crows, Midnight Haunting, Falkenrath Noble, Screeching Bat, Galvanic Juggernaut, Boneyard Wurm, Balefire Dragon and Scourge of Geier Reach.
- Against WU, UB, UR, UG and mono-blue, kill fliers early (Delver of Secrets flips into a real threat; three copies plus Snapcaster Mage won a 3-0 on 2026-11-21). Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation, Grasp of Phantoms and Lost in the Mist will target your best creature, so keep a second threat ready. Blue decks win long grinds, so do not durdle.
- Against WB, expect real removal (Fiend Hunter, Smite the Monstrous, Rebuke, Victim of Night) held for your fliers and best creatures, plus Angelic Overseer, Chapel Geist and Rally the Peasants closing long games. Do not rely on one big threat, and bring card advantage.
- Against BG, WG and RG, expect Typhoid Rats, Somberwald Spider, Spidery Grasp, Villagers of Estwald, Bonds of Faith, Mentor of the Meek, Fiend Hunter, Prey Upon, Garruk Relentless, Travel Preparations, Tormented Pariah and burn to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup, expect reach, keep an answer for combat tricks, and do not flood the board with ground bodies. RG may side in Purify the Grave against graveyard decks.
- Against WR, BR and UR, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Furor of the Bitten and Pitchburn Devils to punish small creatures. Do not overcommit and keep your own fliers alive. WR also runs Rebuke (sometimes two), Smite the Monstrous, Midnight Haunting, Fiend Hunter, Slayer of the Wicked, Rally the Peasants and Bonds of Faith, and will answer your fliers, tricked-up creatures and four-power creatures. Hold back threats against two Rebukes and Fiend Hunter rather than feeding them one at a time.
- Against white decks with Fiend Hunter and Avacynian Priest, expect one-for-one trades on early creatures; prefer threats that leave value behind. Expect Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning in long games.
