# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-06's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Seventeen nights: 136 decks, 504 games. Guided seats are 218-227 in games (49%, 87-93 in matches); unguided are 286-277 (51%, 117-111 in matches). Guided seats have not beaten unguided seats, so the guide has not been shown to help. Treat every line as a hypothesis and lean on the card numbers, not on the archetype lines.

- BR: 56% of games (93 games, 13 decks, 24-15 in matches). Best pair by matches. Black removal plus a few real finishers.
- WR: 54% (101 games, 14 decks, 23-19 in matches). Tonight both guided and unguided WR decks went 1-2 (anecdotes, 3-4 and 4-5 in games), so the earlier "model deck" 3-0 was one deck, not a rule. Cheap bodies, cheap removal and fliers are still the plan, but it needs a late threat: both WR decks lost long grinds to Somberwald Spiders or UB.
- BG: 54% (152 games, 21 decks, 35-28 in matches). Needs evasion or removal-backed finishers and cheap creatures. Somberwald Spider and Spider Spawning beat ground decks tonight.
- WG: 52% (96 games, 13 decks, 22-17 in matches). Tonight two WG decks went 2-1 and 1-2 (anecdotes) on 10 Forest/7 Plains and 9 Forest/8 Plains; the 1-2 deck had four evasive threats but only four removal spells, 2-5 in games. Evasion alone does not win; removal count matters.
- WU: 50% (143 games, 19 decks, 28-29 in matches). Tonight's WU went 0-3 (anecdote) with three Delver of Secrets, Fortress Crab, Makeshift Mauler, Creepy Doll and Village Bell-Ringer. The archetype needs real fliers and removal, not filler.
- WB: 46% (83 games, 11 decks, 15-18 in matches). Only with plenty of cheap removal and fliers. Do not play it without fliers.
- RG: 48% (52 games, 7 decks, 10-11 in matches). Tonight an unguided RG went 3-0 (6-0 in games, anecdote) on 11 Mountain/6 Forest with 18 red playables (Riot Devils, two Pitchburn Devils, Brimstone Volley, Blasphemous Act) and beat a WG deck. Lesson: when red is deep, a red-heavy deck with a light green side works. It is one deck, not a trend.
- Weak: UR 45% (103 games, 14 decks, 17-25 in matches). UB 46% (131 games, 17 decks, 21-30 in matches); tonight two UB decks went 2-1 (anecdotes) on Dead Weight, Victim of Night, Sensory Deprivation, Claustrophobia, Tribute to Hunger, Corpse Lunge, Stitched Drake and Skaab Goliath, but both lost to RG or WR on slow starts or in 48-52-turn stalls. UB needs cheap blockers and a stall-breaker (Moan of the Unhallowed, Murder of Crows). UG 38% (16 games).
- White is well supported on individual cards (see below), but white pairs did not dominate tonight. Take its removal and fliers early, yet stay flexible on the second colour.
- Evasion plus removal breaks stalls. Games stall for 13 to 55 turns.
- Pick a main colour by about pick 5 and a pair by mid pack 1 to mid pack 2 at the latest. Do not leave a colour for one early removal spell unless the new colour is clearly open. Read what is open: if strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8 (red tonight), move in.
- Splashing is poor. Mono-colour is risky (2 mono-red decks 3-3 in matches). Don't run a colour on six basics unless it is a light second colour with excellent depth in the main colour (tonight's 3-0 RG had 6 Forest, anecdote; an earlier RG on six Forests went 3-5 in games, anecdote).

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Smite the Monstrous, Bonds of Faith, Sensory Deprivation, Corpse Lunge, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre. Take a second Dead Weight and a second Victim of Night if offered. Aim for five or more removal spells; a WG deck with four went 2-5 in games (anecdote).
2. Strong uncommons and rares: Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee, Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders, Slayer of the Wicked, Fiend Hunter, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw.
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Somberwald Spider. Aim for three evasive threats or a removal-backed finisher by the end of pack 3 and take one over ground filler. Do not take an off-colour flier (Moon Heron unplayed in a WR deck) over an on-colour one.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Ashmouth Hound, Bump in the Night, Unruly Mob, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils if red, Silver-Inlaid Dagger. Early blockers matter: slow UB starts lost to RG and WR (anecdotes).

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Armored Skaab, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Lost in the Mist, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Festerhide Boar, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Cloistered Youth over a flier, Unburial Rites (cut from a UB deck, anecdote), and Makeshift Mauler as a second copy. Delver of Secrets is marginal (three main-decked in a 0-3 WU, anecdote). Walking Corpse is acceptable as an early blocker when nothing better is left (anecdote). Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell or a dead card: picks 10-14 keep going to Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone and Ghoulcaller's Bell. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician. Card-selection spells did not save a UB deck; take a faster curve.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default. For a low-curve aggro deck, 16 is worth trying (a 4-5 WR on 17 lands suggested it, anecdote). Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 WB), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 9/8 (2-1 and 1-2 WG), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG) (all anecdotes). Count pips for the heavier colour rather than copying a split.
- Avoid double pips in both colours, or give the double-pip colour at least 8 sources. Three GG Somberwald Spiders want 8+ Forests; a BG deck with BB and GG cards on 7 black sources went 1-2 (anecdote).
- Aim for four or more answers to fliers (that can also clear ground blockers) and at least two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers.
- Keep one late threat or finisher. Decks without one lost 16-to-55-turn grinds repeatedly, including both WR decks tonight (anecdotes).
- Removal alone is not enough: UB and RG decks with plenty of removal went 3-5 in games for lack of cheap, sturdy creatures. Pair removal with good cheap creatures.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core.
- Play the removal and flier answers you drafted; check every sideboard swap against your colours.
- Main fliers over filler such as Riot Devils (unless red is your main colour and deep), Walking Corpse or a marginal fourth creature.
- Include one or two card-advantage or stall-breaking cards (Ghoulcaller's Chant, Moan of the Unhallowed) for stalls (anecdote).
- Do not main Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone or Mulch. 1-toughness fliers and creatures die to Brimstone Volley and Geistflame, so weigh that against red decks.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke, Fiend Hunter and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies.
- Expect long games, often 20 to 40 turns, and up to 52. Break stalls with removal, Spider Spawning and evasive threats.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR burn decks; fast starts beat slow hands in 11 to 15 turns (anecdotes).
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Angelic Overseer, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Moon Heron and Murder of Crows.
- Against WU, UB and UR, kill fliers early; Claustrophobia and Sensory Deprivation will target your best creature.
- Against WB, expect real removal held for your fliers; do not rely on one big threat.
- Against BG and WG, expect Typhoid Rats and Somberwald Spider to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup and do not flood the board with ground bodies.
- Against WR and RG, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame and Blasphemous Act to punish small creatures; do not overcommit into a sweeper and keep your own fliers alive.
- WU and UB should keep cheap two-drops and the flier clock even when a stall looks likely (anecdote).
