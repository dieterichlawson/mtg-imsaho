# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-22's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Thirty-three nights: 264 decks, 977 games. Guided seats are 344-361 in games (49%, 141-147 in matches, 96 decks). The conventional guide is 103-98 (51%, 42-42 in matches, 28 decks). Unguided seats are 530-518 (51%, 213-207 in matches, 140 decks). On the fourteen nights with all three kinds of seat, guided went 94-107 (47%, 40-44 in matches), conventional 103-98 (51%) and unguided 214-206 (51%). Guided is still slightly behind and this guide has not been shown to help. Treat archetype lines as hypotheses; lean on card numbers and on advice that is consistent across lessons.

- Commit to two colours early (by mid pack 1; second colour by pick 5 of pack 2 at the latest). Stranded picks are the most repeated lesson. On 2026-11-22 a guided WG-leaning unguided seat took Geist of Saint Traft, Snapcaster Mage and Forbidden Alchemy in pack 2 and played none; a guided WR left Disciple of Griselbrand and Vampire Interloper dead; a conventional mono-green deck left Murder of Crows and Geist-Honored Monk unplayed (anecdotes). Earlier: Olivia Voldaren, Devil's Play, Victim of Night, Into the Maw of Hell, Furor of the Bitten were taken and never played (anecdotes). If you take a bomb, stay in its colour and play it. Once set, do not take removal or black cards outside your colours.
- Fliers decide games. On 2026-11-22 a flierless mono-green went 0-3 (18 to 28 turn games stalled); WRs lost to BR's Bloodline Keeper, two Screeching Bat and Falkenrath Noble (0-2 twice, 24- and 26-turn games); a WR and a WG lost to spirit fliers (Voiceless Spirit, Chapel Geist, Spectral Rider) (all anecdotes). On 2026-11-21 a WU with three Delver of Secrets, Snapcaster Mage and Stitched Drake went 3-0 (anecdote). Take cheap flier answers (Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Silent Departure, Brimstone Volley, Rebuke, Urgent Exorcism) before an extra two-drop, and a third flier over ground filler.
- Cheap curves must also close. Games ran 11 to 51 turns and often 18 to 29. A UB with a low curve stalled with Dissipate, Fortress Crab and a second Lost in the Mist; a WB on 17 lands lost 40- and 29-turn games for lack of card advantage (anecdotes). Have two or three real threats that win in the air or long, backed by removal and card advantage.
- Take removal, but only in your colours, and play what you drafted. Three WRs on 2026-11-22 had only three real removal spells; take a second or third removal over Silverchase Fox, Night Revelers or Unruly Mob.
- Green decks with Somberwald Spider beat fliers; bring removal for it. Mono-colour with no evasion lost (G 2-4 in matches, 15 games); take a second colour with fliers over green filler.

Pairs by games won (decks, matches, game win rate), statistics to 2026-11-22:
- RG: 55% (108 games, 14 decks, 24-18 in matches). Best by rate, still small. Needs red removal or fliers, cheap blockers, and an answer to Claustrophobia and Victim of Night. A conventional RG went 3-0 on Harvest Pyre, three Tormented Pariah, Darkthicket Wolf, Festerhide Boar on 17 lands (anecdote).
- BG: 54% (210 games, 29 decks, 48-39 in matches). Best by matches among big pairs. Take cheap removal and blockers over a fourth fatty, keep a flier answer.
- WG: 52% (204 games, 28 decks, 46-38 in matches). A WG went 2-1 on 2026-11-22 with only Fiend Hunter and Smite the Monstrous as removal and lost 0-2 to flier-heavy WR (anecdote). Needs cheap plays, a 10-source main colour, and evasion or removal.
- WU: 52% (232 games, 31 decks, 49-44 in matches). White removal plus cheap fliers (Midnight Haunting, Invisible Stalker, Chapel Geist, Abbey Griffin, Stitched Drake, Moorland Haunt) and blue pacifism auras work. Delver of Secrets worked only in a deck with three copies and Snapcaster Mage (anecdote).
- WR: 52% (240 games, 33 decks, 52-47 in matches). Three guided WRs went 2-1 on 2026-11-22 (9 Mountain/8 Plains, wins on Bonds of Faith and two Rebuke) but each lost to BR or flier decks for want of removal count, evasion and card advantage; an unguided 11-creature WR went 1-2 (anecdotes). Take Mentor of the Meek and Midnight Haunting over ground bodies; save Rebuke and Brimstone Volley for fliers.
- BR: 50% (240 games, 33 decks, 52-47 in matches). An unguided black-based BR of cheap creatures, two Victim of Night and Tribute to Hunger went 3-0 on 2026-11-22 (6-0 in games, with a six-Mountain splash of Geistflame, Bloodcrazed Neonate and Riot Devils that no game tested; anecdote). Black was deep that night and removal contested. BR wants cheap removal, evasive finishers (Falkenrath Noble, Screeching Bat, Bloodline Keeper if castable) and card advantage. Skip Ancient Grudge and Heartless Summoning.
- WB: 47% (163 games, 22 decks, 32-34 in matches). Go white-first, count BB pips, take card-advantage finishers over Walking Corpse filler.
- UG: 49% (68 games, 9 decks, 12-15 in matches). Weak. Spider Spawning did nothing without self-mill.
- UR: 45% (163 games, 22 decks, 29-37 in matches). Weak. Make it red-first with burn and an evasive clock.
- UB: 45% (266 games, 35 decks, 41-64 in matches). Worst big pair. On 2026-11-22 a guided-style conventional UB (self-mill Armored Skaab, Stitcher's Apprentice, Skaab Goliath without Spider Spawning) went 1-2 and an unguided UB 1-2, losing to WR's Rebuke, Bonds of Faith and Urgent Exorcism (anecdotes). UB needs cheap blockers, a real finisher and answers, plus cheap pressure (Falkenrath Noble, Vampire Interloper). Do not take Think Twice, Dissipate or Dream Twist.
- Mono U is 4-5 in matches (21 games), mono R 3-3 (16 games), mono G 2-4 (15 games).

General notes:
- White is the best-supported colour on individual cards (Slayer of the Wicked, Midnight Haunting, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Bonds of Faith). Red has Stromkirk Noble, Furor of the Bitten, Into the Maw of Hell, Kruin Outlaw, Ashmouth Hound. Green has Villagers of Estwald, Lumberknot, Gatstaf Shepherd. Cheap white removal (Rebuke, Fiend Hunter) is contested; take it early.
- Black is crowded and contested; take black removal early in pack 1, but only if you will play black.
- Read what is flowing after pack 1. If strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8, move in. Do not hedge with a second colour's removal once set.
- Galvanic Juggernaut (56%, 317 games, taken at 1.2) is fine as a colourless finisher, but take removal, Bonds of Faith or a flier over it. It dies to Smite the Monstrous.
- Games run 11 to 62 turns, mostly 15 to 47. Grindy removal and card advantage matter.
- Splashing is poor. A six-Mountain splash went 6-0 in games once, untested for colour screw (anecdote); do not copy it without a dual land and few cards.

- Black: Sever the Bloodline (69 games, 61%, rare, taken at 1.3), Disciple of Griselbrand (146 games, 60%, taken at 9.2; but a WR stranded it, so only in black), Morkrut Banshee (226 games, 53%, taken at 1.7; BB on 7 Swamps went poorly), Bloodgift Demon, Falkenrath Noble, Crossway Vampire (a third is filler), Bloodline Keeper (won games for a BR on 2026-11-22; lost on 7 Swamps in a BG and a WB), Curse of Death's Hold (slow).
- Green: Moldgraf Monstrosity, Gatstaf Shepherd (245 games, 55%), Lumberknot (215 games, 55%), Hollowhenge Scavenger (163 games, 53%), Spider Spawning (184 games, 53%; dead in a mono-green with no enablers), Hamlet Captain, Boneyard Wurm (dies to Smite the Monstrous). Garruk Relentless was too thin to carry a WG with little other green (anecdote).
- Red: Falkenrath Marauders (71 games, 59%, rare, taken at 2.2), Stromkirk Noble (96 games, 56%, rare), Into the Maw of Hell (256 games, 55%, taken at 3.3), Kruin Outlaw (104 games, 55%, rare), Hanweir Watchkeep (152 games, 53%), Rage Thrower (175 games, 53%), Instigator Gang, Vampire Interloper, Balefire Dragon. - White: Slayer of the Wicked (238 games, 61%, taken at 1.8), Midnight Haunting (222 games, 58%), Mentor of the Meek (104 games, 58%, rare), Elite Inquisitor (104 games, 57%, rare), Rally the Peasants (222 games, 54%; closes long games), Dearly Departed (87 games, 53%), Fiend Hunter, Angelic Overseer, Divine Reckoning, Urgent Exorcism (narrow, but a clean answer to spirits), Smite the Monstrous (power 4 or more only; dead against Delver of Secrets and Stitched Drake). - Bombs: Take only if on colour and castable, and play them. Olivia Voldaren and Devil's Play were repeatedly first- or second-picked and never played.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal on colour: Sever the Bloodline, Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Claustrophobia (blue), Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Harvest Pyre, Geistflame, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith, Fiend Hunter, Sensory Deprivation (blue), Silent Departure (blue), Grasp of Phantoms (blue), Corpse Lunge, Spidery Grasp, Urgent Exorcism (vs fliers), Smite the Monstrous (big creatures only), Blazing Torch. Take a second if offered. About five is the target; the 2-1 WRs with three were short. Do not take removal outside your likely colours, and not second pick before you know them.
2. Strong uncommons and rares on colour: Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Moldgraf Monstrosity, Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee (if you can support BB), Falkenrath Marauders, Instigator Gang, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw, Rally the Peasants, Midnight Haunting, Dearly Departed, Angelic Overseer, Spider Spawning (only with self-mill), Garruk Relentless (green only).
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Invisible Stalker, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Lantern Spirit, Mindshrieker, Battleground Geist, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Scourge of Geier Reach, Somberwald Spider, Screeching Bat. Many lessons asked for evasion over ground filler; aim for three or four evasive threats or answers by the end of pack 3 and take a third flier over a second Unruly Mob, Silverchase Fox, Thraben Purebloods, Festerhide Boar or a third Villagers of Estwald. Do not take an off-colour flier over an on-colour one, and play fliers you draft. Card advantage (Mentor of the Meek, Midnight Haunting, Rally the Peasants) was asked for in several lessons.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Avacyn's Pilgrim, Ambush Viper, Kindercatch, Darkthicket Wolf, Ashmouth Hound, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils, Tormented Pariah, Silver-Inlaid Dagger, Unruly Mob (one), Doomed Traveler, Avacynian Priest, Vampire Interloper, Bump in the Night. Take playable two- and three-drops over late junk such as Frightful Delusion, Cobbled Wings, Spare from Evil, Traveler's Amulet and Ranger's Guile. In UB, take real finishers and ground blockers over low-impact fliers and sorceries.

Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell or card draw. Walking Corpse is only a 23rd card.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Stitcher's Apprentice, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Ancient Grudge, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Butcher's Cleaver and Geistcatcher's Rig over a bomb or removal (though Geistcatcher's Rig answered a flier for one WR), Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Bloodcrazed Neonate, Make a Wish, Grimoire of the Dead, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Trepanation Blade, Heartless Summoning, Curiosity, Nightbird's Clutches, Frightful Delusion, Traitorous Blood, Vampiric Fury, Reckless Waif over removal, Mask of Avacyn over a flier, Kessig Wolf, Cloistered Youth over a flier, Intangible Virtue, Ranger's Guile, a second Moment of Heroism or Lost in the Mist, a second Makeshift Mauler, and a third Festerhide Boar. Naturalize, Wooden Stakes and Sharpened Pitchfork are narrow filler. Armored Skaab, Skaab Goliath, Skaab Ruinator and Forbidden Alchemy are only for a deck with Spider Spawning and real self-mill. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default; long games support it. Cut to 16 only for a truly low curve with several two-drops (anecdotes). Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 and 2-1 WB), 11 Plains/6 Swamp (3-0 and 1-2 WB), 9 Plains/8 Islands (3-0 WU), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG), 9 Mountain/8 Plains (2-1 WR on 2026-11-22), 8 Swamp/9 Mountain (1-2 BR) (anecdotes). Count pips and give the main colour 9 to 10 sources. A 9 Forest/7 Plains WG went 0-3 and WRs on 6 or 7 Mountains with a heavy red load went badly (anecdotes).
- Double pips: give a BB or GG colour 8+ sources or cut them. A WB with Morkrut Banshee on 7 Swamps went 2-4 in games, and Bloodline Keeper on 7 Swamps lost; a 3-0 BR with Bloodgift Demon ran 7 Swamps (anecdotes). Avoid double pips in both colours. Three GG Somberwald Spiders want 8+ Forests.
- Aim for about five or six real removal spells (pumps do not count), three or four answers to fliers (Smite the Monstrous does not count), and two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers. Play the removal you drafted and the bomb you took.
- A low curve alone is not enough. 0-3 and 1-2 decks have been top-heavy, ground filler, flierless, or lacking pressure (a 1-2 UB with 18 to 26 turn stalls; an 11-creature WR; a mono-green). Count two-drops and early evasive creatures, keep one or two cheap ground defenders in tempo decks, and trade a marginal two-drop for a third flier or a removal spell.
- Keep a flexible late threat or value source (Mentor of the Meek, Moan of the Unhallowed, Gavony Township, Moorland Haunt, Rally the Peasants, Divine Reckoning), but do not cut good cheap cards to fit one.
- Self-mill needs a real plan: Spider Spawning was dead in a mono-green and a UG, and a UB of Armored Skaab, Stitcher's Apprentice and Skaab Goliath went 1-2 (anecdotes). Either draft enablers and payoffs together or leave them out.
- Tricks have a place after removal and creatures; one or two cheap tricks is fine, but extra Moment of Heroism, Ranger's Guile, Mulch and Spidery Grasp were filler for some. Enchantment-based removal on your best creature is a real risk; keep multiple threats. Toughness above one matters against Geistflame, Brimstone Volley, Harvest Pyre and Tormented Pariah; power 4+ is exposed to Smite the Monstrous. Do not leave yourself with no answer to an opposing colour's key cards (Claustrophobia, Victim of Night, Tormented Pariah, Bonds of Faith).
- Settle your 23 by pack 3. Sideboard plans rarely rescue a deck. Check every swap against your colours and do not side out main removal or threats for situational cards. Keep at least one cheap play main.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke and Fiend Hunter for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies. Hold Smite the Monstrous for power 4+ creatures only. Check creature types before counting on Victim of Night.
- Expect long games, often 15 to 47 turns, and up to 62. Break stalls with removal, Rally the Peasants, Moan of the Unhallowed and evasive threats. Apply pressure early rather than waiting, especially with UB, UR and WU.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR. Pair cheap creatures with late fliers or Rally the Peasants to close.
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Lantern Spirit, Geist of Saint Traft, Angelic Overseer, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Murder of Crows, Midnight Haunting, Falkenrath Noble, Screeching Bat, Bloodline Keeper, Galvanic Juggernaut, Boneyard Wurm, Balefire Dragon and Scourge of Geier Reach.
- Against WU, UB, UR, UG and mono-blue, kill fliers early (Delver of Secrets flips into a real threat). Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation, Grasp of Phantoms and Lost in the Mist will target your best creature, so keep a second threat ready. Blue decks win long grinds, so do not durdle.
- Against WB, expect real removal (Fiend Hunter, Smite the Monstrous, Rebuke, Victim of Night) held for your fliers and best creatures, plus Angelic Overseer, Chapel Geist and Rally the Peasants closing long games. Do not rely on one big threat.
- Against BG, WG and RG, expect Typhoid Rats, Somberwald Spider, Spidery Grasp, Villagers of Estwald, Bonds of Faith, Mentor of the Meek, Fiend Hunter, Prey Upon, Garruk Relentless, Tormented Pariah and burn to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup, expect reach, and do not flood the board with ground bodies.
- Against WR, BR and UR, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Furor of the Bitten and Pitchburn Devils to punish small creatures. Do not overcommit and keep your own fliers alive. WR also runs Rebuke (often two), Urgent Exorcism, Smite the Monstrous, Midnight Haunting, Fiend Hunter, Slayer of the Wicked, Rally the Peasants and Bonds of Faith, and will answer your fliers and four-power creatures. Hold back threats rather than feeding them one at a time.
- Against BR with Bloodline Keeper, Screeching Bat and Falkenrath Noble (it beat two guided WRs 2-0 on 2026-11-22), save removal for Bloodline Keeper and the fliers, and have card advantage for 24-plus turn games.
- Against white decks with Fiend Hunter and Avacynian Priest, expect one-for-one trades on early creatures; prefer threats that leave value behind. Expect Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning in long games.
