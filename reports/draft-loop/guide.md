# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-19's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Thirty nights: 240 decks, 887 games. Guided seats are 322-340 in games (49%, 132-138 in matches, 90 decks). The conventional guide is 82-74 (53%, 34-32 in matches, 22 decks). Unguided seats are 483-473 (51%, 194-190 in matches, 128 decks). On the eleven nights with all three kinds of seat, guided went 72-86 in games (46%, 31-35 in matches), conventional 82-74 (53%) and unguided 167-161 (51%). Guided is still behind both, and this guide has not been shown to help. Treat archetype lines as hypotheses and lean on card numbers and on advice that is consistent across lessons:
- Commit to two colours early (by mid pack 1). Stranded early picks were the most repeated lesson. On 2026-11-19 a conventional WG first-picked Devil's Play and never played it, and Spider Spawning also sat idle (anecdotes). On 2026-11-18 a guided WR took Victim of Night second and never played it; a guided WB that committed by mid pack 1 went 3-0. Earlier: Tribute to Hunger and Typhoid Rats in a WR, Sever the Bloodline in a UG, Curiosity and Spare from Evil in a BR (anecdotes).
- Count playables and keep a curve of cheap creatures, but a cheap curve must also close. Games run long (13 to 38 turns on 2026-11-19), so cheap creatures need evasion, removal or card advantage behind them.
- Take removal, but only in your colours, and play what you drafted: on 2026-11-19 an unguided BR 0-3 left Sensory Deprivation out and lost to WG's blockers (anecdote).
- Have two or three real threats that win in the air or in a long game. Fliers alone do not suffice: on 2026-11-19 a guided WB with four fliers and four removal went 1-2, losing 0-2 to UG and WG in 24 and 32 turn grinds; on 2026-11-18 and 2026-11-17 guided WRs with four fliers went 0-3 (anecdotes). But decks short of fliers lost too: on 2026-11-19 a conventional WG with no fliers needed 22 and 32 turns to beat WB, and an unguided BR of 15 cheap ground creatures went 0-3 against WG's Somberwald Spider and Villagers of Estwald (anecdotes). Green decks with Somberwald Spider beat fliers; bring removal for it.

Pairs by games won (decks, matches, game win rate), from the statistics up to 2026-11-19:
- BG: 54% (210 games, 29 decks, 48-39 in matches). Best by matches among big pairs. Take cheap removal and early blockers over a fourth fatty, and keep a flier answer. A low-curve green-based BG went 3-0 earlier; a top-heavy BG went 0-3 (anecdotes).
- RG: 54% (92 games, 12 decks, 20-16 in matches). Small sample. Needs cheap blockers, ways to reach fliers, and an answer to Claustrophobia and Victim of Night.
- WG: 53% (180 games, 25 decks, 42-33 in matches). Improving. On 2026-11-19 a guided WG went 3-0 on a low curve of Avacyn's Pilgrim, Doomed Traveler and two Gatstaf Shepherd, with Bonds of Faith, Mentor of the Meek and Rally the Peasants closing 13 to 33 turn games; a conventional WG went 2-1 (anecdotes). Needs cheap plays, a 10-source main colour and some evasion or removal.
- WR: 52% (206 games, 28 decks, 44-40 in matches). Slipping: guided WRs of 2026-11-17 and 2026-11-18 went 2-7 in matches combined, with two 0-3s (anecdotes). A heavy red load wants 8 or 9 Mountains; a WR needs removal-backed finishers beyond ground bodies.
- WU: 51% (214 games, 29 decks, 44-43 in matches). White removal plus real cheap fliers (Midnight Haunting, Invisible Stalker, Chapel Geist, Abbey Griffin, Stitched Drake, Moorland Haunt) works. On 2026-11-19 an unguided WU tempo deck went 1-2 (2-5 in games): cantrips like Think Twice did not close a 30-turn loss to UG; keep a cheap ground defender and a late threat (anecdote).
- BR: 50% (219 games, 30 decks, 47-43 in matches). On 2026-11-19 an unguided BR went 2-1 on Victim of Night, Tribute to Hunger, Brimstone Volley and a Vampire and Zombie curve, while another went 0-3 on ground bodies without Sensory Deprivation (anecdotes). BR wants cheap removal, evasive finishers like Falkenrath Noble, and card advantage. Skip Ancient Grudge, Heartless Summoning, and late duplicates of Rage Thrower or Markov Patrician.
- WB: 47% (156 games, 21 decks, 31-32 in matches). On 2026-11-18 an 11 Plains/6 Swamp white-first WB went 3-0; on 2026-11-19 the same split went 1-2 with Dead Weight, Corpse Lunge and Smite the Monstrous (anecdotes), so the build is no guarantee. Go white-first, count BB pips, and take card-advantage finishers over Walking Corpse filler.
- UR: 45% (163 games, 22 decks, 29-37 in matches). Weak. On 2026-11-19 an unguided UR went 1-2; Claustrophobia, Harvest Pyre and two Scourge of Geier Reach won a 2-0 over BR in 21 and 30 turn games, but it lost to WB and WU in 15 to 23 turn games with little card advantage (anecdote). Make UR red-first with burn and an evasive clock; cut a second Delver of Secrets and Frightful Delusion.
- UB: 45% (220 games, 29 decks, 34-53 in matches). Worst big pair. Needs a low curve, cheap blockers and cheap pressure (Falkenrath Noble). Do not take Delver of Secrets, Think Twice or Dream Twist.
- UG: 48% (61 games, 8 decks, 10-14 in matches). Weak overall, but on 2026-11-19 a conventional UG went 2-1, going 4-0 in games in rounds 1 and 2 with Murder of Crows, Moon Heron and Lantern Spirit, then losing to WG and its Somberwald Spider; it ran Spider Spawning with no self-mill enablers and it did nothing (anecdote). Mono U is 4-5 in matches (21 games) and mono R 3-3 (16 games).

General notes:
- White is the best-supported colour on individual cards (Slayer of the Wicked, Midnight Haunting, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Bonds of Faith). Red has many high-win-rate cards (Stromkirk Noble, Furor of the Bitten, Into the Maw of Hell, Kruin Outlaw, Ashmouth Hound). Green has Moldgraf Monstrosity, Lumberknot, Gatstaf Shepherd and Villagers of Estwald. Cheap white removal (Rebuke, Fiend Hunter) is contested, so take it early.
- Black is crowded in general. Take black removal early in pack 1, but only if you will play black.
- Read what is flowing after pack 1. If strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8, move in.
- Commit: main colour by about pick 5, a pair by mid pack 1, your second colour by pick 5 of pack 2 at the latest. Do not leave a colour for one early card, bomb or not, unless the new colour is clearly open (stranded: Devil's Play on 2026-11-19, Victim of Night on 2026-11-18, Olivia Voldaren on 2026-11-16, Into the Maw of Hell and Furor of the Bitten on 2026-11-15; anecdotes). If you take a bomb, stay in its colour.
- Galvanic Juggernaut (55%, 287 games, taken at 1.2) is contested and fine as a colourless finisher, but take removal, Bonds of Faith or a flier over it (a guided WG that ran it went 3-0 and said the same; anecdote).
- Games run 11 to 62 turns, mostly 15 to 38. Grindy removal and card advantage matter.
- Splashing is poor. Do not run a colour on six basics unless it is a light second colour with a dual land and few cards.

## Commons that win

- Green: Villagers of Estwald (266 games, 57%), Woodland Sleuth (246 games, 56%), Grave Bramble (206 games, 56%, taken at 9.0), Avacyn's Pilgrim (138 games, 55%, taken at 9.2), Festerhide Boar (302 games, 54%, filler; take a flier or cheap removal over a third copy, and a third removal over it, as a conventional WG said), Prey Upon (275 games, 53%, taken at 2.8), Kindercatch (303 games, 53%), Ambush Viper (255 games, 53%). Spidery Grasp and Darkthicket Wolf were dependable (anecdotes). Somberwald Spider is a flier answer and beat three decks on 2026-11-19.
- Black: Typhoid Rats (332 games, 55%), Victim of Night (322 games, 53%, taken at 1.6; it cannot target Vampires, Werewolves or Zombies), Dead Weight, Corpse Lunge, Bump in the Night, Ghoulcaller's Chant (self-mill decks only).
- White: Voiceless Spirit (414 games, 55%), Avacynian Priest (358 games, 55%), Bonds of Faith (347 games, 55%), Thraben Sentry (260 games, 53%), Unruly Mob (305 games, 53%, taken at 9.0; one is fine), Selfless Cathar, Chapel Geist, Doomed Traveler, Silverchase Fox (ground filler).
- Red: Furor of the Bitten (160 games, 56%, taken at 9.7; a pump, not removal), Ashmouth Hound (309 games, 54%), Tormented Pariah, Pitchburn Devils, Night Revelers, Geistflame, Brimstone Volley, Harvest Pyre.
- Blue: Spectral Flight, Claustrophobia, Hysterical Blindness (late pick).
- Colourless: Silver-Inlaid Dagger (169 games, 53%). Blazing Torch was praised (anecdotes).
- Claustrophobia, Rebuke, Dead Weight, Tribute to Hunger, Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Sensory Deprivation and Stitched Drake are praised in lessons and go early, but only on colour.

## Uncommons and rares

- Green: Moldgraf Monstrosity (63 games, 59%, rare, taken at 4.3), Gatstaf Shepherd (221 games, 57%), Lumberknot (200 games, 56%), Hollowhenge Scavenger (148 games, 55%), Spider Spawning (177 games, 55%; needs self-mill enablers, and idle without them), Hamlet Captain (193 games, 53%), Boneyard Wurm (dies to Smite the Monstrous; do not take a second). Garruk Relentless carried a 2-1 BG (anecdote).
- Black: Disciple of Griselbrand (140 games, 58%, taken at 9.2), Morkrut Banshee (226 games, 53%, taken at 1.7; BB on 7 Swamps went poorly), Bloodgift Demon, Sever the Bloodline, Falkenrath Noble, Crossway Vampire (a third is filler), Curse of Death's Hold (slow).
- Red: Stromkirk Noble (88 games, 58%, rare), Desperate Ravings (65 games, 57%, taken at 10.3, a late pick), Into the Maw of Hell (227 games, 56%, taken at 3.4), Kruin Outlaw (96 games, 53%, rare), Hanweir Watchkeep (146 games, 53%), Falkenrath Marauders, Rage Thrower, Instigator Gang, Vampire Interloper.
- White: Slayer of the Wicked (214 games, 60%, taken at 1.8), Midnight Haunting (197 games, 58%), Elite Inquisitor (96 games, 56%, rare), Mentor of the Meek (95 games, 57%, rare), Rally the Peasants (200 games, 54%, taken at 6.8; closes long games), Dearly Departed (74 games, 53%, rare), Fiend Hunter, Angelic Overseer, Divine Reckoning, Smite the Monstrous (kills power-4 creatures). Cloistered Youth is only average.
- Blue: Murder of Crows (only if blue), Invisible Stalker, Stitched Drake, Mindshrieker, Battleground Geist, Moon Heron, Lantern Spirit and Orchard Spirit. Blue fliers beat ground decks on several nights but lost to Somberwald Spider, Victim of Night and WG.
- Colourless: Manor Gargoyle (110 games, 55%, rare, taken at 2.2). Galvanic Juggernaut: see notes above. Smite the Monstrous hits it.
- Lands: Moorland Haunt (comes late), Sulfur Falls (60 games, 55%, taken at 11.2, in blue-red decks), Woodland Cemetery in B/G, Gavony Township in a long-game white deck (anecdote).
- Bombs: Bloodline Keeper lost on 7 Swamps in a BG and a WB. Garruk Relentless never made a WU deck but won in a BG. Olivia Voldaren and Devil's Play were first-picked and never played. Take bombs only if they will be on colour and castable, and back them with a second threat.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal on colour: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia (blue), Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Smite the Monstrous, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith, Fiend Hunter, Sensory Deprivation (blue), Corpse Lunge, Harvest Pyre, Spidery Grasp, Blazing Torch. Take a second if offered. About five is the target; six worked in one 3-0 WB, and two Rebuke plus two Blazing Torch felt too few to a WG (anecdotes). Do not take removal outside your likely colours, and not second pick before you know them.
2. Strong uncommons and rares on colour: Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Moldgraf Monstrosity, Sever the Bloodline, Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee (if you can support BB), Falkenrath Marauders, Instigator Gang, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw, Rally the Peasants, Midnight Haunting, Dearly Departed, Angelic Overseer, Spider Spawning (only with self-mill), Garruk Relentless (green only).
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Invisible Stalker, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Lantern Spirit, Mindshrieker, Battleground Geist, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Scourge of Geier Reach, Somberwald Spider. Lessons on 2026-11-19 asked for evasion over ground filler four times, yet fliers alone also lost; aim for three or four evasive threats or answers by the end of pack 3 and take a third flier over a second Unruly Mob, Silverchase Fox, Festerhide Boar or Gatstaf Shepherd. Do not take an off-colour flier over an on-colour one. Against green, count reach. Card advantage (Mentor of the Meek, Rally the Peasants) was asked for in several lessons.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Avacyn's Pilgrim, Ambush Viper, Kindercatch, Darkthicket Wolf, Ashmouth Hound, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils and Tormented Pariah if red, Silver-Inlaid Dagger, Unruly Mob (one), Doomed Traveler, Avacynian Priest, Vampire Interloper, Bump in the Night. Take playable two- and three-drops over late-pick junk such as Delver of Secrets, Frightful Delusion, Cobbled Wings and Spare from Evil, which went unplayed. Against WR, prefer cheap creatures to a second four-power body.

Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell or card draw. Walking Corpse is only a 23rd card.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Stitcher's Apprentice, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Ancient Grudge, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab over a better creature, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Bloodcrazed Neonate, Make a Wish, Grimoire of the Dead, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Trepanation Blade, Delver of Secrets, Heartless Summoning, Curiosity, Spare from Evil, Nightbird's Clutches, Frightful Delusion, Traitorous Blood, Vampiric Fury, Mask of Avacyn over a flier, Riot Devils, Kessig Wolf, Cloistered Youth over a flier, a second Moment of Heroism, a second Makeshift Mauler, and a third Festerhide Boar. Naturalize, Wooden Stakes and Sharpened Pitchfork are narrow filler. Lost in the Mist is only worth a second copy in a controlling blue deck. Armored Skaab, Skaab Goliath and Forbidden Alchemy are only for a deck with Spider Spawning and real self-mill. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default; long games (16 to 33 turns on 2026-11-19) support it. Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 and 2-1 WB), 11 Plains/6 Swamp (3-0 WB on 2026-11-18, 1-2 WB on 2026-11-19), 11 Plains/6 Islands (2-1 WU), 9 Plains/8 Islands (3-0 WU), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG), mostly red with 6 Islands plus Sulfur Falls (3-0 UR) (anecdotes). Count pips and give the main colour 9 to 10 sources. A 9 Forest/7 Plains WG went 0-3 and WRs on 6 or 7 Mountains went badly with a heavy red load (anecdotes). Give a colour with several double-pip or removal cards eight or nine sources.
- Double pips: give a BB or GG colour 8+ sources or cut them. A WB with Morkrut Banshee and Abattoir Ghoul on 7 Swamps went 2-4 in games, and Bloodline Keeper on 7 Swamps lost; but a 3-0 BR with Bloodgift Demon ran 7 Swamps (anecdotes). Avoid double pips in both colours. Three GG Somberwald Spiders want 8+ Forests.
- Aim for about five or six real removal spells (pumps do not count), three or four answers to fliers (which can also clear ground blockers), and two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers. Play the removal you drafted.
- Keep a curve with enough cheap plays. The 0-3 BG, 0-3 WRs and 0-3 ground BRs were top-heavy or full of ground filler, while winning decks (3-0 BG, RG, WU, WB, WG) had early drops and burn or removal. A low curve alone is not enough: the 0-3 BR of 15 cheap ground creatures on 2026-11-19 and UBs with no pressure lost 17 to 33 turn games. Count two-drops and early evasive creatures, and keep one or two cheap ground defenders in tempo decks.
- Keep a flexible late threat or value source (Mentor of the Meek, Moan of the Unhallowed, Gavony Township, Moorland Haunt, Rally the Peasants, Divine Reckoning), but do not cut good cheap cards to fit one.
- Pair removal with good cheap creatures and close with fliers. Do not pad with ground filler when a flier or removal spell is available.
- Self-mill needs a real plan: Spider Spawning idled in a UG with no enablers on 2026-11-19, and a UB with thin fuel lost earlier (anecdotes). Either draft enablers and payoffs together or leave them out.
- Tricks have a place after removal and creatures; one or two cheap tricks in a ground deck is fine. Enchantment-based removal on your best creature is a real risk; keep multiple threats. Toughness above one matters against Geistflame, Brimstone Volley and Harvest Pyre; power 4+ is exposed to Smite the Monstrous. Do not leave yourself with no answer to an opposing colour's key cards (Claustrophobia, Victim of Night, combat tricks like Bonds of Faith).
- Settle your 23 in the main deck by pack 3. Sideboard plans rarely rescue a deck. Plan post-board swaps before round one, check every swap against your colours, and do not side out main removal or threats for situational cards. Keep at least one cheap play main.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core. Do not main Gnaw to the Bone, Mulch, Dream Twist, Make a Wish or self-mill cards without a self-mill deck.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke, Fiend Hunter and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies. Check creature types before counting on Victim of Night.
- Expect long games, often 15 to 40 turns, and up to 62. Break stalls with removal, Rally the Peasants, Moan of the Unhallowed and evasive threats. Apply pressure early rather than waiting, especially with UB, UR and WU.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR. Fast starts win some 9 to 17 turn games, but pair cheap creatures with late fliers or Rally the Peasants to close.
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Lantern Spirit, Angelic Overseer, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Murder of Crows, Midnight Haunting, Falkenrath Noble, Screeching Bat, Galvanic Juggernaut, Boneyard Wurm and Scourge of Geier Reach.
- Against WU, UB, UR, UG and mono-blue, kill fliers early. Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation and Lost in the Mist will target your best creature, so keep a second threat ready. Blue decks win long grinds, so do not durdle.
- Against WB, expect real removal (Fiend Hunter, Smite the Monstrous, Rebuke, Victim of Night) held for your fliers and best creatures, plus Angelic Overseer, Chapel Geist and Rally the Peasants closing long games. Do not rely on one big threat, and bring card advantage.
- Against BG, WG and RG, expect Typhoid Rats, Somberwald Spider, Spidery Grasp, Villagers of Estwald, Bonds of Faith, Mentor of the Meek, Fiend Hunter, Prey Upon, Garruk Relentless and burn to make the ground hard. A WG with those went 3-0 on 2026-11-19. Win in the air with removal backup, expect reach, keep an answer for combat tricks, and do not flood the board with ground bodies.
- Against WR, BR and UR, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Furor of the Bitten and Pitchburn Devils to punish small creatures. Do not overcommit and keep your own fliers alive. WR also runs Rebuke (sometimes two), Smite the Monstrous, Midnight Haunting, Fiend Hunter, Slayer of the Wicked, Rally the Peasants and Bonds of Faith, and will answer your fliers, tricked-up creatures and four-power creatures.
- Against white decks with Fiend Hunter and Avacynian Priest, expect one-for-one trades on early creatures; prefer threats that leave value behind. Expect Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning in long games.
