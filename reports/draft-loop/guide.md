# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-20's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Thirty-one nights: 248 decks, 919 games. Guided seats are 328-349 in games (48%, 134-142 in matches, 92 decks). The conventional guide is 93-80 (54%, 39-33 in matches, 24 decks). Unguided seats are 498-490 (50%, 199-197 in matches, 132 decks). On the twelve nights with all three kinds of seat, guided went 78-95 (45%, 33-39 in matches), conventional 93-80 (54%) and unguided 182-178 (51%). Guided is still behind both, and this guide has not been shown to help. Treat archetype lines as hypotheses and lean on card numbers and on advice that is consistent across lessons.

- Commit to two colours early (by mid pack 1). Stranded early picks were the most repeated lesson. On 2026-11-20 a conventional WU took two Brimstone Volley in pack 2 while firmly WU and called it wasted; an unguided UB first-picked Olivia Voldaren, left her out, and played Butcher's Cleaver and Geistcatcher's Rig instead (anecdotes). Earlier: Devil's Play (2026-11-19), Victim of Night (2026-11-18), Olivia Voldaren (2026-11-16), Into the Maw of Hell and Furor of the Bitten (2026-11-15) were taken and never played (anecdotes). If you take a bomb, stay in its colour and play it.
- Count fliers and flier answers before ground filler. On 2026-11-20 three guided and unguided WG/UB lessons said the same: a WG with two Voiceless Spirit, Abbey Griffin and Somberwald Spiders lost 1-2, a guided WG lost 15 and 11 turn games to UB and WG, an unguided WG lacked flying defence against Stitched Drake and Delver of Secrets, and a UB lost to Geist of Saint Traft and Moon Heron with few cheap answers (all anecdotes). Take cheap flier answers (Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Silent Departure) before an extra two-drop.
- Cheap curves must also close. Games ran 11 to 51 turns on 2026-11-20. A sixteen-creature low curve needed finishers (WG anecdote), and a UB self-mill deck stretched games to 51 and 29 turns without a clock. Have two or three real threats that win in the air or in a long game, backed by removal.
- Take removal, but only in your colours, and play what you drafted.
- Green decks with Somberwald Spider beat fliers; bring removal for it. Balefire Dragon in an RG beat a guided UB 2-0 in 18 and 22 turns on 2026-11-20 (anecdote): keep two flier answers before pack 3.

Pairs by games won (decks, matches, game win rate), from the statistics up to 2026-11-20:
- RG: 56% (100 games, 13 decks, 23-16 in matches). Best by rate, still a small sample. On 2026-11-20 a conventional RG went 3-0 on Harvest Pyre (its only real red removal; Brimstone Volley never came), three Tormented Pariah (picks 2, 5 and 9 of pack 3), Darkthicket Wolf, Festerhide Boar and 17 lands (anecdote). Needs cheap blockers, reach, and an answer to Claustrophobia and Victim of Night.
- BG: 54% (210 games, 29 decks, 48-39 in matches). Best by matches among big pairs. Take cheap removal and early blockers over a fourth fatty, and keep a flier answer. A low-curve green-based BG went 3-0 earlier; a top-heavy BG went 0-3 (anecdotes).
- WG: 52% (196 games, 27 decks, 44-37 in matches). On 2026-11-20 two guided/unguided WGs went 1-2 each, one guided WG 1-2 and one unguided 1-2, all with plenty of cheap ground bodies and too few fliers or removal (anecdotes); a guided WG went 3-0 on 2026-11-19 with Bonds of Faith, Mentor of the Meek and Rally the Peasants. Needs cheap plays, a 10-source main colour, and evasion or removal; the winners had card advantage.
- WR: 52% (206 games, 28 decks, 44-40 in matches). Slipping: guided WRs of 2026-11-17 and 2026-11-18 went 2-7 in matches combined. A heavy red load wants 8 or 9 Mountains; a WR needs removal-backed finishers beyond ground bodies.
- WU: 52% (223 games, 30 decks, 46-44 in matches). White removal plus real cheap fliers (Midnight Haunting, Invisible Stalker, Chapel Geist, Abbey Griffin, Stitched Drake, Moorland Haunt) works. A conventional WU went 2-1 on 2026-11-20 but lost to three Tormented Pariah for lack of cheap answers; Blazing Torch did not make its deck (anecdotes). Keep a cheap ground defender and a late threat.
- BR: 49% (227 games, 31 decks, 48-45 in matches). On 2026-11-20 an unguided BR went 1-2 (0-2 to WG with Somberwald Spider, three Villagers of Estwald and Travel Preparations) because small creatures and one-drops stalled; take removal and bigger bodies over Reckless Waif-type one-drops (anecdote). BR wants cheap removal, evasive finishers like Falkenrath Noble, and card advantage. Skip Ancient Grudge, Heartless Summoning, and late duplicates of Rage Thrower or Markov Patrician.
- WB: 47% (156 games, 21 decks, 31-32 in matches). An 11 Plains/6 Swamp white-first WB went 3-0 on 2026-11-18 and 1-2 on 2026-11-19 (anecdotes). Go white-first, count BB pips, and take card-advantage finishers over Walking Corpse filler.
- UG: 48% (61 games, 8 decks, 10-14 in matches). Weak overall. A conventional UG went 2-1 on 2026-11-19 with Murder of Crows, Moon Heron and Lantern Spirit; Spider Spawning did nothing without self-mill (anecdote).
- UR: 45% (163 games, 22 decks, 29-37 in matches). Weak. Make UR red-first with burn and an evasive clock; cut a second Delver of Secrets and Frightful Delusion.
- UB: 46% (243 games, 32 decks, 38-58 in matches). Worst big pair. On 2026-11-20 three UBs went 2-1 (unguided), 1-2 (guided, with Delver of Secrets and Markov Patrician) and 1-2 (unguided self-mill on Skaab Ruinator and Stitcher's Apprentice) (anecdotes). The 2-1 deck had a good body count (a second Stitched Drake came late) and wished for more removal. UB needs a low curve, cheap blockers and answers (Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation, Sever the Bloodline) plus cheap pressure (Falkenrath Noble). Do not take Delver of Secrets, Think Twice or Dream Twist.
- Mono U is 4-5 in matches (21 games) and mono R 3-3 (16 games).

General notes:
- White is the best-supported colour on individual cards (Slayer of the Wicked, Midnight Haunting, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Bonds of Faith). Red has many high-win-rate cards (Stromkirk Noble, Furor of the Bitten, Into the Maw of Hell, Kruin Outlaw, Ashmouth Hound). Green has Moldgraf Monstrosity, Lumberknot, Gatstaf Shepherd and Villagers of Estwald. Cheap white removal (Rebuke, Fiend Hunter) is contested, so take it early.
- Black is crowded. Take black removal early in pack 1, but only if you will play black.
- Read what is flowing after pack 1. If strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8, move in. Blue-black creatures flowed late on 2026-11-20 (anecdote).
- Commit: main colour by about pick 5, a pair by mid pack 1, your second colour by pick 5 of pack 2 at the latest. Do not hedge with a second colour's removal once you are set.
- Galvanic Juggernaut (55%, 295 games, taken at 1.2) is contested and fine as a colourless finisher, but take removal, Bonds of Faith or a flier over it.
- Games run 11 to 62 turns, mostly 15 to 38. Grindy removal and card advantage matter.
- Splashing is poor. Do not run a colour on six basics unless it is a light second colour with a dual land and few cards.

## Commons that win

- Green: Villagers of Estwald (282 games, 57%), Woodland Sleuth (262 games, 56%), Grave Bramble (214 games, 56%, taken at 8.9), Avacyn's Pilgrim (154 games, 54%, taken at 9.0), Festerhide Boar (318 games, 54%; a conventional 3-0 RG ran it, but it is filler: take a flier or cheap removal over a third copy), Prey Upon (283 games, 53%, taken at 2.8), Kindercatch (311 games, 53%). Spidery Grasp and Darkthicket Wolf were dependable, though an unguided WG called Spidery Grasp and Mulch filler (anecdotes). Somberwald Spider is a flier answer and beat several decks on 2026-11-19 and 2026-11-20.
- Black: Typhoid Rats (347 games, 54%), Victim of Night (330 games, 53%, taken at 1.6; it cannot target Vampires, Werewolves or Zombies), Dead Weight, Corpse Lunge, Bump in the Night, Ghoulcaller's Chant (self-mill decks only).
- White: Voiceless Spirit (422 games, 55%), Avacynian Priest (367 games, 55%), Bonds of Faith (356 games, 55%), Thraben Sentry (260 games, 53%), Unruly Mob (313 games, 53%; one is fine), Selfless Cathar, Chapel Geist, Doomed Traveler, Silverchase Fox (ground filler).
- Red: Furor of the Bitten (168 games, 57%, taken at 9.7; a pump, not removal), Ashmouth Hound (317 games, 55%), Tormented Pariah (carried a 3-0 RG werewolf plan; also beat a WU 2-1; anecdotes), Pitchburn Devils, Night Revelers, Geistflame, Brimstone Volley, Harvest Pyre (take early if red: it was the only red removal in a 3-0 RG).
- Blue: Spectral Flight, Claustrophobia, Hysterical Blindness (late pick).
- Colourless: Silver-Inlaid Dagger (53% earlier). Blazing Torch is praised in some lessons but missed a WU deck on 2026-11-20 (anecdotes).
- Claustrophobia, Rebuke, Dead Weight, Tribute to Hunger, Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Sensory Deprivation and Stitched Drake are praised in lessons and go early, but only on colour.

- Black: Sever the Bloodline (69 games, 61%, rare, taken at 1.3), Disciple of Griselbrand (140 games, 58%, taken at 9.2), Morkrut Banshee (226 games, 53%, taken at 1.7; BB on 7 Swamps went poorly), Bloodgift Demon (65 games, 54%), Falkenrath Noble, Crossway Vampire (a third is filler), Curse of Death's Hold (slow).
- Green: Moldgraf Monstrosity (63 games, 59%, rare, taken at 4.3), Gatstaf Shepherd (237 games, 56%), Lumberknot (208 games, 55%), Hollowhenge Scavenger (148 games, 55%), Spider Spawning (177 games, 55%; idle without self-mill enablers), Hamlet Captain (201 games, 53%), Boneyard Wurm (dies to Smite the Monstrous; do not take a second). Garruk Relentless carried a 2-1 BG (anecdote).
- Red: Falkenrath Marauders (65 games, 58%, rare, taken at 2.3), Stromkirk Noble (88 games, 58%, rare), Desperate Ravings (65 games, 57%, late pick), Into the Maw of Hell (235 games, 55%, taken at 3.4), Kruin Outlaw (104 games, 55%, rare), Hanweir Watchkeep (146 games, 53%), Rage Thrower, Instigator Gang, Vampire Interloper. Balefire Dragon beat a UB 2-0 (anecdote).
- White: Slayer of the Wicked (223 games, 60%, taken at 1.8), Midnight Haunting (206 games, 58%), Mentor of the Meek (95 games, 57%, rare), Elite Inquisitor (96 games, 56%, rare), Rally the Peasants (209 games, 54%, taken at 6.7; closes long games), Dearly Departed, Fiend Hunter, Angelic Overseer, Divine Reckoning, Smite the Monstrous (kills power-4 creatures). Cloistered Youth is only average.
- Blue: Murder of Crows (only if blue), Invisible Stalker, Stitched Drake, Mindshrieker, Battleground Geist, Moon Heron, Lantern Spirit and Orchard Spirit. Geist of Saint Traft and Moon Heron beat a UB on 2026-11-20 (anecdote). Blue fliers lost to Somberwald Spider, Victim of Night and WG.
- Colourless: Manor Gargoyle (110 games, 55%, rare, taken at 2.2). Galvanic Juggernaut: see notes above. Smite the Monstrous hits it.
- Lands: Moorland Haunt (comes late), Sulfur Falls (blue-red), Woodland Cemetery in B/G, Gavony Township in a long-game white deck (anecdote).
- Bombs: Bloodline Keeper lost on 7 Swamps in a BG and a WB. Olivia Voldaren and Devil's Play were first- or second-picked and never played, Olivia a second time on 2026-11-20. Take bombs only if they will be on colour and castable, and play them.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal on colour: Sever the Bloodline, Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Claustrophobia (blue), Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Harvest Pyre, Geistflame, Smite the Monstrous, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith, Fiend Hunter, Sensory Deprivation (blue), Silent Departure (blue), Corpse Lunge, Spidery Grasp, Blazing Torch. Take a second if offered. About five is the target; six worked in one 3-0 WB. Do not take removal outside your likely colours, and not second pick before you know them.
2. Strong uncommons and rares on colour: Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Moldgraf Monstrosity, Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee (if you can support BB), Falkenrath Marauders, Instigator Gang, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw, Rally the Peasants, Midnight Haunting, Dearly Departed, Angelic Overseer, Spider Spawning (only with self-mill), Garruk Relentless (green only).
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Invisible Stalker, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Lantern Spirit, Mindshrieker, Battleground Geist, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Scourge of Geier Reach, Somberwald Spider. Lessons on 2026-11-19 and 2026-11-20 asked for evasion and flier answers over ground filler about seven times; aim for three or four evasive threats or answers by the end of pack 3 and take a third flier over a second Unruly Mob, Silverchase Fox, Festerhide Boar, or a third Villagers of Estwald. Do not take an off-colour flier over an on-colour one. Against green, count reach. Card advantage (Mentor of the Meek, Rally the Peasants) was asked for in several lessons.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Avacyn's Pilgrim, Ambush Viper, Kindercatch, Darkthicket Wolf, Ashmouth Hound, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils, Tormented Pariah (a werewolf plan in RG and a threat to decks with no cheap answers), Silver-Inlaid Dagger, Unruly Mob (one), Doomed Traveler, Avacynian Priest, Vampire Interloper, Bump in the Night. Take playable two- and three-drops over late-pick junk such as Delver of Secrets, Frightful Delusion, Cobbled Wings and Spare from Evil. Against WR, prefer cheap creatures to a second four-power body. In UB, take the early curve before late engines.

Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell or card draw. Walking Corpse is only a 23rd card.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Stitcher's Apprentice, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Ancient Grudge, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Butcher's Cleaver and Geistcatcher's Rig over a bomb or removal, Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab over a better creature, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Bloodcrazed Neonate, Make a Wish, Grimoire of the Dead, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Trepanation Blade, Delver of Secrets, Heartless Summoning, Curiosity, Spare from Evil, Nightbird's Clutches, Frightful Delusion, Traitorous Blood, Vampiric Fury, Reckless Waif over removal, Mask of Avacyn over a flier, Riot Devils, Kessig Wolf, Cloistered Youth over a flier, a second Moment of Heroism, a second Makeshift Mauler, and a third Festerhide Boar. Naturalize, Wooden Stakes and Sharpened Pitchfork are narrow filler. Lost in the Mist is only worth a second copy in a controlling blue deck. Armored Skaab, Skaab Goliath, Skaab Ruinator and Forbidden Alchemy are only for a deck with Spider Spawning and real self-mill. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default; long games (up to 51 turns on 2026-11-20) support it, and a 3-0 RG ran 17 with fine mana (anecdote). Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 and 2-1 WB), 11 Plains/6 Swamp (3-0 and 1-2 WB), 11 Plains/6 Islands (2-1 WU), 9 Plains/8 Islands (3-0 WU), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG), mostly red with 6 Islands plus Sulfur Falls (3-0 UR) (anecdotes). Count pips and give the main colour 9 to 10 sources. A 9 Forest/7 Plains WG went 0-3 and WRs on 6 or 7 Mountains went badly with a heavy red load (anecdotes).
- Double pips: give a BB or GG colour 8+ sources or cut them. A WB with Morkrut Banshee and Abattoir Ghoul on 7 Swamps went 2-4 in games, and Bloodline Keeper on 7 Swamps lost; but a 3-0 BR with Bloodgift Demon ran 7 Swamps (anecdotes). Avoid double pips in both colours. Three GG Somberwald Spiders want 8+ Forests.
- Aim for about five or six real removal spells (pumps do not count), three or four answers to fliers (which can also clear ground blockers), and two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers. Play the removal you drafted and the bomb you took.
- Keep a curve with enough cheap plays, but a low curve alone is not enough. The 0-3 BG, 0-3 WRs and 0-3 ground BRs were top-heavy or ground filler; the 0-3 BR of 15 cheap ground creatures, UBs with no pressure, and 1-2 WGs of 15 to 16 cheap creatures lost to fliers and grinds. Count two-drops and early evasive creatures, keep one or two cheap ground defenders in tempo decks, and trade a marginal two-drop for a third flier or a removal spell.
- Keep a flexible late threat or value source (Mentor of the Meek, Moan of the Unhallowed, Gavony Township, Moorland Haunt, Rally the Peasants, Divine Reckoning), but do not cut good cheap cards to fit one.
- Self-mill needs a real plan: Spider Spawning idled in a UG with no enablers, and UBs on Skaab Ruinator and Stitcher's Apprentice were slow (anecdotes). Either draft enablers and payoffs together or leave them out.
- Tricks have a place after removal and creatures; one or two cheap tricks in a ground deck is fine, but an unguided WG called Mulch and Spidery Grasp filler. Enchantment-based removal on your best creature is a real risk; keep multiple threats. Toughness above one matters against Geistflame, Brimstone Volley, Harvest Pyre and Tormented Pariah; power 4+ is exposed to Smite the Monstrous. Do not leave yourself with no answer to an opposing colour's key cards (Claustrophobia, Victim of Night, Tormented Pariah, combat tricks like Bonds of Faith).
- Settle your 23 in the main deck by pack 3. Sideboard plans rarely rescue a deck. Plan post-board swaps before round one, check every swap against your colours, and do not side out main removal or threats for situational cards. Keep at least one cheap play main.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core. Do not main Gnaw to the Bone, Mulch, Dream Twist, Make a Wish or self-mill cards without a self-mill deck.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke, Fiend Hunter and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies. Check creature types before counting on Victim of Night.
- Expect long games, often 15 to 40 turns, and up to 62. Break stalls with removal, Rally the Peasants, Moan of the Unhallowed and evasive threats. Apply pressure early rather than waiting, especially with UB, UR and WU.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR. Fast starts win some 9 to 17 turn games, but pair cheap creatures with late fliers or Rally the Peasants to close.
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Lantern Spirit, Geist of Saint Traft, Angelic Overseer, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Murder of Crows, Midnight Haunting, Falkenrath Noble, Screeching Bat, Galvanic Juggernaut, Boneyard Wurm, Balefire Dragon and Scourge of Geier Reach.
- Against WU, UB, UR, UG and mono-blue, kill fliers early. Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation and Lost in the Mist will target your best creature, so keep a second threat ready. Blue decks win long grinds, so do not durdle.
- Against WB, expect real removal (Fiend Hunter, Smite the Monstrous, Rebuke, Victim of Night) held for your fliers and best creatures, plus Angelic Overseer, Chapel Geist and Rally the Peasants closing long games. Do not rely on one big threat, and bring card advantage.
- Against BG, WG and RG, expect Typhoid Rats, Somberwald Spider, Spidery Grasp, Villagers of Estwald, Bonds of Faith, Mentor of the Meek, Fiend Hunter, Prey Upon, Garruk Relentless, Travel Preparations, Tormented Pariah and burn to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup, expect reach, keep an answer for combat tricks, and do not flood the board with ground bodies.
- Against WR, BR and UR, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Furor of the Bitten and Pitchburn Devils to punish small creatures. Do not overcommit and keep your own fliers alive. WR also runs Rebuke (sometimes two), Smite the Monstrous, Midnight Haunting, Fiend Hunter, Slayer of the Wicked, Rally the Peasants and Bonds of Faith, and will answer your fliers, tricked-up creatures and four-power creatures.
- Against white decks with Fiend Hunter and Avacynian Priest, expect one-for-one trades on early creatures; prefer threats that leave value behind. Expect Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning in long games.
