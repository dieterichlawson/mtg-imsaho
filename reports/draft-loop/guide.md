# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-10-23's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Three nights: 24 decks, 91 games. Guided seats are 11-16 in games (41%, 27 games) against 52% unguided (155 games), so do not treat this guide as proven; judge by the card numbers.

- BG is still best: 8-1 in matches, 74% of games (23 games, 3 decks). Tonight a BG deck on 10 Forest and 7 Swamp went 2-1, so the split is not a guarantee. It lost to WU fliers.
- UB 5-7 in matches, 50% of games (32 games). Tonight's UB went 2-1 on Bloodgift Demon, Morkrut Banshee and cheap black removal.
- BR 48% (23 games), WU 46% (24 games), WG 44% (27 games), WR 47% (15 games), UR 38% (16 games).
- RG 2-1 (8 games, anecdote). Mono-blue went 1-2 (6 games, anecdote).
- Fliers decided the tables tonight. Ground decks (BR, WG, WR) stalled against 4-toughness green bodies and lost to Stitched Drake, Murder of Crows and Voiceless Spirit.
- Lean green or black first, and add fliers and removal. Choose a main colour by about pick 5 and a pair before pack 3. Do not splash; tonight a WU deck went 3-0 by cutting its red.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal: Victim of Night, Prey Upon, Dead Weight, Claustrophobia, Rebuke, Geistflame, Brimstone Volley, Tribute to Hunger.
2. Bombs and strong uncommons: Morkrut Banshee, Bloodgift Demon, Sever the Bloodline, Murder of Crows, Spider Spawning, Hollowhenge Scavenger.
3. Evasive threats: Stitched Drake, Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit. Take at least two fliers before a third ground two-drop.
4. Good green commons: Villagers of Estwald, Somberwald Spider, Darkthicket Wolf, Festerhide Boar, Orchard Spirit.
5. Two-drops such as Typhoid Rats.

Pass: Ancient Grudge, Dream Twist, Traveler's Amulet, Silver-Inlaid Dagger, a second Night Terrors or Forbidden Alchemy. Take removal and flier answers before pick 10, then playables over speculation.

## Building

- Play 16 or 17 lands. With a low curve of small creatures, play 16: a 17-land small-creature deck stalled in 54-turn games (BR, anecdote).
- Split basics by colour weight. Avoid double-pip cards in the lighter colour.
- Play 5 or more two-drops; a BG deck with only three lost.
- Include at least two evasive finishers and two or three answers to fliers.
- Cut the second copy of weak cards (Night Terrors, Forbidden Alchemy, Harvest Pyre) rather than splash. Keep playable removal and bodies out of the sideboard.

## Playing

- Games run 14 to 54 turns, and stalls are common. Trade early, hold removal for fliers and big blockers, and do not race a deck that cannot finish.
- Break stalled boards with fliers or removal, not more ground bodies.
- Against Rebuke and Smite the Monstrous, cheap evasive attackers are safer than one big threat. Rebuke kills any attacker, and Smite kills power 4 or more.
- Block early when your deck is slow; WU lost quickly to wide green ground creatures.
- Respect Victim of Night and Vampire Interloper from UB.
