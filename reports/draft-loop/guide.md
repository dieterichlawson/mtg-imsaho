# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-27's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Thirty-eight nights: 304 decks, 1127 games. Guided seats are 378-402 in games (48%, 153-165 in matches, 106 decks). The conventional guide is 149-125 (54%, 62-52 in matches, 38 decks). Unguided seats are 600-600 (50%, 241-239 in matches, 160 decks). On the nineteen nights with all three kinds of seat, guided went 128-148 (46%, 52-62 in matches), conventional 149-125 (54%) and unguided 284-288 (50%). Guided is still behind and this guide has not been shown to help. Treat archetype lines as hypotheses; lean on card numbers and on advice that is consistent across lessons.

- Commit to two colours early (by mid pack 1; second colour by mid pack 2 at the latest). Stranded picks are the most repeated lesson. On 2026-11-27 an unguided UR (0-3) registered Curse of the Pierced Heart after taking Cellar Door, Fortress Crab and Manor Skeleton late, leaving too few playables (anecdote). Earlier: Abbey Griffin, Voiceless Spirit, Curse of Oblivion, Claustrophobia, Dead Weight, Invisible Stalker, Into the Maw of Hell, Disciple of Griselbrand, Fiend Hunter, Balefire Dragon, Bloodline Keeper, Olivia Voldaren, Devil's Play and Geist of Saint Traft were taken and not played (anecdotes). If you take a bomb, stay in its colour and play it. Once set, do not take cards outside your colours. A first pick is a bet: do not follow it with a second off-plan pick, but check whether the first pick's colour is still flowing before abandoning it.
- Fliers matter, but reach and answers matter too. On 2026-11-27 three guided WGs all went 1-2 with only four fliers or fewer (Abbey Griffin x2, Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit; or Angelic Overseer, Chapel Geist, Spectral Rider, Voiceless Spirit) and lost 19 to 33 turn games; a conventional WU went 2-1 with about three true fliers (Geist of Saint Traft, two Gallows Warden) (anecdotes). An unguided UB built on ground bodies lost 0-2 in 12 and 15 turns to WU's Geist of Saint Traft and Silent Departure (anecdote). Earlier a guided WG went 3-0 with Angel of Flight Alabaster, Midnight Haunting, Gallows Warden, Orchard Spirit, Spidery Grasp, Bonds of Faith, Urgent Exorcism and Slayer of the Wicked, and a conventional WR went 3-0 on cheap fliers and removal (anecdotes). Take cheap flier answers (Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Silent Departure, Brimstone Volley, Rebuke, Urgent Exorcism) before an extra two-drop, and a third or fourth flier over ground filler such as Festerhide Boar, Kindercatch, Village Bell-Ringer or Silverchase Fox.
- Reach cuts the other way: WG's Somberwald Spider beat a WB and a WU 0-2 earlier (anecdotes). Keep Rebuke, Urgent Exorcism or Slayer of the Wicked for reach blockers.
- Do not sideboard evasive threats out for filler: a conventional WU sided out Stitched Drake for Dream Twist and Mulch and lost (anecdote).
- Cheap curves must also close. Games run 11 to 62 turns; many ran 25 to 46. Have two or three real threats that win in the air or long, backed by removal and card advantage. But early pressure matters too: a conventional WU lost 1-2 to BR (12, 17, 17 turns) with slow removal, and an unguided UG lost 0-2 to WR aggro in 13 to 14 turn games (anecdotes).
- Count creatures: about 14 to 16 with removal is the norm. A UR with 10 creatures went 1-2 and a BR with 12 went 2-1 (anecdotes).
- Take removal, but only in your colours, and play what you drafted. Seats short on removal lost: an unguided UB with few early answers went 1-2 on 2026-11-27 though Victim of Night and Corpse Lunge won its one match; a guided WG with Rebuke, Spidery Grasp and Ghostly Possession as answers lost to WU (anecdotes). Earlier an unguided BR benched Claustrophobia and lost, an unguided UB left Heretic's Punishment in the sideboard, and a WB lost 0-2 to Olivia Voldaren with Urgent Exorcism in the sideboard (anecdotes).
- Mono-colour with no evasion lost (G 2-4 in matches, 15 games; W 3-3, 15 games).
- Green-based RG did best on 2026-11-27: an unguided RG went 3-0 on Villagers of Estwald (three copies), Darkthicket Wolf, Ambush Viper, Prey Upon, Brimstone Volley and Into the Maw of Hell (anecdote). It beat a guided-style UB and shared red with a BR that lost to it. Red removal was contested that night.

Pairs by games won (decks, matches, game win rate), statistics to 2026-11-27:
- RG: 56% (116 games, 15 decks, 27-18 in matches). Best by rate, still small. Needs cheap green bodies (Villagers of Estwald, Darkthicket Wolf), red removal, and an answer to Claustrophobia and Victim of Night.
- BG: 54% (225 games, 31 decks, 51-42 in matches). Cheap removal and blockers over top-end; keep a flier answer and reach (Spidery Grasp).
- WU: 54% (269 games, 36 decks, 59-49 in matches). White removal plus cheap fliers (Midnight Haunting, Invisible Stalker, Chapel Geist, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Stitched Drake, Gallows Warden, Moorland Haunt) and blue pacifism auras. Mix in non-aura answers like Grasp of Phantoms. Fliers are crowded here.
- WG: 52% (256 games, 35 decks, 57-48 in matches). Three guided WGs went 1-2 on 2026-11-27 (9 Forest/8 Plains twice, one with two Avacyn's Pilgrim; 10 Plains/7 Forest once). Needs cheap plays, 8+ real sources for each colour, a third and fourth flier, and removal; do not draft it as ground filler plus four fliers.
- WR: 52% (254 games, 35 decks, 55-50 in matches). Take Mentor of the Meek and Midnight Haunting over ground bodies like Village Ironsmith; save Rebuke and Brimstone Volley for fliers.
- BR: 51% (298 games, 40 decks, 65-55 in matches). Burn (Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Brimstone Volley) plus Dead Weight and Tribute to Hunger. A BR went 2-1 on 2026-11-27, losing to RG (anecdote). Wants cheap removal, evasive finishers (Falkenrath Noble, Screeching Bat) and card advantage. Skip Ancient Grudge and Heartless Summoning.
- WB: 47% (177 games, 24 decks, 35-37 in matches). Go white-first or black-deep, count BB pips, take card-advantage finishers over Walking Corpse filler.
- UG: 46% (98 games, 13 decks, 16-23 in matches). Weak. Spider Spawning did nothing without self-mill.
- UR: 45% (191 games, 26 decks, 34-44 in matches). An unguided UR went 0-3 on 2026-11-27 with ground tempo creatures and thin playables (anecdote). Make it red-first with burn and a cheap evasive clock.
- UB: 44% (303 games, 40 decks, 45-75 in matches). Worst big pair; two more UBs went 1-2 and 2-1 on 2026-11-27 (anecdotes). Needs cheap blockers, a real finisher, answers and pressure. Vampire Interloper cannot block. Do not take Think Twice, Dissipate, Dream Twist, Curse of Oblivion or Markov Patrician. Armored Skaab without self-mill enablers was a poor blocker against werewolves (a conventional UB, anecdote).
- Mono U is 4-5 in matches (21 games), mono R 3-3 (16 games), mono G 2-4 (15 games), mono W 3-3 (15 games).

General notes:
- White is the best-supported colour on individual cards (Slayer of the Wicked, Midnight Haunting, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Bonds of Faith). Red has Stromkirk Noble, Furor of the Bitten, Into the Maw of Hell, Kruin Outlaw, Ashmouth Hound. Green has Villagers of Estwald, Lumberknot, Gatstaf Shepherd. Cheap white removal (Rebuke, Fiend Hunter) is contested; take it early.
- Black is crowded and contested; take black removal early in pack 1, but only if you will play black. Black bombs (Bloodgift Demon 54%, 94 games) work when black is your main colour.
- Read what is flowing after pack 1. If strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8, move in. Red removal was contested on 2026-11-27; when it flows, commit rather than splitting picks.
- Galvanic Juggernaut (54%, 355 games, taken at 1.2) is fine as a colourless finisher, but take removal, Bonds of Faith or a flier over it. It dies to Smite the Monstrous.
- A bomb alone does not carry a deck: Olivia Voldaren went 3-5 in games in a 17-land BR earlier but anchored a 3-0 BR with cheap removal behind it (anecdotes). After the first pick, value playable two-drops and removal.
- Splashing is poor. Do not splash without a dual land and few cards.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal on colour: Sever the Bloodline, Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Claustrophobia (blue), Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Harvest Pyre, Geistflame, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith, Fiend Hunter, Sensory Deprivation (blue), Silent Departure (blue), Grasp of Phantoms (blue), Corpse Lunge, Spidery Grasp, Heretic's Punishment, Urgent Exorcism (vs fliers), Smite the Monstrous (big creatures only), Blazing Torch. Take a second if offered; a third removal spell beats a second Grave Bramble. About five is the target; vanilla green and blue bodies are worth less than removal. Do not take removal outside your likely colours, and not second pick before you know them. Contested red removal (Brimstone Volley) should be taken early if you are red.
2. Strong uncommons and rares on colour: Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Moldgraf Monstrosity, Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee (if you can support BB), Falkenrath Marauders, Instigator Gang, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw, Rally the Peasants, Midnight Haunting, Dearly Departed, Angelic Overseer, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Devil's Play and Balefire Dragon (red), Bloodgift Demon (black), Spider Spawning (only with self-mill), Garruk Relentless (green only).
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Invisible Stalker, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Gallows Warden, Moon Heron, Lantern Spirit, Mindshrieker, Battleground Geist, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Scourge of Geier Reach, Somberwald Spider, Screeching Bat. Aim for five evasive threats or answers by the end of pack 3 in a white-fliers deck (four was not enough for three 1-2 WGs), and take a third flier over a second Unruly Mob, Doomed Traveler, Silverchase Fox, Village Ironsmith, Village Bell-Ringer, Thraben Purebloods, Festerhide Boar, Kindercatch, Markov Patrician or a third Villagers of Estwald in a deck that is not green-based ground. Take an evasive two-drop like Orchard Spirit over Thraben Sentry or a second Woodland Sleuth. Do not take an off-colour flier over an on-colour one, and play fliers you draft. Value evasive creatures above Curse of the Bloody Tome and over duplicate tricks. Card advantage (Mentor of the Meek, Midnight Haunting, Rally the Peasants) was asked for in several lessons.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald (a 3-0 RG ran three), Avacyn's Pilgrim, Ambush Viper, Kindercatch, Darkthicket Wolf, Ashmouth Hound, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils, Riot Devils (one; a second was weak in a 1-2 UR), Tormented Pariah, Silver-Inlaid Dagger, Unruly Mob (one), Doomed Traveler, Avacynian Priest, Bump in the Night. Take playable two- and three-drops over weak one-drops (Brain Weevil, Walking Corpse) and late junk such as Frightful Delusion, Cobbled Wings, Spare from Evil, Traveler's Amulet and Ranger's Guile. In UB, take real finishers, removal and ground blockers over low-impact fliers, Vampire Interloper, Demonmail Hauberk and sorceries.

Late in a pack, take a playable creature, removal or flier over an off-colour spell, a duplicate situational card or card draw. Late novelties (Cellar Door, Fortress Crab, Manor Skeleton) left a 0-3 UR short of playables on 2026-11-27 (anecdote). Walking Corpse is only a 23rd card.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Stitcher's Apprentice, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Ancient Grudge, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists (a 3-0 RG called it key, a single anecdote; not otherwise supported), Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Butcher's Cleaver and Geistcatcher's Rig over a bomb or removal (though Geistcatcher's Rig answered a flier for some, and its power 4+ is exposed to Smite the Monstrous), Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Pierced Heart, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Rolling Temblor (benched), Fortress Crab, Full Moon's Rise, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Bloodcrazed Neonate, Make a Wish, Grimoire of the Dead, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Trepanation Blade, Heartless Summoning, Curiosity, Nightbird's Clutches, Frightful Delusion, Traitorous Blood, Vampiric Fury, Reckless Waif over removal, Mask of Avacyn over a flier, Cloistered Youth over a flier, Intangible Virtue, Ranger's Guile, a second Moment of Heroism (fine in an aggressive white deck) or Lost in the Mist, a second Makeshift Mauler, a second Ghoulcaller's Chant, and a third Festerhide Boar. Naturalize, Wooden Stake and Sharpened Pitchfork are narrow filler (sideboard Naturalize against aura decks; do not take a second). Armored Skaab, Skaab Goliath, Skaab Ruinator, Stitcher's Apprentice and Forbidden Alchemy are only for a deck with Spider Spawning and real self-mill. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default; long games support it. Cut to 16 only for a truly low curve with several two-drops (anecdotes); a BR cut Feral Ridgewolf for a 17th land and went 2-1 (anecdote). Do not cut good spells to reach 17 if it leaves you without answers. Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 and 2-1 WB), 11 Plains/6 Swamp (3-0 and 1-2 WB), 9 Plains/8 Islands (3-0 WU), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 9 Forest/8 Plains (3-0 WG once, other 9/8 WGs 0-3, 1-2 and 1-2 on 2026-11-27), 10 Plains/7 Forest with Somberwald Spider (1-2 WG), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG), 9 Mountain/8 Plains (2-1 WR), 8 Swamp/9 Mountain (1-2 BR) (anecdotes). Count pips and give the main colour 9 to 10 sources and the second at least 8 (a Pilgrim counts as fixing but is not a land). In a green-based deck, put green first; with GG cards like Somberwald Spider, 8+ Forests. WRs on 6 or 7 Mountains with a heavy red load went badly.
- Double pips: give a BB, GG or UU colour 8+ sources or cut them. A guided UG ran Stitched Drake (1UU) on 7 Islands and went 0-3, and a guided BG ran two Victim of Night (BB) on 7 Swamps and went 1-2 (anecdotes). Earlier a WB with Morkrut Banshee on 7 Swamps went 2-4 in games, and Bloodline Keeper on 7 Swamps lost (anecdotes). Go 9/8 or cut the double-pip cards. Avoid double pips in both colours.
- Aim for about five or six real removal spells (pumps do not count), three or four answers to fliers (Smite the Monstrous does not count), and two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers. Play the removal you drafted and the bomb you took; do not leave Claustrophobia, Urgent Exorcism, Heretic's Punishment or similar answers in the sideboard when the opponent has fliers or a bomb.
- A low curve alone is not enough. 0-3 and 1-2 decks have been top-heavy, ground filler, flierless, short on creatures or lacking pressure. Count two-drops and early evasive creatures, keep one or two cheap ground defenders in tempo decks, and trade a marginal two-drop or weak one-drop for a third flier or a removal spell. Cut 6-drops and filler rather than forcing them in. Count creatures (14 to 16) before adding another removal spell.
- Keep a flexible late threat or value source (Mentor of the Meek, Moan of the Unhallowed, Gavony Township, Moorland Haunt, Rally the Peasants, Divine Reckoning), but do not cut good cheap cards to fit one.
- Self-mill needs a real plan: either draft enablers and payoffs together or leave them out.
- Tricks have a place after removal and creatures; one or two cheap tricks is fine. Enchantment-based removal on your best creature is a real risk, and Naturalize exists; keep multiple threats that survive removal and a few non-aura answers. Toughness above one matters against Geistflame, Brimstone Volley, Harvest Pyre and Tormented Pariah; power 4+ is exposed to Smite the Monstrous. Do not leave yourself with no answer to an opposing colour's key cards (Claustrophobia, Victim of Night, Tormented Pariah, Bonds of Faith, Olivia Voldaren).
- Settle your 23 by pack 3. Do not play cards you planned to cut; take real playables, not filler, in the middle picks (a 1-2 BR ended with Desperate Ravings as its 23rd card; a 1-2 UB had to cut Laboratory Maniac to reach 23; anecdotes). Check the final list against your colours before registering (the 0-3 UR registered an off-colour Curse of the Pierced Heart) and do not side out main removal or evasive threats for situational cards. Keep at least one cheap play main.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core. Secure cheap early plays before piling on top end.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke and Fiend Hunter for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies. Hold Smite the Monstrous for power 4+ creatures only (not Bloodline Keeper, power 3); keep Rebuke or Claustrophobia for it. Check creature types before counting on Victim of Night (werewolves such as Villagers of Estwald and Darkthicket Wolf are immune).
- Expect games of 12 to 47 turns, up to 62; many ran 25 to 46 with stalled boards. Break stalls with removal, Rally the Peasants, Moan of the Unhallowed and evasive threats. Apply pressure early rather than waiting, especially with UB, UR, UG and WU.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR; WR aggro won 13 to 14 turn games against a UG with few early blockers. Pair cheap creatures with late fliers or Rally the Peasants to close.
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Lantern Spirit, Geist of Saint Traft, Angelic Overseer, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Murder of Crows, Midnight Haunting, Gallows Warden, Falkenrath Noble, Screeching Bat, Bloodline Keeper, Olivia Voldaren, Bloodgift Demon, Galvanic Juggernaut, Boneyard Wurm, Balefire Dragon and Scourge of Geier Reach. Hold Brimstone Volley for Murder of Crows and Angel of Flight Alabaster rather than spending it early.
- Against WU, UB, UR, UG and mono-blue, kill fliers early (Geist of Saint Traft with Silent Departure beat a UB twice quickly; Delver of Secrets flips into a real threat). Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation, Grasp of Phantoms, Feeling of Dread and Lost in the Mist will target your best creature or tap your small board, so keep a second threat ready. WU also runs two Smite the Monstrous and Rebuke, so do not rely on one four-power creature. Blue decks win long grinds, so do not durdle.
- Against WB, expect real removal (Fiend Hunter, Smite the Monstrous, Rebuke, Victim of Night) held for your fliers and best creatures, plus Angelic Overseer, Chapel Geist and Rally the Peasants closing long games, and sometimes black bombs like Bloodline Keeper and Reaper from the Abyss. Do not rely on one big threat, and keep evasive creatures in your deck.
- Against BG, WG and RG, expect Typhoid Rats, Somberwald Spider, Spidery Grasp, Villagers of Estwald, Darkthicket Wolf, Ambush Viper, Bonds of Faith, Urgent Exorcism, Mentor of the Meek, Fiend Hunter, Prey Upon, Garruk Relentless, Tormented Pariah and burn to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup, expect reach (kill Somberwald Spider first), and do not flood the board with ground bodies.
- Against WR, BR and UR, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils, Dead Weight and Tribute to Hunger to punish small creatures. Do not overcommit and keep your own fliers alive. WR also runs Rebuke (often two), Urgent Exorcism, Smite the Monstrous, Midnight Haunting, Chapel Geist, Bonds of Faith, Silverchase Fox, Fiend Hunter, Slayer of the Wicked and Rally the Peasants, and will answer your fliers and four-power creatures. Hold back threats rather than feeding them one at a time.
- Against BR with Olivia Voldaren, Bloodline Keeper, Bloodgift Demon, Screeching Bat and Falkenrath Noble, save removal for the bombs and the fliers, and have card advantage for long games.
- Against white decks with Fiend Hunter and Avacynian Priest, expect one-for-one trades on early creatures; prefer threats that leave value behind. Expect Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning in long games.
