# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-10-27's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Seven nights: 56 decks, 205 games. Guided seats are 70-71 in games (50%), the same as unguided (135-134). The guide is not proven; judge by the card numbers.

- BG is the best pair: 25-8 in matches, 67% of games (78 games, 11 decks). Tonight seat 6 went 3-0 with it, seat 2 went 2-1 and a third BG deck went 1-2.
- WR 54% (57 games, 8 decks), BR 52% (29 games), RG 50% (14 games).
- Weak: WU 48% (46 games), UR 44% (45 games), UB 43% (46 games), WG 43% (35 games), UG 38% (16 games).
- WB is 2-7 in matches, 27% of games (22 games, 3 decks). Tonight one WB deck went 2-1, so it is thin, not proven.
- Games stall for 15 to 40 turns. Removal plus evasive finishers or Spider Spawning break stalls. Fliers alone did not (anecdote).
- Lean green or black first. White-red is the fallback. Pick a main colour by about pick 5 and a pair by mid pack 1. Do not move off a colour for one early removal spell unless the new colour is clearly open. Do not splash. Tonight's UR deck on five Mountains and twelve Islands could not cast its red (anecdote).

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal: Victim of Night, Prey Upon, Dead Weight, Corpse Lunge, Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Bonds of Faith, Rebuke, Claustrophobia, Fiend Hunter.
2. Strong uncommons and rares: Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee, Sever the Bloodline, Galvanic Juggernaut.
3. Good commons: Villagers of Estwald, Typhoid Rats, Festerhide Boar, Kindercatch, Woodland Sleuth.
4. On-colour evasive threats: Chapel Geist, Midnight Haunting, Stitched Drake.

Take removal or evasion over filler. Runic Repetition was cut twice (anecdote). Skip equipment and marginal tricks. Take on-colour two-drops over speculation. Once your pair is set, do not take off-colour cards.

## Building

- Land count is unsettled. 17 lands went 3-0 (BG), 2-1 (BG), 2-1 (WB) and 1-2 (WU). Play 16 with a low curve, 17 with several 5-drops.
- Count pips first. A 9 Forest and 6 Swamp BG deck with double-black Victim of Night went 6-1 in games (anecdote). Split lands by pips and card quality, and avoid double pips in both colours.
- Play 5 or more two-drops, at least two evasive or removal-backed finishers, and at least two answers to fliers.
- Do not main Naturalize.

## Playing

- Trade early and hold removal for fliers and big blockers.
- Break stalls with removal, fight spells, Spider Spawning and evasive threats, not more ground bodies.
- Spread threats, since Rebuke and Smite the Monstrous kill attackers. Expect opposing Victim of Night to kill your best early threat.
- Slow fliers like Moon Heron lost to BG (anecdote). Pressure early or hold the ground.
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit and Murder of Crows.
