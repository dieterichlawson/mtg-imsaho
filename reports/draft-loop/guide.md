# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-24's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Thirty-five nights: 280 decks, 1037 games. Guided seats are 354-382 in games (48%, 143-157 in matches, 100 decks). The conventional guide is 121-109 (53%, 50-46 in matches, 32 decks). Unguided seats are 562-546 (51%, 227-217 in matches, 148 decks). On the sixteen nights with all three kinds of seat, guided went 104-128 (45%, 42-54 in matches), conventional 121-109 (53%) and unguided 246-234 (51%). Guided is still behind and this guide has not been shown to help. Treat archetype lines as hypotheses; lean on card numbers and on advice that is consistent across lessons.

- Commit to two colours early (by mid pack 1; second colour by pick 5 of pack 2 at the latest). Stranded picks are the most repeated lesson. On 2026-11-24 a guided UG took Fiend Hunter at p3p1 and Abbey Griffin at p3p7 and played neither; an unguided WU's pack 3 first pick Balefire Dragon never made its deck; an unguided UR took Gallows Warden and Voiceless Spirit before settling blue (all anecdotes). Earlier: Victim of Night, Bloodline Keeper, Sever the Bloodline, Geist of Saint Traft, Snapcaster Mage, Olivia Voldaren and Devil's Play were taken and not played in off-colour decks (anecdotes). If you take a bomb, stay in its colour and play it. Once set, do not take removal or cards outside your colours. A first pick is a bet: do not follow it with a second off-plan pick.
- Fliers decide games. On 2026-11-24 a guided BG went 1-2 (3-5 in games) losing to WU's Chapel Geist, Invisible Stalker and Stitched Drake after playing Markov Patrician twice; a guided UG with four fliers went 0-3; WU decks won rounds with Chapel Geist and Spectral Rider (anecdotes). Earlier three guided WRs went 0-3 with few fliers and a flierless mono-green went 0-3 (anecdotes). Take cheap flier answers (Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Silent Departure, Brimstone Volley, Rebuke, Urgent Exorcism) before an extra two-drop, and a third flier over ground filler.
- Reach cuts the other way: a WB air plan lost 0-2 to WG's two Somberwald Spider (anecdote). Keep Rebuke or Slayer of the Wicked for reach blockers.
- Do not sideboard evasive threats out of a deck for filler: a conventional WU sided out Stitched Drake for Dream Twist and Mulch against WB and lost (anecdote).
- Cheap curves must also close. Games run 11 to 62 turns; on 2026-11-24 they ran about 12 to 26. Have two or three real threats that win in the air or long, backed by removal and card advantage.
- Count creatures: a UR with only 10 creatures went 1-2 (anecdote). About 14 to 16 creatures with removal is the norm.
- Take removal, but only in your colours, and play what you drafted. Count removal that hits fliers and big threats; a WB lost 0-2 to Olivia Voldaren with only Rebuke and Sever the Bloodline as answers while Urgent Exorcism sat in the sideboard (anecdote).
- Mono-colour with no evasion lost (G 2-4 in matches, 15 games).

Pairs by games won (decks, matches, game win rate), statistics to 2026-11-24:
- RG: 55% (108 games, 14 decks, 24-18 in matches). Best by rate, still small. Needs red removal or fliers, cheap blockers, and an answer to Claustrophobia and Victim of Night.
- BG: 54% (225 games, 31 decks, 51-42 in matches). Good on paper, but a guided BG went 1-2 on 2026-11-24 (anecdote). Cheap removal and blockers over top-end; keep a flier answer and reach (Spidery Grasp).
- WU: 53% (246 games, 33 decks, 53-46 in matches). Best by matches among big pairs on this data. White removal plus cheap fliers (Midnight Haunting, Invisible Stalker, Chapel Geist, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Stitched Drake, Moorland Haunt) and blue pacifism auras. Two WUs went 2-1 on 2026-11-24 (anecdotes).
- WG: 52% (220 games, 30 decks, 49-41 in matches). Needs cheap plays, 10 sources for the main colour (Avacyn's Pilgrim helps), and evasion or removal.
- WR: 51% (247 games, 34 decks, 52-50 in matches). Take Mentor of the Meek and Midnight Haunting over ground bodies like Village Ironsmith; save Rebuke and Brimstone Volley for fliers.
- BR: 51% (265 games, 36 decks, 57-51 in matches). A conventional BR went 3-0 on 2026-11-24 with Olivia Voldaren, Bloodgift Demon, Devil's Play, Harvest Pyre, Blazing Torch, Riot Devils and Tormented Pariah; an unguided BR went 1-2 with situational filler (anecdotes). Wants cheap removal, evasive finishers (Falkenrath Noble, Screeching Bat) and card advantage. Skip Ancient Grudge and Heartless Summoning.
- WB: 47% (177 games, 24 decks, 35-37 in matches). Go white-first or black-deep, count BB pips, take card-advantage finishers over Walking Corpse filler. An unguided WB went 2-1 on 2026-11-24 with Bloodline Keeper, Reaper from the Abyss and Morkrut Banshee (anecdote).
- UG: 47% (83 games, 11 decks, 14-19 in matches). Weak; the 0-3 guided UG on 2026-11-24 ran Stitched Drake on 7 Islands (anecdote). Spider Spawning did nothing without self-mill.
- UR: 46% (177 games, 24 decks, 33-39 in matches). Mixed. Make it red-first with burn and a cheap evasive clock; a 1-2 UR with 10 creatures lost to WU (anecdote).
- UB: 45% (266 games, 35 decks, 41-64 in matches). Worst big pair. Needs cheap blockers, a real finisher and answers, plus cheap pressure. Do not take Think Twice, Dissipate or Dream Twist.
- Mono U is 4-5 in matches (21 games), mono R 3-3 (16 games), mono G 2-4 (15 games).

General notes:
- White is the best-supported colour on individual cards (Slayer of the Wicked, Midnight Haunting, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Bonds of Faith). Red has Stromkirk Noble, Furor of the Bitten, Into the Maw of Hell, Kruin Outlaw, Ashmouth Hound. Green has Villagers of Estwald, Lumberknot, Gatstaf Shepherd. Cheap white removal (Rebuke, Fiend Hunter) is contested; take it early.
- Black is crowded and contested; take black removal early in pack 1, but only if you will play black. Black bombs (Bloodgift Demon 56%, 73 games) work when black is your main colour.
- Read what is flowing after pack 1. If strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8, move in.
- Galvanic Juggernaut (55%, 332 games, taken at 1.2) is fine as a colourless finisher, but take removal, Bonds of Faith or a flier over it. It dies to Smite the Monstrous.
- A bomb alone does not carry a deck: Olivia Voldaren went 3-5 in games in a 17-land BR earlier but anchored a 3-0 BR on 2026-11-24 with cheap removal behind it (anecdotes). After the first pick, value playable two-drops and removal.
- Splashing is poor. Do not splash without a dual land and few cards.

## Uncommons and rares

- Black: Sever the Bloodline (84 games, 60%, rare, taken at 1.3), Disciple of Griselbrand (154 games, 58%, taken at 9.2; only in black), Bloodgift Demon (73 games, 56%, taken at 1.1), Morkrut Banshee (248 games, 53%, taken at 1.8; BB on 7 Swamps went poorly), Falkenrath Noble, Crossway Vampire (a third is filler), Bloodline Keeper (won games for a BR and a WB on 2026-11-24; lost on 7 Swamps earlier), Reaper from the Abyss, Curse of Death's Hold (slow).
- Green: Moldgraf Monstrosity (70 games, 59%, rare), Gatstaf Shepherd (253 games, 54%), Lumberknot (231 games, 55%), Hollowhenge Scavenger (170 games, 53%), Spider Spawning (191 games, 53%; dead without enablers), Hamlet Captain, Boneyard Wurm (dies to Smite the Monstrous). Garruk Relentless was too thin to carry a WG with little other green (anecdote).
- Red: Desperate Ravings (82 games, 57%, taken at 9.9, a late pick, though it was a 23rd card in a 1-2 BR), Falkenrath Marauders (79 games, 57%, rare, taken at 2.1), Stromkirk Noble (96 games, 56%, rare), Into the Maw of Hell (270 games, 55%, taken at 3.3), Kruin Outlaw (104 games, 55%, rare), Hanweir Watchkeep (160 games, 54%), Rage Thrower (183 games, 54%; a 6-drop), Instigator Gang, Vampire Interloper, Devil's Play and Balefire Dragon (strong on colour).
- White: Slayer of the Wicked (252 games, 59%, taken at 1.8), Mentor of the Meek (104 games, 58%, rare), Midnight Haunting (243 games, 57%), Elite Inquisitor (104 games, 57%, rare), Dearly Departed (87 games, 53%), Rally the Peasants (closes long games), Fiend Hunter, Angelic Overseer, Divine Reckoning, Urgent Exorcism (narrow, but a clean answer to spirits; play it against fliers), Smite the Monstrous (power 4 or more only; dead against Delver of Secrets, Stitched Drake, Moon Heron and Bloodline Keeper). Cloistered Youth is only average.
- Blue: Murder of Crows (only if blue), Invisible Stalker, Stitched Drake (1UU, needs 8 Islands), Mindshrieker, Battleground Geist, Moon Heron, Lantern Spirit, Orchard Spirit, Snapcaster Mage, Grasp of Phantoms, Geist of Saint Traft (only if blue). Blue fliers lost to Somberwald Spider, Victim of Night and WG.
- Colourless: Manor Gargoyle (127 games, 55%, rare, taken at 2.2), Galvanic Juggernaut (see notes).
- Lands: Moorland Haunt (comes late), Sulfur Falls, Woodland Cemetery in B/G, Gavony Township in a long-game white deck (anecdote).
- Bombs: Take only if on colour and castable, and play them. Olivia Voldaren, Devil's Play, Balefire Dragon and Bloodline Keeper were repeatedly taken early and stranded in off-colour decks, but won when the deck was built around black or red.
- Avoid Markov Patrician (played twice in a 1-2 BG).

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal on colour: Sever the Bloodline, Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Claustrophobia (blue), Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Harvest Pyre, Geistflame, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith, Fiend Hunter, Sensory Deprivation (blue), Silent Departure (blue), Grasp of Phantoms (blue), Corpse Lunge, Spidery Grasp, Urgent Exorcism (vs fliers), Smite the Monstrous (big creatures only), Blazing Torch. Take a second if offered. About five is the target, but five was not enough when the rest was ground bodies. Do not take removal outside your likely colours, and not second pick before you know them.
2. Strong uncommons and rares on colour: Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Moldgraf Monstrosity, Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee (if you can support BB), Falkenrath Marauders, Instigator Gang, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw, Rally the Peasants, Midnight Haunting, Dearly Departed, Angelic Overseer, Devil's Play and Balefire Dragon (red), Bloodgift Demon (black), Spider Spawning (only with self-mill), Garruk Relentless (green only).
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Invisible Stalker, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Lantern Spirit, Mindshrieker, Battleground Geist, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Scourge of Geier Reach, Somberwald Spider, Screeching Bat. Aim for three or four evasive threats or answers by the end of pack 3, and take a third flier over a second Unruly Mob, Doomed Traveler, Silverchase Fox, Village Ironsmith, Thraben Purebloods, Festerhide Boar, Markov Patrician or a third Villagers of Estwald. Take an evasive two-drop like Orchard Spirit over Thraben Sentry or a second Woodland Sleuth. Do not take an off-colour flier over an on-colour one, and play fliers you draft. Value evasive creatures above Curse of the Bloody Tome. Card advantage (Mentor of the Meek, Midnight Haunting, Rally the Peasants) was asked for in several lessons.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Avacyn's Pilgrim, Ambush Viper, Kindercatch, Darkthicket Wolf, Ashmouth Hound, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils, Riot Devils, Tormented Pariah, Silver-Inlaid Dagger, Unruly Mob (one), Doomed Traveler, Avacynian Priest, Vampire Interloper, Bump in the Night. Take playable two- and three-drops over weak one-drops (Brain Weevil, Walking Corpse) and late junk such as Frightful Delusion, Cobbled Wings, Spare from Evil, Traveler's Amulet and Ranger's Guile. In UB, take real finishers and ground blockers over low-impact fliers and sorceries.

Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell or card draw. Walking Corpse is only a 23rd card.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Stitcher's Apprentice, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Ancient Grudge, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Butcher's Cleaver and Geistcatcher's Rig over a bomb or removal (though Geistcatcher's Rig answered a flier for some), Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Rolling Temblor, Fortress Crab, Full Moon's Rise, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Bloodcrazed Neonate, Make a Wish, Grimoire of the Dead, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Trepanation Blade, Heartless Summoning, Curiosity, Nightbird's Clutches, Frightful Delusion, Traitorous Blood, Vampiric Fury, Reckless Waif over removal, Mask of Avacyn over a flier, Cloistered Youth over a flier, Intangible Virtue, Ranger's Guile, a second Moment of Heroism or Lost in the Mist, a second Makeshift Mauler, a second Ghoulcaller's Chant, and a third Festerhide Boar. Naturalize, Wooden Stakes and Sharpened Pitchfork are narrow filler. Armored Skaab, Skaab Goliath, Skaab Ruinator and Forbidden Alchemy are only for a deck with Spider Spawning and real self-mill. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default; long games support it. Cut to 16 only for a truly low curve with several two-drops (anecdotes). Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 and 2-1 WB), 11 Plains/6 Swamp (3-0 and 1-2 WB), 9 Plains/8 Islands (3-0 WU), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG), 9 Mountain/8 Plains (2-1 WR), 8 Swamp/9 Mountain (1-2 BR) (anecdotes). Count pips and give the main colour 9 to 10 sources. 9 Forest/8 Plains WG went 0-3 and 1-2; WRs on 6 or 7 Mountains with a heavy red load went badly (anecdotes). Count Avacyn's Pilgrim as fixing.
- Double pips: give a BB, GG or UU colour 8+ sources or cut them. On 2026-11-24 a guided UG ran Stitched Drake (1UU) on 7 Islands and went 0-3 (2-6 in games), and a guided BG ran two Victim of Night (BB) on 7 Swamps and went 1-2 (anecdotes). Earlier a WB with Morkrut Banshee on 7 Swamps went 2-4 in games, and Bloodline Keeper on 7 Swamps lost (anecdotes). Go 9/8 or cut the double-pip cards. Avoid double pips in both colours. Three GG Somberwald Spiders want 8+ Forests.
- Aim for about five or six real removal spells (pumps do not count), three or four answers to fliers (Smite the Monstrous does not count), and two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers. Play the removal you drafted and the bomb you took; do not leave Urgent Exorcism or similar answers in the sideboard when the opponent has fliers or a bomb.
- A low curve alone is not enough. 0-3 and 1-2 decks have been top-heavy, ground filler, flierless, short on creatures or lacking pressure. Count two-drops and early evasive creatures, keep one or two cheap ground defenders in tempo decks, and trade a marginal two-drop or weak one-drop for a third flier or a removal spell. Cut 6-drops and filler rather than forcing them in.
- Keep a flexible late threat or value source (Mentor of the Meek, Moan of the Unhallowed, Gavony Township, Moorland Haunt, Rally the Peasants, Divine Reckoning), but do not cut good cheap cards to fit one.
- Self-mill needs a real plan: either draft enablers and payoffs together or leave them out.
- Tricks have a place after removal and creatures; one or two cheap tricks is fine. Enchantment-based removal on your best creature is a real risk; keep multiple threats that survive removal. Toughness above one matters against Geistflame, Brimstone Volley, Harvest Pyre and Tormented Pariah; power 4+ is exposed to Smite the Monstrous. Do not leave yourself with no answer to an opposing colour's key cards (Claustrophobia, Victim of Night, Tormented Pariah, Bonds of Faith, Olivia Voldaren).
- Settle your 23 by pack 3. Do not play cards you planned to cut; that means taking real playables, not filler, in the middle picks (a 1-2 BR ended with Desperate Ravings as its 23rd card after taking Fortress Crab, Curse of Oblivion and Full Moon's Rise; anecdote). Check every swap against your colours and do not side out main removal or evasive threats for situational cards. Keep at least one cheap play main.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core. Secure cheap early plays before piling on top end.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke and Fiend Hunter for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies. Hold Smite the Monstrous for power 4+ creatures only (not Bloodline Keeper, power 3); keep Rebuke or Claustrophobia for it. Check creature types before counting on Victim of Night.
- Expect games of 15 to 47 turns, up to 62; on 2026-11-24 they ran 12 to 26. Break stalls with removal, Rally the Peasants, Moan of the Unhallowed and evasive threats. Apply pressure early rather than waiting, especially with UB, UR, UG and WU.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR. Pair cheap creatures with late fliers or Rally the Peasants to close.
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Lantern Spirit, Geist of Saint Traft, Angelic Overseer, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Murder of Crows, Midnight Haunting, Falkenrath Noble, Screeching Bat, Bloodline Keeper, Olivia Voldaren, Bloodgift Demon, Galvanic Juggernaut, Boneyard Wurm, Balefire Dragon and Scourge of Geier Reach.
- Against WU, UB, UR, UG and mono-blue, kill fliers early (Delver of Secrets flips into a real threat). Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation, Grasp of Phantoms, Feeling of Dread and Lost in the Mist will target your best creature or tap your small board, so keep a second threat ready. Blue decks win long grinds, so do not durdle.
- Against WB, expect real removal (Fiend Hunter, Smite the Monstrous, Rebuke, Victim of Night) held for your fliers and best creatures, plus Angelic Overseer, Chapel Geist and Rally the Peasants closing long games, and sometimes black bombs like Bloodline Keeper and Reaper from the Abyss. Do not rely on one big threat, and keep evasive creatures in your deck.
- Against BG, WG and RG, expect Typhoid Rats, Somberwald Spider, Spidery Grasp, Villagers of Estwald, Bonds of Faith, Mentor of the Meek, Fiend Hunter, Prey Upon, Garruk Relentless, Tormented Pariah and burn to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup, expect reach (kill Somberwald Spider first), and do not flood the board with ground bodies.
- Against WR, BR and UR, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Furor of the Bitten and Pitchburn Devils to punish small creatures. Do not overcommit and keep your own fliers alive. WR also runs Rebuke (often two), Urgent Exorcism, Smite the Monstrous, Midnight Haunting, Fiend Hunter, Slayer of the Wicked, Rally the Peasants and Bonds of Faith, and will answer your fliers and four-power creatures. Hold back threats rather than feeding them one at a time.
- Against BR with Olivia Voldaren, Bloodline Keeper, Bloodgift Demon, Screeching Bat and Falkenrath Noble (a BR with Olivia and Bloodgift Demon went 3-0 on 2026-11-24, and another beat two guided WRs 2-0 on 2026-11-22), save removal for the bombs and the fliers, and have card advantage for long games.
- Against white decks with Fiend Hunter and Avacynian Priest, expect one-for-one trades on early creatures; prefer threats that leave value behind. Expect Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning in long games.
