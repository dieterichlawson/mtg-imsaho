# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-14's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Twenty-five nights: 200 decks, 740 games. Guided seats are 285-303 in games (48%, 116-124 in matches, 80 decks). Unguided seats are 409-399 (51%, 164-160 in matches, 108 decks). The conventional guide is 46-38 (55%, 20-16 in matches, 12 decks). On the six nights with all three kinds of seat, guided went 35-49 in games (42%, 15-21 in matches), conventional 46-38 (55%) and unguided 93-87 (52%). Guided is still behind both on those nights, and on 2026-11-14 the guided seats went 0-3 (WR) and 2-1 (BR). This guide has not been shown to help. Treat archetype lines as hypotheses and lean on card numbers and on advice that is consistent across lessons:
- Commit to two colours early.
- Count playables and keep a curve of cheap creatures.
- Take removal.
- Have some way to win in the air or in a long game.

Pairs by games won (decks, matches, game win rate):
- WR: 54% (159 games, 22 decks, 36-30 in matches). Still above average, but down from 56% after a guided WR went 0-3 (0-6 in games) on 2026-11-14 (anecdote). That deck had only three fliers (Abbey Griffin, Chapel Geist, Spectral Rider), ground filler (Unruly Mob, two Thraben Purebloods, Silverchase Fox) and 6 Mountains for Brimstone Volley, Traitorous Blood and Curse of the Pierced Heart. It lost all six games in 17 to 29 turns. A WR with two Thraben Sentry, two Selfless Cathar, two Unruly Mob and three fliers also lost 1-2 earlier. Earlier, guided WR decks went 3-0 on 2026-11-11 and 2026-11-12 (anecdotes). Core: cheap creatures (Ashmouth Hound, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar), removal (Rebuke, Into the Maw of Hell), Rally the Peasants, Furor of the Bitten and real fliers (Voiceless Spirit). Take a fourth flier or removal over ground filler, and give red enough Mountains.
- BR: 53% (160 games, 22 decks, 38-28 in matches). Good by matches, but it splits by build. On 2026-11-14 a guided BR went 2-1, with two wins in 9 and 17 turns, so fast starts with early drops beat slow boards. It lost to RG with only Harvest Pyre and Corpse Lunge as real removal (anecdotes). Earlier, ground-heavy BR decks stalled (Kessig Wolf, Markov Patrician, Night Revelers went 1-2). An eight-removal BR with no finishers went 0-3. BR wants early drops, removal and evasive finishers like Falkenrath Noble, with Vampire Interloper or Rakish Heir over ground filler. Wooden Stakes is poor.
- RG: 53% (76 games, 10 decks, 16-14 in matches). On 2026-11-14 an unguided RG went 2-1, carried by red top end like Into the Maw of Hell. It lost the 24- and 25-turn games to WB's Chapel Geist pair and Screeching Bat, and its black pack 2 picks (Victim of Night, Abattoir Ghoul, Diregraf Ghoul) were all cut (anecdotes). RG needs cheap blockers, ways to reach fliers, and an answer to Claustrophobia and Victim of Night.
- BG: 52% (190 games, 26 decks, 41-37 in matches). It needs cheap creatures and evasion or removal-backed finishers. Somberwald Spider and Spider Spawning beat ground decks. On 2026-11-14 an unguided BG went 0-3. It was top-heavy (Boneyard Wurm, Lumberknot, two Prey Upon), lost in 15 to 26 turns, and had no answer to Chapel Geist, Falkenrath Noble or Vampire Interloper (anecdote). Take cheap two-drops and flier answers over a fourth fatty.
- WG: 51% (156 games, 22 decks, 35-31 in matches). It swings with the build. Earlier a guided WG with four fliers went 0-3 on 9 Forest/7 Plains, with early picks (two Dead Weight, Riot Devils, two Vampiric Fury) never played. Fliers alone are not enough: you need cheap plays too and a 10-source main colour (anecdote).
- WU: 51% (192 games, 26 decks, 39-39 in matches). It is average and often too slow. White removal plus real cheap fliers works. On 2026-11-14 a conventional WU went 2-1, closing both wins with Midnight Haunting tokens and two Invisible Stalkers, with Moorland Haunt as a 17th land. It lost a 17-to-24-turn game to UR (anecdotes). Think Twice-style filler does not work.
- UR: 46% (149 games, 20 decks, 27-33 in matches). It is weak overall, but on 2026-11-14 an unguided UR went 3-0 (anecdote). It was red-based, with two Geistflame, Rage Thrower and red creatures, burn clearing blockers, and games of 17 to 24 turns. Blue was a light second colour: five blue cards on six Islands plus Sulfur Falls. Earlier UR decks (2-1 twice, and a 0-3 Skaab-mill) leaned on bounce, Curses and Galvanic Juggernaut and did not close. Make UR red-first with burn and efficient creatures; take blue only when it is open.
- UB: 46% (183 games, 24 decks, 29-43 in matches). It is below average. On 2026-11-14 an unguided UB went 1-2 with Victim of Night, Dead Weight and Corpse Lunge. Its games ran 16 to 22 turns, it had too few cheap threats and too few instant-speed answers to the fliers of UR (Stitched Drake) and WU (Midnight Haunting, Invisible Stalker), and it sided in six cards for game 2 and still lost (anecdote). UB needs a low curve, early creatures and cheap removal. Do not take Delver of Secrets or Dream Twist.
- WB: 47% (131 games, 17 decks, 25-26 in matches). It is below average. Earlier an unguided WB went 3-0 with Angelic Overseer, Rebuke, Victim of Night, Moan of the Unhallowed, Ghoulraiser and Abattoir Ghoul. On 2026-11-14 a conventional WB with Chapel Geist and Bloodline Keeper went 5-4 in games and lost 1-2 to UR's two Geistflame and Harvest Pyre (anecdotes). WB works with removal, fliers and grindy value, but keep a plan that survives burn. Count BB pips.
- UG: 42% (31 games, 4 decks, 3-9 in matches). It is weak, with slow spells and little pressure (anecdote). Stay two colours. Mono U is 4-5 in matches (21 games) and mono R 3-3 (16 games).

General notes:
- White is the best-supported colour on individual cards (Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Midnight Haunting, Bonds of Faith). Red also has many high-win-rate cards (Furor of the Bitten, Ashmouth Hound, Into the Maw of Hell, Stromkirk Noble, Kruin Outlaw, Desperate Ravings). Cheap white removal (Rebuke, Fiend Hunter) is contested, so take it early.
- Black is crowded. Take black removal early in pack 1.
- Read what is flowing after pack 1. If strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8, move in. Judge by real playables, not filler like Lost in the Mist.
- Commit: main colour by about pick 5, a pair by mid pack 1 to mid pack 2, and your second colour by pick 5 of pack 2 at the latest. Off-colour early picks were wasted repeatedly (anecdotes):
  - Claustrophobia, stranded in a WG and in a BR.
  - Sensory Deprivation, first-picked in pack 1 and pack 3 by a BR that never played blue (2026-11-14).
  - Garruk Relentless, never played in a WU.
  - Two Dead Weight and two Vampiric Fury, stranded in a WG.
  - Angel of Flight Alabaster and Abbey Griffin, unused in a BG.
  - A first-pick Fiend Hunter, which went to an opponent's WG.
  Do not leave a colour for one early removal spell unless the new colour is clearly open.
- Evasion plus removal breaks stalls, but a fast curve with removal also won. Games run 11 to 62 turns, mostly 17 to 37, so grindy removal and card advantage matter.
- Splashing is poor. Do not run a colour on six basics unless it is a light second colour with a dual land and only a handful of cards (the 3-0 UR, anecdote).

- Red: Furor of the Bitten (122 games, 60%, taken at 10.1; it is a pump, not removal), Ashmouth Hound (248 games, 57%, taken at 4.0), Tormented Pariah (250 games, 54%), Pitchburn Devils (298 games, 53%), Night Revelers (235 games, 53%), Curse of the Pierced Heart and Traitorous Blood (both 53-54% earlier, so only fine marginal playables), Geistflame (praised in the 3-0 UR). Take playables before Curses.
- Blue: Spectral Flight, Hysterical Blindness (late picks), Frightful Delusion (anecdote). Take a second Spectral Flight only after you have the creatures.
- Colourless: Silver-Inlaid Dagger (145 games, 53%, uncommon). Blazing Torch was praised by one lesson (anecdote).
- Claustrophobia, Rebuke, Dead Weight, Tribute to Hunger, Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Sensory Deprivation and Stitched Drake are praised in lessons and go early, but only on colour.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal on colour: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia (blue), Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Smite the Monstrous, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith, Fiend Hunter, Sensory Deprivation (blue), Corpse Lunge, Harvest Pyre, Spidery Grasp. Take a second Dead Weight, Victim of Night, Claustrophobia or Brimstone Volley if offered. Aim for five or more removal spells; decks short of real removal lost to RG and to fliers (anecdotes). First-pick Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation, Dead Weight and Fiend Hunter all ended up stranded or in an opponent's deck, so do not take removal outside the colours you are likely to play.
2. Strong uncommons and rares: Sever the Bloodline, Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee, Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders, Instigator Gang, Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw, Rally the Peasants, Midnight Haunting, Angelic Overseer.
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Invisible Stalker, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Mindshrieker, Battleground Geist, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Somberwald Spider. Many losing decks (RG, BG, UB) lacked fliers or answers, but a three-flier WR and a four-flier WG both went 0-3, so fliers do not replace cheap plays and removal. Aim for about three or four evasive threats or answers by the end of pack 3, and take a third or fourth flier over a second Unruly Mob, Silverchase Fox, Thraben Purebloods or Festerhide Boar. Do not take an off-colour flier over an on-colour one.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Avacyn's Pilgrim, Ashmouth Hound, Feral Ridgewolf, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils and Tormented Pariah if red, Silver-Inlaid Dagger, Unruly Mob (one), Doomed Traveler, Avacynian Priest, Vampire Interloper, Bump in the Night. Take cheap threats so one removal spell cannot blank your curve, and take them over card-draw filler and bounce. In a white deck planning Travel Preparations, count humans.

Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell or card draw. Walking Corpse was a 23rd card in a 2-1 deck while two Think Twice picks never made it (anecdote).

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Stitcher's Apprentice, Cobbled Wings (it made a main deck and was sided out for Sharpened Pitchfork, anecdote), Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Ancient Grudge, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Lost in the Mist, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Trepanation Blade, Darkthicket Wolf, Make a Wish, Delver of Secrets (taken over playables, then cut, anecdote), Mask of Avacyn over a flier, Riot Devils unless red is deep, Cloistered Youth over a flier, a second Moment of Heroism, and a second Makeshift Mauler. Naturalize and Wooden Stakes are sideboard cards at best. Armored Skaab is only for a deck with real self-mill. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician and ground bodies like Kessig Wolf.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default; 16 is worth trying for low-curve aggro (anecdote). Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 WB, 2-1 WB), 11 Plains/6 Islands (2-1 WU), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG), mostly red with 6 Islands plus Sulfur Falls (3-0 UR) (anecdotes). Count pips and give the main colour 10 sources. A 9 Forest/7 Plains WG went 0-3, and a WR on 6 or 7 Mountains went badly with a heavy red burn-and-Curse load (anecdotes). If red or another colour carries several double-pip or removal cards, do not give it only six sources.
- Double pips: give a BB or GG colour 8+ sources where you can. Evidence is mixed (anecdotes): Bloodline Keeper and Victim of Night on 7 Swamps lost in a WB, while a 3-0 BR with Bloodgift Demon ran 7 Swamps. Avoid double pips in both colours. Three GG Somberwald Spiders want 8+ Forests.
- Aim for five or more real removal spells (pumps like Furor of the Bitten do not count), three or four answers to fliers (which can also clear ground blockers), and two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers.
- Keep a curve with enough cheap plays. The 0-3 BG and the 0-3 WR were top-heavy or full of ground filler, while the winning decks had early drops and burn or removal.
- Keep a flexible late threat or value source (Mentor of the Meek, Moan of the Unhallowed, Gavony Township, Moorland Haunt, Rally the Peasants, Divine Reckoning, Spider Spawning), but do not cut good cheap cards to fit one.
- Pair removal with good cheap creatures and close with fliers. Do not pad with ground filler when a flier or removal spell is available. If you have no real fliers, keep your reach.
- Do not leave yourself with no answer to an opposing colour's key cards: an RG with no black had nothing for Claustrophobia and Victim of Night decks. Enchantment-based removal on your best creature is a real risk; keep multiple threats. Burn-resistant creatures with toughness above one matter against Geistflame, Brimstone Volley and Harvest Pyre.
- Settle your 23 in the main deck by pack 3. Sideboard plans (six swaps in one UB, anecdote) rarely rescue a deck. Check every sideboard swap against your colours and do not side out main removal. Keep at least one cheap play main.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core. Do not main Gnaw to the Bone, Mulch, Dream Twist, Make a Wish or self-mill cards without a self-mill deck. Cut a redundant combat trick before a playable creature, and cut filler before a proactive creature.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke, Fiend Hunter and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies.
- Expect long games, often 17 to 40 turns, and up to 62 (most games on 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-14 ran 15 to 31 turns). Break stalls with removal, Spider Spawning, Rally the Peasants, Moan of the Unhallowed and evasive threats. Apply pressure early rather than waiting, especially with UB and WU.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR. Fast starts beat slow hands in 9 to 17 turns (anecdotes: a WR beat a mono-blue and a WG in 13 to 15 turns; a BR won in 9 and 17 turns on 2026-11-14, but a BR with Kessig Wolf and Vampire Interloper lost in 12 and 17 turns to a UG).
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Angelic Overseer, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Moon Heron, Murder of Crows, Midnight Haunting, Falkenrath Noble, Screeching Bat, Galvanic Juggernaut, Boneyard Wurm and Scourge of Geier Reach.
- Against WU, UB, UR and mono-blue, kill fliers early. Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation and Lost in the Mist will target your best creature, so keep a second threat ready. Blue decks win long grinds, so do not durdle.
- Against WB, expect real removal (Rebuke, Victim of Night) held for your fliers, plus Angelic Overseer, Chapel Geist and Ghoulraiser value. Do not rely on one big threat.
- Against BG and WG, expect Typhoid Rats, Somberwald Spider, Spidery Grasp, Ghostly Possession, Fiend Hunter and Garruk Relentless to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup and do not flood the board with ground bodies.
- Against WR, RG, BR and UR, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Furor of the Bitten and Pitchburn Devils to punish small creatures. Do not overcommit and keep your own fliers alive. WR also runs Rebuke, Smite the Monstrous, Midnight Haunting, Fiend Hunter, Slayer of the Wicked, Rally the Peasants and Bonds of Faith, and will answer your fliers.
- Against white decks with Fiend Hunter and Avacynian Priest, expect one-for-one trades on early creatures; prefer threats that leave value behind. Expect Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning in long games.
