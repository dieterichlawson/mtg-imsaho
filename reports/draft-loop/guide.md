# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-10-30's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Ten nights: 80 decks, 291 games. Guided seats are 106-125 in games (46%, 42-54 in matches); unguided are 185-166 (53%, 78-66). Guided seats have done worse than unguided every night so far, so the guide has not been shown to help. Judge by the card numbers and do not follow any line here blindly.

- BG is the best pair: 30-18 in matches, 58% of games (115 games, 16 decks). Tonight BG was 1-2 (a guided 17-land deck with Spider Spawning, Vampire Interloper and Orchard Spirit, no Somberwald Spider, two Spidery Grasp; it lost to fliers). Three lessons from that deck agree: its problem was thin evasion and flier answers, not land count (one deck, anecdote). BG with only ground bodies loses; BG with fliers or reach plus Spider Spawning wins.
- WR 56% (63 games, 9 decks), BR 54% (63 games, 9 decks; tonight one BR deck went 3-0 and another went 1-2 with Geistflame and one flier, so BR depends on fliers and cheap removal), WU 51% (73 games, 10 decks; tonight a 9 Plains, 8 Island WU deck with four Delver of Secrets went 0-3, an anecdote, but the pair is no longer clearly good).
- Middling: WG 47% (51 games, 7 decks; tonight a WG deck went 2-1), RG 50% (14 games).
- Weak: UR 45% (58 games, 8 decks), UB 44% (70 games, 9 decks; tonight one UB deck went 2-1 with Stitched Drake and Armored Skaab, so UB is weak but not hopeless), UG 38% (16 games).
- WB is 4-8 in matches, 34% of games (29 games, 4 decks). Thin, avoid.
- Games stall for 14 to 55 turns. Removal plus evasive finishers or Spider Spawning break stalls. Ground bodies alone and slow fliers did not.
- Green or black first, with white-red or black-red as alternatives. Pick a main colour by about pick 5 and a pair by mid pack 1. Do not leave a colour for one early removal spell unless the new colour is clearly open. A first pick that never fits (Fiend Hunter tonight) is wasted, so favour flexible early picks.
- Splashing is poor (a UR deck with five Mountains could not cast its red). Once the pair is clear, off-colour picks do not make decks: tonight a BR deck cut four white cards and two Burning Vengeance, and a WR deck cut two Cobbled Wings.
- Mono-colour is risky: a mono-red deck with little removal went 1-2 (anecdote).
- Read what is passed to you. Two decks of the same pair often share a table, so take contested playables earlier or move.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Brimstone Volley, Sever the Bloodline, Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Claustrophobia, Smite the Monstrous, Bonds of Faith, Geistflame.
2. Strong uncommons and rares: Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee, Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders, Slayer of the Wicked, Galvanic Juggernaut, Manor Gargoyle.
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Moon Heron, Crossway Vampire, Somberwald Spider. Tonight three losing decks (BG, BR, BR) were beaten by fliers they could not answer, so take these over a fourth removal spell or a duplicate trick.
4. Good commons: Typhoid Rats, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Kindercatch, Avacynian Priest, Ashmouth Hound, Silver-Inlaid Dagger.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Walking Corpse, Armored Skaab (one UB deck won with it, anecdote, but it is filler), Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Delver of Secrets (four copies in a 0-3 deck), Dissipate, Think Twice, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Lost in the Mist, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity. Take on-colour two-drops over speculation.

## Building

- Land count is unsettled. 17 lands went 1-2 (BG, UB) and 2-1 (WG); 16 is untested. Try 16 with a low curve, 17 with several 5-drops. Tonight's BG loss was about evasion, not lands.
- Count pips and match the basics to them: a UB deck with 11 Island and 6 Swamp, despite early black cards, was awkward; 9/8 splits suit even decks (a 9 Mountain, 8 Swamp BR deck went 3-0, anecdote). Avoid double pips in both colours.
- Play 5 or more two-drops, at least four evasive or removal-backed finishers (three did not save a BG deck full of ground bodies), and at least three answers to fliers. In green that means Somberwald Spider and Spider Spawning: take one early.
- Cap ground filler at a few cards. Ground-body decks lost long stalls repeatedly (BR with two Manor Skeletons, Feral Ridgewolf; WR and WU with Unruly Mob and Thraben Purebloods).
- Keep 4 or more removal in the main, but pair it with evasion. Include one bigger late-game threat over another two-drop.
- Keep one or two card-advantage cards (Moan of the Unhallowed, Altar's Reap) in decks that expect stalls (anecdote).
- Do not main Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone or Mulch.

## Playing

- Trade early and hold removal for fliers and big blockers. Do not spend Rebuke or Smite the Monstrous on small ground bodies in a stall.
- Break stalls with removal, fight spells, Spider Spawning and evasive threats, not more ground bodies. Fast fliers beat slow evasion, though a WG deck's Moon Herons and Somberwald Spiders beat BG 2-1 (anecdote).
- Spread threats, since Rebuke and Smite the Monstrous kill attackers. Expect Victim of Night to kill your best early threat.
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Battleground Geist, Abbey Griffin and Murder of Crows.
- Against BR (it beat WG, WU and WR tonight), block early and value two-drops over top-end; Brimstone Volley and Typhoid Rats punish slow hands.
- Against BG, expect Typhoid Rats and Somberwald Spider to make the ground hard. Win through the air with removal backup.
