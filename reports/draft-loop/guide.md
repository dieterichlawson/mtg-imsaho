# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-15's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Twenty-six nights: 208 decks, 766 games. Guided seats are 292-309 in games (49%, 119-127 in matches, 82 decks). Unguided seats are 422-412 (51%, 170-166 in matches, 112 decks). The conventional guide is 52-45 (54%, 23-19 in matches, 14 decks). On the seven nights with all three kinds of seat, guided went 42-55 in games (43%, 18-24 in matches), conventional 52-45 (54%) and unguided 106-100 (51%). Guided is still behind both on those nights. This guide has not been shown to help. Treat archetype lines as hypotheses and lean on card numbers and on advice that is consistent across lessons:
- Commit to two colours early (by mid pack 1). Early picks stranded in a colour you do not play were the most repeated lesson.
- Count playables and keep a curve of cheap creatures.
- Take removal, but only in your colours.
- Have two or three real threats that win in the air or in a long game; fliers alone do not suffice.

Pairs by games won (decks, matches, game win rate), from the statistics up to 2026-11-15:
- WR: 55% (166 games, 23 decks, 38-31 in matches). Best pair by games. On 2026-11-15 a guided WR went 2-1: two wins in 14 and 15 turns on cheap creatures (Thraben Sentry, Avacynian Priest), and a 27-turn loss to UG with only two fliers (Voiceless Spirit, Spectral Rider) (anecdotes). Earlier, a guided WR with three fliers and ground filler went 0-3 on 6 Mountains (2026-11-14). Core: cheap creatures (Ashmouth Hound, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar), removal (Rebuke, Into the Maw of Hell), Rally the Peasants, Furor of the Bitten and real fliers (Voiceless Spirit). Take a third or fourth flier or removal over ground filler, and give red enough Mountains.
- BG: 54% (196 games, 27 decks, 44-37 in matches). On 2026-11-15 an unguided BG went 3-0: green-based with Kindercatch, Moldgraf Monstrosity, two Ambush Vipers, two Lumberknots, Dead Weight, Curse of Death's Hold and Walking Corpse, with a low curve (anecdote). It beat WB and a guided deck. It needs cheap creatures and removal-backed finishers; earlier a top-heavy BG (Boneyard Wurm, Lumberknot, two Prey Upon) went 0-3 with no answer to fliers. Take cheap two-drops and cheap removal over a fourth fatty or expensive blue fliers.
- BR: 52% (174 games, 24 decks, 40-32 in matches). On 2026-11-15 an unguided BR went 0-3 with ground creatures (Walking Corpse, Manor Skeleton) on 17 lands, stalling in 20 and 31 turns, while another unguided BR went 2-1 and lost 0-2 to a UG in 25 and 22 turns with only Geistcatcher's Rig against fliers (anecdotes). Two BR drafters fought over Geistflame, Brimstone Volley and Feral Ridgewolf, so red removal is contested. BR wants early drops, removal and evasive finishers like Falkenrath Noble. Play your removal (a benched Sensory Deprivation was a lesson) and skip Ancient Grudge and Heartless Summoning-type speculation.
- WR/RG: RG 53% (76 games, 10 decks, 16-14 in matches). Needs cheap blockers, ways to reach fliers, and an answer to Claustrophobia and Victim of Night.
- WG: 51% (156 games, 22 decks, 35-31 in matches). It swings with the build. Earlier a guided WG with four fliers went 0-3 on 9 Forest/7 Plains with early picks never played. Fliers alone are not enough: you need cheap plays and a 10-source main colour (anecdote).
- WU: 51% (192 games, 26 decks, 39-39 in matches). Average and often too slow. White removal plus real cheap fliers works (Midnight Haunting, Invisible Stalker, Moorland Haunt). Think Twice-style filler does not.
- BR aside, UR: 46% (149 games, 20 decks, 27-33 in matches). Weak overall, but an unguided red-first UR with Geistflame, Rage Thrower and light blue (six Islands plus Sulfur Falls) went 3-0 on 2026-11-14 (anecdote). Make UR red-first with burn; take blue only when open.
- UB: 46% (189 games, 25 decks, 30-45 in matches). Below average. On 2026-11-15 an unguided UB went 1-2 on 10 Island/7 Swamp, losing to fast green and red boards after cutting Fortress Crab for Snapcaster Mage and Dissipate, though Murder of Crows and Falkenrath Noble won a 2-0 over WB (anecdotes). UB needs a low curve, cheap blockers and cheap removal. Do not take Delver of Secrets or Dream Twist, and take cheap blockers over counterspells.
- WB: 45% (143 games, 19 decks, 27-30 in matches). Below average and unproven. On 2026-11-15 a guided WB with Ghoulraiser, Abattoir Ghoul and Morkrut Banshee on 10 Plains/7 Swamps went 1-2 (2-4 in games), and a conventional WB with Bloodline Keeper, Chapel Geist and Voiceless Spirit went 1-2, losing to WR humans in 14-15 turns and to UB in 16-21 (anecdotes). The earlier 3-0 WB (Angelic Overseer, Rebuke) is outweighed. Count BB pips, and take a second threat over a fourth removal spell.
- UG: 45% (38 games, 5 decks, 5-10 in matches). Still weak overall, but on 2026-11-15 a conventional UG with Claustrophobia, Moon Heron and two Orchard Spirits beat a guided WR 2-1 and a BR 2-0 in 20-27 turn games (anecdotes). Its self-mill build (Armored Skaab, Skaab Goliath, Forbidden Alchemy) lacked Spider Spawning, and its early Victim of Night and Dead Weight were never played. Fliers plus Claustrophobia work; do not draft self-mill without the payoff. Mono U is 4-5 in matches (21 games) and mono R 3-3 (16 games).

General notes:
- White is the best-supported colour on individual cards (Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Midnight Haunting, Bonds of Faith). Red also has many high-win-rate cards (Furor of the Bitten, Ashmouth Hound, Into the Maw of Hell, Stromkirk Noble, Kruin Outlaw). Green has Moldgraf Monstrosity, Lumberknot, Gatstaf Shepherd and Villagers of Estwald. Cheap white removal (Rebuke, Fiend Hunter) is contested, so take it early.
- Black is crowded. Take black removal early in pack 1, but only if you will play black.
- Read what is flowing after pack 1. If strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8, move in. Judge by real playables.
- Commit: main colour by about pick 5, a pair by mid pack 1, your second colour by pick 5 of pack 2 at the latest. Off-colour early picks were wasted repeatedly (anecdotes):
  - On 2026-11-15: Into the Maw of Hell and Furor of the Bitten hedged in a WB; Stitched Drake, Battleground Geist and Moon Heron in a 3-0 BG; Stitched Drake in a WB; Victim of Night and Dead Weight in a UG; Heartless Summoning first-picked in pack 3 by a BR.
  - Earlier: Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation, Garruk Relentless, two Dead Weight and two Vampiric Fury, Angel of Flight Alabaster and Abbey Griffin, a first-pick Fiend Hunter.
  Do not leave a colour for one early spell unless the new colour is clearly open.
- Games run 11 to 62 turns, mostly 15 to 37, so grindy removal and card advantage matter, but cheap starts also won.
- Splashing is poor. Do not run a colour on six basics unless it is a light second colour with a dual land and few cards (the 3-0 UR).

## Uncommons and rares

- Green: Moldgraf Monstrosity (57 games, 61%, rare, taken at 3.9; prioritised by the 3-0 BG), Lumberknot (169 games, 56%), Gatstaf Shepherd (184 games, 56%), Spider Spawning (147 games, 54%), Hamlet Captain (178 games, 53%), Hollowhenge Scavenger (132 games, 53%), Boneyard Wurm. A BG built on Lumberknot and Boneyard Wurm alone stalled earlier (anecdote). Spider Spawning needs self-mill enablers, and enablers need it.
- Black: Disciple of Griselbrand (126 games, 58%, taken at 9.1), Morkrut Banshee (190 games, 55%, but BB on 7 Swamps went poorly), Bloodgift Demon, Sever the Bloodline (rare, 70% earlier in 46 games), Falkenrath Noble (carried a UB win, anecdote), Crossway Vampire, Curse of Death's Hold (in the 3-0 BG, anecdote). Abattoir Ghoul and Ghoulraiser went 1-2 in a WB (anecdote).
- Red: Into the Maw of Hell (205 games, 58%), Stromkirk Noble (74 games, 57%, rare), Kruin Outlaw (81 games, 56%, rare), Rage Thrower (130 games, 53%), Rakish Heir (118 games, 53%), Skirsdag Cultist, Hanweir Watchkeep, Falkenrath Marauders (67% earlier in 49 games), Instigator Gang, Vampire Interloper.
- White: Slayer of the Wicked (178 games, 60%, taken at 1.7), Midnight Haunting (160 games, 59%), Mentor of the Meek (81 games, 59%, rare), Elite Inquisitor (83 games, 55%, rare), Rally the Peasants (156 games, 55%, taken at 6.8), Angelic Overseer, Fiend Hunter, Divine Reckoning, Smite the Monstrous. Cloistered Youth is only average.
- Blue: Murder of Crows (only if blue; carried a UB win, anecdote), Invisible Stalker, Stitched Drake, Mindshrieker, Battleground Geist (183 games, 52%), Moon Heron and Orchard Spirit (in a 2-1 UG, anecdote).
- Colourless: Manor Gargoyle (95 games, 56%, rare, taken at 2.2). Galvanic Juggernaut (224 games, 53%, taken at 1.2) is overdrafted: do not take it over removal or a strong on-colour card, and do not rely on it alone to close.
- Lands: Moorland Haunt (63% earlier in 51 games, comes late), Sulfur Falls (60 games, 55%, in blue-red decks), Woodland Cemetery in B/G, Gavony Township in a long-game white deck (anecdote).
- Bombs: Bloodline Keeper lost on 7 Swamps in a BG and a WB, and on 2026-11-15 was the only finisher in a conventional WB that went 1-2. Garruk Relentless never made a WU deck. Take bombs only if they will be on colour and castable, and back them with a second threat.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal on colour: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia (blue), Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Smite the Monstrous, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith, Fiend Hunter, Sensory Deprivation (blue), Corpse Lunge, Harvest Pyre, Spidery Grasp. Take a second if offered, but aim for about five, not more; a fourth removal spell loses to a second threat in a WB (anecdote). Do not take removal outside the colours you are likely to play: Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation, Dead Weight, Victim of Night and Fiend Hunter were all stranded or ended in opponents' decks. Red removal is contested by other BR drafters.
2. Strong uncommons and rares on colour: Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Moldgraf Monstrosity, Sever the Bloodline, Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee (if you can support BB), Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders, Instigator Gang, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw, Rally the Peasants, Midnight Haunting, Angelic Overseer.
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Invisible Stalker, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Mindshrieker, Battleground Geist, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Somberwald Spider. Many losing decks lacked fliers or answers, but a three-flier WR, a two-flier WR, and a four-flier WG all lost, so fliers do not replace cheap plays and removal. Aim for three or four evasive threats or answers by the end of pack 3, and take a third flier over a second Unruly Mob, Silverchase Fox, Thraben Purebloods or Festerhide Boar. Do not take an off-colour flier over an on-colour one.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Avacyn's Pilgrim, Ambush Viper, Kindercatch, Ashmouth Hound, Feral Ridgewolf, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils and Tormented Pariah if red, Silver-Inlaid Dagger, Unruly Mob (one), Doomed Traveler, Avacynian Priest, Vampire Interloper, Bump in the Night. Take cheap threats so one removal spell cannot blank your curve, and take them over card-draw filler, counterspells and bounce.

Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell or card draw. Walking Corpse made the 3-0 BG and a 0-3 BR (both anecdotes), so it is only a 23rd card.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Stitcher's Apprentice, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Ancient Grudge, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Lost in the Mist, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab over a better creature (it was cut for counterspells and the deck lost, so keep it only as a cheap blocker), Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Trepanation Blade, Darkthicket Wolf, Make a Wish, Delver of Secrets, Heartless Summoning, Mask of Avacyn over a flier, Riot Devils unless red is deep, Cloistered Youth over a flier, a second Moment of Heroism, and a second Makeshift Mauler. Naturalize and Wooden Stakes are sideboard cards at best. Armored Skaab, Skaab Goliath and Forbidden Alchemy are only for a deck with Spider Spawning and real self-mill. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician and ground bodies like Kessig Wolf.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default; 16 is worth trying for low-curve aggro (anecdote). Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 WB, 2-1 WB), 11 Plains/6 Islands (2-1 WU), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG), mostly red with 6 Islands plus Sulfur Falls (3-0 UR) (anecdotes). Count pips and give the main colour 10 sources. A 9 Forest/7 Plains WG went 0-3, and a WR on 6 or 7 Mountains went badly with a heavy red load (anecdotes). Do not give a colour with several double-pip or removal cards only six or seven sources.
- Double pips: give a BB or GG colour 8+ sources or cut them. On 2026-11-15 a WB with Morkrut Banshee and Abattoir Ghoul on 7 Swamps went 2-4 in games, and Bloodline Keeper on 7 Swamps lost earlier; but a 3-0 BR with Bloodgift Demon ran 7 Swamps (anecdotes). Avoid double pips in both colours. Three GG Somberwald Spiders want 8+ Forests. A 10 Island/7 Swamp UB risked stranding black removal; with removal in both colours aim for 9/8.
- Aim for about five real removal spells (pumps like Furor of the Bitten do not count), three or four answers to fliers (which can also clear ground blockers), and two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers. Play the removal you drafted: a BR benched Sensory Deprivation with only three removal spells (anecdote).
- Keep a curve with enough cheap plays. The 0-3 BG, the 0-3 WR and the 0-3 ground BR were top-heavy or full of ground filler, while the winning decks (3-0 BG, 2-1 WR) had early drops and burn or removal.
- Keep a flexible late threat or value source (Mentor of the Meek, Moan of the Unhallowed, Gavony Township, Moorland Haunt, Rally the Peasants, Divine Reckoning, Spider Spawning), but do not cut good cheap cards to fit one.
- Pair removal with good cheap creatures and close with fliers. Do not pad with ground filler when a flier or removal spell is available. If you have no real fliers, keep your reach.
- Do not leave yourself with no answer to an opposing colour's key cards: an RG with no black had nothing for Claustrophobia and Victim of Night decks. Enchantment-based removal on your best creature is a real risk; keep multiple threats. Creatures with toughness above one matter against Geistflame, Brimstone Volley and Harvest Pyre.
- Settle your 23 in the main deck by pack 3. Sideboard plans rarely rescue a deck. Check every sideboard swap against your colours and do not side out main removal. Keep at least one cheap play main.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core. Do not main Gnaw to the Bone, Mulch, Dream Twist, Make a Wish or self-mill cards without a self-mill deck. Cut a redundant combat trick before a playable creature, and cut filler before a proactive creature.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke, Fiend Hunter and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies.
- Expect long games, often 15 to 40 turns, and up to 62. Break stalls with removal, Spider Spawning, Rally the Peasants, Moan of the Unhallowed and evasive threats. Apply pressure early rather than waiting, especially with UB and WU. Ground-only decks stalled into 20- and 31-turn losses (anecdote).
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR. Fast starts beat slow hands in 9 to 17 turns (anecdotes: a WR beat a BR and a WB in 14 and 15 turns on 2026-11-15, and WR humans beat a WB in 14-15 turns).
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Angelic Overseer, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Murder of Crows, Midnight Haunting, Falkenrath Noble, Screeching Bat, Galvanic Juggernaut, Boneyard Wurm and Scourge of Geier Reach.
- Against WU, UB, UR, UG and mono-blue, kill fliers early. Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation and Lost in the Mist will target your best creature, so keep a second threat ready. Blue decks win long grinds (a UG beat a WR and a BR in 20 to 27 turns on 2026-11-15, anecdotes), so do not durdle and keep a flier answer.
- Against WB, expect real removal (Rebuke, Victim of Night) held for your fliers, plus Angelic Overseer, Chapel Geist and Ghoulraiser value. Do not rely on one big threat.
- Against BG and WG, expect Typhoid Rats, Somberwald Spider, Spidery Grasp, Ghostly Possession, Fiend Hunter and Garruk Relentless to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup and do not flood the board with ground bodies.
- Against WR, RG, BR and UR, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Furor of the Bitten and Pitchburn Devils to punish small creatures. Do not overcommit and keep your own fliers alive. WR also runs Rebuke, Smite the Monstrous, Midnight Haunting, Fiend Hunter, Slayer of the Wicked, Rally the Peasants and Bonds of Faith, and will answer your fliers.
- Against white decks with Fiend Hunter and Avacynian Priest, expect one-for-one trades on early creatures; prefer threats that leave value behind. Expect Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning in long games.
