# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-07's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Eighteen nights: 144 decks, 535 games. Guided seats are 231-243 in games (49%, 92-100 in matches); unguided are 304-292 (51%, 124-116 in matches). Guided seats have not beaten unguided seats, so the guide has not been shown to help. Treat archetype lines as hypotheses and lean on the card numbers.

- BR: 57% of games (102 games, 14 decks, 27-15 in matches). Best pair by matches. Tonight an unguided BR went 3-0 (anecdote) on Tormented Pariah, Pitchburn Devils, Brimstone Volley, Victim of Night and Harvest Pyre, and beat a UR and a WB deck. Black removal plus a few real finishers.
- BG: 54% (152 games, 21 decks, 35-28 in matches). Needs evasion or removal-backed finishers and cheap creatures. Somberwald Spider and Spider Spawning beat ground decks.
- WR: 54% (101 games, 14 decks, 23-19 in matches). Cheap bodies, cheap removal and fliers, but it needs a late threat: WR decks have lost long grinds to Somberwald Spider or UB.
- WU: 51% (150 games, 20 decks, 30-30 in matches). Tonight a guided WU went 2-1 (5-2 in games, anecdote) on 11 Plains/6 Islands with two Chapel Geist, two Rebuke, Sensory Deprivation and Invisible Stalker. An earlier WU built on Delver of Secrets, Fortress Crab and Creepy Doll went 0-3 (anecdote). White removal plus real fliers works; filler does not. Its loss was to BR in a 37-turn game after cutting cheap blockers.
- WG: 50% (103 games, 14 decks, 22-20 in matches). A WG that dropped Dead Weight and Corpse Lunge for filler kept only Prey Upon and Urgent Exorcism and went 0-3, 1-6 in games (anecdote). Evasion alone does not win; removal count matters.
- RG: 50% (60 games, 8 decks, 12-12 in matches). Tonight an unguided RG went 2-1 on two Brimstone Volley, Geistflame and Harvest Pyre, losing to UR's fliers (Moon Heron x2, Murder of Crows, Stitched Drake). Needs ways to reach fliers. When red is deep, a red-heavy deck with a light green side works (anecdotes).
- WB: 44% (97 games, 13 decks, 17-22 in matches). Weak. Tonight two WB decks went 1-2 (anecdotes), one with five fliers and eight removal spells (2-4 in games) and one on only 8 Swamps (3-5 in games). Fliers and removal are not enough: it also needs cheap early blockers and enough sources for both colours.
- UB: 46% (139 games, 18 decks, 22-32 in matches). Tonight an unguided UB went 1-2 (anecdote); its skaabs were too slow to close and it had one hard removal spell (Claustrophobia). Needs cheap blockers, removal, and a stall-breaker (Moan of the Unhallowed, Murder of Crows). Do not take Delver of Secrets repeatedly.
- UR: 46% (112 games, 15 decks, 19-26 in matches). Tonight a UR went 2-1 (5-4 in games, anecdote) on Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation and two Curse of the Pierced Heart; it ran only about four removal spells. Still below average overall; take blue removal.
- UG 38% (16 games, anecdote). Mono-colour: R 3-3 in matches (16 games).
- White is well supported on individual cards, but white pairs have not dominated. Cheap white removal (Rebuke) is contested, so take it early. Stay flexible on the second colour.
- Count playables before committing: pick a main colour by about pick 5 and a pair by mid pack 1 to mid pack 2. Do not leave a colour for one early removal spell unless the new colour is clearly open. If strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8, move in. Blue was thin for a WU drafter: in mid pack 2 take a second on-colour blue flier over filler.
- Expect rivals to share commons (Victim of Night, Walking Corpse); take removal early.
- Evasion plus removal breaks stalls. Games stall for 13 to 55 turns.
- Splashing is poor. Don't run a colour on six basics unless it is a light second colour with excellent depth in the main colour (a 3-0 RG had 6 Forest, anecdote; an earlier RG on six Forests went 3-5 in games, anecdote).

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Smite the Monstrous, Bonds of Faith, Sensory Deprivation, Corpse Lunge, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre. Take a second Dead Weight, Victim of Night or Brimstone Volley if offered. Aim for five or more removal spells; WG decks with four or fewer lost (anecdotes). Take removal before Silverchase Fox or Abbey Griffin.
2. Strong uncommons and rares: Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee, Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders, Slayer of the Wicked, Fiend Hunter, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw.
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Somberwald Spider. Aim for three evasive threats or a removal-backed finisher by the end of pack 3. Do not take an off-colour flier over an on-colour one.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Ashmouth Hound, Bump in the Night, Unruly Mob, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils and Tormented Pariah if red, Silver-Inlaid Dagger. Early blockers matter: slow UB and WB starts lost to RG, BR and WR (anecdotes).

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Armored Skaab, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Wooden Stakes, Ancient Grudge, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Lost in the Mist, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Festerhide Boar, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Cloistered Youth over a flier, Unburial Rites, and Makeshift Mauler as a second copy. Delver of Secrets // Insectile Aberration is marginal (taken three times in a 1-2 UB and main-decked three times in a 0-3 WU, anecdotes). Walking Corpse is acceptable as an early blocker when nothing better is left (anecdote). Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell or a dead card. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician. Card-selection spells did not save a UB deck; take a faster curve.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default. For a low-curve aggro deck, 16 is worth trying (anecdote). Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 WB), 11 Plains/6 Islands (2-1 WU), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG) (all anecdotes). Count pips for the heavier colour rather than copying a split.
- Avoid double pips in both colours, or give the double-pip colour at least 8 sources. Three GG Somberwald Spiders want 8+ Forests. A BG deck with BB and GG cards on 7 black sources went 1-2, and a WB deck with a white core on 8 Swamps went 3-5 in games (anecdotes).
- Aim for four or more answers to fliers (that can also clear ground blockers) and at least two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers. Keep Prey Upon and Urgent Exorcism-type answers for opposing fliers.
- Keep one late threat or finisher. Decks without one lost 16-to-55-turn grinds repeatedly (anecdotes).
- Removal alone is not enough: UB, WB and RG decks with plenty of removal still lost for lack of cheap, sturdy creatures. Pair removal with good cheap creatures, and do not cut cheap blockers for a marginal fourth flier (a 2-1 WU lost to BR after doing so, anecdote).
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core. Play the removal and flier answers you drafted; check every sideboard swap against your colours.
- Main fliers over filler such as Riot Devils (unless red is your main colour and deep), Walking Corpse or a marginal fourth creature.
- Include one or two card-advantage or stall-breaking cards (Ghoulcaller's Chant, Moan of the Unhallowed) for stalls (anecdote).
- Do not main Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone or Mulch. 1-toughness creatures die to Brimstone Volley and Geistflame.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke, Fiend Hunter and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies.
- Expect long games, often 20 to 40 turns, and up to 52. Break stalls with removal, Spider Spawning and evasive threats.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR burn decks; fast starts beat slow hands in 11 to 15 turns (anecdotes).
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Angelic Overseer, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Moon Heron, Murder of Crows, Galvanic Juggernaut and Scourge of Geier Reach.
- Against WU, UB and UR, kill fliers early; Claustrophobia and Sensory Deprivation will target your best creature.
- Against WB, expect real removal held for your fliers; do not rely on one big threat.
- Against BG and WG, expect Typhoid Rats and Somberwald Spider to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup and do not flood the board with ground bodies.
- Against WR, RG and BR, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame and Blasphemous Act to punish small creatures; do not overcommit into a sweeper and keep your own fliers alive.
- WU and UB should keep cheap two-drops and the flier clock even when a stall looks likely (anecdote).
