# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-13's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Twenty-four nights: 192 decks, 711 games. Guided seats are 280-295 in games (49%, 114-120 in matches); unguided are 395-384 (51%, 158-154 in matches); the conventional guide is 36-32 (53%, 16-14 in matches, 10 decks). On the five nights with all three kinds of seat, guided went 30-41 in games (42%, 13-17 in matches), conventional 36-32 (53%) and unguided 79-72 (52%). Guided has not beaten the others and is behind on those nights, so this guide has not been shown to help. Treat archetype lines as hypotheses and lean on card numbers and on advice that is consistent across lessons: commit to two colours early, count playables, have a curve, removal, and some way to win in the air or in a long game.

Pairs by games won (decks, matches, game win rate):
- WR: 56% (153 games, 21 decks, 36-27 in matches). Best pair so far. On 2026-11-11 and 2026-11-12 a guided WR went 3-0 (anecdotes). Core: cheap creatures (Ashmouth Hound, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar), removal (Rebuke, Into the Maw of Hell), Rally the Peasants, Furor of the Bitten, and some fliers (Voiceless Spirit). A couple of marginal red cards (Traitorous Blood, Curse of the Pierced Heart) were fine. A WR with two Thraben Sentry, two Selfless Cathar, two Unruly Mob and only three fliers lost 1-2 earlier: take a third flier or removal over a second Unruly Mob. Red and white flowed at picks 4-8 of packs 2 and 3.
- BG: 54% (183 games, 25 decks, 41-34 in matches). Needs cheap creatures and evasion or removal-backed finishers. Somberwald Spider and Spider Spawning beat ground decks. Take cheap two-drops and flier answers over a fourth fatty.
- BR: 52% (153 games, 21 decks, 36-27 in matches). Good by matches, but splits by build. On 2026-11-13 a conventional BR with ground bodies (Kessig Wolf, Markov Patrician, Night Revelers) went 1-2, stalling in 18-to-26-turn games (anecdote). Earlier an eight-removal BR went 0-3 for lack of finishers and a cheap-evasion BR lost 18-to-34-turn grinds to blue (anecdotes). BR needs evasive finishers like Falkenrath Noble, plus Vampire Interloper or Rakish Heir over ground filler, and answers to fliers. Wooden Stakes is poor.
- WG: 51% (156 games, 22 decks, 35-31 in matches). Swings with the build. On 2026-11-13 a guided WG with four fliers went 0-3 (1-6 in games), losing 12- and 22-turn games to BR, with 9 Forest/7 Plains and early picks (two Dead Weight, Riot Devils, two Vampiric Fury) never played; a conventional WG went 2-1 but had almost no way over the board (anecdotes). Fliers alone are not enough: you need cheap plays too, and a 10-source main colour.
- RG: 51% (68 games, 9 decks, 14-13 in matches). Needs ways to reach fliers and an answer to Claustrophobia and Victim of Night.
- WU: 50% (185 games, 25 decks, 37-38 in matches). Average and often too slow. White removal plus real cheap fliers works; Think Twice-style filler does not.
- WB: 46% (122 games, 16 decks, 23-25 in matches). Below average on 122 games, but on 2026-11-13 an unguided WB went 3-0 with Angelic Overseer, Rebuke, Victim of Night, Moan of the Unhallowed, Ghoulraiser and Abattoir Ghoul (18-to-31-turn games), beating a guided WB 2-1; the guided WB went 1-2 with only two fliers and Bloodline Keeper and Victim of Night on 7 Swamps (anecdotes). WB works with removal, fliers and grindy value; count BB pips.
- UB: 46% (177 games, 23 decks, 28-41 in matches). Below average. Needs a low curve, early creatures and cheap removal. Do not take Delver of Secrets repeatedly or Dream Twist; marginal fliers and a second Spectral Flight or Moon Heron lost earlier.
- UR: 45% (141 games, 19 decks, 24-33 in matches). Weak. On 2026-11-13 two unguided UR decks went 2-1 each (anecdotes): one carried by Sensory Deprivation, Harvest Pyre, Brimstone Volley, two Makeshift Mauler, two Ashmouth Hound, Moon Heron and Mindshrieker; the other with Stitched Drake, Murder of Crows and Silent Departure, but its bounce spells bought time without closing and Galvanic Juggernaut carried too much. Earlier an UR Skaab-mill deck went 0-3. Take removal, efficient creatures and a second finisher over bounce, Curses and card-draw filler.
- UG: 42% (31 games, 4 decks, 3-9 in matches). On 2026-11-13 an unguided UG went 1-2, losing 14-to-29-turn grinds; it played two Festerhide Boar and slow spells (Lost in the Mist, Claustrophobia) with little pressure (anecdote). Stay two colours; mono U 4-5 in matches (21 games), R 3-3 (16 games).
- White is the best-supported colour on individual cards (Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Midnight Haunting, Bonds of Faith). Cheap white removal (Rebuke, Fiend Hunter) is contested, so take it early.
- Black is crowded. Take black removal early in pack 1.
- Read what is flowing after pack 1. If strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8, move in. Judge by real playables, not filler like Lost in the Mist.
- Commit: main colour by about pick 5, a pair by mid pack 1 to mid pack 2. Off-colour early picks were wasted repeatedly: Claustrophobia stranded in WG and again on 2026-11-13 in a BR (taken second in pack 2, never played, then used against it by UG); Garruk Relentless never played in WU; two Dead Weight and two Vampiric Fury stranded in a WG; a first-pick Fiend Hunter went to an opponent's WG (anecdotes). Do not leave a colour for one early removal spell unless the new colour is clearly open, and decide your second colour by pick 5 of pack 2 at the latest.
- Evasion plus removal breaks stalls, but a fast curve with removal also won. Games run 11 to 62 turns, mostly 17 to 37; on 2026-11-13 most games ran 18 to 31 turns, so grindy removal and card advantage mattered.
- Splashing is poor. Do not run a colour on six basics (anecdotes).

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Smite the Monstrous, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith, Fiend Hunter, Sensory Deprivation, Corpse Lunge, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Spidery Grasp. Take a second Dead Weight, Victim of Night, Claustrophobia or Brimstone Volley if offered. Aim for five or more removal spells; removal and cheap creatures carried the 3-0 decks (a 3-0 WB had only Rebuke and Victim of Night as removal and wanted more, anecdote). Take removal on colour: first-pick Claustrophobia, Dead Weight, Rebuke and Fiend Hunter all ended up stranded or in an opponent's deck.
2. Strong uncommons and rares: Sever the Bloodline, Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee, Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders, Instigator Gang, Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw, Rally the Peasants, Angelic Overseer.
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Mindshrieker, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Somberwald Spider. Many losing decks were short of fliers or answers, but a four-flier WG went 0-3 on 2026-11-13, so fliers do not replace cheap plays. Aim for about three evasive threats or answers by the end of pack 3 and take a third flier over a second Unruly Mob, a second Festerhide Boar or a fourth ground two-drop; do not take an off-colour flier over an on-colour one.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Avacyn's Pilgrim, Ashmouth Hound, Feral Ridgewolf, Bump in the Night, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils and Tormented Pariah if red, Silver-Inlaid Dagger, Unruly Mob, Doomed Traveler, Avacynian Priest, Vampire Interloper. Take cheap threats below power four so one removal spell cannot blank your curve, and take them over card-draw filler and bounce. Take removal over a tribal creature like Champion of the Parish (anecdote). In a white deck planning Travel Preparations, count humans.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Stitcher's Apprentice, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Ancient Grudge, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Lost in the Mist, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Trepanation Blade, Darkthicket Wolf, Mask of Avacyn over a flier, Riot Devils unless red is deep, Cloistered Youth over a flier, a second Moment of Heroism, and Makeshift Mauler as a second copy (though two helped a 2-1 UR, anecdote). Naturalize is a sideboard card at best. Armored Skaab is only for a deck with real self-mill. Wooden Stakes is at most a sideboard card. Walking Corpse only as an early blocker when nothing better is left. Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician and ground bodies like Kessig Wolf.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default; 16 is worth trying for low-curve aggro (anecdote). Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 WB, 2-1 WB), 11 Plains/6 Islands (2-1 WU), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG) (anecdotes). Count pips for the heavier colour; give the main colour 10 sources (a 9 Forest/7 Plains WG went 0-3, anecdote). Seven Mountains was too few for a red-heavy WR.
- Double pips: give a BB or GG colour 8+ sources where you can. Evidence is mixed (anecdotes): Bloodline Keeper and Victim of Night on 7 Swamps lost in a WB on 2026-11-13, while a 3-0 BR with Bloodgift Demon ran 7 Swamps. Avoid double pips in both colours. Three GG Somberwald Spiders want 8+ Forests.
- Aim for five or more removal spells, three or four answers to fliers (which can also clear ground blockers), and two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers.
- Keep a flexible late threat or value source (Mentor of the Meek, Moan of the Unhallowed, Gavony Township, Rally the Peasants, Divine Reckoning, Spider Spawning). Decks without one lost many 16-to-62-turn grinds, but do not cut good cheap cards to fit one. Ground BR aggro stalled in long games; include evasive finishers or card advantage.
- Pair removal with good cheap creatures and close with fliers. Do not pad with ground filler when a flier or removal spell is available. If you have no real fliers, keep your reach.
- Do not leave yourself with no answer to an opposing colour's key cards: an RG with no black had nothing for Claustrophobia and Victim of Night decks. Enchantment-based removal on your best creature is a real risk; keep multiple threats.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core, but a playable on-colour card is no reason to restructure. Play the removal and flier answers you drafted; check every sideboard swap against your colours. Do not side out your main removal. Keep at least one cheap play main rather than cutting every early creature.
- Do not main Gnaw to the Bone, Mulch, Dream Twist or self-mill cards without a self-mill deck. 1-toughness creatures die to Brimstone Volley and Geistflame. Cut a redundant combat trick before a playable creature.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke, Fiend Hunter and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies.
- Expect long games, often 17 to 40 turns, and up to 62. Break stalls with removal, Spider Spawning, Rally the Peasants, Moan of the Unhallowed and evasive threats.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR; fast starts beat slow hands in 11 to 17 turns (anecdotes: a WR beat a mono-blue and a WG in 13 to 15 turns; a BR with Kessig Wolf and Vampire Interloper lost in 12 and 17 turns to a UG).
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Angelic Overseer, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Moon Heron, Murder of Crows, Galvanic Juggernaut, Boneyard Wurm and Scourge of Geier Reach.
- Against WU, UB, UR and mono-blue, kill fliers early; Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation and Lost in the Mist will target your best creature, so keep a second threat ready. Blue decks win long grinds, so do not durdle.
- Against WB, expect real removal (Rebuke, Victim of Night) held for your fliers, plus Angelic Overseer and Ghoulraiser value; do not rely on one big threat.
- Against BG and WG, expect Typhoid Rats, Somberwald Spider, Spidery Grasp, Ghostly Possession, Fiend Hunter and Garruk Relentless to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup and do not flood the board with ground bodies.
- Against WR, RG and BR, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Furor of the Bitten and Pitchburn Devils to punish small creatures; do not overcommit and keep your own fliers alive. WR also runs Rebuke, Smite the Monstrous, Midnight Haunting, Fiend Hunter, Slayer of the Wicked, Rally the Peasants and Bonds of Faith, and will answer your fliers.
- Against white decks with Fiend Hunter and Avacynian Priest, expect one-for-one trades on early creatures; prefer threats that leave value behind. Expect Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning in long games.
- WU and UB should keep cheap two-drops and the flier clock even when a stall looks likely, and apply pressure early rather than waiting (anecdote).
