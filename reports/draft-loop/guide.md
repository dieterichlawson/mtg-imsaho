# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-05's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Sixteen nights: 128 decks, 475 games. Guided seats are 207-209 in games (50%, 83-85 in matches); unguided are 268-266 (50%, 109-107 in matches). Guided seats have not beaten unguided seats, so the guide has not been shown to help. Treat every line as a hypothesis and lean on the card numbers.

- BR: 56% of games (93 games, 13 decks, 24-15 in matches). Best pair by matches. Black removal plus a few real finishers; cheap red creatures with Dead Weight worked in one deck (anecdote).
- WR: 56% (85 games, 12 decks, 21-15 in matches). Tonight's guided WR deck went 3-0 (anecdote, 6-1 in games) on Abbey Griffin, Chapel Geist, Rebuke, Geistflame and Brimstone Volley, with Elder Cathar, Thraben Sentry and Ashmouth Hound as cheap blockers. It cut black filler for a clean two-colour core. This is the model deck: cheap bodies, cheap removal, a few fliers.
- BG: 54% (152 games, 21 decks, 35-28 in matches). Needs evasion or removal-backed finishers and cheap creatures. Tonight's BG deck went 1-2 (anecdote) with double pips in both colours (Victim of Night and Screeching Bat are BB, Somberwald Spider is GG) on only 7 black sources.
- WG: 54% (82 games, 11 decks, 19-14 in matches). Tonight's WG went 2-1 on 9 Forest and 8 Plains with only three evasive threats and lost to WR (anecdote).
- WU: 52% (135 games, 18 decks, 28-26 in matches). Fliers, cheap removal and a late threat. Ground filler like a second Makeshift Mauler hurts.
- WB: 46% (83 games, 11 decks, 15-18 in matches). Works with plenty of cheap removal and fliers (Victim of Night, Rebuke, Tribute to Hunger, Dead Weight, Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit). Tonight's WB deck had no evasive fliers, lost 0-2 to BG's Somberwald Spiders in a 40-turn grind, and went 1-2 (anecdote). Do not play it without fliers.
- Weak: UR 45% (103 games, 14 decks, 17-25 in matches). UB 44% (117 games, 15 decks, 17-28 in matches); tonight three UB decks went 1-2, 1-2 and 2-1 (anecdotes) and beat BG. The data still says weak, but UB with Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation, Invisible Stalker and Stitched Drake can win; it loses to fast starts and lacks cheap creatures. RG 41% (46 games, 6 decks, 7-11 in matches); tonight's RG went 1-2 with a ground-bound deck and few flier answers (anecdote). UG 38% (16 games).
- White is the best-supported colour: its fliers and removal were on the winning WR, WB and WG decks. Take them early.
- Evasion plus removal breaks stalls. Games stall for 13 to 55 turns.
- Pick a main colour by about pick 5 and a pair by mid pack 1 to mid pack 2 at the latest. Do not leave a colour for one early removal spell unless the new colour is clearly open. Brimstone Volley first-picked in pack 2 did help a 3-0 WR deck tonight (anecdote), so take it when red is open and you are or may be red. Tonight's RG drafter first-picked Murder of Crows in pack 3 and never played it (anecdote).
- Splashing is poor. Mono-colour is risky (2 mono-red decks 3-3 in matches). Don't run a colour on six basics (an RG deck on six Forests went 3-5 in games, anecdote).
- Read what is passed. If a neighbour is in your colours, move or take contested playables earlier.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Smite the Monstrous, Bonds of Faith, Sensory Deprivation, Corpse Lunge, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre. Take a second Dead Weight and a second Victim of Night if offered.
2. Strong uncommons and rares: Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee, Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders, Slayer of the Wicked, Fiend Hunter, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw.
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Moon Heron, Somberwald Spider. Have at least three evasive threats by the end of pack 3 and take one over ground filler. Keep green's Prey Upon over a marginal flier if you are green.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Ashmouth Hound, Bump in the Night, Unruly Mob, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Silver-Inlaid Dagger. Early blockers matter: slow UB starts lost to Brimstone Volley and Elder Cathar (anecdote).

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Walking Corpse, Armored Skaab, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Lost in the Mist, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Naturalize, Memory's Journey, Festerhide Boar, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Riot Devils, Cellar Door, Stensia Bloodhall, Cloistered Youth over a flier, and Makeshift Mauler as a second copy. Delver of Secrets and Darkthicket Wolf are marginal. Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell or a dead card: drafters keep getting nothing from picks 10-14 (Naturalize and Memory's Journey over green creatures tonight). Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician. Card-selection spells (Think Twice, Forbidden Alchemy, Curiosity) did not save a 4-4 UB deck (anecdote); take a faster curve.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default. For a low-curve aggro deck, 16 is worth trying (anecdote). A 3-0 WB deck ran 12 Plains and 5 Swamp; a 3-0 WG deck ran 10 Forest and 7 Plains; a 2-1 WG ran 9 and 8 (anecdotes). Count pips for the heavier colour rather than copying a split.
- Avoid double pips in both colours, or give the double-pip colour at least 8 sources. A BG deck with BB and GG cards on 7 black sources went 1-2 (anecdote).
- Aim for four or more answers to fliers (that can also clear ground blockers) and at least two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers. Ground-bound RG decks lost to Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake and Moon Heron with only Geistflame and Harvest Pyre as answers (anecdote).
- Keep one late threat or finisher. Decks without one lost 20-to-55-turn grinds repeatedly (anecdotes); tonight's WB with Curse of Death's Hold and two Silverchase Fox stalled out the same way.
- Removal alone is not enough: UB and RG decks with plenty of removal went 3-5 in games for lack of cheap, sturdy creatures. Pair removal with good cheap creatures.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core (the 3-0 WR deck did, anecdote).
- Play the removal and flier answers you drafted; check every sideboard swap against your colours.
- Main fliers over filler such as Riot Devils, Walking Corpse or a marginal fourth creature.
- Include one or two card-advantage cards (Ghoulcaller's Chant, Unburial Rites) for stalls (anecdote).
- Do not main Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone or Mulch. Fragile creatures like Typhoid Rats and Brain Weevil were exposed to burn in one matchup (anecdote), but Typhoid Rats is still 59% in 179 games.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke, Fiend Hunter and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies.
- Expect long games, often 20 to 40 turns. Break stalls with removal, Spider Spawning and evasive threats.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR burn decks; fast starts beat slow hands in 14 to 15 turns (anecdote).
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Angelic Overseer, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Moon Heron and Murder of Crows.
- Against WU, UB and UR, kill fliers early; Claustrophobia and Sensory Deprivation will target your best creature.
- Against WB, expect real removal held for your fliers; do not rely on one big threat.
- Against BG, expect Typhoid Rats and Somberwald Spider to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup.
- Against WR, expect Brimstone Volley and Geistflame to punish small creatures; keep your own fliers alive.
- WU and UB should keep cheap two-drops and the flier clock even when a stall looks likely (anecdote).
