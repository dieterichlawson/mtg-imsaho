# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-12's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Twenty-three nights: 184 decks, 681 games. Guided seats are 276-284 in games (49%, 113-115 in matches); unguided are 376-371 (50%, 150-150 in matches); the conventional guide is 29-26 (53%, 13-11 in matches, 8 decks). On the four nights with all three kinds of seat, guided went 26-30 in games (46%, 12-12 in matches), conventional 29-26 (53%) and unguided 60-59 (50%). That is a small sample, but guided has not beaten the others, so this guide has not been shown to help. Treat archetype lines as hypotheses and lean on the card numbers and on advice that is consistent across lessons: commit to two colours early, count playables, have a curve, fliers and removal.

Pairs by games won (decks, matches, game win rate):
- WR: 56% (153 games, 21 decks, 36-27 in matches). Best pair so far. On 2026-11-11 and again on 2026-11-12 a guided WR went 3-0 (anecdotes). The 2026-11-11 deck ran Ashmouth Hound, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Rebuke, Into the Maw of Hell and Rally the Peasants with one flier (Voiceless Spirit). The 2026-11-12 deck went 6-2 in games with three flier sources and kept Traitorous Blood and Curse of the Pierced Heart, so a couple of marginal red cards are fine if the core is cheap creatures, removal and fliers. It beat U and UG decks in 18-to-25-turn grinds. A WR with two Thraben Sentry, two Selfless Cathar, two Unruly Mob and only three fliers lost 1-2 earlier: take a third flier or removal over a second Unruly Mob. Rebuke, Rally the Peasants, Furor of the Bitten and Voiceless Spirit are the cards to take early. Red and white flowed at picks 4-8 of packs 2 and 3.
- BR: 53% (146 games, 20 decks, 35-25 in matches). Good by matches, but results split by build. On 2026-11-12 two BR seats met: the unguided 0-3 (cheap-evasion aggro, lost every 18-to-34-turn grind to blue decks with Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation, Lost in the Mist and Stitched Drake) and the unguided 2-1 (lost to WG in 23-, 25- and 62-turn games; anecdotes). Both shared Crossway Vampire, Falkenrath Noble, Harvest Pyre and Corpse Lunge: mirrored commons split the pool, so check neighbours' colours by pick 5. Earlier a BR with only Geistflame and Brimstone Volley as flier answers went 1-2, and an eight-removal BR went 0-3 for lack of finishers (anecdotes). BR needs evasive finishers like Falkenrath Noble and answers to fliers, not filler ground bodies. Wooden Stakes is poor.
- BG: 54% (183 games, 25 decks, 41-34 in matches). Needs evasion or removal-backed finishers and cheap creatures. Somberwald Spider and Spider Spawning beat ground decks. An unguided BG went 1-2 on 2026-11-11 with a fourth top-end fatty and too few cheap plays (anecdote). Take cheap two-drops and flier answers over more top end.
- WG: 52% (143 games, 20 decks, 33-27 in matches). Results swing with the build. On 2026-11-12 a guided WG went 2-1 and a conventional WG 2-1 (anecdotes). The guided one lost 0-2 to a WG with two Orchard Spirit, Abbey Griffin and two Spidery Grasp while it ran two Festerhide Boar. The conventional one lost 0-2 in 14 and 15 turns to WR, whose Smite the Monstrous, Rebuke and Midnight Haunting answered its fliers Abbey Griffin and Chapel Geist, and it was short on humans for Travel Preparations. Removal creatures and late value beat cheap ground bodies; fliers are not enough alone against a WR with answers.
- RG: 51% (68 games, 9 decks, 14-13 in matches). Needs ways to reach fliers and an answer to Claustrophobia and Victim of Night.
- WU: 50% (185 games, 25 decks, 37-38 in matches). Average and often too slow. White removal plus real cheap fliers works; Think Twice-style filler and a stalled shell do not. Needs a lower curve and early pressure.
- UB: 46% (177 games, 23 decks, 28-41 in matches). Below average. An unguided UB went 0-3 on 2026-11-11 with marginal fliers (Cobbled Wings, Trepanation Blade), a second Spectral Flight and a second Moon Heron instead of curve creatures (anecdote). Needs a low curve, early creatures and cheap removal. Do not take Delver of Secrets repeatedly or Dream Twist.
- UR: 43% (126 games, 17 decks, 20-31 in matches). Worst pair. On 2026-11-12 an unguided UR Skaab-mill deck with Galvanic Juggernaut went 0-3 (1-6 in games, anecdote); it took Think Twice, Curiosity and Forbidden Alchemy over bodies and cut Bloodcrazed Neonate, leaving no one-drop. Take cheap creatures over card-draw filler; Stitched Drake and Moon Heron over Trepanation Blade.
- WB: 45% (105 games, 14 decks, 19-23 in matches). Below average. Needs cheap blockers and sources for both colours.
- Others: U 4-5 in matches (21 games), UG 2-7 (23 games), R 3-3 (16 games). On 2026-11-12 an unguided mono-blue deck with 16 Islands and Nephalia Drownyard went 2-1 (5-2 in games, anecdote) but its wins came in 25-to-34-turn games, and it lost to WR in 16, 25 and 13 turns for lack of cheap plays. A conventional-guide UG self-mill deck went 1-2 with thin removal (Prey Upon, Naturalize, Silent Departure) after early picks went to Fiend Hunter, two Rebukes, Curse of Oblivion and Bloodcrazed Neonate that never made the deck (anecdotes). Stay two colours.
- White is the best-supported colour on individual cards (Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Midnight Haunting, Bonds of Faith). Cheap white removal (Rebuke, Fiend Hunter) is contested, so take it early.
- Black is crowded. Take black removal early in pack 1 rather than waiting for Victim of Night in pack 3.
- Read what is flowing after pack 1. If strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8, move in; a WR seat did this on 2026-11-11 and went 3-0. On 2026-11-12 Lost in the Mist arrived at picks 6 and 7 of pack 1 and signalled open blue (anecdote), but a Lost in the Mist is filler, so judge by real playables.
- Commit: main colour by about pick 5, a pair by mid pack 1 to mid pack 2. Off-colour early picks were wasted repeatedly: Claustrophobia stranded in WG, Garruk Relentless never played in WU, and on 2026-11-12 a pick-one Dead Weight and Riot Devils stranded in a 3-0 WR, Moon Heron and Makeshift Mauler unused in a WG, and Fiend Hunter and two Rebukes unused in a UG (anecdotes). Do not leave a colour for one early removal spell unless the new colour is clearly open, and decide on your second colour by pick 5 of pack 2 at the latest.
- Evasion plus removal breaks stalls, but a fast curve with removal also won. Games run 11 to 62 turns, mostly 17 to 37.
- Splashing is poor. Do not run a colour on six basics (anecdotes).

## Commons that win

Deck game win rate for decks running the card.

- Green: Woodland Sleuth (194 games, 56%), Avacyn's Pilgrim (115 games, 56%, taken at 9.1), Villagers of Estwald (178 games, 55%), Grave Bramble (169 games, 53%, taken at 8.9), Kindercatch, Prey Upon. Somberwald Spider is needed as a flier answer. Festerhide Boar is only filler; a second one cost a WG a flier or cheap answer.
- Black: Typhoid Rats (263 games, 55%), Victim of Night (261 games, 53%), Ghoulcaller's Chant, Dead Weight, Bump in the Night.
- White: Bonds of Faith (259 games, 58%), Thraben Sentry (216 games, 56%, taken at 7.6), Avacynian Priest (255 games, 55%), Unruly Mob (216 games, 54%, taken at 9.1; one is fine, a second is not worth more than a flier), Selfless Cathar (192 games, 53%), Voiceless Spirit (326 games, 53%), Chapel Geist, Silverchase Fox.
- Red: Furor of the Bitten (115 games, 59%, taken at 10.3), Ashmouth Hound (234 games, 56%, taken at 4.0), Curse of the Pierced Heart (128 games, 54%), Traitorous Blood (100 games, 53%, taken at 9.4), Tormented Pariah (220 games, 53%), Night Revelers, Pitchburn Devils.
- Blue: Spectral Flight, Hysterical Blindness (late picks), Frightful Delusion (anecdote). Take a second Spectral Flight only after you have the creatures.
- Colourless: Silver-Inlaid Dagger (130 games, 56%, uncommon).
- Claustrophobia, Rebuke, Dead Weight, Tribute to Hunger, Brimstone Volley, Geistflame and Stitched Drake are praised in lessons and go early. Blue decks that ran Claustrophobia twice and Sensory Deprivation beat a BR on 2026-11-12 (anecdote).

A UG self-mill deck with Spider Spawning and Boneyard Wurm went 1-2 (anecdote); take them in a deck that has the cheap plays.

- White: Mentor of the Meek (68 games, 63%, rare, taken at 4.4), Slayer of the Wicked (163 games, 60%), Elite Inquisitor (76 games, 59%, rare), Rally the Peasants (129 games, 59%, taken at 7.0), Midnight Haunting (153 games, 58%), Fiend Hunter, Divine Reckoning, Smite the Monstrous.

- Colourless: Manor Gargoyle (82 games, 56%, rare), Galvanic Juggernaut (202 games, 54%, taken at 1.2; only average, so do not take it over removal or a strong on-colour card; a UR Juggernaut deck went 0-3 on 2026-11-12, anecdote).
- Lands: Moorland Haunt (44 games, 61%, comes late), Woodland Cemetery in B/G (58 games, 53%), Gavony Township in a long-game white deck (anecdote), Nephalia Drownyard only in a heavy-blue deck (anecdote).
- Bloodline Keeper, Olivia Voldaren, Garruk Relentless and other bombs as first picks: a BG that played Bloodline Keeper lost on 7 Swamps, an unguided UB cut Olivia Voldaren and Balefire Dragon, and Garruk never made a WU deck (anecdotes). Take bombs only if they will be on colour and castable.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Smite the Monstrous, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith, Fiend Hunter, Sensory Deprivation, Corpse Lunge, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Spidery Grasp. Take a second Dead Weight, Victim of Night, Claustrophobia or Brimstone Volley if offered. Aim for five or more removal spells, but removal alone did not save an eight-removal BR or a three-removal BG. Smite the Monstrous hits only power four or more but answered fliers and big blockers well (a WR used it, and a WG won with it and Ghostly Possession; anecdotes). Take removal on colour: first-pick Claustrophobia, Dead Weight and Rebuke all ended up stranded.
2. Strong uncommons and rares: Sever the Bloodline, Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee, Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders, Instigator Gang, Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw, Rally the Peasants.
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Angelic Overseer, Somberwald Spider. Many losing decks were short of fliers or flier answers, though a one-flier WR went 3-0 on a good curve plus removal, and fliers alone lost to a WR holding answers. Aim for about three evasive threats or answers by the end of pack 3, and take a third flier over a second Unruly Mob, a second Festerhide Boar or a fourth ground two-drop. Do not take an off-colour flier over an on-colour one.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Avacyn's Pilgrim, Ashmouth Hound, Feral Ridgewolf, Bump in the Night, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils and Tormented Pariah if red, Silver-Inlaid Dagger, Unruly Mob, Doomed Traveler, Avacynian Priest. Take cheap threats below power four so one removal spell cannot blank your curve. Take them over card-draw filler (Think Twice, Curiosity, Forbidden Alchemy). In a white deck planning Travel Preparations, count humans.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Stitcher's Apprentice, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Ancient Grudge, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Lost in the Mist, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Trepanation Blade, Darkthicket Wolf, Riot Devils unless red is deep, Cloistered Youth over a flier, a second Moment of Heroism, and Makeshift Mauler as a second copy. Naturalize is a sideboard card at best (a WG that maindecked one went 2-1, anecdote, but it is dead against most decks). Armored Skaab is only for a deck with real self-mill. Wooden Stakes is at most a sideboard card. Unburial Rites is a possible grind card (anecdote). Delver of Secrets // Insectile Aberration is marginal. Walking Corpse is acceptable only as an early blocker when nothing better is left. Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell or a dead card. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default (a mono-blue deck ran 16 Islands plus Nephalia Drownyard, anecdote). For a low-curve aggro deck, 16 is worth trying (anecdote). Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 WB, 2-1 WB), 11 Plains/6 Islands (2-1 WU), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG) (anecdotes). Count pips for the heavier colour rather than copying a split. Seven Mountains was too few for a red-heavy WR (1-2, anecdote). A 10 Swamp/7 Island UB still went 0-3, so sources are not the whole story.
- Double pips: give a BB or GG colour 8+ sources where you can. Evidence is mixed (anecdotes): BB cards on 7 Swamps went 0-2 in a 2-1 BG, while a 3-0 BR with Bloodgift Demon ran 7 Swamps. Avoid double pips in both colours. Three GG Somberwald Spiders want 8+ Forests.
- Aim for five or more removal spells, three or four answers to fliers (which can also clear ground blockers), and two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers.
- Keep a flexible late threat or value source where you can (Mentor of the Meek, Gavony Township, Rally the Peasants, Divine Reckoning, Spider Spawning). Decks without one lost many 16-to-62-turn grinds, though a WR without one won four long games on 2026-11-11 (anecdote), so do not cut good cheap cards to fit one. Do not cut Rally the Peasants or Divine Reckoning from white decks for marginal cards. Ground BR aggro stalled in long games against WG (23 to 62 turns) and against blue control; include evasive finishers or card advantage.
- Pair removal with good cheap creatures and close with fliers. Do not pad with ground filler when a flier or removal spell is available. Do not cut your only evasion (Cobbled Wings) for a ground base that cannot pass flying blockers; if you have no real fliers, keep your reach.
- Do not leave yourself with no answer to an opposing colour's key cards: an RG with no black had nothing for Claustrophobia and Victim of Night decks. Enchantment-based removal on your best creature is a real risk; keep multiple threats.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core, but a playable on-colour card like Traitorous Blood or Curse of the Pierced Heart is no reason to restructure (a 3-0 WR kept both). Play the removal and flier answers you drafted; check every sideboard swap against your colours. Do not side out your main removal. Keep at least one one-drop or cheap play main rather than cutting every early creature.
- Include one or two card-advantage or stall-breaking cards (Ghoulcaller's Chant, Moan of the Unhallowed) for stalls (anecdote).
- Do not main Gnaw to the Bone, Mulch, Dream Twist or self-mill cards without a self-mill deck. 1-toughness creatures die to Brimstone Volley and Geistflame. Cut a redundant combat trick before a playable creature.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke, Fiend Hunter and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies.
- Expect long games, often 17 to 40 turns, and up to 62. Break stalls with removal, Spider Spawning, Rally the Peasants and evasive threats.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR; fast starts beat slow hands in 11 to 16 turns (anecdotes: a WR beat a mono-blue and a WG in 13 to 15 turns).
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Angelic Overseer, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Moon Heron, Murder of Crows, Galvanic Juggernaut, Boneyard Wurm and Scourge of Geier Reach.
- Against WU, UB, UR and mono-blue, kill fliers early; Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation and Lost in the Mist will target your best creature, so keep a second threat ready. Blue decks win long grinds, so do not durdle.
- Against WB, expect real removal held for your fliers; do not rely on one big threat.
- Against BG and WG, expect Typhoid Rats, Somberwald Spider, Spidery Grasp, Ghostly Possession, Fiend Hunter and Garruk Relentless to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup and do not flood the board with ground bodies.
- Against WR, RG and BR, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Furor of the Bitten and Pitchburn Devils to punish small creatures; do not overcommit into a sweeper and keep your own fliers alive. WR also runs Rebuke, Smite the Monstrous, Midnight Haunting, Fiend Hunter, Slayer of the Wicked, Rally the Peasants and Bonds of Faith, and will answer your fliers.
- Against white decks with Fiend Hunter and Avacynian Priest, expect one-for-one trades on early creatures; prefer threats that leave value behind. Expect Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning from white decks in long games.
- WU and UB should keep cheap two-drops and the flier clock even when a stall looks likely, and apply pressure early rather than waiting (anecdote).
