# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-10's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Twenty-one nights: 168 decks, 619 games. Guided seats are 255-274 in games (48%, 103-113 in matches); unguided are 351-333 (51%, 143-133 in matches); the conventional guide is 13-12 (52%, 6-6 in matches, 4 decks). On the two nights with all three kinds of seat, guided went 5-20 in games (2-10 in matches), conventional 13-12 and unguided 35-21 (62%; 16-8 in matches). That is only two nights, but it points the same way each time: this guide has not been shown to help, and it may be hurting. Treat archetype lines as hypotheses and lean on the card numbers, and on the plain advice that is consistent across lessons: commit to two colours early, count playables, have fliers and removal.

Pairs by games won (decks, matches, game win rate):
- BR: 55% (123 games, 17 decks, 32-19 in matches). The best pair. A guided BR with eight removal spells went 0-3 in long grinds earlier, because its ground bodies could not close (anecdote); BR needs fliers or real finishers as well as removal. An unguided BR went 2-1 on 2026-11-10 (anecdote) off red picks like Galvanic Juggernaut and Into the Maw of Hell, but its two main Wooden Stakes were dead against a Human/Spirit WG deck and it lost 0-2 to it.
- BG: 54% (174 games, 24 decks, 40-32 in matches). Needs evasion or removal-backed finishers and cheap creatures. Somberwald Spider and Spider Spawning beat ground decks. A BG with Corpse Lunge, Dead Weight and Tribute to Hunger went 1-2 on 2026-11-10 (anecdote), 0-2 against a WU with Stitched Drake and Angel of Flight Alabaster. Count fliers and flier answers.
- WR: 53% (129 games, 18 decks, 28-26 in matches). A guided WR went 1-2 on 2026-11-10 (anecdote) with only two fliers (Abbey Griffin, Spectral Rider), no flexible late threat, and lost two 17-24 turn grinds after beating WU 2-0 in 12 turns. WR humans also beat a Moon Heron/Stitched Drake WU 0-2 in 12 turns, so fast low-curve decks are real. Play Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning.
- WU: 51% (169 games, 23 decks, 34-35 in matches). White removal plus real fliers works; filler does not. On 2026-11-10 two WU decks went 2-1 (unguided, built on blue depth: Stitched Drake, Forbidden Alchemy, Armored Skaab) and 1-2 (conventional guide, which wasted early picks Brimstone Volley, Darkthicket Wolf, Prey Upon and Riot Devils off-colour) (anecdotes). It needs a lower curve than "just fliers", as WR humans ran over it.
- WG: 50% (121 games, 17 decks, 27-24 in matches). 2026-11-10 showed the spread: an unguided WG went 3-0 (anecdote) on Fiend Hunter, Bonds of Faith, Spidery Grasp, Slayer of the Wicked and late value (Gavony Township, Mentor of the Meek, Grizzled Outcasts), going 6-0 in games in 20-to-34-turn games. A guided WG went 0-3 (anecdote) with Avacyn's Pilgrim, Thraben Sentry, Orchard Spirit and two Somberwald Spiders, out-fliered by Chapel Geist, Moon Heron and Stitched Drake. Same pair, different result: removal creatures and late value, not cheap ground bodies.
- RG: 51% (68 games, 9 decks, 14-13 in matches). Four removal spells and cheap green bodies went 2-1 earlier (anecdote), but it had no answer to Claustrophobia and Victim of Night. Needs ways to reach fliers.
- UB: 47% (168 games, 22 decks, 28-38 in matches). Below average, and variable (an unguided 3-0 with fliers, two Claustrophobia and Victim of Night; a 1-2 with tempo 2-drops and thin top end; a conventional-guide UB with Armored Skaab went 2-1, anecdotes). Needs cheap removal, blockers and enough finishers. Do not take Delver of Secrets repeatedly or Dream Twist.
- UR: 45% (119 games, 16 decks, 20-28 in matches). Needs a closer. Take Stitched Drake and Moon Heron over fillers like Trepanation Blade.
- WB: 45% (105 games, 14 decks, 19-23 in matches). Below average. Needs cheap blockers and enough sources for both colours.
- Mono-colour and others: U 2-4 in matches (14 games), R 3-3 (16 games), UG 1-5 (16 games). Stay two colours.
- White is the best-supported colour on individual cards (Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Midnight Haunting, Bonds of Faith). Cheap white removal (Rebuke, Fiend Hunter) is contested, so take it early.
- Black is crowded. Take black removal early in pack 1 rather than waiting for Victim of Night in pack 3.
- Read what is flowing after pack 1. On 2026-11-10 an unguided drafter noted white-green going 3-0 while his BG went 1-2; red was open for the BR seat. If strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8, move in.
- Commit: main colour by about pick 5, a pair by mid pack 1 to mid pack 2. Off-colour first picks and hedges were wasted repeatedly (Claustrophobia stranded in WG, Garruk Relentless never played in WU, Brimstone Volley and Darkthicket Wolf in WU; anecdotes). Do not leave a colour for one early removal spell unless the new colour is clearly open.
- Evasion plus removal breaks stalls. Games stall for 11 to 55 turns; 2026-11-10's were mostly 17 to 37.
- Splashing is poor. Don't run a colour on six basics unless it is a light second colour with excellent depth in the main colour (anecdotes).

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Smite the Monstrous, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith, Fiend Hunter, Sensory Deprivation, Corpse Lunge, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Spidery Grasp. Take a second Dead Weight, Victim of Night, Claustrophobia or Brimstone Volley if offered. Aim for five or more removal spells, but removal alone did not save an eight-removal BR or a three-removal BG. Take hard removal over tempo 2-drops if the table has big green threats (Boneyard Wurm, Scourge of Geier Reach). Take it as an on-colour pick: a first-pick Claustrophobia stranded in WG.
2. Strong uncommons and rares: Sever the Bloodline, Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee, Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders, Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw, Rally the Peasants.
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Angelic Overseer, Somberwald Spider. Three losing decks on 2026-11-10 (WR, BG, WG) had too few fliers. Aim for three evasive threats or a removal-backed finisher by the end of pack 3, and take a third flier over a fourth ground two-drop or late ground filler. Take a second flier or Spider Spawning over Walking Corpse. Do not take an off-colour flier over an on-colour one.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Avacyn's Pilgrim, Ashmouth Hound, Feral Ridgewolf, Bump in the Night, Unruly Mob, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils and Tormented Pariah if red, Silver-Inlaid Dagger. A curve wins fast games (a WR beat WU 2-0 in 12 turns) but loses long ones without fliers or a late threat.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Stitcher's Apprentice, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Ancient Grudge, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Lost in the Mist, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Trepanation Blade, Darkthicket Wolf, Riot Devils unless red is deep, Cloistered Youth over a flier, and Makeshift Mauler as a second copy. Armored Skaab is only for a deck with real self-mill: it was dead weight in one WU but a UB with it went 2-1 and a WU with it went 2-1 (anecdotes), so it is marginal. Wooden Stakes: one BR main-decked two and lost 0-2 to Humans and Spirits, while a BR that beat a WU 2-1 ran two (anecdotes); at most a sideboard card. Unburial Rites is a possible grind card (one lesson, anecdote). Delver of Secrets // Insectile Aberration is marginal. Walking Corpse is acceptable only as an early blocker when nothing better is left. Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell or a dead card. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default. For a low-curve aggro deck, 16 is worth trying (anecdote). Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 WB earlier, 2-1 WB), 11 Plains/6 Islands (2-1 WU), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG) (all anecdotes). Count pips for the heavier colour rather than copying a split. Seven Mountains was too few for a red-heavy WR (1-2, anecdote).
- Double pips: give a BB or GG colour 8+ sources where you can. Evidence is mixed (anecdotes): BB cards on 7 Swamps went 0-2 in a 2-1 BG, while a 3-0 BR with Bloodgift Demon ran 7 Swamps. Treat 8 as the safe target, and avoid double pips in both colours. Three GG Somberwald Spiders want 8+ Forests.
- Aim for five or more removal spells, four or more answers to fliers (that can also clear ground blockers), and at least two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers. The 3-0 WG ran removal creatures (Fiend Hunter, Slayer of the Wicked) plus Bonds of Faith and Spidery Grasp.
- Keep one flexible late threat or finisher or value source (Mentor of the Meek, Gavony Township, Rally the Peasants, Divine Reckoning, Spider Spawning). Decks without one lost 16-to-55-turn grinds repeatedly (anecdotes), including 2026-11-10's WR. Take it over a cheap Thraben Sentry if you must choose. Do not cut Rally the Peasants or Divine Reckoning from white decks for marginal cards.
- Pair removal with good cheap creatures and close with fliers. Do not pad with ground filler (Rotting Fensnake) when a flier or removal spell is available.
- Do not leave yourself with no answer to an opposing colour's key cards: an RG with no black had nothing for Claustrophobia and Victim of Night decks. Enchantment-based removal on your best creature is a real risk; keep multiple threats rather than a board of equals.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core. Play the removal and flier answers you drafted; check every sideboard swap against your colours. Do not side out your main removal.
- Include one or two card-advantage or stall-breaking cards (Ghoulcaller's Chant, Moan of the Unhallowed) for stalls (anecdote).
- Do not main Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone, Mulch, Dream Twist or self-mill cards without a self-mill deck. 1-toughness creatures die to Brimstone Volley and Geistflame.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke, Fiend Hunter and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies.
- Expect long games, often 17 to 40 turns, and up to 55. Break stalls with removal, Spider Spawning, Rally the Peasants and evasive threats.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR; fast starts beat slow hands in 11 to 15 turns (anecdotes, including WR humans beating a WU 0-2 in 12 turns).
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Angelic Overseer, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Moon Heron, Murder of Crows, Galvanic Juggernaut, Boneyard Wurm and Scourge of Geier Reach.
- Against WU, UB and UR, kill fliers early; Claustrophobia and Sensory Deprivation will target your best creature, so keep a second threat ready.
- Against WB, expect real removal held for your fliers; do not rely on one big threat.
- Against BG and WG, expect Typhoid Rats and Somberwald Spider to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup and do not flood the board with ground bodies.
- Against WR, RG and BR, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame and Blasphemous Act to punish small creatures; do not overcommit into a sweeper and keep your own fliers alive.
- Against white decks with Fiend Hunter and Avacynian Priest, expect one-for-one trades on early creatures; prefer threats that leave value behind. Expect Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning from white decks in long games.
- WU and UB should keep cheap two-drops and the flier clock even when a stall looks likely (anecdote).
