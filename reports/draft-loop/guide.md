# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-08's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Nineteen nights: 152 decks, 566 games. Guided seats are 250-254 in games (50%, 101-103 in matches); unguided are 316-312 (50%, 127-125 in matches). Guided seats have not beaten unguided seats, so the guide has not been shown to help. Treat archetype lines as hypotheses and lean on the card numbers.

- BR: 59% of games (109 games, 15 decks, 30-15 in matches). Best pair by a clear margin. Tonight a guided BR went 3-0 (6-1 in games, anecdote) on Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Into the Maw of Hell, Curse of the Pierced Heart and Sever the Bloodline, with Typhoid Rats, Ashmouth Hound and two Feral Ridgewolf as early bodies. Cheap removal plus cheap bodies plus a few finishers. BR also beat both BG decks tonight.
- BG: 54% (167 games, 23 decks, 39-30 in matches). Needs evasion or removal-backed finishers and cheap creatures. Somberwald Spider and Spider Spawning beat ground decks. Tonight two guided BGs went 2-1 each (anecdotes) and both lost to BR: one with only three real removal spells, one with only Falkenrath Noble as a flier, and a BB deck (Bloodline Keeper, Screeching Bat) on 7 Swamps.
- WR: 54% (109 games, 15 decks, 24-21 in matches). Cheap bodies, cheap removal and fliers, but it needs one flexible late card. Tonight an unguided WR went 1-2 (anecdote) on seven Mountains with a heavy red package and had cut Divine Reckoning and Rally the Peasants; both losses ran 17 to 32 turns.
- WU: 51% (150 games, 20 decks, 30-30 in matches). White removal plus real fliers works (an earlier 2-1 on Chapel Geist, Rebuke, Sensory Deprivation, Invisible Stalker, anecdote); filler such as Delver of Secrets, Fortress Crab and Creepy Doll does not (0-3, anecdote).
- WG: 50% (103 games, 14 decks, 22-20 in matches). A WG that cut Dead Weight and Corpse Lunge for filler went 0-3 (anecdote). Removal count matters.
- RG: 50% (60 games, 8 decks, 12-12 in matches). Needs ways to reach fliers (UR's Moon Heron, Murder of Crows and Stitched Drake beat it). When red is deep, a red-heavy deck with a light green side works (anecdotes).
- UB: 45% (148 games, 19 decks, 22-35 in matches). Tonight an unguided UB went 0-3 (3-6 in games, anecdote): early Balefire Dragon and Olivia Voldaren picks were cut, and every match went 1-2. Needs cheap blockers, removal and a stall-breaker (Moan of the Unhallowed, Murder of Crows). Do not take Delver of Secrets repeatedly.
- UR: 45% (119 games, 16 decks, 20-28 in matches). Tonight a UR went 1-2 (2-5 in games, anecdote), with no closer in 16 to 26-turn losses to BR and WR, and WR's Fiend Hunter and Avacynian Priest traded with its early creatures. Take Stitched Drake and Moon Heron over fillers like Trepanation Blade.
- WB: 45% (105 games, 14 decks, 19-23 in matches). Below average, but tonight a guided WB went 2-1 (4-4 in games, anecdote) on two Dead Weight, Victim of Night, Urgent Exorcism, Ghostly Possession and cheap white bodies, losing to a BG deck. Earlier WBs failed from slow starts and too few Swamps. Needs cheap blockers and enough sources for both colours.
- Mono-colour and others: U 2-4 in matches (14 games; tonight's mono-blue went 1-2 with Dead Weight, Smite the Monstrous, two Rebuke and Brimstone Volley left out, anecdote), R 3-3 (16 games), UG 1-5 (16 games). Stay two colours.
- White is well supported on individual cards. Cheap white removal (Rebuke) is contested, so take it early.
- Count playables before committing: pick a main colour by about pick 5 and a pair by mid pack 1 to mid pack 2. Do not leave a colour for one early removal spell unless the new colour is clearly open. If strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8, move in.
- Expect rivals to share commons (Victim of Night, Morkrut Banshee, Walking Corpse); take removal early. Blue staples are contested too.
- Evasion plus removal breaks stalls. Games stall for 13 to 55 turns.
- Splashing is poor. Don't run a colour on six basics unless it is a light second colour with excellent depth in the main colour (a 3-0 RG had 6 Forest, anecdote; an earlier RG on six Forests went 3-5 in games, anecdote).

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Smite the Monstrous, Bonds of Faith, Sensory Deprivation, Corpse Lunge, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre. Take a second Dead Weight, Victim of Night or Brimstone Volley if offered. Aim for five or more removal spells; BG and WG decks with three or four lost to BR (anecdotes). Take removal before Silverchase Fox or Abbey Griffin.
2. Strong uncommons and rares: Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee, Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders, Slayer of the Wicked, Fiend Hunter, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw.
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Somberwald Spider. Aim for three evasive threats or a removal-backed finisher by the end of pack 3. Take a second flier or Spider Spawning over Walking Corpse. Do not take an off-colour flier over an on-colour one.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Ashmouth Hound, Feral Ridgewolf, Bump in the Night, Unruly Mob, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils and Tormented Pariah if red, Silver-Inlaid Dagger. Early blockers matter: slow starts lost to BR, RG and WR (anecdotes).

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Armored Skaab, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Wooden Stakes, Ancient Grudge, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Lost in the Mist, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Festerhide Boar, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Trepanation Blade, Cloistered Youth over a flier, Unburial Rites, and Makeshift Mauler as a second copy. Delver of Secrets // Insectile Aberration is marginal. Walking Corpse is acceptable only as an early blocker when nothing better is left; a sturdier body like Villagers of Estwald is better. Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell or a dead card. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician. Card-selection spells did not save a UB deck; take a faster curve.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default. For a low-curve aggro deck, 16 is worth trying (anecdote). Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 WB earlier, 2-1 WB tonight), 11 Plains/6 Islands (2-1 WU), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG) (all anecdotes). Count pips for the heavier colour rather than copying a split. Seven Mountains was too few for a red-heavy WR (1-2, anecdote).
- Double pips: give a BB or GG colour 8+ sources where you can. Evidence is mixed: BB cards (Bloodline Keeper, Screeching Bat) on 7 Swamps went 0-2 in a 2-1 BG, a WB on 8 Swamps went 3-5 in games earlier, a WB with Victim of Night and Morkrut Banshee on 5 Swamps went 2-1, and a 3-0 BR with Bloodgift Demon and Ghoulraiser ran 7 Swamps (all anecdotes). Treat 8 as the safe target, and avoid double pips in both colours. Three GG Somberwald Spiders want 8+ Forests.
- Aim for five or more removal spells, four or more answers to fliers (that can also clear ground blockers), and at least two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers. Keep Prey Upon and Urgent Exorcism-type answers for opposing fliers.
- Keep one flexible late threat or finisher. Decks without one lost 16-to-55-turn grinds repeatedly (anecdotes). Do not cut Rally the Peasants or Divine Reckoning-type cards from WR for marginal ones.
- Removal alone is not enough: decks with plenty of removal still lost for lack of cheap, sturdy creatures. Pair removal with good cheap creatures (the 3-0 BR did exactly this), and do not cut cheap blockers for a marginal fourth flier.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core. Play the removal and flier answers you drafted; check every sideboard swap against your colours. Do not side out your main removal (Grasp of Phantoms sided out, 0-3 UB, anecdote).
- Main fliers over filler such as Riot Devils (unless red is your main colour and deep), Walking Corpse or a marginal fourth creature.
- Include one or two card-advantage or stall-breaking cards (Ghoulcaller's Chant, Moan of the Unhallowed) for stalls (anecdote).
- Do not main Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone or Mulch. 1-toughness creatures die to Brimstone Volley and Geistflame.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke, Fiend Hunter and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies.
- Expect long games, often 20 to 40 turns, and up to 52. Break stalls with removal, Spider Spawning and evasive threats.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR burn decks; fast starts beat slow hands in 11 to 15 turns (anecdotes, including a 14-turn BG loss to BR tonight).
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Angelic Overseer, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Moon Heron, Murder of Crows, Galvanic Juggernaut and Scourge of Geier Reach.
- Against WU, UB and UR, kill fliers early; Claustrophobia and Sensory Deprivation will target your best creature.
- Against WB, expect real removal held for your fliers; do not rely on one big threat.
- Against BG and WG, expect Typhoid Rats and Somberwald Spider to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup and do not flood the board with ground bodies.
- Against WR, RG and BR, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame and Blasphemous Act to punish small creatures; do not overcommit into a sweeper and keep your own fliers alive.
- Against white decks with Fiend Hunter and Avacynian Priest, expect one-for-one trades on early creatures; prefer threats that leave value behind.
- WU and UB should keep cheap two-drops and the flier clock even when a stall looks likely (anecdote).
