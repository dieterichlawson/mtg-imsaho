# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-17's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Twenty-eight nights: 224 decks, 828 games. Guided seats are 308-327 in games (49%, 125-133 in matches, 86 decks). The conventional guide is 62-64 (49%, 26-28 in matches, 18 decks). Unguided seats are 458-437 (51%, 185-175 in matches, 120 decks). On the nine nights with all three kinds of seat, guided went 58-73 in games (44%, 24-30 in matches), conventional 62-64 (49%) and unguided 142-125 (53%). Guided is still behind both, and this guide has not been shown to help. Treat archetype lines as hypotheses and lean on card numbers and on advice that is consistent across lessons:
- Commit to two colours early (by mid pack 1). Stranded early picks were the most repeated lesson. On 2026-11-17 a guided WR never played its picks 2-3 (Tribute to Hunger, Typhoid Rats), a UG never played its first-pick Sever the Bloodline, and a BR spent mid-pack picks on off-colour Curiosity and Spare from Evil (anecdotes).
- Count playables and keep a curve of cheap creatures. Games run long (13 to 34 turns on 2026-11-17), so cheap creatures need a way to finish.
- Take removal, but only in your colours.
- Have two or three real threats that win in the air or in a long game. Fliers alone do not suffice: on 2026-11-17 a guided 0-3 WR with Voiceless Spirit, Spectral Rider, Falkenrath Marauders and Scourge of Geier Reach lost in 13 to 24 turns (anecdote). But decks short of fliers lost to fliers too (an RG with two Orchard Spirits lost to Moon Heron and Murder of Crows; a BG lost 0-2 to Stitched Drake and Abbey Griffin; anecdotes).

Pairs by games won (decks, matches, game win rate), from the statistics up to 2026-11-17:
- WR: 54% (200 games, 27 decks, 44-37 in matches). Still among the best pairs, but on 2026-11-17 the two guided WR decks went 0-3 and 2-1 (anecdotes). The 0-3 lost to WU and BR with ground filler (an extra Unruly Mob); the 2-1 beat RG with cheap white creatures plus Rebuke, Smite the Monstrous and Brimstone Volley but lost 0-2 to WU, having only two or three fliers (Chapel Geist, Midnight Haunting, Spectral Rider). Core: cheap creatures (Ashmouth Hound, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Avacynian Priest), removal (Rebuke, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith, Smite the Monstrous), Rally the Peasants, Furor of the Bitten and real fliers (Voiceless Spirit, Midnight Haunting, Chapel Geist). Take a third or fourth flier or a second removal spell over ground filler, and give red enough Mountains.
- BG: 54% (210 games, 29 decks, 48-39 in matches). Best by matches among big pairs. On 2026-11-17 a conventional BG went 2-1 on Dead Weight, Corpse Lunge, Moan of the Unhallowed and Morkrut Banshee but lost 0-2 to WU fliers with few answers (anecdote). Earlier a low-curve green-based BG went 3-0 (Ambush Viper, Kindercatch, Lumberknot, Moldgraf Monstrosity), and a top-heavy BG went 0-3. Take cheap removal and early blockers over a fourth fatty, and keep a flier answer.
- RG: 54% (92 games, 12 decks, 20-16 in matches). Small sample. On 2026-11-17 a conventional RG went 1-2 with only two Orchard Spirits for evasion, one Ranger's Guile and no Moment of Heroism, losing to UG fliers and to WR removal (anecdote). Earlier an unguided RG went 3-0 on burn and fight (Prey Upon, Brimstone Volley, Geistflame). Needs cheap blockers, ways to reach fliers, and an answer to Claustrophobia and Victim of Night.
- WU: 52% (207 games, 28 decks, 43-41 in matches). On 2026-11-17 an unguided WU went 3-0 and 6-0 in games with white-based fliers and humans, Claustrophobia, Smite the Monstrous and Galvanic Juggernaut on 9 Plains/8 Islands, cutting Delver of Secrets and weak auras; it beat both guided WR decks (anecdote). Earlier an unguided WU with Lost in the Mist went 1-2 in slow games. WU is not to be dismissed: white removal plus real cheap fliers (Midnight Haunting, Invisible Stalker, Chapel Geist, Abbey Griffin, Stitched Drake, Moorland Haunt) works. Take evasive threats over more interaction.
- WG: 51% (156 games, 22 decks, 35-31 in matches). Swings with the build. Earlier a guided WG with four fliers went 0-3 on 9 Forest/7 Plains with early picks never played. Needs cheap plays and a 10-source main colour (anecdote).
- BR: 49% (188 games, 26 decks, 41-37 in matches). On 2026-11-17 an unguided BR went 1-2 (Dead Weight, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Corpse Lunge) and lost 0-2 each to RG and UB in 19 to 24 turn stalls, though cheap creatures beat WR (anecdote). Earlier ground BRs went 0-3. BR wants early drops, removal and evasive finishers like Falkenrath Noble. Skip Ancient Grudge and Heartless Summoning.
- UR: 46% (149 games, 20 decks, 27-33 in matches). Weak overall, but an unguided red-first UR with Geistflame, Rage Thrower and six Islands plus Sulfur Falls went 3-0 on 2026-11-14 (anecdote). Make UR red-first with burn.
- UB: 45% (212 games, 28 decks, 33-51 in matches). Worst big pair. On 2026-11-17 an unguided self-mill UB with Curse of Death's Hold and Lost in the Mist went 1-2 with 17 to 23 turn losses; it had taken two Delver of Secrets and a second Curse of the Bloody Tome and played none (anecdote). UB needs a low curve, cheap blockers and cheap pressure (Falkenrath Noble). Do not take Delver of Secrets or Dream Twist.
- WB: 45% (143 games, 19 decks, 27-30 in matches). Below average and unproven. Count BB pips, and take a second threat over a fourth removal spell.
- UG: 46% (46 games, 6 decks, 7-11 in matches). Weak overall. On 2026-11-17 an unguided UG went 2-1, built on Lumberknot, Makeshift Mauler, Moon Heron and Murder of Crows, and lost 0-2 to BG in a 34-turn grind (anecdote). Mono U is 4-5 in matches (21 games) and mono R 3-3 (16 games).

General notes:
- White is the best-supported colour on individual cards (Slayer of the Wicked, Midnight Haunting, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Bonds of Faith). Red also has many high-win-rate cards (Falkenrath Marauders, Furor of the Bitten, Into the Maw of Hell, Stromkirk Noble, Kruin Outlaw, Ashmouth Hound). Green has Moldgraf Monstrosity, Lumberknot, Gatstaf Shepherd and Villagers of Estwald. Cheap white removal (Rebuke, Fiend Hunter) is contested, so take it early.
- Black is crowded. Take black removal early in pack 1, but only if you will play black. Black is a safe anchor when blue and red are thin (anecdote).
- Read what is flowing after pack 1. If strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8, move in. Judge by real playables.
- Commit: main colour by about pick 5, a pair by mid pack 1, your second colour by pick 5 of pack 2 at the latest. Off-colour early picks were wasted repeatedly (anecdotes): on 2026-11-17 Tribute to Hunger and Typhoid Rats in a WR, Sever the Bloodline and Trepanation Blade in a UG, Curiosity and Spare from Evil in a BR; on 2026-11-16 a first-pick Olivia Voldaren in a WU; on 2026-11-15 Into the Maw of Hell and Furor of the Bitten in a WB; earlier Claustrophobia, Garruk Relentless, a first-pick Fiend Hunter and others. Do not leave a colour for one early card, bomb or not, unless the new colour is clearly open. If you take a bomb, stay in its colour.
- Colourless playables are a reasonable early hedge: Galvanic Juggernaut was taken second in pack 1 and stayed in the 3-0 WU while off-colour cards were cut (anecdote). It is still overdrafted and exposed to Smite the Monstrous.
- Games run 11 to 62 turns, mostly 15 to 37. Grindy removal and card advantage matter, and cheap starts only win if you can close.
- Splashing is poor. Do not run a colour on six basics unless it is a light second colour with a dual land and few cards.

## Commons that win

Deck game win rate for decks running the card (statistics to 2026-11-17).

- Green: Villagers of Estwald (235 games, 55%), Woodland Sleuth (230 games, 55%), Grave Bramble (191 games, 54%, taken at 8.9), Avacyn's Pilgrim (122 games, 53%, taken at 9.2), Festerhide Boar (279 games, 53%, filler; take a flier or cheap removal over it), Kindercatch (280 games, 53%), Spidery Grasp (270 games, 53%, taken at 8.4), Prey Upon (260 games, 53%, taken at 2.8). Ambush Viper and Darkthicket Wolf were dependable (anecdotes). Somberwald Spider is a flier answer.
- Black: Typhoid Rats (303 games, 55%), Victim of Night (53% earlier, taken at 1.6; it cannot target Vampires, Werewolves or Zombies, so check the opponent), Dead Weight, Corpse Lunge, Bump in the Night, Ghoulcaller's Chant.
- White: Bonds of Faith (326 games, 55%), Avacynian Priest (330 games, 55%), Voiceless Spirit (393 games, 54%), Thraben Sentry (260 games, 53%, taken at 7.6), Unruly Mob (283 games, 53%, taken at 9.0; one is fine, a second lost a WR on 2026-11-17), Selfless Cathar, Chapel Geist, Silverchase Fox (ground filler).
- Red: Furor of the Bitten (154 games, 58%, taken at 9.7; a pump, not removal), Ashmouth Hound (280 games, 56%), Tormented Pariah, Pitchburn Devils, Night Revelers, Geistflame, Brimstone Volley, Curse of the Pierced Heart and Traitorous Blood (marginal playables).
- Blue: Spectral Flight (260 games, 53%), Hysterical Blindness (late pick), Frightful Delusion (anecdote).
- Colourless: Silver-Inlaid Dagger (53% earlier, uncommon). Blazing Torch was praised twice (anecdotes).
- Claustrophobia, Rebuke, Dead Weight, Tribute to Hunger, Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Sensory Deprivation and Stitched Drake are praised in lessons and go early, but only on colour.

## Uncommons and rares

- Green: Moldgraf Monstrosity (63 games, 59%, rare, taken at 4.3), Lumberknot (184 games, 57%), Gatstaf Shepherd (192 games, 56%), Spider Spawning (162 games, 55%), Hollowhenge Scavenger (140 games, 54%), Hamlet Captain (185 games, 53%), Boneyard Wurm (dies to Smite the Monstrous; do not take a second). Spider Spawning needs self-mill enablers, and enablers need it. Garruk Relentless carried a 2-1 BG (anecdote).
- Black: Disciple of Griselbrand (133 games, 56%, taken at 9.1), Morkrut Banshee (218 games, 53%, taken at 1.7; BB on 7 Swamps went poorly), Bloodgift Demon, Sever the Bloodline (rare, 70% earlier in 46 games), Falkenrath Noble (carried a UB win), Crossway Vampire (a third is filler), Curse of Death's Hold (slow in a 1-2 UB). Abattoir Ghoul and Ghoulraiser went 1-2 in a WB (anecdote).
- Red: Falkenrath Marauders (57 games, 61%, rare, taken at 2.2), Into the Maw of Hell (221 games, 57%, taken at 3.4), Stromkirk Noble (74 games, 57%, rare), Kruin Outlaw (89 games, 55%, rare), Rage Thrower, Rakish Heir, Skirsdag Cultist, Hanweir Watchkeep (146 games, 53%), Instigator Gang, Vampire Interloper.
- White: Slayer of the Wicked (201 games, 60%, taken at 1.7), Midnight Haunting (183 games, 60%), Mentor of the Meek (81 games, 59%, rare), Elite Inquisitor (83 games, 55%, rare), Rally the Peasants (173 games, 54%, taken at 6.7), Angelic Overseer, Fiend Hunter, Divine Reckoning, Smite the Monstrous (kills power-4 creatures; took part in the 3-0 WU and a 2-1 WR, anecdotes). Cloistered Youth is only average.
- Blue: Murder of Crows (only if blue), Invisible Stalker, Stitched Drake, Mindshrieker (72 games, 53%, rare), Battleground Geist (204 games, 53%), Moon Heron and Orchard Spirit. Blue fliers beat ground decks on 2026-11-17 (Stitched Drake, Abbey Griffin, Moon Heron, Murder of Crows; anecdotes) but lost earlier to Somberwald Spider and Victim of Night.
- Colourless: Manor Gargoyle (95 games, 56%, rare, taken at 2.2). Galvanic Juggernaut (255 games, 54%, taken at 1.2) is overdrafted: do not take it over removal or a strong on-colour card, and do not rely on it alone to close. Smite the Monstrous hits it.
- Lands: Moorland Haunt (63% earlier in 51 games, comes late), Sulfur Falls (60 games, 55%, in blue-red decks), Woodland Cemetery in B/G, Gavony Township in a long-game white deck (anecdote).
- Bombs: Bloodline Keeper lost on 7 Swamps in a BG and a WB and was the only finisher in a 1-2 WB. Garruk Relentless never made a WU deck but won in a BG. Olivia Voldaren was first-picked and never played in a WU. Take bombs only if they will be on colour and castable, and back them with a second threat.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal on colour: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia (blue), Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Smite the Monstrous, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith, Fiend Hunter, Sensory Deprivation (blue), Corpse Lunge, Harvest Pyre, Spidery Grasp, Bramblecrush, Blazing Torch. Take a second if offered, but aim for about five; a fourth removal spell lost to a second threat in a WB (anecdote). Do not take removal outside the colours you are likely to play. Red removal is contested by other BR drafters.
2. Strong uncommons and rares on colour: Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Moldgraf Monstrosity, Sever the Bloodline, Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee (if you can support BB), Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders, Instigator Gang, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw, Rally the Peasants, Midnight Haunting, Angelic Overseer, Garruk Relentless (green only).
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Invisible Stalker, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Mindshrieker, Battleground Geist, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Somberwald Spider. Many losing decks lacked fliers or answers (an RG, a BG and a WR with two or three on 2026-11-17), but several decks with three or four fliers also lost (WR, WG, UB), so fliers do not replace cheap plays and removal. Aim for three or four evasive threats or answers by the end of pack 3, and take a third flier over a second Unruly Mob, Silverchase Fox, Thraben Purebloods, Festerhide Boar or Gatstaf Shepherd. Do not take an off-colour flier over an on-colour one. Against green, count reach.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Avacyn's Pilgrim, Ambush Viper, Kindercatch, Darkthicket Wolf (in green), Ashmouth Hound, Feral Ridgewolf, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils and Tormented Pariah if red, Silver-Inlaid Dagger, Unruly Mob (one), Doomed Traveler, Avacynian Priest, Vampire Interloper, Bump in the Night. Take cheap threats and early evasive creatures so one removal spell cannot blank your curve, and take them over card-draw filler, counterspells and bounce. Against WR, prefer cheap creatures to a second four-power body (Smite the Monstrous).

Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell or card draw. Walking Corpse made a 3-0 BG and a 0-3 BR (both anecdotes), so it is only a 23rd card. Galvanic Juggernaut is an acceptable early colourless hedge but not over removal.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Stitcher's Apprentice, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Ancient Grudge, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Bloody Tome (a second was taken and cut), Lost in the Mist, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab over a better creature (keep it only as a cheap blocker), Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Bloodcrazed Neonate, Make a Wish, Grimoire of the Dead, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Trepanation Blade, Delver of Secrets (taken twice and never played on 2026-11-17), Heartless Summoning, Curiosity, Spare from Evil, Mask of Avacyn over a flier, Riot Devils, Kessig Wolf, Cloistered Youth over a flier, a second Moment of Heroism, and a second Makeshift Mauler. Naturalize and Wooden Stakes are sideboard cards at best. Armored Skaab, Skaab Goliath and Forbidden Alchemy are only for a deck with Spider Spawning and real self-mill. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default; 16 is untested beyond one anecdote. Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 WB, 2-1 WB), 11 Plains/6 Islands (2-1 WU), 9 Plains/8 Islands (3-0 WU, 6-0 in games), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG), mostly red with 6 Islands plus Sulfur Falls (3-0 UR) (anecdotes). Count pips and give the main colour 9 to 10 sources. A 9 Forest/7 Plains WG went 0-3, and a WR on 6 or 7 Mountains went badly with a heavy red load (anecdotes). Do not give a colour with several double-pip or removal cards only six or seven sources.
- Double pips: give a BB or GG colour 8+ sources or cut them. A WB with Morkrut Banshee and Abattoir Ghoul on 7 Swamps went 2-4 in games, and Bloodline Keeper on 7 Swamps lost; but a 3-0 BR with Bloodgift Demon ran 7 Swamps (anecdotes). Avoid double pips in both colours. Three GG Somberwald Spiders want 8+ Forests. With removal in both colours of a UB, aim for 9/8.
- Aim for about five real removal spells (pumps like Furor of the Bitten do not count), three or four answers to fliers (which can also clear ground blockers), and two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers. Play the removal you drafted.
- Keep a curve with enough cheap plays. The 0-3 BG, 0-3 WR and 0-3 ground BRs were top-heavy or full of ground filler, while winning decks (3-0 BG, 3-0 RG, 3-0 WU, 2-1 WR) had early drops and burn or removal. A low curve alone is not enough: UBs with cheap cards but no early pressure or evasion lost 17 to 26 turn games. Count two-drops and early evasive creatures.
- Keep a flexible late threat or value source (Mentor of the Meek, Moan of the Unhallowed, Gavony Township, Moorland Haunt, Rally the Peasants, Divine Reckoning, Spider Spawning), but do not cut good cheap cards to fit one.
- Pair removal with good cheap creatures and close with fliers. Do not pad with ground filler when a flier or removal spell is available. If you have no real fliers, keep your reach.
- Tricks have a place: an RG with one Ranger's Guile and no Moment of Heroism had its werewolves answered by Rebuke and Smite the Monstrous (anecdote). One or two cheap tricks in a ground deck is fine, after removal and creatures.
- Do not leave yourself with no answer to an opposing colour's key cards: an RG with no black had nothing for Claustrophobia and Victim of Night decks. Enchantment-based removal on your best creature is a real risk; keep multiple threats. Creatures with toughness above one matter against Geistflame, Brimstone Volley and Harvest Pyre. Creatures with power 4+ are exposed to Smite the Monstrous.
- Settle your 23 in the main deck by pack 3. Sideboard plans rarely rescue a deck. Check every sideboard swap against your colours and do not side out main removal or threats for situational cards (a UG sided out Moldgraf Monstrosity and Moon Heron for Maw of the Mire and Mulch and won, one anecdote that does not recommend it). Keep at least one cheap play main.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core. Do not main Gnaw to the Bone, Mulch, Dream Twist, Make a Wish or self-mill cards without a self-mill deck. Cut a redundant combat trick before a playable creature, and cut filler before a proactive creature.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke, Fiend Hunter and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies. Check creature types before counting on Victim of Night (no Vampires, Werewolves or Zombies).
- Expect long games, often 15 to 40 turns, and up to 62. Break stalls with removal, Spider Spawning, Rally the Peasants, Moan of the Unhallowed and evasive threats. A UG lost a 34-turn ground grind; favour Murder of Crows-style evasion over extra ground blockers when the board stalls. Apply pressure early rather than waiting, especially with UB and WU.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR. Fast starts beat slow hands in 9 to 17 turns on some nights (anecdotes), but WR games ran 13 to 27 turns, so pair cheap creatures with late fliers or Rally the Peasants to close.
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Angelic Overseer, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Murder of Crows, Midnight Haunting, Falkenrath Noble, Screeching Bat, Galvanic Juggernaut, Boneyard Wurm and Scourge of Geier Reach.
- Against WU, UB, UR, UG and mono-blue, kill fliers early. Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation and Lost in the Mist will target your best creature, so keep a second threat ready. WU beat two guided WR decks and a BG on 2026-11-17, so respect its fliers and removal. Blue decks win long grinds, so do not durdle and keep a flier answer.
- Against WB, expect real removal (Rebuke, Victim of Night) held for your fliers, plus Angelic Overseer, Chapel Geist and Ghoulraiser value. Do not rely on one big threat.
- Against BG, WG and RG, expect Typhoid Rats, Somberwald Spider, Spidery Grasp, Ghostly Possession, Fiend Hunter, Prey Upon, Garruk Relentless and burn to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup, expect reach, and do not flood the board with ground bodies.
- Against WR, BR and UR, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Furor of the Bitten and Pitchburn Devils to punish small creatures. Do not overcommit and keep your own fliers alive. WR also runs Rebuke (sometimes two), Smite the Monstrous, Midnight Haunting, Fiend Hunter, Slayer of the Wicked, Rally the Peasants and Bonds of Faith, and will answer your fliers, tricked-up creatures and four-power creatures.
- Against white decks with Fiend Hunter and Avacynian Priest, expect one-for-one trades on early creatures; prefer threats that leave value behind. Expect Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning in long games.
