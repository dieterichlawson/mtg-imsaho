# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-04's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Fifteen nights: 120 decks, 444 games. Guided seats are 190-196 in games (49%, 76-80 in matches); unguided are 254-248 (51%, 104-100 in matches). Guided seats have not beaten unguided seats, so the guide has not been shown to help. Treat every line as a hypothesis and lean on the card numbers.

- BR: 56% of games (93 games, 13 decks, 24-15 in matches). Best pair by matches. Black removal plus a few real finishers; tonight an unguided BR deck went 2-1 on two Dead Weight plus cheap red creatures (anecdote), though its 17-land build lost a 27-turn game to WU (anecdote on 16 lands).
- BG: 54% (145 games, 20 decks, 34-26 in matches). Needs evasion or removal-backed finishers and cheap creatures. Tonight's BG deck ran two Markov Patrician, went 3-5 in games and lost 20- and 22-turn games (anecdote).
- WU: 52% (135 games, 18 decks, 28-26 in matches). Fliers, cheap removal and a late threat. Tonight two WU decks went 1-2 and 0-3 (anecdotes); the 0-3 deck had four fliers but ground filler like a second Makeshift Mauler.
- WR 54% (78 games, 11 decks, 18-15). WG 53% (74 games, 10 decks, 17-13 in matches).
- WB: 47% (75 games, 10 decks, 14-16 in matches). It was rated terrible, but it has now gone 3-0, 2-1 and 2-1 tonight (anecdotes) on cheap removal (Victim of Night, Rebuke, Tribute to Hunger, Dead Weight) and fliers (Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit). The 3-0 deck had two Victim of Night, two Chapel Geist, Slayer of the Wicked and Bonds of Faith, 12 Plains and 5 Swamp. Playable when it has plenty of cheap removal and fliers; filler like Walking Corpse is what loses.
- Weak: UR 45% (103 games, 14 decks, 17-25 in matches). UB 44% (93 games, 12 decks, 13-23 in matches); tonight's UB went 2-1 on Stitched Drake, Murder of Crows, Corpse Lunge and Sensory Deprivation (anecdote) but lost 0-2 to WB. RG 42% (38 games, 5 decks, 6-9 in matches); tonight's RG went 1-2 with removal but a weak creature base (anecdote). UG 38% (16 games).
- White is the best-supported colour this week: its fliers and removal were on the winning WB and WG decks. Take them early.
- Evasion plus removal breaks stalls. Games stall for 13 to 55 turns.
- Pick a main colour by about pick 5 and a pair by mid pack 1 to mid pack 2 at the latest. Do not leave a colour for one early removal spell unless the new colour is clearly open: a first-pick Brimstone Volley went unused again tonight, and so did red picks in pack 3 of a BG deck (anecdotes).
- Splashing is poor. Mono-colour is risky (2 mono-red decks 3-3 in matches).
- Read what is passed. If a neighbour is in your colours, move or take contested playables earlier. Commons you pass can come back against you (a BR deck beat an RG drafter with Kruin Outlaw, Hanweir Watchkeep and Ashmouth Hound, anecdote).

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Smite the Monstrous, Bonds of Faith, Brimstone Volley (only if red stays open), Sensory Deprivation, Corpse Lunge, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre. Take a second Dead Weight early.
2. Strong uncommons and rares: Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee, Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders, Slayer of the Wicked, Fiend Hunter, Murder of Crows, Manor Gargoyle, Mentor of the Meek, Kruin Outlaw, Galvanic Juggernaut.
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Moon Heron, Somberwald Spider. Take a few before pack 3, and take one over ground filler. Keep green's Prey Upon over a marginal flier if you are green.
4. Good commons: Typhoid Rats, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Ashmouth Hound, Bump in the Night, Thraben Sentry, Unruly Mob, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Silver-Inlaid Dagger.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Walking Corpse, Armored Skaab, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Lost in the Mist, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Festerhide Boar, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Riot Devils, Cellar Door, Stensia Bloodhall, Makeshift Mauler as a second copy. Delver of Secrets and Darkthicket Wolf are marginal. Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell or a dead card: several drafters this week got nothing from picks 10-14 (Ancient Grudge, Brain Weevil, Traitorous Blood off-colour) and pack 3 playables run thin. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default. For a low-curve aggro deck, 16 is worth trying (anecdote). A 3-0 WB deck ran 12 Plains and 5 Swamp; a 3-0 WG deck ran 10 Forest and 7 Plains (anecdotes).
- Count pips and match basics to them. Avoid double pips in both colours. A 10 Island / 7 Swamp UB deck with few black playables was thin on answers (anecdote).
- Aim for four or more answers to fliers (that can also clear ground blockers) and at least two evasive or removal-backed finishers. A WB deck with one flier went 2-1 but lost a 38-turn game (anecdote), so more is safer.
- Keep one late threat or finisher. Decks without one lost 20-to-55-turn grinds repeatedly (anecdotes).
- Removal alone is not enough: an RG deck with five removal spells went 3-5 in games for lack of a sturdy creature base (anecdote). Pair removal with good cheap creatures.
- Play the removal and flier answers you drafted; check every sideboard swap against your colours.
- Main fliers over filler such as Riot Devils, Walking Corpse or a marginal fourth creature.
- Include one or two card-advantage cards (Ghoulcaller's Chant, Unburial Rites) for stalls (anecdote).
- Do not main Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone or Mulch.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke, Fiend Hunter and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies.
- Expect long games, often 20 to 40 turns. Break stalls with removal, Spider Spawning and evasive threats.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry against BR and RG; fast BR starts beat slow hands in 14 to 15 turns (anecdote).
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Angelic Overseer, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker and Murder of Crows.
- Against WU and UR, kill fliers early; Claustrophobia and Sensory Deprivation will target your best creature.
- Against WB, expect real removal held for your fliers; do not rely on one big threat.
- Against BG, expect Typhoid Rats and Somberwald Spider to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup.
- WU should keep cheap two-drops and its flier clock even when a stall looks likely (anecdote).
