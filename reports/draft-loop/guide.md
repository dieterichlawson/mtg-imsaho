# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-11's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Twenty-two nights: 176 decks, 652 games. Guided seats are 266-279 in games (49%, 108-114 in matches); unguided are 364-354 (51%, 146-142 in matches); the conventional guide is 22-19 (54%, 10-8 in matches, 6 decks). On the three nights with all three kinds of seat, guided went 16-25 in games (39%, 7-11 in matches), conventional 22-19 (54%) and unguided 48-42 (53%). That is only three nights, but it points the same way each time: this guide has not been shown to help. Treat archetype lines as hypotheses and lean on the card numbers and on advice consistent across lessons: commit to two colours early, count playables, have a curve, fliers and removal.

Pairs by games won (decks, matches, game win rate):
- WR: 55% (145 games, 20 decks, 33-27 in matches). Now among the best. On 2026-11-11 a guided WR went 3-0 (anecdote) with Ashmouth Hound, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Rebuke, Into the Maw of Hell and Rally the Peasants, with only one flier (Voiceless Spirit); it cut green, Traitorous Blood and Curse of the Pierced Heart for a clean two-colour core. Another guided WR went 2-1 and won 24-, 21-, 20- and 28-turn games without a late threat, so long games are not automatically lost. But a WR with two Thraben Sentry, two Selfless Cathar and two Unruly Mob and only three fliers lost 1-2 to that 3-0 deck: take a third flier or removal over a second Unruly Mob. Rebuke, Rally the Peasants, Furor of the Bitten and Voiceless Spirit are the cards to take early. Red and white flowed at picks 4-8 of packs 2 and 3 (Pitchburn Devils, Rage Thrower, Rebuke, Bonds of Faith).
- BR: 54% (131 games, 18 decks, 33-21 in matches). Strong by matches. But a BR with only Geistflame and Brimstone Volley as flier answers went 1-2 on 2026-11-11 (anecdote), losing to WR with two Abbey Griffin and to WU with Angel of Flight Alabaster, and Smite the Monstrous exposed its power-four top end (Morkrut Banshee, Falkenrath Noble). Earlier, an eight-removal BR went 0-3 in grinds for lack of finishers (anecdote). It needs fliers or reach and cheaper threats, not just removal. Wooden Stakes is poor.
- BG: 54% (183 games, 25 decks, 41-34 in matches). Needs evasion or removal-backed finishers and cheap creatures. Somberwald Spider and Spider Spawning beat ground decks. On 2026-11-11 an unguided BG went 1-2 (4-5 in games, anecdote) with a fourth top-end fatty and too few cheap plays, losing to WU fliers and WR tricks. Take cheap two-drops and flier answers over more top end.
- WG: 51% (129 games, 18 decks, 29-25 in matches). Results swing with the build. An unguided WG went 3-0 earlier on Fiend Hunter, Bonds of Faith, Spidery Grasp, Slayer of the Wicked and late value (Gavony Township, Mentor of the Meek); a guided WG went 0-3 with cheap ground bodies and was out-fliered. On 2026-11-11 a conventional-guide WG went 2-1 (anecdote) with only Chapel Geist and Voiceless Spirit as fliers and few cheap answers (Blazing Torch, Feeling of Dread). Removal creatures and late value beat cheap ground bodies.
- RG: 51% (68 games, 9 decks, 14-13 in matches). Needs ways to reach fliers and an answer to Claustrophobia and Victim of Night.
- WU: 50% (185 games, 25 decks, 37-38 in matches). Average and often too slow. On 2026-11-11 two WU decks went 2-1 (conventional guide, 4-4 in games, lost 0-2 to WR in a 28-turn grind) and 1-2 (unguided, lost 26- and 24-turn games to WG; anecdotes). White removal plus real cheap fliers works; Think Twice-style filler and a stalled shell do not. Needs a lower curve and early pressure.
- UB: 46% (177 games, 23 decks, 28-41 in matches). Below average. An unguided UB went 0-3 on 2026-11-11 (3-6 in games, anecdote) with marginal fliers (Cobbled Wings, Trepanation Blade), a second Spectral Flight and a second Moon Heron instead of curve creatures, and 10 Swamp/7 Island. Needs a low curve, early creatures and cheap removal. Do not take Delver of Secrets repeatedly or Dream Twist.
- UR: 45% (119 games, 16 decks, 20-28 in matches). Needs a closer. Take Stitched Drake and Moon Heron over fillers like Trepanation Blade.
- WB: 45% (105 games, 14 decks, 19-23 in matches). Below average. Needs cheap blockers and sources for both colours.
- Others: U 2-4 in matches (14 games), R 3-3 (16 games), UG 1-5 (16 games). Stay two colours.
- White is the best-supported colour on individual cards (Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Midnight Haunting, Bonds of Faith). Cheap white removal (Rebuke, Fiend Hunter) is contested, so take it early.
- Black is crowded. Take black removal early in pack 1 rather than waiting for Victim of Night in pack 3.
- Read what is flowing after pack 1. If strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8, move in; on 2026-11-11 a WR seat did this and went 3-0.
- Commit: main colour by about pick 5, a pair by mid pack 1 to mid pack 2. Off-colour first picks and hedges were wasted repeatedly (Claustrophobia stranded in WG, Garruk Relentless never played in WU; anecdotes). Do not leave a colour for one early removal spell unless the new colour is clearly open.
- Evasion plus removal breaks stalls, but a fast curve with removal also won. Games run 11 to 55 turns, mostly 17 to 37.
- Splashing is poor. Do not run a colour on six basics (anecdotes).

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Smite the Monstrous, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith, Fiend Hunter, Sensory Deprivation, Corpse Lunge, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Spidery Grasp. Take a second Dead Weight, Victim of Night, Claustrophobia or Brimstone Volley if offered. Aim for five or more removal spells, but removal alone did not save an eight-removal BR or a three-removal BG. Instant-speed answers like Smite the Monstrous clear blockers; note that it hits only power four or more. Take it as an on-colour pick: a first-pick Claustrophobia stranded in WG.
2. Strong uncommons and rares: Sever the Bloodline, Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee, Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders, Instigator Gang, Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw, Rally the Peasants.
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Angelic Overseer, Somberwald Spider. Many losing decks (BR, WG, WU, BG on 2026-11-11) were short of fliers or flier answers, though a one-flier WR went 3-0 on a good curve plus removal. Aim for about three evasive threats or answers by the end of pack 3, and take a third flier over a second Unruly Mob or a fourth ground two-drop. Do not take an off-colour flier over an on-colour one.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Avacyn's Pilgrim, Ashmouth Hound, Feral Ridgewolf, Bump in the Night, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils and Tormented Pariah if red, Silver-Inlaid Dagger, Unruly Mob. Take cheap threats below power four so one removal spell cannot blank your curve. A curve wins fast games and the 3-0 WR shows it can also win long ones with removal.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Stitcher's Apprentice, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell (a pick-14 waste again), Ancient Grudge, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Lost in the Mist, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Trepanation Blade, Darkthicket Wolf, Riot Devils unless red is deep, Cloistered Youth over a flier, a second Moment of Heroism, and Makeshift Mauler as a second copy. Armored Skaab is only for a deck with real self-mill. Wooden Stakes is at most a sideboard card. Unburial Rites is a possible grind card (anecdote). Delver of Secrets // Insectile Aberration is marginal. Walking Corpse is acceptable only as an early blocker when nothing better is left. Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell or a dead card. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default. For a low-curve aggro deck, 16 is worth trying (anecdote). Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 WB, 2-1 WB), 11 Plains/6 Islands (2-1 WU), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG) (anecdotes). Count pips for the heavier colour rather than copying a split. Seven Mountains was too few for a red-heavy WR (1-2, anecdote). A 10 Swamp/7 Island UB still went 0-3, so sources are not the whole story.
- Double pips: give a BB or GG colour 8+ sources where you can. Evidence is mixed (anecdotes): BB cards on 7 Swamps went 0-2 in a 2-1 BG, while a 3-0 BR with Bloodgift Demon ran 7 Swamps. Avoid double pips in both colours. Three GG Somberwald Spiders want 8+ Forests.
- Aim for five or more removal spells, three or four answers to fliers (which can also clear ground blockers), and two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers.
- Keep a flexible late threat or value source where you can (Mentor of the Meek, Gavony Township, Rally the Peasants, Divine Reckoning, Spider Spawning). Decks without one lost many 16-to-55-turn grinds, though a WR without one won four long games on 2026-11-11 (anecdote), so do not cut good cheap cards to fit one. Do not cut Rally the Peasants or Divine Reckoning from white decks for marginal cards.
- Pair removal with good cheap creatures and close with fliers. Do not pad with ground filler when a flier or removal spell is available. Do not cut your only evasion (Cobbled Wings) for a ground base that cannot pass flying blockers; if you have no real fliers, keep your reach.
- Do not leave yourself with no answer to an opposing colour's key cards: an RG with no black had nothing for Claustrophobia and Victim of Night decks. Enchantment-based removal on your best creature is a real risk; keep multiple threats.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core. Play the removal and flier answers you drafted; check every sideboard swap against your colours. Do not side out your main removal.
- Include one or two card-advantage or stall-breaking cards (Ghoulcaller's Chant, Moan of the Unhallowed) for stalls (anecdote).
- Do not main Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone, Mulch, Dream Twist or self-mill cards without a self-mill deck. 1-toughness creatures die to Brimstone Volley and Geistflame. Cut a redundant combat trick before a playable creature.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke, Fiend Hunter and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies.
- Expect long games, often 17 to 40 turns, and up to 55. Break stalls with removal, Spider Spawning, Rally the Peasants and evasive threats.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR; fast starts beat slow hands in 11 to 15 turns (anecdotes).
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Angelic Overseer, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Moon Heron, Murder of Crows, Galvanic Juggernaut, Boneyard Wurm and Scourge of Geier Reach.
- Against WU, UB and UR, kill fliers early; Claustrophobia and Sensory Deprivation will target your best creature, so keep a second threat ready.
- Against WB, expect real removal held for your fliers; do not rely on one big threat.
- Against BG and WG, expect Typhoid Rats, Somberwald Spider and Garruk Relentless to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup and do not flood the board with ground bodies.
- Against WR, RG and BR, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Furor of the Bitten and Pitchburn Devils to punish small creatures; do not overcommit into a sweeper and keep your own fliers alive. WR also runs Rebuke, Fiend Hunter, Slayer of the Wicked and Bonds of Faith.
- Against white decks with Fiend Hunter and Avacynian Priest, expect one-for-one trades on early creatures; prefer threats that leave value behind. Expect Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning from white decks in long games.
- WU and UB should keep cheap two-drops and the flier clock even when a stall looks likely, and apply pressure early rather than waiting (anecdote).
