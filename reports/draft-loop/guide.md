# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-09's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Twenty nights: 160 decks, 593 games. Guided seats are 253-264 in games (49%, 102-108 in matches); unguided are 334-323 (51%, 135-129 in matches). On the one night with all three kinds of seat, guided went 3-10 in games, conventional 6-6 and unguided 18-11 (a single night, anecdote). Guided seats have not beaten unguided seats, so the guide has not been shown to help. Treat archetype lines as hypotheses and lean on the card numbers.

- BR: 56% of games (115 games, 16 decks, 30-18 in matches). Still the best pair, but it fell tonight: a guided BR with eight removal spells went 0-3 in 18-to-32-turn grinds, its ground bodies (including Rotting Fensnake) won none of six games (anecdote). Removal alone does not close; BR needs fliers or real finishers. The earlier 3-0 BR won with Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Into the Maw of Hell, Sever the Bloodline and cheap bodies (Typhoid Rats, Ashmouth Hound, Feral Ridgewolf) (anecdote).
- BG: 54% (167 games, 23 decks, 39-30 in matches). Needs evasion or removal-backed finishers and cheap creatures. Somberwald Spider and Spider Spawning beat ground decks.
- WR: 54% (123 games, 17 decks, 27-24 in matches). Tonight a guided WR went 1-2 (anecdote): it took Moon Heron, Murder of Crows and Stitched Drake off colour, ran only four removal spells and two fliers, and had no flexible late card (it leaned on Scourge of Geier Reach and three Tormented Pariah through 38- and 18-turn grinds). An unguided WR with Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning went 2-1 (anecdote), winning 19- and 22-turn games. Play Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning.
- WU: 51% (156 games, 21 decks, 31-32 in matches). White removal plus real fliers works; filler does not. Tonight a WU went 1-2 (anecdote), losing 0-2 to UB's two Claustrophobia and 0-2 to WR humans. Armored Skaab and Stitcher's Apprentice were dead weight without self-mill.
- WG: 50% (109 games, 15 decks, 24-21 in matches). Cheap creatures carried a 2-1 WG tonight (Avacyn's Pilgrim and two-drops, 4-2 in games, anecdote). Removal count matters: an earlier WG that cut Dead Weight and Corpse Lunge went 0-3 (anecdote).
- RG: 51% (68 games, 9 decks, 14-13 in matches). Tonight an unguided RG went 2-1 (anecdote) on four removal spells (Harvest Pyre, Prey Upon, Rolling Temblor, Spidery Grasp) and cheap green bodies (Reckless Waif, Villagers of Estwald), beating UB once in 11 turns, but it had no answer to UB's two Claustrophobia and Victim of Night. Needs ways to reach fliers.
- UB: 46% (162 games, 21 decks, 26-37 in matches). Below average, but tonight one unguided UB went 3-0 (anecdote) with fliers, two Claustrophobia and Victim of Night, while another UB went 1-2 (anecdote): its tempo 2-drops (Sensory Deprivation, Grasp of Phantoms, Tribute to Hunger) could not stop Boneyard Wurm or Scourge of Geier Reach, and it had only Bloodgift Demon and Morkrut Banshee as top end. Needs cheap removal, blockers and enough finishers. Do not take Delver of Secrets repeatedly or Dream Twist.
- UR: 45% (119 games, 16 decks, 20-28 in matches). Needs a closer. Take Stitched Drake and Moon Heron over fillers like Trepanation Blade.
- WB: 45% (105 games, 14 decks, 19-23 in matches). Below average. Needs cheap blockers and enough sources for both colours.
- Mono-colour and others: U 2-4 in matches (14 games), R 3-3 (16 games), UG 1-5 (16 games). Stay two colours.
- White is well supported on individual cards. Cheap white removal (Rebuke) is contested, so take it early.
- Black was crowded at tonight's table (several UB and BR decks). Take black removal early in pack 1 rather than waiting for Victim of Night in pack 3.
- Count playables before committing: pick a main colour by about pick 5 and a pair by mid pack 1 to mid pack 2. Speculative picks (Darkthicket Wolf, Festerhide Boar, Kindercatch) that never made the deck cost a WU drafter tonight (anecdote). Do not leave a colour for one early removal spell unless the new colour is clearly open. If strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8, move in.
- Evasion plus removal breaks stalls. Games stall for 11 to 55 turns.
- Splashing is poor. Don't run a colour on six basics unless it is a light second colour with excellent depth in the main colour (anecdotes).

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Smite the Monstrous, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith, Sensory Deprivation, Corpse Lunge, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre. Take a second Dead Weight, Victim of Night, Claustrophobia or Brimstone Volley if offered. Aim for five or more removal spells, but remember removal alone did not save an eight-removal BR tonight. Take removal before Silverchase Fox or Abbey Griffin; take hard removal over tempo 2-drops if the table has big green threats (Boneyard Wurm, Scourge of Geier Reach).
2. Strong uncommons and rares: Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee, Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders, Slayer of the Wicked, Fiend Hunter, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw, Rally the Peasants.
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Angelic Overseer, Somberwald Spider. Aim for three evasive threats or a removal-backed finisher by the end of pack 3. Take a second flier or Spider Spawning over Walking Corpse. Do not take an off-colour flier over an on-colour one.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Avacyn's Pilgrim, Ashmouth Hound, Feral Ridgewolf, Bump in the Night, Unruly Mob, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils and Tormented Pariah if red, Silver-Inlaid Dagger. Early blockers matter: an 11-turn loss to a fast RG and slow starts against BR and WR (anecdotes).

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Armored Skaab, Stitcher's Apprentice, Cobbled Wings (taken twice and never played tonight), Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Wooden Stakes, Ancient Grudge, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Lost in the Mist, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Festerhide Boar, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Trepanation Blade, Cloistered Youth over a flier, Unburial Rites, and Makeshift Mauler as a second copy. Delver of Secrets // Insectile Aberration is marginal. Walking Corpse is acceptable only as an early blocker when nothing better is left. Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell or a dead card. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default. For a low-curve aggro deck, 16 is worth trying (anecdote). Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 WB earlier, 2-1 WB), 11 Plains/6 Islands (2-1 WU), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG) (all anecdotes). Count pips for the heavier colour rather than copying a split. Seven Mountains was too few for a red-heavy WR (1-2, anecdote).
- Double pips: give a BB or GG colour 8+ sources where you can. Evidence is mixed (all anecdotes): BB cards on 7 Swamps went 0-2 in a 2-1 BG, while a 3-0 BR with Bloodgift Demon ran 7 Swamps. Treat 8 as the safe target, and avoid double pips in both colours. Three GG Somberwald Spiders want 8+ Forests.
- Aim for five or more removal spells, four or more answers to fliers (that can also clear ground blockers), and at least two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers. A four-removal WR lost; a four-removal RG went 2-1 (anecdotes), so count threats as well as removal.
- Keep one flexible late threat or finisher. Decks without one lost 16-to-55-turn grinds repeatedly (anecdotes). Do not cut Rally the Peasants or Divine Reckoning from white decks for marginal cards; a WG left Rally out and lost 0-2 to a deck running two (anecdote).
- Pair removal with good cheap creatures and close with fliers. Do not cut cheap blockers for a marginal fourth flier, and do not pad with ground filler (Rotting Fensnake) when a flier or removal spell is available.
- Do not leave yourself with no answer to an opposing colour's key cards: an RG with no black had nothing for Claustrophobia and Victim of Night decks. Enchantment-based removal on your best creature is a real risk; keep multiple threats rather than one 15-creature board of equals.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core. Play the removal and flier answers you drafted; check every sideboard swap against your colours. Do not side out your main removal.
- Main fliers over filler such as Riot Devils (unless red is your main colour and deep), Walking Corpse or a marginal fourth creature.
- Include one or two card-advantage or stall-breaking cards (Ghoulcaller's Chant, Moan of the Unhallowed) for stalls (anecdote).
- Do not main Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone, Mulch, Dream Twist or self-mill cards without a self-mill deck. 1-toughness creatures die to Brimstone Volley and Geistflame.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke, Fiend Hunter and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies.
- Expect long games, often 18 to 40 turns, and up to 55. Break stalls with removal, Spider Spawning, Rally the Peasants and evasive threats.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR; fast starts beat slow hands in 11 to 15 turns (anecdotes, including an 11-turn loss to RG tonight).
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Angelic Overseer, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Moon Heron, Murder of Crows, Galvanic Juggernaut, Boneyard Wurm and Scourge of Geier Reach.
- Against WU, UB and UR, kill fliers early; Claustrophobia and Sensory Deprivation will target your best creature, so keep a second threat ready.
- Against WB, expect real removal held for your fliers; do not rely on one big threat.
- Against BG and WG, expect Typhoid Rats and Somberwald Spider to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup and do not flood the board with ground bodies.
- Against WR, RG and BR, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame and Blasphemous Act to punish small creatures; do not overcommit into a sweeper and keep your own fliers alive.
- Against white decks with Fiend Hunter and Avacynian Priest, expect one-for-one trades on early creatures; prefer threats that leave value behind. Expect Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning from white decks in long games.
- WU and UB should keep cheap two-drops and the flier clock even when a stall looks likely (anecdote).
