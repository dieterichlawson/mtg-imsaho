# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-02's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Thirteen nights: 104 decks, 379 games. Guided seats are 153-167 in games (48%, 62-70 in matches); unguided are 226-212 (52%, 94-86). Guided seats have done worse than unguided every night, so the guide has not been shown to help. Judge by the card numbers and treat every line here as a hypothesis, not a rule.

- BR is the best pair by game rate: 58% of games (76 games, 11 decks, 21-12 in matches). Tonight a guided BR deck went 3-0 on 17 lands: Dead Weight, Victim of Night, Devil's Play and Morkrut Banshee answered threats, while Ashmouth Hound, Kessig Wolf, two Vampire Interlopers and Screeching Bat won (anecdote). It beat a BG deck 2-0 in 11-turn games, so block early against BR.
- BG: 33-24 in matches, 55% of games (137 games, 19 decks). Tonight a BG deck with one true evasive finisher (Orchard Spirit) went 2-1, only 4-4 in games (anecdote). Earlier a BG deck of ground bodies went 1-2. BG needs evasion or removal-backed finishers.
- WU: 54% (108 games, 15 decks, 25-20 in matches). Tonight an unguided WU deck went 1-2: it lost long grinds (24 to 35 turns) to UB and UR after cutting its 5-drops, and lost to UR evasive threats (anecdote). WU works with cheap removal and a real clock; keep one late threat.
- WR 54% (78 games, 11 decks). WG 49% (67 games, 9 decks; tonight 2-1 with Spider Spawning and about ten ground creatures, anecdote). RG 45% (22 games, 3 decks).
- Weak: UR 47% (88 games, 12 decks, 16-20 in matches). Tonight two UR decks went 1-2 and 2-1 (anecdotes); the 1-2 deck lacked late power and evasive threats. UB 44% (85 games, 11 decks, 11-22 in matches). Tonight a UB deck with Dead Weight, Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation, Tribute to Hunger and five fliers still went 1-2, losing to BR (anecdote). So UB is poor even with removal and evasion. UG 38% (16 games).
- WB is 5-13 in matches, 33% of games (43 games, 6 decks). Tonight WB went 0-3 with Walking Corpse filler and no clock (anecdote). Avoid.
- Evasion plus removal breaks stalls. Games stall for 14 to 55 turns, and ground bodies alone do not break them.
- Black, green or white-blue first are all fine; black with red is the best-scoring pair. Pick a main colour by about pick 5 and a pair by mid pack 1. Do not leave a colour for one early removal spell unless the new colour is clearly open. Tonight a WG drafter's pack 2 first pick, Brimstone Volley, went unused, and a UR drafter's Smite the Monstrous went unused (anecdotes).
- Splashing is poor: off-colour picks rarely make decks.
- Mono-colour is risky: 2 mono-red decks 3-3 in matches (16 games).
- Read what is passed to you. Two decks of the same pair often share a table, so take contested playables earlier or move. Do not pass a second removal spell to a neighbour in your pair's lane.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Brimstone Volley, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Smite the Monstrous, Bonds of Faith, Sensory Deprivation, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Hysterical Blindness. Take removal over Curiosity or Curse of the Bloody Tome (anecdote).
2. Strong uncommons and rares: Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee, Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders, Slayer of the Wicked, Galvanic Juggernaut, Murder of Crows, Fiend Hunter, Manor Gargoyle, Stromkirk Noble, Kruin Outlaw.
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Moon Heron, Abbey Griffin, Somberwald Spider. Cheap flying blockers matter late.
4. Good commons: Typhoid Rats, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Ashmouth Hound, Bump in the Night, Thraben Sentry, Unruly Mob, Furor of the Bitten, Silver-Inlaid Dagger.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Walking Corpse, Armored Skaab, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Delver of Secrets, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Lost in the Mist, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Festerhide Boar, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Riot Devils, Cellar Door. Darkthicket Wolf is marginal (a BG deck with it went 2-1 in matches, 4-4 in games, anecdote). Late in a pack, take a playable creature over a dead card.

## Building

- Land count is unsettled: 17 lands has produced 3-0, 2-1 and 0-3 decks alike. Use 17 unless the curve is low.
- Count pips and match basics to them. Avoid double pips in both colours. A focused UR deck on 9 Island and 8 Mountain won 2-0 in two rounds after cutting Curses, Furor and Vampiric Fury (anecdote).
- Ground bodies are fine when removal answers the fliers: a 3-0 BR deck was mostly ground creatures, and a WG deck with about ten ground creatures went 2-1 (anecdotes). But that WG deck lost a 35-turn stall that needed evasion, and ground-only decks lose when they lack removal.
- Aim for four or more answers to fliers and four or more evasive or removal-backed finishers.
- Do not cut every top-end card for a low curve: keep one late threat, since a deck without one lost the 24-to-55-turn grinds (anecdotes).
- Play the removal and flier answers you drafted rather than leaving them in the sideboard.
- Main your fliers over filler such as Riot Devils or a fourth Walking Corpse.
- Keep good two-drops, but do not play a third mediocre one over a removal spell or evasive threat.
- Include one or two card-advantage cards for stalls (anecdote).
- Do not main Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone or Mulch.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies.
- Expect long games, often 20 to 35 turns. Break stalls with removal, fight spells, Spider Spawning and evasive threats.
- Spread threats, since Rebuke and Smite the Monstrous kill attackers.
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Angelic Overseer, Battleground Geist, Invisible Stalker and Murder of Crows.
- Against WU and UR, kill the fliers early; Claustrophobia and Sensory Deprivation will target your best creature.
- Against BR and RG, block early with cheap creatures like Ambush Viper; Brimstone Volley, Geistflame and Typhoid Rats punish slow hands.
- Against BG, expect Typhoid Rats and Somberwald Spider to make the ground hard. Win through the air with removal backup, and answer Somberwald Spider and Tribute to Hunger early.
