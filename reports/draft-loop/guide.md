# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-28's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Thirty-nine nights: 312 decks, 1155 games. Guided seats are 385-409 in games (48%, 156-168 in matches, 108 decks). The conventional guide is 159-128 (55%, 67-53 in matches, 40 decks). Unguided seats are 611-618 (50%, 245-247 in matches, 164 decks). On the twenty nights with all three kinds of seat, guided went 135-155 (47%, 55-65 in matches), conventional 159-128 (55%) and unguided 295-306 (49%). Guided is still behind and this guide has not been shown to help. Treat archetype lines as hypotheses; lean on card numbers and on advice that is consistent across lessons.

- Commit to two colours early (by mid pack 1; second colour by mid pack 2 at the latest). Stranded picks are the most repeated lesson. On 2026-11-28 an unguided UB (0-3) never played its pack 3 first pick Olivia Voldaren, and an unguided BG (1-2) cut an off-colour Lost in the Mist; a WR spent late picks on Bloodcrazed Neonate, Ghoulcaller's Bell and Cobbled Wings and played none (anecdotes). Earlier: Abbey Griffin, Voiceless Spirit, Curse of Oblivion, Claustrophobia, Dead Weight, Invisible Stalker, Into the Maw of Hell, Disciple of Griselbrand, Fiend Hunter, Balefire Dragon, Bloodline Keeper, Olivia Voldaren, Devil's Play, Geist of Saint Traft and Curse of the Pierced Heart were taken and not played (anecdotes). If you take a bomb, stay in its colour and play it. Once set, do not take cards outside your colours. A first pick is a bet: do not follow it with a second off-plan pick, but check whether the first pick's colour is still flowing before abandoning it.
- Fliers matter, but reach and answers matter too. On 2026-11-28 a conventional WU went 3-0 with fliers 6-0 in games (Moon Heron, Battleground Geist, with Slayer of the Wicked, Fiend Hunter, Silent Departure x2 and Feeling of Dread as answers); a conventional WR with only Voiceless Spirit and Balefire Dragon as fliers lost 0-2 to WU's fliers; an unguided BG and an unguided RG with almost no flier answers (RG had only Geistflame) lost to Moon Heron, Spectral Rider, Chapel Geist and Abbey Griffin (anecdotes). A guided BG with two evasive threats (Falkenrath Noble, Orchard Spirit) played Festerhide Boar and Kindercatch over a third flier and went 1-2 (anecdote). Earlier three guided WGs went 1-2 with four fliers or fewer, while a guided WR with four fliers (Abbey Griffin, Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Gallows Warden) went 2-1 on 2026-11-28 (anecdotes). Take cheap flier answers (Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Silent Departure, Brimstone Volley, Rebuke, Urgent Exorcism) before an extra two-drop, and a third or fourth flier over ground filler such as Festerhide Boar, Kindercatch, Village Bell-Ringer, Riot Devils or Silverchase Fox.
- Reach cuts the other way: WG's Somberwald Spider beat a WB and a WU 0-2 earlier (anecdotes). Keep Rebuke, Urgent Exorcism or Slayer of the Wicked for reach blockers.
- Do not sideboard evasive threats out for filler: a conventional WU sided out Stitched Drake for Dream Twist and Mulch and lost (anecdote).
- Cheap curves must also close. Games run 11 to 62 turns; many ran 25 to 46. Have two or three real threats that win in the air or long, backed by removal and card advantage. An unguided UB lost 39- and 25-turn games to BG for lack of a clock (anecdote). But early pressure matters too: a conventional WU lost 1-2 to BR with slow removal, an unguided UG lost 0-2 to WR aggro in 13 to 14 turn games, and an unguided BG lost to fast RG starts with Gatstaf Shepherd and Walking Corpse too slow (anecdotes).
- Count creatures: about 14 to 16 with removal is the norm. A UR with 10 creatures went 1-2 and a BR with 12 went 2-1 (anecdotes).
- Take removal, but only in your colours, and play what you drafted. Seats short on removal lost. On 2026-11-28 an unguided UB took Victim of Night, Sever the Bloodline and Corpse Lunge with blue fliers and went 2-1, losing only to a WR with Balefire Dragon, Fiend Hunter and Blazing Torch (anecdote). Earlier an unguided BR benched Claustrophobia and lost, an unguided UB left Heretic's Punishment in the sideboard, and a WB lost 0-2 to Olivia Voldaren with Urgent Exorcism in the sideboard (anecdotes).
- Mono-colour with no evasion lost (G 2-4 in matches, 15 games; W 3-3, 15 games).
- Green-based RG did well earlier: an unguided RG went 3-0 on Villagers of Estwald (three copies), Darkthicket Wolf, Ambush Viper, Prey Upon, Brimstone Volley and Into the Maw of Hell; on 2026-11-28 an unguided RG went 1-2, with deep green and red creatures but no flier answers (anecdotes).

Pairs by games won (decks, matches, game win rate), statistics to 2026-11-28:
- WU: 55% (275 games, 37 decks, 62-49 in matches). White removal plus cheap fliers (Midnight Haunting, Invisible Stalker, Chapel Geist, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Stitched Drake, Gallows Warden, Moon Heron, Battleground Geist, Moorland Haunt) and blue answers (Silent Departure, Feeling of Dread, Grasp of Phantoms). A 3-0 conventional WU on 2026-11-28 ran 17 lands on a 10-7 split with Dissipate, Ghost Quarter and Mask of Avacyn cut (anecdote).
- RG: 54% (123 games, 16 decks, 28-20 in matches). Still small. Needs cheap green bodies (Villagers of Estwald, Darkthicket Wolf), red removal, and answers to fliers.
- BG: 53% (240 games, 33 decks, 53-46 in matches). Cheap removal (Dead Weight, Victim of Night) and blockers over top-end; two BGs went 1-2 on 2026-11-28, one with too few flier answers and one with Falkenrath Noble on 6 Swamps (anecdotes).
- WR: 53% (267 games, 37 decks, 59-52 in matches). Take Mentor of the Meek and Midnight Haunting over ground bodies like Village Ironsmith; save Rebuke and Brimstone Volley for fliers. Two WRs went 2-1 on 2026-11-28 (anecdotes).
- WG: 52% (256 games, 35 decks, 57-48 in matches). Needs cheap plays, 8+ real sources for each colour, a third and fourth flier, and removal.
- BR: 51% (298 games, 40 decks, 65-55 in matches). Burn (Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Brimstone Volley) plus Dead Weight and Tribute to Hunger. Wants cheap removal, evasive finishers (Falkenrath Noble, Screeching Bat) and card advantage. Skip Ancient Grudge and Heartless Summoning.
- WB: 47% (177 games, 24 decks, 35-37 in matches). Go white-first or black-deep, count BB pips, take card-advantage finishers over Walking Corpse filler.
- UG: 46% (98 games, 13 decks, 16-23 in matches). Weak. Spider Spawning did nothing without self-mill.
- UR: 45% (191 games, 26 decks, 34-44 in matches). Make it red-first with burn and a cheap evasive clock.
- UB: 44% (318 games, 42 decks, 47-79 in matches). Worst big pair; on 2026-11-28 an unguided UB went 2-1 on black removal and blue fliers while another went 0-3 without a clock (anecdotes). Needs cheap blockers, a real finisher (Stitched Drake over defensive filler), answers and pressure. Vampire Interloper cannot block. Do not take Think Twice, Dissipate, Dream Twist, Curse of Oblivion or Markov Patrician. Armored Skaab without self-mill was a poor blocker. Stitcher's Apprentice-style value is not pressure.
- Mono U is 4-5 in matches (21 games), mono R 3-3 (16 games), mono G 2-4 (15 games), mono W 3-3 (15 games).

General notes:
- White is the best-supported colour on individual cards (Slayer of the Wicked, Midnight Haunting, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Bonds of Faith). Red has Stromkirk Noble, Furor of the Bitten, Into the Maw of Hell, Kruin Outlaw, Ashmouth Hound. Green has Villagers of Estwald, Lumberknot, Gatstaf Shepherd. Cheap white removal (Rebuke, Fiend Hunter) is contested; take it early.
- Black is crowded and contested; take black removal early in pack 1, but only if you will play black. Black bombs (Bloodgift Demon 54%, 94 games) work when black is your main colour.
- Read what is flowing after pack 1. If strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8, move in. When red removal flows, commit rather than splitting picks.
- Galvanic Juggernaut (55%, 361 games, taken at 1.2) is fine as a colourless finisher, but take removal, Bonds of Faith or a flier over it. It dies to Smite the Monstrous.
- A bomb alone does not carry a deck: Olivia Voldaren went 3-5 in games in a 17-land BR earlier but anchored a 3-0 BR with cheap removal behind it (anecdotes). After the first pick, value playable two-drops and removal.
- Splashing is poor. Do not splash without a dual land and few cards.

- Blue: Murder of Crows (only if blue), Invisible Stalker, Stitched Drake (1UU, needs 8 Islands), Moon Heron, Mindshrieker, Battleground Geist, Lantern Spirit, Orchard Spirit, Snapcaster Mage, Grasp of Phantoms, Gallows Warden, Geist of Saint Traft (only if blue). Blue fliers lost to Somberwald Spider, Victim of Night and WG. Delver of Secrets is weak (anecdote).

- Bombs: Take only if on colour and castable, and play them. Olivia Voldaren, Devil's Play, Balefire Dragon and Bloodline Keeper were repeatedly taken early and stranded in off-colour decks, but won when the deck was built around black or red.
- Avoid Markov Patrician. Ghostly Possession was not a substitute for evasive creatures (anecdote).

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal on colour: Sever the Bloodline, Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Claustrophobia (blue), Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Harvest Pyre, Geistflame, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith, Fiend Hunter, Sensory Deprivation (blue), Silent Departure (blue), Grasp of Phantoms (blue), Feeling of Dread (white), Corpse Lunge, Spidery Grasp, Heretic's Punishment, Urgent Exorcism (vs fliers), Smite the Monstrous (big creatures only), Blazing Torch. Take a second if offered; a third removal spell beats a second Grave Bramble. About five is the target; vanilla green and blue bodies are worth less than removal. Do not take removal outside your likely colours, and not second pick before you know them. Contested red removal (Brimstone Volley) should be taken early if you are red.
2. Strong uncommons and rares on colour: Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Moldgraf Monstrosity, Falkenrath Noble (only with 8+ black sources), Morkrut Banshee (if you can support BB), Falkenrath Marauders, Instigator Gang, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw, Rally the Peasants, Midnight Haunting, Dearly Departed, Angelic Overseer, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Devil's Play and Balefire Dragon (red), Bloodgift Demon (black), Spider Spawning (only with self-mill), Garruk Relentless (green only).
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Invisible Stalker, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Gallows Warden, Moon Heron, Lantern Spirit, Mindshrieker, Battleground Geist, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Scourge of Geier Reach, Somberwald Spider, Screeching Bat. Aim for five evasive threats or answers by the end of pack 3 in a white-fliers deck (four was not enough for three 1-2 WGs, though a 2-1 WR got by on four), and take a third flier over a second Unruly Mob, Doomed Traveler, Silverchase Fox, Village Ironsmith, Village Bell-Ringer, Riot Devils, Thraben Purebloods, Festerhide Boar, Kindercatch, Markov Patrician or a third Villagers of Estwald in a deck that is not green-based ground. Take an evasive two-drop like Orchard Spirit over Thraben Sentry or a second Woodland Sleuth. Do not take an off-colour flier over an on-colour one, and play fliers you draft. Card advantage (Mentor of the Meek, Midnight Haunting, Rally the Peasants) was asked for in several lessons.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald (a 3-0 RG ran three), Avacyn's Pilgrim, Ambush Viper, Kindercatch, Darkthicket Wolf, Ashmouth Hound, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils, Riot Devils (one), Tormented Pariah, Silver-Inlaid Dagger, Unruly Mob (one), Doomed Traveler, Avacynian Priest, Bump in the Night. Take playable two- and three-drops over weak one-drops (Brain Weevil, Walking Corpse) and late junk such as Frightful Delusion, Cobbled Wings, Spare from Evil, Traveler's Amulet and Ranger's Guile. In UB, take real finishers, removal and ground blockers over low-impact fliers, Vampire Interloper, Demonmail Hauberk and sorceries.

Late in a pack, take a playable creature, removal or flier over an off-colour spell, a duplicate situational card or card draw. Late novelties (Cellar Door, Fortress Crab, Manor Skeleton, Bloodcrazed Neonate, Ghoulcaller's Bell) left decks short of playables or went unplayed (anecdotes). Walking Corpse is only a 23rd card.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Stitcher's Apprentice, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Ancient Grudge, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists (a 3-0 RG called it key, a single anecdote; not otherwise supported), Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Butcher's Cleaver and Geistcatcher's Rig over a bomb or removal (though Geistcatcher's Rig answered a flier for some, and its power 4+ is exposed to Smite the Monstrous), Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Pierced Heart, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Rolling Temblor (benched), Fortress Crab, Full Moon's Rise, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Bloodcrazed Neonate, Make a Wish, Grimoire of the Dead, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Trepanation Blade, Heartless Summoning, Curiosity, Nightbird's Clutches, Frightful Delusion, Traitorous Blood, Vampiric Fury, Lost in the Mist, Reckless Waif over removal, Mask of Avacyn over a flier, Cloistered Youth over a flier, Intangible Virtue, Ranger's Guile, a second Moment of Heroism (fine in an aggressive white deck), a second Makeshift Mauler, a second Ghoulcaller's Chant, and a third Festerhide Boar. Naturalize, Wooden Stake and Sharpened Pitchfork are narrow filler (sideboard Naturalize against aura decks; do not take a second). Armored Skaab, Skaab Goliath, Skaab Ruinator, Stitcher's Apprentice and Forbidden Alchemy are only for a deck with Spider Spawning and real self-mill. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default; long games support it. Cut to 16 only for a truly low curve with several two-drops (anecdotes; a conventional WR with 17 lands and 15 creatures stalled in 25-26 turn games against BG and suggested 16, a single anecdote). A BR cut Feral Ridgewolf for a 17th land and went 2-1 (anecdote). Do not cut good spells to reach 17 if it leaves you without answers. Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 and 2-1 WB), 11 Plains/6 Swamp (3-0 and 1-2 WB), 10 Plains/7 Island (3-0 WU on 2026-11-28), 9 Plains/8 Islands (3-0 WU), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 9 Forest/8 Plains (3-0 WG once, other 9/8 WGs 0-3, 1-2 and 1-2), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG), 9 Mountain/8 Plains (2-1 WR), 10 Plains/7 Mountain with seven red cards (2-1 WR on 2026-11-28), 9 Swamp/8 Island (2-1 UB on 2026-11-28, BB costs held), 11 Forest/6 Swamp with Falkenrath Noble (1-2 BG), 8 Swamp/9 Mountain (1-2 BR) (anecdotes). Count pips and give the main colour 9 to 10 sources and the second at least 8 (7 can work when that colour is light and has no double pips; a Pilgrim counts as fixing but is not a land). In a green-based deck, put green first; with GG cards like Somberwald Spider, 8+ Forests.
- Double pips: give a BB, GG or UU colour 8+ sources or cut them. A guided UG ran Stitched Drake (1UU) on 7 Islands and went 0-3, a guided BG ran two Victim of Night (BB) on 7 Swamps and went 1-2, and a guided BG ran Falkenrath Noble on 6 Swamps and went 1-2 (anecdotes). Earlier a WB with Morkrut Banshee on 7 Swamps went 2-4 in games, and Bloodline Keeper on 7 Swamps lost (anecdotes). Go 9/8 or cut the double-pip cards. Avoid double pips in both colours.
- Aim for about five or six real removal spells (pumps do not count), three or four answers to fliers (Smite the Monstrous does not count), and two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers. Play the removal you drafted and the bomb you took; do not leave Claustrophobia, Urgent Exorcism, Heretic's Punishment or similar answers in the sideboard when the opponent has fliers or a bomb.
- A low curve alone is not enough. 0-3 and 1-2 decks have been top-heavy, ground filler, flierless, short on creatures or lacking pressure. Count two-drops and early evasive creatures, keep one or two cheap ground defenders in tempo decks, and trade a marginal two-drop or weak one-drop for a third flier or a removal spell. Cut 6-drops and filler rather than forcing them in. Count creatures (14 to 16) before adding another removal spell.
- Keep a flexible late threat or value source (Mentor of the Meek, Moan of the Unhallowed, Gavony Township, Moorland Haunt, Rally the Peasants, Divine Reckoning), but do not cut good cheap cards to fit one.
- Self-mill needs a real plan: either draft enablers and payoffs together or leave them out.
- Tricks have a place after removal and creatures; one or two cheap tricks is fine. Enchantment-based removal on your best creature is a real risk, and Naturalize exists; keep multiple threats that survive removal and a few non-aura answers. Toughness above one matters against Geistflame, Brimstone Volley, Harvest Pyre and Tormented Pariah; power 4+ is exposed to Smite the Monstrous. Do not leave yourself with no answer to an opposing colour's key cards (Claustrophobia, Victim of Night, Tormented Pariah, Bonds of Faith, Olivia Voldaren, Moon Heron, Spectral Rider).
- Settle your 23 by pack 3. Do not play cards you planned to cut; take real playables, not filler, in the middle picks (a 1-2 BR ended with Desperate Ravings as its 23rd card; a 1-2 UB had to cut Laboratory Maniac to reach 23; anecdotes). Check the final list against your colours before registering and do not side out main removal or evasive threats for situational cards. Keep at least one cheap play main.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core. Secure cheap early plays before piling on top end.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke and Fiend Hunter for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies. Hold Smite the Monstrous for power 4+ creatures only (not Bloodline Keeper, power 3); keep Rebuke or Claustrophobia for it. Check creature types before counting on Victim of Night (werewolves such as Villagers of Estwald and Darkthicket Wolf are immune).
- Expect games of 12 to 47 turns, up to 62; many ran 25 to 46 with stalled boards. Break stalls with removal, Rally the Peasants, Moan of the Unhallowed and evasive threats. Apply pressure early rather than waiting, especially with UB, UR, UG and WU.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR; WR aggro won 13 to 14 turn games against a UG with few early blockers, and fast RG starts beat a slow BG. Pair cheap creatures with late fliers or Rally the Peasants to close.
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Lantern Spirit, Geist of Saint Traft, Angelic Overseer, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Murder of Crows, Midnight Haunting, Gallows Warden, Falkenrath Noble, Screeching Bat, Bloodline Keeper, Olivia Voldaren, Bloodgift Demon, Galvanic Juggernaut, Boneyard Wurm, Balefire Dragon and Scourge of Geier Reach. Hold Brimstone Volley for Murder of Crows and Angel of Flight Alabaster rather than spending it early.
- Against WU, UB, UR, UG and mono-blue, kill fliers early (Geist of Saint Traft with Silent Departure beat a UB twice quickly; Moon Heron and Spectral Rider beat a BG, an RG and a WR 0-2 on 2026-11-28; Delver of Secrets flips into a real threat). Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation, Grasp of Phantoms, Silent Departure, Feeling of Dread and Lost in the Mist will target your best creature or tap your small board, so keep a second threat ready. WU also runs two Smite the Monstrous and Rebuke, so do not rely on one four-power creature. Blue decks win long grinds, so do not durdle.
- Against WB, expect real removal (Fiend Hunter, Smite the Monstrous, Rebuke, Victim of Night) held for your fliers and best creatures, plus Angelic Overseer, Chapel Geist and Rally the Peasants closing long games, and sometimes black bombs like Bloodline Keeper and Reaper from the Abyss. Do not rely on one big threat, and keep evasive creatures in your deck.
- Against BG, WG and RG, expect Typhoid Rats, Somberwald Spider, Spidery Grasp, Villagers of Estwald, Darkthicket Wolf, Ambush Viper, Ashmouth Hound, Bonds of Faith, Urgent Exorcism, Mentor of the Meek, Fiend Hunter, Prey Upon, Garruk Relentless, Tormented Pariah and burn to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup, expect reach (kill Somberwald Spider first), and do not flood the board with ground bodies.
- Against WR, BR and UR, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils, Dead Weight and Tribute to Hunger to punish small creatures. Do not overcommit and keep your own fliers alive. WR also runs Rebuke (often two), Urgent Exorcism, Smite the Monstrous, Midnight Haunting, Chapel Geist, Bonds of Faith, Silverchase Fox, Fiend Hunter, Blazing Torch, Balefire Dragon, Slayer of the Wicked and Rally the Peasants, and will answer your fliers and four-power creatures. Hold back threats rather than feeding them one at a time.
- Against BR with Olivia Voldaren, Bloodline Keeper, Bloodgift Demon, Screeching Bat and Falkenrath Noble, save removal for the bombs and the fliers, and have card advantage for long games.
- Against white decks with Fiend Hunter and Avacynian Priest, expect one-for-one trades on early creatures; prefer threats that leave value behind. Expect Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning in long games.
