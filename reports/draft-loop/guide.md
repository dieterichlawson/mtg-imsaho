# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-10-26's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Six nights: 48 decks, 174 games. Guided seats are 51-59 in games (46%) against 52% unguided (238 games). The guide is not proven; judge by the card numbers.

- BG is best but not proven: 20-7 in matches, 66% of games (64 games, 9 decks). Tonight's three BG decks went 1-2, 2-1 and 2-1, and the only 3-0 was WR.
- WR 56% (41 games, 6 decks), UR 53% (30 games), BR 52% (29 games), RG 50% (14 games).
- Weak: WU 47% (38 games), UB 43% (46 games, 6 decks), WG 43% (35 games), UG 38% (16 games).
- WB is 0-6 in matches (13 games, 2 decks).
- Games stall for 15 to 30 turns, sometimes more. Removal plus evasive finishers break stalls. Fliers alone did not (a WU deck with five fliers lost to BG ground bodies, anecdote).
- Lean black or green first, but white removal and fliers are a real alternative. Pick a main colour by about pick 5 and a pair before pack 3. Do not splash. Do not hope for a late splash; commit to a second colour's playables early.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal: Victim of Night, Prey Upon, Dead Weight, Corpse Lunge, Geistflame, Brimstone Volley, Fiend Hunter, Bonds of Faith, Claustrophobia, Rebuke.
2. Strong uncommons and rares: Morkrut Banshee, Sever the Bloodline, Falkenrath Noble, Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders.
3. Good commons: Villagers of Estwald, Typhoid Rats as the black two-drop, Festerhide Boar, Kindercatch.
4. On-colour evasive threats and closers: Chapel Geist, Midnight Haunting, Stitched Drake, Bloodline Keeper.

Take removal or evasion over second copies of tricks and over Wreath of Geists, Moonmist, Vampiric Fury and equipment such as Butcher's Cleaver. Those went unplayed. Take on-colour two-drops over speculation. Once your pair is set, do not take an off-colour card such as Falkenrath Marauders in a BG deck.

## Building

- Land count is unsettled. Tonight 17-land decks went 1-2 and 2-1 (BG), 3-0 (WR) and 1-2 (WU). Play 16 with a low curve, and 17 with several 5-drops.
- Count pips before choosing basics, and do it before the deck is built. Liliana of the Veil with 5 Swamps and Bloodline Keeper on 10 Plains/7 Swamp both failed. Go near an even split or cut the double-pip cards in the lighter colour.
- Play 5 or more two-drops, at least two evasive finishers, and at least one reliable closer.
- Play at least two answers to fliers.
- Naturalize hits only enchantments and artifacts, never creatures. Do not main it. Main creature removal instead.

## Playing

- Trade early and hold removal for fliers and big blockers.
- Break stalls with removal plus evasive threats, not more ground bodies.
- Spread threats, since Rebuke and Smite the Monstrous kill attackers.
- Expect opposing Claustrophobia and Rebuke from blue-white. Threats that leave value when removed, like Pitchburn Devils, beat plain bodies.
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit and Murder of Crows.
- Respect Victim of Night from black decks.
