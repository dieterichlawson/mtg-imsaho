# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-01's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Twelve nights: 96 decks, 350 games. Guided seats are 135-154 in games (47%, 54-66 in matches); unguided are 215-196 (52%, 90-78). Guided seats have done worse than unguided every night so far, so the guide has not been shown to help. Judge by the card numbers and treat every line here as a hypothesis, not a rule.

- BG is the best pair by results: 33-24 in matches, 55% of games (137 games, 19 decks). Tonight a guided BG deck went 1-2 with Darkthicket Wolf, Gruesome Deformity, two Grizzled Outcasts and only five evasive threats (anecdote). Earlier BG losses agree: BG with only ground bodies loses, and Spider Spawning does not carry a ground deck.
- WU 55% (102 games, 14 decks, 24-18 in matches). Tonight WU went 3-0 (guided, 6-0 in games: Grasp of Phantoms, Hysterical Blindness, Rebuke, Invisible Stalker, Battleground Geist) and 1-2 (guided, Stitched Drake, Voiceless Spirit, Moon Heron; lost to a WG deck and BR pressure). Both anecdotes. WU works when it has cheap removal and a real clock; fliers alone were not enough.
- BR 55% (69 games, 10 decks), WR 54% (78 games, 11 decks). Tonight WR went 2-1 with 17 lands, and every game ran 17 to 30 turns (anecdote).
- Middling: WG 48% (58 games, 8 decks; tonight 2-1 on Spectral Riders, Abbey Griffin and Angel of Flight Alabaster, anecdote), RG 45% (22 games, 3 decks; tonight 1-2).
- Weak: UR 45% (74 games, 10 decks), UB 44% (78 games, 10 decks), UG 38% (16 games). Tonight a UB deck with Murder of Crows, Victim of Night and Tribute to Hunger beat a BG deck, while another UB deck of Walking Corpse, Manor Skeleton and filler went 1-2 (anecdotes): UB works only with removal and evasion, not filler.
- WB is 5-10 in matches, 36% of games (36 games, 5 decks). Avoid.
- Evasion plus removal breaks stalls. Games stall for 14 to 40 turns, and ground bodies alone do not break them.
- Black, green or white-blue first are all fine. Pick a main colour by about pick 5 and a pair by mid pack 1. Do not leave a colour for one early removal spell unless the new colour is clearly open.
- Splashing is poor: off-colour picks rarely make decks.
- Mono-colour is risky: 2 mono-red decks 3-3 in matches (16 games).
- Read what is passed to you. Two decks of the same pair often share a table, so take contested playables earlier or move. Do not pass a second removal spell to a neighbour in your pair's lane.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal: Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Brimstone Volley, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Smite the Monstrous, Bonds of Faith, Sensory Deprivation, Geistflame, Hysterical Blindness.
2. Strong uncommons and rares: Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee, Spider Spawning, Falkenrath Marauders, Slayer of the Wicked, Galvanic Juggernaut, Murder of Crows, Fiend Hunter, Manor Gargoyle.
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Moon Heron, Abbey Griffin, Somberwald Spider.
4. Good commons: Typhoid Rats, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Avacynian Priest, Ashmouth Hound, Thraben Sentry, Unruly Mob, Silver-Inlaid Dagger.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Walking Corpse, Armored Skaab, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Delver of Secrets, Think Twice, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Lost in the Mist, Rooftop Storm, Fortress Crab, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Darkthicket Wolf, Festerhide Boar, Brain Weevil. Late in a pack, take a playable creature over a dead card (tonight Ghost Quarter, Memory's Journey and a second Naturalize went unused).

## Building

- Land count is unsettled: 17 lands has produced 3-0, 2-1 and 0-3 decks alike. Use 17 unless the curve is low; a 17-land WR aggro deck tonight was slow (anecdote).
- Count pips and match basics to them. Avoid double pips in both colours.
- Cap ground filler, but the cap is soft: tonight a WU deck with about six ground bodies went 6-0 because cheap removal answered threats, and a WG deck with about ten ground creatures went 2-1 (anecdotes). Ground bodies lose when the deck lacks removal and evasion.
- Aim for four or more answers to fliers and four or more evasive or removal-backed finishers. Three answers did not save a BG deck tonight (Dead Weight, Tribute to Hunger, Victim of Night). Geistcatcher's Rig counts as an answer, not a finisher.
- Play the removal and flier answers you drafted: an RG deck left Fiend Hunter and Moan of the Unhallowed in the sideboard and lost to fliers (anecdote).
- Main your fliers over filler such as Riot Devils.
- Keep good two-drops, but do not play a third mediocre one over a removal spell or evasive threat.
- Include one bigger late-game threat, and one or two card-advantage cards for stalls (anecdote).
- Do not main Naturalize, Gnaw to the Bone or Mulch.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke and Smite the Monstrous for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies.
- Break stalls with removal, fight spells, Spider Spawning and evasive threats.
- Spread threats, since Rebuke and Smite the Monstrous kill attackers.
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Angelic Overseer, Battleground Geist and Murder of Crows.
- Against WU, kill the fliers early; Claustrophobia and Sensory Deprivation will target your best creature, and Dissipate can stall a slow deck.
- Against BR and RG, block early; Brimstone Volley, Geistflame and Typhoid Rats punish slow hands.
- Against BG, expect Typhoid Rats and Somberwald Spider to make the ground hard. Win through the air with removal backup, and answer Somberwald Spider and Tribute to Hunger early.
