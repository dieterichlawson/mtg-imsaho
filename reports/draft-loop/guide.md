# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-11-23's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Thirty-four nights: 272 decks, 1007 games. Guided seats are 349-371 in games (48%, 142-152 in matches, 98 decks). The conventional guide is 111-105 (51%, 45-45 in matches, 30 decks). Unguided seats are 547-531 (51%, 221-211 in matches, 144 decks). On the fifteen nights with all three kinds of seat, guided went 99-117 (46%, 41-49 in matches), conventional 111-105 (51%) and unguided 231-219 (51%). Guided is still behind and this guide has not been shown to help. Treat archetype lines as hypotheses; lean on card numbers and on advice that is consistent across lessons.

- Commit to two colours early (by mid pack 1; second colour by pick 5 of pack 2 at the latest). Stranded picks are the most repeated lesson. On 2026-11-23 a guided WR cut Victim of Night (p1p1) and Bloodline Keeper (p3p1); a guided WG's first-pick Sever the Bloodline sat in the sideboard; a WG's early Sensory Deprivation and Brimstone Volley went unused; a UG took two Curse of the Bloody Tome and cut both (anecdotes). Earlier: Geist of Saint Traft, Snapcaster Mage, Forbidden Alchemy, Murder of Crows, Olivia Voldaren, Devil's Play, Into the Maw of Hell were taken and never played (anecdotes). If you take a bomb, stay in its colour and play it. Once set, do not take removal or black cards outside your colours. A first pick is a bet: do not follow it with a second off-plan pick.
- Fliers decide games. On 2026-11-23 three guided WRs went 0-3 (one with only two Voiceless Spirit and Midnight Haunting as fliers, one 1-6 in games with Village Ironsmith and Selfless Cathar filling slots); a WG with only Abbey Griffin and two Somberwald Spider lost to UG; a BG lost 0-2 to UR's Balefire Dragon and Stitched Drake after cutting Spidery Grasp (all anecdotes). Earlier a WU with three Delver of Secrets, Snapcaster Mage and Stitched Drake went 3-0, and a flierless mono-green went 0-3 (anecdotes). Take cheap flier answers (Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Sever the Bloodline, Claustrophobia, Silent Departure, Brimstone Volley, Rebuke, Urgent Exorcism) before an extra two-drop, and a third flier over ground filler.
- Reach cuts the other way: a WB's air plan lost 0-2 to WG's two Somberwald Spider (anecdote). Keep Rebuke or Slayer of the Wicked for reach blockers.
- Cheap curves must also close. Games run 11 to 51 turns and often 18 to 29, but short ones happen (a UG round on 2026-11-23 ended at 11, 19 and 22 turns). Have two or three real threats that win in the air or long, backed by removal and card advantage.
- Take removal, but only in your colours, and play what you drafted. Count removal that hits fliers: a 1-2 BR had four such answers (Harvest Pyre, Corpse Lunge, Night Terrors, Geistcatcher's Rig) against many fliers (anecdote).
- Green decks with Somberwald Spider beat fliers; bring removal for it. Mono-colour with no evasion lost (G 2-4 in matches, 15 games); take a second colour with fliers over green filler.

Pairs by games won (decks, matches, game win rate), statistics to 2026-11-23:
- RG: 55% (108 games, 14 decks, 24-18 in matches). Best by rate, still small. Needs red removal or fliers, cheap blockers, and an answer to Claustrophobia and Victim of Night.
- BG: 54% (217 games, 30 decks, 50-40 in matches). Best by matches among big pairs. Cheap removal and blockers over a fourth fatty; keep a flier answer and reach (Spidery Grasp).
- WG: 52% (220 games, 30 decks, 49-41 in matches). Two guided WGs went 1-2 on 2026-11-23 (9 Forest/8 Plains; one first-pick off-colour); a conventional WG went 2-1 (anecdotes). Needs cheap plays, 10 sources for the main colour (Avacyn's Pilgrim helps), and evasion or removal.
- WU: 52% (232 games, 31 decks, 49-44 in matches). White removal plus cheap fliers (Midnight Haunting, Invisible Stalker, Chapel Geist, Abbey Griffin, Stitched Drake, Moorland Haunt) and blue pacifism auras work.
- WR: 51% (247 games, 34 decks, 52-50 in matches). Three guided WRs went 0-3 on 2026-11-23 (one cut off-colour first picks, one with five removal but ground bodies, one with few fliers; Smite the Monstrous was dead against Stitched Drake and Moon Heron). Earlier guided WRs went 2-1 each. Take Mentor of the Meek and Midnight Haunting over ground bodies like Village Ironsmith; save Rebuke and Brimstone Volley for fliers.
- BR: 50% (248 games, 34 decks, 53-49 in matches). A 1-2 BR on 2026-11-23 spent early picks on Brain Weevil (twice), Walking Corpse and Typhoid Rats, played Olivia Voldaren and went 3-5 in games (anecdote). Wants cheap removal, evasive finishers (Falkenrath Noble, Screeching Bat, Bloodline Keeper if castable) and card advantage. Skip Ancient Grudge and Heartless Summoning.
- WB: 46% (170 games, 23 decks, 33-36 in matches). Go white-first, count BB pips, take card-advantage finishers over Walking Corpse filler.
- UG: 49% (75 games, 10 decks, 14-16 in matches). Weak. A 2-1 UG on 2026-11-23 was carried by Festerhide Boar, Boneyard Wurm and Moldgraf Monstrosity but had a 4-3 game record (anecdote). Spider Spawning did nothing without self-mill.
- UR: 47% (171 games, 23 decks, 32-37 in matches). Mixed. A 3-0 UR on 2026-11-23 (6-2 in games, anecdote) ran cheap removal (Geistflame x2, Claustrophobia, Grasp of Phantoms), Devil's Play, Balefire Dragon, Stitched Drake and 15 creatures, cutting 6-drops. Make it red-first with burn and a cheap evasive clock.
- UB: 45% (266 games, 35 decks, 41-64 in matches). Worst big pair. Needs cheap blockers, a real finisher and answers, plus cheap pressure. Do not take Think Twice, Dissipate or Dream Twist.
- Mono U is 4-5 in matches (21 games), mono R 3-3 (16 games), mono G 2-4 (15 games).

General notes:
- White is the best-supported colour on individual cards (Slayer of the Wicked, Midnight Haunting, Mentor of the Meek, Elite Inquisitor, Bonds of Faith), though white pairs have been uneven. Red has Stromkirk Noble, Furor of the Bitten, Into the Maw of Hell, Kruin Outlaw, Ashmouth Hound. Green has Villagers of Estwald, Lumberknot, Gatstaf Shepherd. Cheap white removal (Rebuke, Fiend Hunter) is contested; take it early.
- Black is crowded and contested; take black removal early in pack 1, but only if you will play black.
- Read what is flowing after pack 1. If strong playables in one colour keep coming at picks 4-8, move in. Do not hedge with a second colour's removal once set.
- Galvanic Juggernaut (55%, 324 games, taken at 1.2) is fine as a colourless finisher, but take removal, Bonds of Faith or a flier over it. It dies to Smite the Monstrous.
- Games run 11 to 62 turns, mostly 15 to 47. Grindy removal and card advantage matter.
- A bomb alone does not carry a deck: Olivia Voldaren went 3-5 in games in a 17-land BR (anecdote). After the first pick, value playable two-drops and removal.
- Splashing is poor. Do not splash without a dual land and few cards.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal on colour: Sever the Bloodline, Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Prey Upon, Claustrophobia (blue), Tribute to Hunger, Rebuke, Brimstone Volley, Harvest Pyre, Geistflame, Into the Maw of Hell, Bonds of Faith, Fiend Hunter, Sensory Deprivation (blue), Silent Departure (blue), Grasp of Phantoms (blue), Corpse Lunge, Spidery Grasp, Urgent Exorcism (vs fliers), Smite the Monstrous (big creatures only), Blazing Torch. Take a second if offered. About five is the target, but five was not enough when the rest was ground bodies. Do not take removal outside your likely colours, and not second pick before you know them.
2. Strong uncommons and rares on colour: Slayer of the Wicked, Mentor of the Meek, Moldgraf Monstrosity, Falkenrath Noble, Morkrut Banshee (if you can support BB), Falkenrath Marauders, Instigator Gang, Elite Inquisitor, Stromkirk Noble, Murder of Crows (only if blue), Manor Gargoyle, Kruin Outlaw, Rally the Peasants, Midnight Haunting, Dearly Departed, Angelic Overseer, Devil's Play and Balefire Dragon (red), Spider Spawning (only with self-mill), Garruk Relentless (green only).
3. Evasive finishers and flier answers on colour: Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Invisible Stalker, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Lantern Spirit, Mindshrieker, Battleground Geist, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Scourge of Geier Reach, Somberwald Spider, Screeching Bat. Several lessons asked for evasion over ground filler; aim for three or four evasive threats or answers by the end of pack 3, and take a third flier over a second Unruly Mob, Silverchase Fox, Village Ironsmith, Thraben Purebloods, Festerhide Boar or a third Villagers of Estwald. Take an evasive two-drop like Orchard Spirit over Thraben Sentry or a second Woodland Sleuth. Do not take an off-colour flier over an on-colour one, and play fliers you draft. Card advantage (Mentor of the Meek, Midnight Haunting, Rally the Peasants) was asked for in several lessons.
4. Good cheap creatures: Typhoid Rats, Thraben Sentry, Elder Cathar, Woodland Sleuth, Villagers of Estwald, Avacyn's Pilgrim, Ambush Viper, Kindercatch, Darkthicket Wolf, Ashmouth Hound, Selfless Cathar, Furor of the Bitten, Pitchburn Devils, Tormented Pariah, Silver-Inlaid Dagger, Unruly Mob (one), Doomed Traveler, Avacynian Priest, Vampire Interloper, Bump in the Night. Take playable two- and three-drops over weak one-drops (Brain Weevil, Walking Corpse) and late junk such as Frightful Delusion, Cobbled Wings, Spare from Evil, Traveler's Amulet and Ranger's Guile. In UB, take real finishers and ground blockers over low-impact fliers and sorceries.

Late in a pack, take a playable creature over an off-colour spell or card draw. Walking Corpse is only a 23rd card.

Skip: Manor Skeleton, Stitcher's Apprentice, Cobbled Wings, Burning Vengeance, Think Twice, Night Terrors, Ghoulcaller's Bell, Ancient Grudge, Traveler's Amulet, Runic Repetition, Wreath of Geists, Maw of the Mire, Graveyard Shovel, Inquisitor's Flail, Butcher's Cleaver and Geistcatcher's Rig over a bomb or removal (though Geistcatcher's Rig answered a flier for some), Curse of Oblivion, Curse of the Bloody Tome, Dissipate, Rooftop Storm, Rolling Temblor, Fortress Crab, Rotting Fensnake, Gruesome Deformity, Mulch, Gnaw to the Bone, Memory's Journey, Brain Weevil, Dream Twist, Cellar Door, Bloodcrazed Neonate, Make a Wish, Grimoire of the Dead, Stensia Bloodhall, Creepy Doll, Trepanation Blade, Heartless Summoning, Curiosity, Nightbird's Clutches, Frightful Delusion, Traitorous Blood, Vampiric Fury, Reckless Waif over removal, Mask of Avacyn over a flier, Kessig Wolf, Cloistered Youth over a flier, Intangible Virtue, Ranger's Guile, a second Moment of Heroism or Lost in the Mist, a second Makeshift Mauler, and a third Festerhide Boar. Naturalize, Wooden Stakes and Sharpened Pitchfork are narrow filler. Armored Skaab, Skaab Goliath, Skaab Ruinator and Forbidden Alchemy are only for a deck with Spider Spawning and real self-mill. Avoid top-end bloat such as Markov Patrician.

## Building

- 17 lands is the default; long games support it. Cut to 16 only for a truly low curve with several two-drops (anecdotes). Splits seen: 12 Plains/5 Swamp (3-0 and 2-1 WB), 11 Plains/6 Swamp (3-0 and 1-2 WB), 9 Plains/8 Islands (3-0 WU), 10 Forest/7 Plains (3-0 and 2-1 WG), 11 Mountain/6 Forest (3-0 RG), 9 Mountain/8 Plains (2-1 WR), 8 Swamp/9 Mountain (1-2 BR) (anecdotes). Count pips and give the main colour 9 to 10 sources. 9 Forest/8 Plains WG went 0-3 and 1-2 (on 2026-11-23); WRs on 6 or 7 Mountains with a heavy red load went badly (anecdotes). Count Avacyn's Pilgrim as fixing.
- Double pips: give a BB or GG colour 8+ sources or cut them. A WB with Morkrut Banshee on 7 Swamps went 2-4 in games, and Bloodline Keeper on 7 Swamps lost (anecdotes). Avoid double pips in both colours. Three GG Somberwald Spiders want 8+ Forests.
- Aim for about five or six real removal spells (pumps do not count), three or four answers to fliers (Smite the Monstrous does not count), and two or three evasive or removal-backed finishers. Play the removal you drafted and the bomb you took.
- A low curve alone is not enough. 0-3 and 1-2 decks have been top-heavy, ground filler, flierless, or lacking pressure. Count two-drops and early evasive creatures, keep one or two cheap ground defenders in tempo decks, and trade a marginal two-drop or weak one-drop for a third flier or a removal spell. Cut 6-drops and filler (a 3-0 UR cut Geistcatcher's Rig, Rage Thrower, Rolling Temblor and Traitorous Blood) rather than forcing them in.
- Keep a flexible late threat or value source (Mentor of the Meek, Moan of the Unhallowed, Gavony Township, Moorland Haunt, Rally the Peasants, Divine Reckoning), but do not cut good cheap cards to fit one.
- Self-mill needs a real plan: either draft enablers and payoffs together or leave them out.
- Tricks have a place after removal and creatures; one or two cheap tricks is fine. Enchantment-based removal on your best creature is a real risk; keep multiple threats. Toughness above one matters against Geistflame, Brimstone Volley, Harvest Pyre and Tormented Pariah; power 4+ is exposed to Smite the Monstrous. Do not leave yourself with no answer to an opposing colour's key cards (Claustrophobia, Victim of Night, Tormented Pariah, Bonds of Faith).
- Settle your 23 by pack 3. Do not play cards you planned to cut (a 1-2 BR played Brain Weevil, Typhoid Rats and Walking Corpse anyway; anecdote); that means taking real playables, not filler, in the middle picks. Check every swap against your colours and do not side out main removal or threats for situational cards. Keep at least one cheap play main.
- Cut weak third-colour cards for a clean two-colour core. Secure cheap early plays before piling on top end.

## Playing

- Trade early, but hold Victim of Night, Dead Weight, Rebuke and Fiend Hunter for fliers and big blockers, not small ground bodies. Hold Smite the Monstrous for power 4+ creatures only. Check creature types before counting on Victim of Night.
- Expect long games, often 15 to 47 turns, and up to 62; short ones of 11 to 22 turns happen too. Break stalls with removal, Rally the Peasants, Moan of the Unhallowed and evasive threats. Apply pressure early rather than waiting, especially with UB, UR and WU.
- Block early with cheap creatures like Thraben Sentry and Elder Cathar against BR, RG and WR. Pair cheap creatures with late fliers or Rally the Peasants to close.
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Stitched Drake, Spectral Rider, Abbey Griffin, Orchard Spirit, Moon Heron, Lantern Spirit, Geist of Saint Traft, Angelic Overseer, Angel of Flight Alabaster, Manor Gargoyle, Invisible Stalker, Murder of Crows, Midnight Haunting, Falkenrath Noble, Screeching Bat, Bloodline Keeper, Galvanic Juggernaut, Boneyard Wurm, Balefire Dragon and Scourge of Geier Reach.
- Against WU, UB, UR, UG and mono-blue, kill fliers early (Delver of Secrets flips into a real threat). Claustrophobia, Sensory Deprivation, Grasp of Phantoms and Lost in the Mist will target your best creature, so keep a second threat ready. Blue decks win long grinds, so do not durdle. UR with Balefire Dragon, Devil's Play and two Geistflame beat a BG and a WB on 2026-11-23; hold removal for the Dragon and keep creatures with toughness above one.
- Against WB, expect real removal (Fiend Hunter, Smite the Monstrous, Rebuke, Victim of Night) held for your fliers and best creatures, plus Angelic Overseer, Chapel Geist and Rally the Peasants closing long games. Do not rely on one big threat.
- Against BG, WG and RG, expect Typhoid Rats, Somberwald Spider, Spidery Grasp, Villagers of Estwald, Bonds of Faith, Mentor of the Meek, Fiend Hunter, Prey Upon, Garruk Relentless, Tormented Pariah and burn to make the ground hard. Win in the air with removal backup, expect reach (kill Somberwald Spider first), and do not flood the board with ground bodies.
- Against WR, BR and UR, expect Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Harvest Pyre, Furor of the Bitten and Pitchburn Devils to punish small creatures. Do not overcommit and keep your own fliers alive. WR also runs Rebuke (often two), Urgent Exorcism, Smite the Monstrous, Midnight Haunting, Fiend Hunter, Slayer of the Wicked, Rally the Peasants and Bonds of Faith, and will answer your fliers and four-power creatures. Hold back threats rather than feeding them one at a time.
- Against BR with Bloodline Keeper, Screeching Bat and Falkenrath Noble (it beat two guided WRs 2-0 on 2026-11-22), save removal for Bloodline Keeper and the fliers, and have card advantage for 24-plus turn games.
- Against white decks with Fiend Hunter and Avacynian Priest, expect one-for-one trades on early creatures; prefer threats that leave value behind. Expect Rally the Peasants and Divine Reckoning in long games.
