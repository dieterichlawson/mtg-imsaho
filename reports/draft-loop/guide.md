# Innistrad draft guide

_Written by the nightly draft loop (`scripts/draft-loop/`) from 2026-10-28's lessons and every night's statistics. Drafters read it at every pick._

## Colours and archetypes

Eight nights: 64 decks, 233 games. Guided seats are 82-88 in games (48%, 34-38 in matches); unguided are 151-145 (51%). The guide has not been shown to help, so judge by the card numbers.

- BG is still the best pair on the totals: 28-14 in matches, 61% of games (100 games, 14 decks). Tonight it was bad: three BG decks went 3-6 in matches (one 0-3, one 2-1, one 1-2), and the 0-3 deck lost every game to WU and UR fliers and burn. Last night's 67% fell to 61%. Crowded BG with only ground bodies loses; BG with evasive finishers or removal still wins.
- WR 54% (57 games, 8 decks), WU 53% (60 games, 8 decks; tonight WU went 3-0 and 2-1), BR 50% (36 games, 5 decks), RG 50% (14 games).
- Weak: UR 45% (58 games, 8 decks), UB 43% (46 games), WG 43% (35 games), UG 38% (16 games).
- WB is 2-7 in matches, 27% of games (22 games, 3 decks). Thin, not proven.
- WU is no longer weak. Its 3-0 deck ran two Voiceless Spirit, two Slayer of the Wicked, Rebuke, Smite the Monstrous, Claustrophobia and Silent Departure (anecdote, one deck). Two WU drafters shared a table, so expect it to be contested.
- Games stall for 14 to 48 turns. Removal plus evasive finishers or Spider Spawning break stalls. Ground bodies alone and slow fliers did not (anecdotes).
- Green or black first, with white-blue or white-red as the alternatives. Pick a main colour by about pick 5 and a pair by mid pack 1. Do not move off a colour for one early removal spell unless the new colour is clearly open. Do not splash. A UR deck on five Mountains and twelve Islands could not cast its red (anecdote).
- Read what is passed to you. Two UR decks and two WU decks shared a table tonight, so take contested playables earlier or move.

## Pick order

1. Cheap removal: Victim of Night, Prey Upon, Dead Weight, Corpse Lunge, Brimstone Volley, Geistflame, Rebuke, Claustrophobia, Smite the Monstrous, Bonds of Faith, Fiend Hunter.
2. Strong uncommons and rares: Spider Spawning, Morkrut Banshee, Falkenrath Noble, Slayer of the Wicked, Falkenrath Marauders, Galvanic Juggernaut, Manor Gargoyle.
3. Evasive finishers on colour: Voiceless Spirit, Chapel Geist, Midnight Haunting, Stitched Drake, Moon Heron, Invisible Stalker. Take them over a fourth removal spell or a duplicate trick.
4. Good commons: Villagers of Estwald, Typhoid Rats, Kindercatch, Woodland Sleuth, Somberwald Spider, Festerhide Boar.

Take removal or evasion over filler. Do not take Ranger's Guile, a second Ambush Viper, or marginal tricks over a finisher. Skip equipment and filler like Moonmist, Gnaw to the Bone, Altar's Reap, Armored Skaab and the second Frightful Delusion. Take on-colour two-drops over speculation. Once your pair is set, do not take off-colour cards. Early picks in a colour you then abandon (Moan of the Unhallowed and Diregraf Ghoul in a red deck) are wasted.

## Building

- Land count is unsettled. 17 lands went 0-3 (BG, low curve) and 1-2 (BG), but also 3-0 and 2-1 earlier. Play 16 with a low curve (try this), 17 with several 5-drops.
- Count pips first. Split lands by pips and card quality, and avoid double pips in both colours. A 9 Forest and 6 Swamp BG deck with double-black Victim of Night went 6-1 in games (anecdote).
- Play 5 or more two-drops, at least two evasive or removal-backed finishers, and at least two answers to fliers. Spiders (Somberwald Spider) and Spider Spawning answer fliers in green.
- Cap ground filler (Markov Patrician, Walking Corpse type cards). A deck of ground bodies lost 0-6 in games tonight (anecdote).
- Keep removal count at 4 or more in the main, but trade one spare removal for a finisher if you have none.
- Do not main Naturalize. Leave Delver of Secrets out of two-colour decks if a better clock is available (anecdote).

## Playing

- Trade early and hold removal for fliers and big blockers. Cheap early pressure ended games in 14 and 27 turns (anecdote).
- Break stalls with removal, fight spells, Spider Spawning and evasive threats, not more ground bodies. Fast fliers beat slow evasion like Spectral Flight (anecdote).
- Spread threats, since Rebuke and Smite the Monstrous kill attackers. Expect opposing Victim of Night to kill your best early threat.
- Keep removal for Chapel Geist, Voiceless Spirit, Abbey Griffin and Murder of Crows. Slow fliers like Moon Heron lost to BG earlier (anecdote), so pressure early or hold the ground.
- Against BG, expect Typhoid Rats and Somberwald Spider to make the ground hard. Win through the air or with removal.
