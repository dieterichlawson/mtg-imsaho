# Playtest night — 2026-08-29

Three agents played the Competitor, Rules Lawyer, and Vandal personas in
parallel, hotseat self-play through the real CLI, 10 games each (30 games
total, 15 missions). Details per mission are in `reports/playtests/LEDGER.md`.

## Issues filed

9 new issues, `bug` + `phase:playtest`: **#35–#43**. 5 were already fixed and
closed by the Daily bug fixer during the night (#35, #40, #41, #42, #43); 4
remain open (#36, #37, #38, #39).

- **#35** (closed) — declare-attackers/blockers free-text input line retains
  stale characters from a rejected entry and silently misparses the next
  entry; in one variant it silently declared an attacker with no error at
  all. Found by Vandal (V1), reproduced again by Rules Lawyer (L4) on the
  blockers side (added as a comment, more dangerous variant: a legal block
  was silently dropped rather than mis-numbered).
- **#36** (open) — activated/loyalty abilities that target a player show
  indistinguishable duplicate menu entries, risking accidental
  self-targeting. Found by Competitor; reproduced twice more (Stensia
  Bloodhall, Liliana of the Veil -2) across C5/C7.
- **#37** (open) — the `rr` hot-reload shortcut reads/writes a hardcoded
  global `/tmp/mtg-hot-reload.json`, so with multiple concurrent
  `mtg-runner` processes on the same host (exactly this routine's setup),
  pressing `rr` can silently swap in an unrelated session's game. Found by
  Vandal (V3). Most severe finding of the night.
- **#38** (open) — the "look at top N, choose one" library picker (e.g.
  Forbidden Alchemy) renders raw `obj#NN` placeholders instead of card
  names in both its list and detail header, even though the log line
  printed right after resolves real names correctly. Found by Vandal (V7);
  reproduced twice more (Skaab Ruinator/Stitched Drake, Unburial Rites)
  across Competitor's C4/C5/C7 games.
- **#39** (open) — `f` auto-pass can skip an entire opposing turn's land-play
  opportunity. Found by Competitor (C4); a more damaging variant (2
  consecutive missed land drops forcing a discard to hand size) reproduced
  in C7.
- **#40** (closed) — declare-blockers menu lists non-flying/non-reach
  creatures as legal blockers against a flyer, then silently drops the
  illegal block instead of erroring. Found by Competitor (C5, Bitterheart
  Witch repro added as comment).
- **#41** (closed) — an anthem'd creature incorrectly died when its anthem
  source died from lethal combat damage in the same state-based-action
  check (dependency/ordering bug). Found by Competitor (C4).
- **#42** (closed) — Concede's (y/n) confirmation prompt swallows invalid
  input with no error or re-prompt. Found by Competitor (C4).
- **#43** (closed) — declare-blockers' invalid-input error message shows the
  wrong syntax example (`0->0`) for the accepted `blocker:attacker` format.
  Found by Competitor (C5).

## Clean missions (no bugs found)

L1 (stack battles), L5 (cost edges), L7 (zone identity), L8 (SBA order), V2
(the wrong number), and V4 (degenerate decks) all completed their full game
counts with no confirmed engine bugs — the Rules Lawyer and Vandal agents
verified several suspected issues against the Comprehensive Rules and ruled
them correct (first-strike/double-strike damage steps, intervening-if
werewolf transforms, characteristic-defining 0/0 creatures dying to SBA,
curse upkeep triggers stacking correctly under simultaneous resolution,
library depletion at scale).

## Notable moments

- Competitor's C5 games both went the distance chasing the planeswalker
  plan — game 2 closed at turn 25 via a reanimated Moldgraf Monstrosity.
- Vandal's V4 built a 24-card all-five-color curses pile and stacked 2
  curses simultaneously on one player without a crash across ~18 turns.
- Rules Lawyer's L7 confirmed a returned-and-recast Diregraf Ghoul got a
  genuinely new internal object ID (`#69` → `#72`) with counters and
  summoning sickness reset, and separately confirmed marked combat damage
  clears at cleanup while +1/+1 counters persist — both correct, distinct
  mechanisms.
- Two minor UX-only observations were noted but not filed (out of scope for
  the Rules Lawyer mission, no CR violation): `ActivateAbility` menu entries
  never show target names, and this overlaps with #38's picker-naming bug.
