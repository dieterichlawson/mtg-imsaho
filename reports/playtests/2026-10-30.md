## Method notes

- Under the sandbox's proxy, Chromium sends `ws://` to a non-loopback `--bind` address through `HTTPS_PROXY` and gets a 502. Unset `HTTPS_PROXY`/`https_proxy` in the driver's environment.
- Wait one frame (about 250 ms) after `mtg.decision` arrives before reading `mtg.hits`.
- `STUB_CALLS` must be an absolute path: a relative one fails inside the child, and the seat retries for 9 minutes.
