## G36: every driver failure has an exit code

**Ran:** about 70 runs of port-patched copies of every driver, and 12 rehearsal tables. Cases: binary missing, port held by a dummy listener or by a real runner, a silent port, a page that never loads, no Chromium, refused arguments, game ports held, and signals.

**#871 holds:**
- An all-human table exits 0 with a true tally: 84 picks, 2 game pages, 434 decisions.
- Every early FAIL tried exits 1.

**What is wrong:**
- **Exit 0 on games never played (#941):** a driver plays whatever process holds its port, and the rehearsal exits 0 having played no game.
- **FAIL, then no exit (#942):** a match that cannot end keeps the rehearsal alive forever, and a Chromium failure before the `try` hangs some drivers and orphans the runners of others.
- **Wrong reasons (#943):** a runner's refusal is reported as a decision timeout, a silent port as a 21-minute retry, and `--games four` as "NaN game(s) … ok".
- **A false "stuck" (#944):** autoplay's blocker step clicks a flyer its blocker cannot block. The page then refuses "No blocks", and the rehearsal stops there.

## G38: request heads against both servers

**Ran:**
- About 340 raw-socket probes against the game page, the lobby, the lobby's game proxy and the keyed game port.
- About 25 timed Chromium loads with 0-40 KB of cookies and 50 idle sockets.

**#933 holds up to 16 KiB:**
- Cookies of 1.5-9 KiB before or after `Upgrade`, in one write or in five pieces, give 101 everywhere.
- `Upgrade:websocket`, `UPGRADE: WebSocket`, a tab and `Connection: keep-alive, Upgrade` give 101.
- `upgrade: websocket` inside a cookie value, the query or the body is never taken for an upgrade.
- 50 silent, partial or trickling sockets slow no real load: the view still arrived in 104-133 ms.

**Past `HEAD_LIMIT`:**
- An upgrade with its `Upgrade` line after 16 KiB is a 404 again (#935). Chromium sends `Upgrade` before `Cookie` and is safe; Firefox, by report, does not.
- Through the proxy, a large head gets truncated responses and a black page (#936).

**Noted under #935:** `Upgrade: websocket, h2c` passes the router and then closes with no status line.

**Checked, acceptable:** a head that never ends is answered with the page after 10 s (20 s through the proxy). It stalls nobody else.
