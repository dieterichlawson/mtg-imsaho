## D43: the time between games (re-run; last 2026-10-22)

The table was `table_rehearsal.js` copied to scratch, with two people at a two-seat table, best-of-3, seed 4301. Before game 2, a raw WebSocket on seat 0's join key sent every deck the server must refuse.

- **Refused, each with its reason:**
  - 39 cards
  - a card from the other seat's pool (Cobbled Wings)
  - Gavony Township
  - Wastes
  - a negative land count (as "unreadable message", which no page sends)
  - a second copy of a card drafted once
  - `sideboard` after `sideboard_ready`
  - `play_draw` from the non-chooser
- **Taken:** a card named as `Front // Something Else`, recorded by its front face as #403 requires. Also taken: a card moved out and back in, and the same deck with `Plains: 0`. After those, the host's terminal and the log read `SIDEBOARD before game 2: no change`. Holds.
- **Chooser:** the game-1 loser was asked (seat 1 after seat 0 won game 1). Game 2's log is "p0 on the play" after seat 1 chose to draw. Holds.
- **#998, found:** a second run (seed 4302) had the chooser send play, ready, then draw, while the other seat waited 6 s. The server took the change after ready, and it decided who played, in game 2 and game 3. The page disables those buttons once ready. The terminal client sends `play_draw` after `ready` without a word.
- **A trap in the driver, not in the program:** `page.click` auto-waits up to 30 s. A probe that holds the rehearsal loop past the moment it read the view lets that loop's `#sideboard-ready` click land on the *next* game's between. Seat 0 was then ready for game 3 without choosing ("no choice was made"). The guide now says so.
