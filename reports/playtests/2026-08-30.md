# Playtest night — 2026-08-30

Three agents played the Competitor, Rules Lawyer, and Vandal personas in
parallel, hotseat self-play through the real CLI, 10 games each (30 games
total, 15 missions). All missions from the last two weeks (2026-08-29) were
off the menu; five new missions were introduced (C9, L9, L10, V9, V10) and
added to `docs/plans/playtest-missions.md`. Details per mission are in
`reports/playtests/LEDGER.md`.

## Issues filed

4 new issues, `bug` + `phase:playtest`: **#45–#48**. All are open as of this
report.

- **#45** (open) — `f` auto-pass on your own turn can skip a castable spell
  in your own Main Phase 1. Found by Competitor (C9), tracked separately
  from the related #48.
- **#46** (open) — Memory's Journey's interactive cast flow offers a
  graveyard card belonging to the *wrong* player as a legal target, and only
  ever lets the caster choose one card instead of "up to three." The
  resolve-time logic has a safety net that prevents real state corruption
  (it silently skips a wrongly-owned card and shuffles the target player's
  library per the real card's Wizards ruling), but the illegal target
  should never have been offered at cast time (CR 601.2c). Root-caused to
  `build_cast_target_spec`'s `TwoTargets` arm in
  `mtg-engine/src/engine/targeting.rs` not narrowing the second target slot
  by the first target's choice, unlike the correct
  `generate_cast_actions_with_targets` path. Found by Rules Lawyer (L2).
- **#47** (open) — the game-over screen isn't cleared before printing the
  final "Game over! / Total actions / Final turn" summary, so it visually
  merges with the leftover TUI frame (e.g. `"Total actions: 59zed"`). Purely
  cosmetic (the log file's numbers are correct) but reliably reproducible
  on every ending path. Found by Vandal (V6), reproduced independently via
  a V5 natural deck-out too.
- **#48** (open) — pressing `f` discards the current prompt's land-play or
  spell-cast option before the auto-pass break-check runs, so a legal
  action available at the moment `f` is pressed can be silently lost. Found
  by Competitor (C9); related to the already-fixed #39 auto-pass family of
  bugs but a distinct mechanism.

## Clean missions (no bugs found)

C2 (control mirror), C3 (attrition), C6 (equipment voltron vs token swarm),

## Notable moments

- Rules Lawyer confirmed a werewolf transforming back and forth (Daybreak
  Ranger ⇄ Nightfall Predator) keeps the *same* object ID both directions —
  a transform is not a new object, unlike a zone change, and the engine
  gets this right.
- Rules Lawyer confirmed "can't attack" correctly beats "must attack" when
  both apply to the same creature simultaneously (CR 101.2c) — the creature
  was omitted from the eligible-attackers list entirely, neither offered
  nor force-attacked. Separately confirmed a pure "must attack" effect
  (no conflicting "can't") really does force the attack even when the
  player answers `none`.
- Rules Lawyer directly verified CR 605.3a live: activating a mana ability
  in response to a targeted spell on the stack leaves the stack size
  unchanged and returns to the identical priority prompt instantly, with
  no missed-priority or fizzle-timing anomalies.
- L9 (replacement effects) turned up an honest negative result worth
  flagging for future mission curation: the br-coverage/wg-coverage pairing
  has no two cards that replace the *same* event, so CR 616's
  "affected object's controller chooses the order" logic could not actually
  be exercised. A future run of L9 should pick a pairing with a genuine
  dual-replacement collision.
- Vandal's V9 confirmed the fix for the previously-closed #37 (PID-scoped
  hot-reload file) holds under rapid, low-sleep relaunch churn — no
  cross-process state bleed across ~12 launch/concede/relaunch cycles.
- Vandal's V10 mashed bare pass-priority and `f` with 50-80ms gaps across
  hundreds of keypresses per game (one ran to turn 108, 204 actions) with
  no hangs, panics, or `INVARIANT` log lines, and traced every
  discard-to-hand-size prompt through a 106-turn stretch to confirm none
  were silently skipped under the mash.
- Competitor's C9 (ub-coverage vs wg-coverage) played two extremely close
  races, both sides dipping to 1-3 life; game 2 was sealed by countering
  the opponent's last flying blocker with Dissipate while they sat at 1
  life.
- Two deck-file path slips: Competitor substituted `wg-coverage` for
  `gw-humans` (C6) and `ub-coverage`/`wg-coverage` for `ub-zombies`/
  `gw-humans` (C9) after searching under `decks/coverage/` instead of the
  top-level `decks/` directory where those tribal decks actually live —
  noted here since it changed the deck pairing actually played, not because
  it's an engine bug.
