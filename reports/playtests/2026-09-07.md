# Playtest night — 2026-09-07

**Crew**: 9 testers in parallel, three personas, one tmux socket each.
**Games**: 34 driven through the real CLI (9 Competitor, 9 Rules Lawyer, 16
Vandal), all hotseat `--p1 cli --p2 cli` with both seats played by hand, plus
~12 minimal-repro replays and ~55 non-interactive probe runs. Nearly all under
`--check-invariants`.
**Zero panics, zero hangs and zero invariant failures in any game.**
**Issues**: 22 filed (#313–#334), one cross-reference comment.

Every mission id in the guides — C1–C34, L1–L36, V1–V35, M1–M5 — had been
played inside the two-week floor, so all nine of tonight's missions are new
ideas written for it: C35–C37, L37–L39 and V36–V38. The untried D and H ideas
were left alone; they need `cc` seats and this was a game-and-CLI night.

## The three that matter

**#319 — a permanent that enters as a copy loses every static continuous
ability of the copied card.** `become_copy_of` ends by writing an empty
instance-effect list, and `continuous_effects_of` treats `Some(_)` as a total
override. An Evil Twin copying Grimgrin, Corpse-Born untapped in its own untap
step and was offered as an attacker while the real Grimgrin beside it stayed
tapped. Everything else about the copy was right — name, P/T, types, legendary
flag, "enters tapped", triggered and activated abilities, the Evil Twin grant —
and the inspector even prints the ability the permanent does not have. This is
the shared path for Evil Twin *and* Essence of the Wild, so the same loss would
hit Mayor of Avabruck's anthem, Invisible Stalker's "can't be blocked", Elite
Inquisitor's protection, Angelic Overseer and Instigator Gang.

**#323 — two replacement or prevention effects on one damage event are applied
in a hardcoded order and the affected player is never asked (CR 616.1).**
`damage.rs::deal_damage_to_player` runs prevention → multiplier → replacement.
Same 2/2 Walking Corpse, four combats, one game: 2 real damage → mill 2 → **mill
4 with Inquisitor's Flail** → prevented by Ghostly Possession. Per CR 616.1 the
defending player picks the order; picking Undead Alchemist first leaves the
Flail with no damage event to double, so the right answer is mill 2.
`replacement.rs::apply` still carries a comment asserting no board in this card
pool can produce two effects applicable to one event. It can, twice, and both of
the extra effects live outside that loop.

**#314 — the save file chooses the player backend.** Since #248,
`SaveData.seats` is an unvalidated string handed straight to `make_player`, so
`--resume file.save` with no seat flags starts whatever seats the *file* names —
a metered API seat and a file-chosen model included — on a command line that
mentions neither, and `--quiet` deletes the banner that was the only
announcement. Verified safely: no API key is set on this host, so both metered
seats died at the key guard and none was ever spawned. Its neighbour **#315** is
the same shape one field over: `player_names` is interpolated raw into the
banner, the game-over line and the `--log` `GAME_START`, so a save can set your
terminal title and clear your screen, and `cat`ing the log re-fires it.
`sanitize_for_display` exists (from #282) but is wired only into the input echo.

## Competitor

- **C35 token deck vs the sweeper** (3 games). Parallel Lives is correct on both
  halves of CR 614.1b — the same card cast from both seats with one Parallel
  Lives out made 4 tokens for its controller and 2 for the opponent. Curse of
  Death's Hold turning four freshly-doubled 1/1 Spirits into 0/0s that all died
  on the spot is the tidiest 614.1b → 7c → 704.5f demonstration of the night.
  CR 111.7 verified in three zones; Blasphemous Act priced at exactly `{R}` at 31
  creatures; Divine Reckoning prompted APNAP and skipped a creatureless player.
  The break is the log: #92's fix landed only on the two cards that issue named,
  so Army of the Damned still says "created 13 tapped Zombie tokens" when 26
  enter — which understates a lethal board by 13 attackers (#329).
- **C36 +1/+1 counters as an accumulating clock** (3 games, 59 turns, 1331
  actions). **Nothing broke.** One trigger per combat-damage event and never per
  point; four simultaneous triggers on one Marauders hit resolved as one CR
  603.3b group into exactly 5 counters; APNAP correct when the opponent cursed
  *themselves* so the Curse and the Heir had different controllers; damage to a
  blocker and to a planeswalker fired nothing; trample excess did fire, then
  correctly landed nowhere because the trampler died in the same step (CR 121.1);
  counters ran 0→3→6→9 with zero drift and a Marauders exiled with 8 counters
  reads 2/2.
- **C37 creature type as a live resource** (3 games). Every Human-conditional
  recomputed live. One Bonds of Faith switched between its two halves on each
  werewolf transform with no re-target and no new object; Butcher's Cleaver lost
  lifelink in the same window as an instant-speed Moonmist; Angelic Overseer's
  hexproof appeared mid-stack when a flash Human resolved and fizzled the Smite
  already targeting it (CR 608.2b), then vanished at the very SBA that killed the
  last Human. Only defect is a pane overflow (#328). Two corrections for the
  guide: Furor of the Bitten is not a type changer, and every ISD werewolf front
  face is already "Human Werewolf".

## Rules Lawyer

- **L37 abilities outside the battlefield** (3 games + 2 repro runs). Flashback
  itself is solid: only the owner is ever offered a graveyard card, sorcery-speed
  flashback and the Skaab Ruinator graveyard cast are absent from instant-speed
  menus rather than offered-then-rejected, and all three exits — resolution, a
  counter, and a fizzle — exile the card and never return it. Burning Vengeance
  is right about everything except the zone: it is gated on the flashback flag,
  so Skaab Ruinator's graveyard cast never triggers it (#330). Runic Repetition's
  filter is exactly right and its target list is unusable, because the CLI's name
  resolver never looks in exile (#332).
- **L38 damage replaced by mill** (3 games + 2 repro replays). Every "the damage
  never happened" claim held, including the sharpest check of the night: a 6/9
  trampling Skaab Goliath blocked by a Grave Bramble with protection from Zombies
  assigned 4 lethal to the Bramble (prevented, correctly without reducing the
  assignment) and only the 2 excess became a mill. The two-Alchemist 2011 ruling
  is implemented to the letter. What broke is #323 above. Also #324, cosmetic:
  `protections_of` pluralises with a literal `+s`, so Elite Inquisitor reads
  "protection from Werewolfs" beside a CARDS pane saying "Werewolves".
- **L39 the name as a game object** (3 games + 3 repro runs). CR 704.5j is per
  player and per name; the keep-choice goes only to the duplicate's controller;
  an Olivia stolen with `{3}{B}{B}` and lost to the legend rule reached its
  *owner's* graveyard (CR 404.3); a Cackling Counterpart token copy fired the
  rule then ceased to exist with no residue; Nevermore's name was chosen as it
  entered, removed the banned spell *and* its graveyard flashback from both
  seats' menus, accepted a card in neither deck, and lifted in the same priority
  window Urgent Exorcism killed it. Two guide corrections: Sever the Bloodline's
  real Oracle text has no "its controller controls" clause, and the engine's
  cross-controller exile is correct — do not "fix" it toward the brief. #333 is
  the night's most annoying gap: nothing anywhere in the CLI ever says a
  permanent is Legendary, so the legend rule fires with no prior warning.

## Vandal

- **V36 the trigger-ordering prompt** (4 games + 2 `--resume` forks). Reached
  `ChooseTriggerOrder` seven times with groups of 2, 3, 5, 8, 10, 11 and 12,
  including both players ordering in one event. The reader held against 24
  hostile inputs — including `0 0 1`, the exact shape that broke the attackers
  reader in #108 — with `--save` md5 and `--log` length identical before and
  after the whole battery. The rules half was right everywhere: APNAP, re-raise
  until interchangeable, strict LIFO, and two resumes of one saved prompt
  answered oppositely producing two different stacks. What broke is the shell:
  past 8 options `choose_action`'s `naming_cards` gate (written for Nevermore in
  #255) swallows the prompt into the card browser, and board, stack, log and every
  pane shortcut disappear mid-decision, flipping back at 8 remaining (#325).
- **V37 the cardinality prompts** (8 runs, 3 played out). The mission's premise
  was wrong in a useful way: there is no family of multi-select readers. The
  engine enumerates `combinations(hand, N)` and the CLI shows them as an ordinary
  numbered menu, so "duplicate / fewer than N / more than N" are *inexpressible*
  at bottoming and cleanup discard. That is a real robustness win — and it is
  exactly what creates #318, because one menu row now has to name three cards
  inside 52 columns, and 14 of 25 distinct `BOTTOM 3` options render as 5
  identical lines with no id, for a choice that is mandatory and irreversible.
- **V38 the resume boundary** (9 games/positions + ~55 probes). The state half of
  resume is solid: six chained resumes deep-diff to **zero differing fields**
  against one uninterrupted 30-decision run, 20 of 20 SIGKILL-mid-play saves
  resumed with no torn or zero-length file, and seven hostile save paths all gave
  a clean exit-1 with an honest message. Everything *around* the state leaks —
  #313 through #317 — and V27's "resuming a finished game is refused cleanly" no
  longer holds (#316).

## Notes for the next crew

- Two crews filed the same token-naming defect independently (#331 from C35,
  #334 from L39); cross-referenced on #334 so the fixer closes one as a
  duplicate. The two reports are complementary — #331 comes at it from Sever the
  Bloodline's name matching, #334 from the CR text.
- Keystrokes typed ahead at a priority prompt are consumed by the *other* seat's
  next priority prompt of the same kind (#71's design). Batching inputs silently
  plays the opponent's turn; one keystroke per capture is the only safe cadence
  in a hotseat game.
- `pkill -f "<name>.save"` matches the driver shell's own command line. The
  README's "target by seed, never a blanket pkill" rule extends to `-f` patterns;
  a `/proc/*/cmdline` filter over `pgrep -x mtg-runner` is the safe form.
- Unreached and worth budgeting for: a real six-land mana base in the L37 deck
  (it stalled on three and left Back from the Brink, Past in Flames and nine
  other cards untouched), a 9+ `ChooseDamageAssignmentOrder`, and a trigger
  ordering whose final *board* differs rather than only the stack and log.
