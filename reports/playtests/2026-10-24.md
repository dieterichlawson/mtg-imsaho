**The ones to read first:**
- **#930.** X funding's answer check refuses the shape its own parser was written to accept. The amounts are string enums, and the format line types them `string`. An answer in numbers is re-asked every time ("`floating.black` is 0; it must be one of \"0\""), then accepted on the second try: one extra call per X spell. An answer that names only the colours actually floating is re-asked too ("`white` is missing"). That happens because the line asks for six pool colours, and an empty pool can only fill them with "0".
- **#931.** #920's game twin records `MTG_LLM_HISTORY` and `MTG_CLAUDE_CODE_ANSWERS` in a save, but not `MTG_LLM_PASS_UNTIL`. That setting adds 1,359 B to the system prompt and moves every priority row down one. A kept position replayed under the other setting gets another question, with no note.
- **#932.** Who Abattoir Ghoul damaged this turn is on no surface after one prompt, because `GameView` has no `damaged_by`. A stub game cast Victim of Night on the Crab the Ghoul had hit and gained 6 life. The prompt it decided from showed the Crab as `[S,3dmg]` and nothing else.
- **#933.** Both servers decide "page or WebSocket" by a substring search over the first 2 KiB of the request head. An upgrade whose `Upgrade:` header falls after a long Cookie header is served as a page: the game page answers 200, the lobby 404. So does `Upgrade:websocket` with no space.

## H39: every field the format line leaves out

**Setup:**
- The stand-in `claude` reads nothing but the `Answer with only this JSON object:` line. It has a small parser for the line's type grammar, and it fills every field from the type alone.
- It wrapped its answers six ways: plain; "rich" (optional fields filled, lists of 0-2 entries); prose with `{G}{G}` and `{T}` around the object; a draft object (`"action": 999`) before the final one; a trailing `}`; a code fence.
- Games: `--p1 cc --p2 random` over the ten coverage decks, 31 games. Then nine Mikaeus/Plains games to reach X funding, answered three ways: numbers, strings, and only the floating colours.

**Read: every wrapping, 0 UNREADABLE in about 3,700 calls.** `json_in_text` takes the last whole object. It took the final answer after a draft, and it skipped the mana symbols and the stray brace.

**Re-asked: X funding, #930.** Numbers were re-asked 14 of 14 times, and "only the floating colours" 14 of 14. Strings for all six colours: 0 of 14.

**Re-asks that were the stand-in's own fault:**
- `card_indices` and `order` count re-asks. The count is in the prompt ("pick 1 card", "as many as the prompt says"), not in the line.
- `then` names. Its values are the hand's card names, which the system prompt describes.

**Not reached:** an ordering of nothing, and an "up to N" set with nothing eligible.
