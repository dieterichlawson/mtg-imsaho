## G21 — `s` cannot keep its promise (#645)

I copied `mtg-gui/tests/autoplay.js`, pressed `s` once the first decision was
up (the page reads back `stopAtPass: true`), and counted two things on every
decision: pass-only menus, and decisions with `view.stack.length > 0`. I ran
the same seed and decks twice:

| runner | decisions | pass-only stops | stack visible |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--check-invariants` | 588 | 473 | 32 (Swords to Plowshares, Doom Blade, Lightning Bolt, creature spells…) |
| no flag (as `mtg-gui/README.md` invokes it) | 115 | **0** | 2 (only when the seat held an instant) |

The engine passes priority for a seat with no meaningful action and only
calls the seat with a pass-only menu under `observe_every_submit`. The runner
sets that flag only for `--check-invariants`. So `s` does nothing in an
ordinary game, and `f`'s "stops for a spell on the stack" never fires either,
because the page never sees the spell.

## G18 — a notice belongs to one tab but is sent to all (#647, #648)

There were two tabs on one seat at the mulligan. Tab A sent `{Nope: 1}`, then
`"AbandonGame"`. Each time, **tab B**, which had sent nothing, showed the
refusal text (`not an action: unknown variant \`Nope\`…`, `AbandonGame is the
harness's, not a player's`). Tab A got its decision back, which is correct.

The `seq: 0` half: `mtgSend(undefined)` produces a message the seat cannot
parse. The reply is `Notice { seq: 0 }`, and `main.ts` can only restore the
decision on an exact `seq` match. Tab A sat at `seq: null`, with no widget
and no way to answer, until another tab answered. Its `unreadable message`
notice then stayed on screen over the next decision.

Both are reachable only from the console today, and the issues say so.

## G19 — the typed field after a refusal: checked, correct

I staged a `ChooseXFunding` at the live `seq` and the X box came up focused.
Then I sent `AbandonGame`. The refusal notice restored the decision, and
`draw()`'s trailing `syncField()` put the box back, visible and focused,
with no click needed: `{ widget: 'number', field: { display: 'block',
focused: true } }`. The idea's worry (`beginDecision` without `syncField`)
does not happen, because every frame ends in `syncField`.
