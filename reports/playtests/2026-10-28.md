## V60: the turn's facts on the CLI

One hotseat game was played to turn 12 with Civilized Scholar, Darkthicket
Wolf and Moonmist against Grizzly Bears. Moonmist transforms Humans, so it
flips the Scholar into Homicidal Brute. **Checked, correct:**
- The rule reads "BATTLEFIELD (Moonmist in force this turn)", fits at 60
  columns, and is gone on the next turn's upkeep.
- The inspector says "Once-per-turn ability used this turn: true" after
  the pump, and "Attacked this turn: true" only once the Brute has left
  combat.
- Moonmist prevented the Bears' and the Brute's combat damage and not the
  Wolf's (CR 615).

  one that attacked stayed.

Hotseat trap: when the seat that acts next has nothing to do, the engine
moves straight to the other seat, and an Enter meant as "pass" answers the
other player's main phase. Twice I took this for a bug; both times it was
the tester.

## M3: determinism from the outside (re-run)

The subagent recorded 4 keystroke scripts and replayed them 31 times, 35
games in all, changing one thing at a time:
- `--check-invariants`, `--save` and `--log`
- 80x24 against 200x50, and a mid-game resize
- every viewer opened before every answer
- 12 kills (SIGKILL, SIGTERM, SIGHUP, Ctrl-C) followed by `--resume`,
  including mid-card-browser and mid-set-screen

**Every replay's log (`cut -f4-`) and final save were identical.** A
resume differs only by its header lines. A kill between the sub-prompts
of one decision rewinds to that decision's start, every time. The CLI
seat answers #956's observed passes without reading input and without
ending auto-pass. One finding: the STACK pane cuts an entry mid-sentence
and also counts it in "N more" (on #959).
