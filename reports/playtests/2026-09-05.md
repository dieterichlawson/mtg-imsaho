## The night in one sentence per subject

**The rules engine is in good shape; what surrounds it is not.** Of roughly
seventy CR claims checked by hand tonight, sixty-seven held. Whole areas came
back clean on every single check: static prohibitions (L29 — nothing named by
Nevermore, silenced by Stony Silence or hexproofed by Witchbane Orb was ever
offered-and-then-rejected), the sacrifice family (L31 — cost timing, who
chooses, thirteen dies-triggers off six simultaneous deaths, and sacrifice
beating both regeneration and indestructible), alternative costs and cost
reduction (C26), X spells (C29), the nonbasic mana base (C27), and Laboratory
Maniac's replacement of the fatal draw (L30). Three engine defects survived
that scrutiny, and all three are one card's arithmetic rather than a broken
mechanism:

- **#246** — trample past an attacked planeswalker's loyalty spills onto its
  *controller's life total*. A 5/2 trampler unblocked into a 3-loyalty Liliana
  dealt 3 to her and 2 to the player. CR 510.1a and 702.19b allow assignment
  only among blockers and the entity being attacked. The wrong behaviour is
  pinned by an existing test, `planeswalker_combat.rs::trample_past_the_walker_spills_to_the_player`,
  and the code cites a rule number (702.19i) that does not exist — so the
  fixer has to change the test too.
- **#253** — Olivia Voldaren's `{3}{B}{B}` is a no-op when you already control
  the target, because the control helper early-returns and records no effect.
  Steal a creature with Traitorous Blood, point Olivia at it, and the log says
  "gains control" while the creature goes home at cleanup.
- **#252** — the auto-tap planner pays generic costs before coloured ones, so
  Shimmering Grotto's `{1}` filter eats the very mana it just made; the offered
  cast is then silently refused with a Debug-only log line and nothing happens.

**Two prompts take irreversible actions the player did not ask for.** #249:
the concede confirmation reads a *single key*, so typing "maybe" concedes the
game instantly, with no Enter — and the save is deleted with the game. #254: a
spell whose only legal target is you is cast at yourself with no prompt, no
confirmation and no Cancel, and the menu row never names its target; C28 hit
the same auto-pick destroying its own Stony Silence.

**The screen does not always describe the game.** V28's whole mission was
rendering accuracy, and five panes disagree with the engine: a transformed
double-faced card still shows its front face in the CARDS reference, `/`
search and `i` inspect (#238, #240), the `d` browser counts permanents it
then omits (#241), no pane anywhere shows a permanent's live keywords (#243)
or its combat state (#245). V29 found the STACK pane showing 2½ of 94 objects
with no indicator (#247); V26 found the whole action menu, its truncation
marker and every error notice invisible below 24 rows (#260); C27 found the
`Lands:` line overrunning the frame (#244). The common shape is a renderer
reading printed card data instead of the live view.

**Saves and the process around them.** V30 read a live save 34,638 times
across two full games and resumed all 2,173 distinct snapshots — every one
loaded, no torn writes, 39 of 40 SIGKILLs mid-write left a resumable file.
The atomic-write fix from #75/#76 holds completely. The defects are all
*around* the file: a world-readable `/tmp` hot-reload save holding both
players' hands is stranded on every abnormal exit (#239, 87 files after one
night), `--save` unlinks whatever is at its path when the game ends (#242,
#237), and `--resume` silently re-seats a two-human save with a `random` bot
that immediately takes irreversible actions (#248).

## Missions

### Rules Lawyer

- **L26 planeswalker combat leftovers** (the idea proposed 2026-09-04, first
  played tonight). Twelve of thirteen CR claims held — per-attacker targeting,
  the split damage of two attackers at different targets, killing the attacked
  walker after blockers (the attacker correctly deals *nothing*), first-strike
  ordering, and CR 509.1h. The trample leg failed: **#246**.
- **L28 change-of-control effects** (new). Layer 2 had never been exercised.
  A stolen creature correctly loses its old controller's anthem, is summoning
  sick, leaves combat as attacker *and* as blocker while the attacker stays
  blocked, and dies to its owner's graveyard; Traitorous Blood expires at
  cleanup and killing Olivia snaps control back mid-combat. **#253**, **#256**.
- **L29 static prohibitions** (new). Every rule correct, including Nevermore's
  flashback coverage, its choice being made as it enters, and a spell whose
  only target is a hexproofed player vanishing from the menu entirely.
  **#254**, **#255**.
- **L30 alternate win/loss at the empty library** (new). The Maniac's
  replacement fires inside the resolution rather than on an SBA; killing it in
  response restores the CR 704.5b loss; "draw two" from a one-card and a
  zero-card library each fire exactly one replacement. **#236**.
- **L31 the sacrifice family** (new). Every CR claim held. **#263**.

### Competitor

- **C25 the graveyard as a live stat line** (new). Characteristic-defining P/T

  mid-combat *before* damage; CR 704.5f kills on the very next check.
  **#266**, **#267**.

  Heartless Summoning correct on every point. **#264**, **#265**.

- **C29 X spells and variable damage** (new). X locked at announcement, X=0
  legal and harmless, flashback's second independent X, CR 202.3b in the
  graveyard, and Blasphemous Act re-pricing live off the board. **#259**, **#262**.

### Operator

## What could not be reached

Worth knowing before someone plans a night around them:

- **Bouncing an attacked planeswalker** — no implemented ISD card returns a
  walker to hand. Added as L33.
- **A double-striker vs a walker** — needs Kruin Outlaw transformed, which
  needs a spell-free turn. Also L33.
- **A zombie *non-creature* card** for Rooftop Storm's filter, and any second
  simultaneous alternative-cost source — neither exists in the pool.
- **Blasphemous Act's `{R}` floor** — needs 8+ creatures on the battlefield at
  once; three attempts never held a board that wide.
- **Demonmail Hauberk's "Equip—Sacrifice a creature", Runechanter's Pike,
  Inquisitor's Flail and all four Ancient Grudge modes** — undrawn across 50
  turns of C28. Stack them higher next time.
- **Life-payment costs** — still unreachable, as L22 reported on 2026-09-02.
- **A drawn game (CR 104.4a)** — no card makes both players draw.

## Housekeeping

Four crews independently ran `tmux kill-server` on the shared default socket
and killed each other's games; eight had to be restarted, one tester SIGTERM'd
another's runner via `pgrep -x mtg-runner | tail -1`, and one wiped
`logs/playtest/` out from under a running mission. Everyone recovered onto
private sockets. The `play-cli` skill's cleanup snippet is unsafe for a
parallel crew, so `docs/playtest/README.md` now says to give each tester its
own `tmux -L <mission-id>` socket, to match processes by `--seed`, and to
treat `logs/playtest/` as shared.

Guides updated with tonight's fifteen missions plus two untried follow-ups
(L32 stacked control effects, L33 the walker-combat cases L26 could not
reach). L26 and M5 lost their `[proposed]` tags.
