## L52 — the order the announcement asks its questions in

**The order is the engine's, not the CLI's.** Here is what each CR 601.2
step is on every surface:

| CR step | What this program does | When |
|---|---|---|
| 601.2a move to stack | **not done**: the card stays in hand or graveyard until every question is answered (`pending_spell_cast`) | — |
| 601.2b modes | never asked. Ghoulcaller's Chant, the only modal card, has its mode read back off the target count (`detect_modal_choice_mode`), which is unambiguous here (1 vs 2 targets) | after cast |
| 601.2b X / exile count | `ChooseXFunding` (Devil's Play, Mikaeus, Kessig Wolf Run) or `ChooseExileFromGraveyard` (Harvest Pyre's X), raised by the submitted action | **3rd** |
| 601.2c targets | inside `CastSpell` / `ActivateAbility`: CLI `select a target`, LLM action rows, GUI target widget. An "up to N" slot is `ChooseTargetSet`, raised by the cast | **1st** |
| 601.2h which creature to sacrifice | inside the action, after the target on the CLI | **2nd** |
| 601.2g/h mana | tap plan in the row, or the funding answer | last |

The LLM seat and the GUI follow the same order, because they submit the same
actions. The engine's X-spell path returns before `pending_slot` and the
exile prompt, so an X spell that also had an "up to N" slot or an exile cost
would skip them. No card in the pool has both, so this is latent.

Inside this pool, putting the target before X changes no legality. What it
does cost: when X is asked, **the target is on no surface**. The CLI header
is `Devil's Play (#22): choose X funding (0-2)`, the stack pane reads
`(empty)`, and nothing is logged. A person who just typed the target
remembers it. A resumed game does not: `--save` writes this position. The
`cc` seat resumed there got a recap ending `You drew a card` and was asked to
fund Devil's Play with no target anywhere in the call. The GUI passes the
same one-line description as the widget title. Filed as **#613**. Following
601.2a (spell on the stack, with its targets, during announcement) would fix
it on every surface at once.

Harvest Pyre with an empty graveyard raises `choose 0-0 cards to exile …
Mark 0 of the 0 cards`, and bare Enter answers `nothing marked — mark the
cards you want, or press n for none`. That is #262's guard applied where there
is no choice (**#614**).

Checked, correct: Skaab Ruinator cannot exile itself to its own cost
(`costs.rs`, `o.id != spell`), which the engine has to guarantee by hand
because the card has not moved to the stack. Targeting the creature you
sacrifice to an ability is offered, deliberately and correctly
(`legal/abilities.rs` comment, 601.2c before 601.2h).

## L10 — mana ability edges

**Every CR 605 claim held.**
- Mana abilities never used the stack.
- A Pilgrim targeted by Geistflame was tapped in response and its mana spent
  before it died (605.3a).
- Floated mana emptied at each step (500.4).
- Nephalia Drownyard, Kessig Wolf Run and Ghost Quarter used the stack
  (605.1a).
- No mana ability was offered at the blocker, target, damage-order, cleanup or
  discard prompts.
- Infernal Plunge let a Pilgrim tap for mana and then be sacrificed to the
  same spell (601.2g before 601.2h).

**The auto-tap planner is what fails:**
- **#615**: Phase 2 of `compute_autotap` prefers Shimmering Grotto's filter
  over a mana creature for a coloured pip, then adds the filter's `{1}`. With
  Grotto and a Pilgrim untapped, Avacynian Priest is not offered. Tap the
  Pilgrim by hand and it appears. Elsewhere 3 sources are tapped for a
  2-mana spell.
- **#616**: ability tap plans pass `&[]` as the hand's demand.
- **#617**: potential mana counts every ability of a source.

Smaller finds:
- #618: the auto-pass notice, stale after the attack prompt and counting
  per target.
- #619: `(#id)` lands after the target clause.
- #620: the stack pane clips `#82` to `#8`.
- #621: `Pay {1}` taps an unnamed source.
- A comment on #613: ability funding says "cancel the cast".

## L30 — alternate win and loss at the empty library

**Every rule held:**
- The Maniac wins inside the draw, with no SBA or priority between.
- Divination from 0 cards gives one failure and one replacement. From 1 card
  the draws are sequential.
- A Maniac killed in response to Think Twice gives the ordinary 704.5b loss.
- The opponent's empty draw never fires my Maniac.
- A stolen Maniac works for the thief and not for the owner.
- 40 random games under invariants were clean.

**The defects are what follows:**
- **#622**: Desperate Ravings still discards, and Bloodgift Demon still
  drains, below "wins the game" (CR 104.1). `end_game` returns into the
  card's `on_resolve`, which carries on.
- **#623**: Bloodgift Demon says "drew a card" after a failed draw. This is
  deterministic at random seed 304, re-run by the lead.
- **#624**: a Maniac win is reported as "the opponent won (CR 104.2a)". It is
  a 104.2b win, and the line says nothing of how it happened.
- **#625**: a 5-card deck trips an invariant at action 1.

Unreached: 104.4a, because no card in the pool makes each player draw.

## L31 — the sacrifice family

**Every L31 rule passed again:**
- Altar's Reap's sacrifice survives the counter, and the dies trigger
  resolves first.
- Only controlled creatures appear in the picker, including a stolen one.
  The stolen card went to its owner's graveyard and its token to the thief.
- An unpayable Reap is absent even with mana floating.
- Tribute to Hunger prompts the targeted seat.
- Self-sacrificing abilities (Cathar, Weevil) resolve.
- Rolling Temblor's and Blasphemous Act's simultaneous deaths all saw each
  other: 8 Falkenrath Noble triggers from 4 deaths, in APNAP order.
- A regeneration shield does not stop a sacrifice.

**The night's sharpest find is #626.** An ability with a free sacrifice cost
is offered only when its mana is already floating. Auto-pass then skips any
window whose only actions are mana abilities. Disciple of Griselbrand with two
untapped Swamps passed on Lightning Bolt 3 ms after the Bolt, never prompted.
The LLM seat's advice to "tap a land and try again" cannot be followed in a
window it never sees. The random seat almost never reaches the case, so the
fuzzer does not either.

Other L31 issues:
- **#627**: Olivia Voldaren's summary line is false both when Unbreathing
  Horde prevents the damage and when Olivia died in response. This closes
  L51's leftover.
- **#628**: a loyalty activation's log never names its target, and the log
  double-reports life gain.
- **#629**: Brain Weevil's two picks use two widgets. Low confidence; it may
  be a size threshold.

Unreached: sacrificing an indestructible creature in play (read in code only),
Skirsdag Cultist in hotseat play, and Liliana's −2 on herself.
