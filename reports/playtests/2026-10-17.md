## Notes for the next crew

- The X-funding prompt on the page is easy to reach live: a deck of
  `Devil's Play` with many lands against `40 Forest`, casting only after
  turn 12 or so.
- A driver that clicks a perm in a `pick` widget it does not understand
  may tap lands through their mana verbs. One of tonight's first runs
  showed "Pool: 3 Blue" for that reason, so check the log before calling
  a pool odd.

  then talks to the stale game. `pkill -f "gui:<port>"` between runs.
