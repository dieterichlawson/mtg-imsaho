## D39: every answer sent twice (new)

**Picks are safe on both surfaces.**
- Client: 42 picks, each sent twice with gaps of 0-400 ms. Every second copy within 300 ms was refused, either by the client's window (21 times) or by the server's `pack_id` check (12 times). At 320 ms and 400 ms the second copy landed on the next pack, as designed.
- Page: 10 doubled-input variants (double, triple and native double-clicks, a doubled click on Pick, held Enter on three focus targets) each produced exactly one pick.

**Findings:**
- **#851:** the client exits with code 1 on *any* refusal whose text contains "key" or "seat". `lands turkey=1` → `refused: 'Turkey' is not a basic land…` → exit 1.
- **#852:** the page's build-row toggle has no repeat guard. A held Enter flipped a card 9 times, and a double-click puts it back. The client's `add` and `drop` are idempotent, so the two surfaces disagree.

**Checked, correct:**
- A doubled `ready` keeps the deck final; the second copy is refused, and the refusal is true.
- Every colour letter (W/U/B/R/G/C) is right in `costText`, the pool strip and both pages' pips.
- ISD has no hybrid or Phyrexian mana. The draft page's `pips()` would draw {W/U} as a grey W, but no card can reach that.

## D36: the uneven finish (new)

The stub slept 40-150 s on the AI seats' Pack 3 Pick 13/14, which opened a gap of 70-150 s between the people finishing and the AIs finishing.

**Checked, correct:**
- The page and the client each switch to the deck screen at their own last pick, with a full 60 s clock, while the AIs are still at `picking:40`. #811 holds.
- A deck made Ready in the gap is the deck in `decks/seat-N.txt`. So is one sent but not marked Ready (#810 holds while the AIs still draft), and so is a short one topped up with 7 Island.
- All three hold through a page reload and a client restart.
- The log's DECK BUILDING blocks stay readable, headed per seat, with the AIs' last picks interleaved.

**Found:**
- **#856:** every client reconnect prints "Cli (seat 1) is now called Cli", and nothing says the seat is back.
- The pick timer's auto-pick also prints after "has drafted all 42 cards", a third path for the wrong order in #819 (commented).

## Notes for the next crew

- **Test a host action at both moments: when the target seat is the one being asked, and when its opponent is.** #821's fix and its test only cover the first, and #854 sits in the second.
- **A minimal `--game-ports` range shows resource leaks within one round.** It is how #853 surfaced. The default 99-port range hides it.
- **To open a real gap at the end of the draft, slow only the AIs' Pack 3 Pick 13/14 in the stub.** Slowing every pick by 5-10 s gives a gap of about 3 s, because a seat's last pick waits on its upstream seat's 13th.
- **Do not `pkill -f` a pattern that can appear in your own shell's command line.** Three probes killed their own shell this way tonight. Kill by PID, and never by a generic pattern such as `tail -f /dev/null` that another probe may also be using.
