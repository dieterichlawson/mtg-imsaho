# Playtest night 2026-09-01

15 missions, 27 hotseat games plus two non-game CLI-abuse sweeps, three
personas. Every mission on the menu had been played on 08-29, 08-30 or
08-31, so 14 of tonight's 15 were new (C15-C18, L16-L20, V16-V20); the
fifteenth was a deliberate re-probe of C12, which last night's row
recorded as never actually exercised.

**Filed #69-#96 (28 issues), plus 8 comments on existing issues.**

## Headline

Three real rules bugs, all in code paths no previous night had reached:

- **#85 — Evil Twin copies via an ETB trigger, not an enters-as
  replacement** (CR 614.1c / 706.9a). A 0/0 "Evil Twin" sits on the
  battlefield across a full round of priority without dying (CR 704.5f),
  the copied "enters tapped" replacement never applies, and both players
  get a priority window that should not exist.
- **#88 — an attacker that left the battlefield is still listed at
  declare-blockers and can be "blocked"** (CR 506.4 / 509.1a). Exploitable:
  sacrifice an attacker after declaration and the defender's only blocker
  can be baited onto a permanent that no longer exists, letting the real
  attackers through unblocked.
- **#93 — a creature entering as an Essence of the Wild copy keeps its own
  activated ability** (CR 706.2). Daybreak Ranger entered as "Essence of
  the Wild 6/6" and still activated its own {T} ping. Its *triggered*
  ability was correctly dropped, and the inspector shows the correct
  one-line ability list — so the action generator and the renderer
  disagree, and the action generator is the one that is wrong.

Two more that lose games without breaking a rule:

- **#71 — type-ahead crosses the seat boundary.** Two ordinary keystrokes
  sent with no delay: the second answered the *other seat's* mandatory
  cleanup discard, at a prompt never drawn on screen. A sharper instance
  (comment on #71) has a stale `0` — "Pass priority" on the caster's own
  menu — making the opponent's Tribute to Hunger sacrifice choice, with
  full index control over which creature dies.
- **#91 — declare-blockers input intermittently dropped entirely.** Twice
  in ~8 multi-pair declarations, a valid block line produced "declared no
  blockers" with no error. In one case it was exactly lethal and lost the
  game on the spot.

## By persona

### Competitor (5 missions, 10 games)

**C12 re-probe succeeded.** ub-zombies vs wg-coverage decked the opponent
in both games. CR 704.5b is verified properly for the first time: a player
at 0 cards in library was still prompted for priority and *took a real
action* (Gavony Township activation) before losing on their own next draw
step. The defect found was that nothing anywhere says the game ended that
way (#86) — and that affects every loss reason, not just decking.

C15 confirmed mana-pool emptying at every step boundary, no mana burn, and
correct land-drop gating; it found the menace blocker bug (#72) and the
Nightfall Predator targeting question (#90) incidentally. C16 exercised all
five combat priority windows and confirmed CR 509.1h, marked damage
persistence, and removed-attacker damage — then found #88. C17 verified
activation costs, stack use and counter accumulation across long games.
C18 played five Divine Reckoning resolutions including the zero-creature
and one-creature edge cases, and confirmed APNAP choice order, all-choices-
before-any-destruction, tokens ceasing to exist, and simultaneous death
triggers — no rules bugs, two cosmetic ones.

### Rules Lawyer (5 missions, 10 games)

**L17 (morbid) came back completely clean** across ten distinct checks:
condition re-checked on resolution (Brimstone Volley dealt 5 after a
kill in response), Festerhide Boar entering *directly* with counters as a
replacement effect, Morkrut Banshee's trigger correctly not going on the
stack at all when no creature had died, per-turn reset, tokens counting,
and exile/discard correctly not counting.

**L19 (curses) also came back clean** on every rule: player-only targeting,
casting a curse on yourself, layer-7c recomputation, the SBA kill of a 4/0,
P/T restored with no stale values on removal, Curse of the Nightly Hunt's
attack requirement refusing incomplete declarations by index, and Curse of
Oblivion triggering on the *enchanted* player's upkeep.

L16 found the two copy bugs above plus three UI defects. L18 confirmed
CR 111.7 token cessation, that dead tokens never inflate Spider Spawning's
creature-card count, and that Parallel Lives doubles exactly 2N and only
for its controller — then found the blocker-drop bug. L20 verified
"can't block", flying in both directions, hexproof, Crossway Vampire's
one-turn restriction and Geist of Saint Traft's token exile.

### Vandal (5 missions, 7 games + 2 sweeps)

**V16 (deck-file abuse) was completely clean** — 15 malformed deck files
(empty, comments-only, zero/negative/overflowing counts, unknown and
unicode names, missing counts, no separator, duplicates, binary bytes,
CRLF, a 100k-character name) each produced a clear one-line error and
exit 1. No panics.

V17 found the opposite for output paths (#69, #70). V18 confirmed Ctrl-D,
tmux detach/reattach and pane-kill are all safe and that save files survive
SIGKILL at any offset — then found the raw-mode residue (#78), Ctrl-U
(#79), and that `--log` never writes the game log (#77). V19 pinned down
exactly which prompt transitions leak buffered input (raw→raw survives,
raw→cooked drops), which scopes the fix. V20 established that saves are
written non-atomically (#75) but that **no corrupt save ever loaded** —
serde and the unconditional `invariants::check_core` on the resume path
caught every one of 6 cross-game hybrids out of 70,334 snapshots.

## Cross-cutting themes worth one fix each

- **Menu labels that hide the choice** (#80, with three independent crews
  and comments): the `targeting X` suffix is generated correctly and then
  hard-clipped to the panel width with no ellipsis, defeating the fix for
  #36. Seen as 4, 5, 6 and finally **16** byte-identical menu lines. Caused
  a real self-hit. The same missing annotation applies to cost choices
  (Grimgrin's sacrifice) as to targets.
- **The engine knows things it never says** (#77, #86, #89, #92, #94):
  `--log` writes 7 lines for a 219-action game; no loss reason is ever
  reported; combat damage to creatures and lifelink are unlogged; token
  counts are logged pre-doubling; `--resume` logs the default deck names.
- **Prompt identity is decided per-prompt, not by one rule** (#73, #76,
  #95): bare Enter means attack-with-everything at declare attackers, is
  silently ignored at discard, and any card choice with >3 options gets a
  hardcoded "Search Library" header that drops the real prompt text.

## Gaps and corrections, for future nights

- **L20 could not verify Invisible Stalker ("can't be blocked") or Blazing
  Torch (conditional Vampire/Zombie evasion)** — both are 1-ofs in a
  47-card singleton deck and were never drawn across three games. Those two
  mission items remain unverified and want a stacked or smaller deck.
- **L16 did not reach**: copying a transformed DFC, Essence of the Wild
  interacting with a token entering (rg-coverage makes no tokens), or two
  ETB replacement effects on one object for CR 616 ordering.
- **L18 could not test CR 616 ordering of two token doublers** — no deck
  can put a second doubler onto the battlefield.
- **L17 could not test "returned to hand does not count" for morbid** —
  neither coverage deck has a bounce effect.
- **The C18 mission brief had Divine Reckoning wrong** (it said "{2}{W} …
  sacrifices the rest"). The real card is {2}{W}{W}, "Each player chooses
  a creature they control. Destroy the rest," with flashback {5}{W}{W};
  the engine matches the real card. Brief error, not an engine bug — the
  menu entry has been left as-is since it names the card, not the text.
- **Scryfall is unreachable from this environment** (`CONNECT tunnel
  failed, 403`), so #90's oracle-text claim rests on in-repo evidence and
  is flagged in the issue as needing confirmation before a fix.
- `data/oracle_cache.json` has no entry for **Nightfall Predator** (the DFC
  is cached only under its front face) or for **Swords to Plowshares**,
  which wg-coverage runs. Not filed — likely intentional set-scoping — but
  it means back-face text has nothing validating it, which is the probable
  origin of #90.

## Unconfirmed, deliberately not filed

- Fiend Hunter's ETB appeared to prompt the target choice and the "you may"
  decision in one combined prompt. Per CR 603.3d the target is locked in
  when the ability goes on the stack and the optional choice is made on
  resolution, so merging them would mean declining never puts a target on
  the stack for the opponent to respond to. The crew did not investigate
  the engine's actual stack timing — worth a targeted L-mission.
- Two crews independently reported a main phase passing without a prompt
  while a land was in hand; both traced it to their own stray keystrokes on
  re-examination and retracted it. The auto-pass code never skips a
  `PlayLand`. Recorded so a third sighting is recognised as a pattern
  rather than re-litigated from scratch.
