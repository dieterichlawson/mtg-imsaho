## H18 — the legal action that never becomes a row

H18 asked whether `choose_action`'s `legal.activatable_abilities` lookup can
**miss**, dropping a row silently instead of falling through to the `Display`
fallback. The answer is that the lookup cannot miss, and the row disappears
anyway, one level up from where the idea was looking.

The seat dedupes activations on `(object_id, ability_index)`
(`mtg-player/src/llm.rs:3906`). The engine keys the same offer on the
**triple** `(object_id, source_card_id, ability_index)` — deliberately, and
the comment #533 added says so in as many words
(`mtg-engine/src/engine/legal/abilities.rs:73`). `source_card_id` is the
field whose whole job is to "disambiguate aura-granted abilities from native
ones" (`mtg-engine/src/actions.rs:318`). So when an attached Aura or
Equipment grants an ability whose index collides with one the host creature
already has, the engine has two actions and two `activatable_abilities`
entries and the seat builds **one row**. Natives are collected before the
`attached` loop, so the half that disappears is always the granted one.

Two boards, both captured:

**Lantern Spirit (`{U}: Return to hand`, index 0) under Skeletal Grimace
(grants `{B}: Regenerate`, index 0).** Turn 25, seed 7, five untapped Swamps
and `Tap Swamp: Add {B}` on the same menu:

```
  Lantern Spirit (#4) 3/2 blue, flying [S] (Skeletal Grimace: Enchanted creature gets +1/+1 and has "{B}: Regenerate this creature.")
[MAIN PHASE 1]
Available actions:
0: Pass
1: Tap Island: Add {U}
2: Tap Swamp: Add {B}
3: Activate Lantern Spirit (#4) ({U}: Return to hand) (tap Island)
4: Cast Skeletal Grimace (tap Swamp, Island)
5: Concede
```

**718 of 718** menus taken with that creature on the board had no regenerate
row.

**Ulvenwald Mystics (`{G}: Regenerate`) under the same Aura** is the sharper
one, because the two abilities are the *same effect at a different cost*:
`{G}` shown, `{B}` gone, **479 of 479**. A seat that cannot make green mana
cannot regenerate at all, and nothing in the prompt hints the option exists.

The three other surfaces are all correct, which is what makes this a harness
defect and not a rules one:

- the **CLI**, replayed to the identical board at the identical seed, shows
  both rows (`1: ... {U}: Return to hand` / `2: ... {B}: Regenerate`);
- the **random seat** activates the dropped ability — 11, 8 and 5 times over
  seeds 1, 3 and 7;
- the **GUI seat** hands `LegalActions` through whole, so the page sees both.

No existing check can catch it. `legal.actions` and
`legal.activatable_abilities` agree with each other, so
`invariants/legal.rs:840` passes; the seat's own `COLLAPSED` debug line reads
like an ordinary copy-collapse; and the game ran to turn 108 and exited 0.

Blast radius: 26 creatures in the pool carry a native index-0 activated
ability, and Blazing Torch grants at index 1, colliding with Olivia Voldaren,
Bloodline Keeper and Mikaeus, the Lunarch. `decks/coverage/bg-coverage.txt`
already ships a Skeletal Grimace alongside Brain Weevil, Disciple of
Griselbrand and Ulvenwald Mystics, so ordinary play reaches this.

One thing a fix has to reach beyond the dedupe, found in the Mystics capture:
three copies, only one enchanted, collapsed into one `Copies` range
`3-5: Activate Ulvenwald Primordials ({G}: Regenerate) ... 3=#2, 4=#4, 5=#6`.
That grouping is right for the `{G}` ability. Once the `{B}` row exists, the
`AbilityCopy` key has to carry the granting card too, or the restored row
will be grouped across copies that do not all wear the Aura.

Latent, not observed: the cast arm one block above has the same asymmetry —
the seat keys casts on `(object_id, alternative_cost.is_some())` while the
invariant keys them on `(object_id, format!("{alternative_cost:?}"))`. No
card in this pool offers two *different* alternative costs for one object, so
nothing was captured; it is written into the issue at low confidence.

## H15 — the refusals the watchdog can never see — **negative**

H15's premise is sound: `progress_fingerprint` catches a livelock only while
the board stands still, so a refusal that *mutates* state is invisible to it,
and since it keeps only the previous fingerprint and resets on any change, a
cycle of period ≥ 2 is invisible by construction too. Every remedy it doubted
turned out to be there.

**The mulligan floor is real and exact.** A stub answering `mull: true` at
every mulligan prompt, rg vs wb seed 3: **exactly 7** mulligan prompts, then

```
llm.rs:4118  AUTO-KEEP [P1]  seven mulligans taken: any further mulligan keeps the same empty hand
GAME  p0 keeps (7 mulligans)
```

and an ordinary game to turn 41. It is also not the only floor — H15 says it
is "a policy floor in ONE seat", and `random.rs` has its own `MAX_MULLIGANS`
cap of 3 (#63/#456). The other two answering surfaces are people.

**The forfeit worry is closed by construction, not by policy.**
`LegalActions::permits` returns `true` for `AbandonGame` and for `Concede`
**unconditionally**, in the first two arms of the match, before anything
prompt-specific (`mtg-engine/src/engine.rs:177-180`). So neither runner's
stall exit depends on a flat action being listed at a structured prompt —
which is #559's shape, and it cannot recur on either loop. Both loops also
increment `action_count` once per decision callback
(`mtg-runner/src/main.rs:737`, `mtg-draft-runner/src/main.rs:1672`), so the
50,000-action ceiling is a live backstop in exactly the state-mutating case
the fingerprint is blind to.

**The free cycle cannot be built from this pool.** A period-≥2 livelock needs
a repeatable state change that costs nothing. Sweeping every
`ActivatedAbilityDef` in `mtg-engine/src/cards/` for one that is
`ManaCost::free()`, `requires_tap: false`, `SacrificeCost::None`,
`counter_cost: None` and not `once_per_turn` returns **nothing**. Every
toggle in the pool costs mana; mana needs an untap; an untap costs a turn;
and a turn costs a card off the top. The engine's own decking rule is the
bound.

Checked and correct, with the evidence above.

## H14 — the budget for ONE decision — **partial, and its target is fixed**

Walked all 11 `send_message_structured` call sites.
`choose_blockers_structured` is the **only** loop in `llm.rs` that re-asks one
decision, and #496's fix has landed: `MAX_RETRIES = 1`, with the comment
spelling out why twenty was pointless — the constraint is joint over several
blockers, a per-blocker `enum` cannot express it, so attempt 20's schema was
byte-identical to attempt 1's. It now repairs the answer through
`partition_under_minimum_blocks` rather than discarding it.

H14's second question — which prompts have a client-side validator that can
reject an answer the schema still permits — has the answer H14 itself
predicted, everywhere: the ordering permutation check, the
mulligan-not-on-offer guard, `card_indices`, X-funding, the action-index
range and `attacker_indices` all **substitute** a legal answer and log it.
None re-asks. The worst single-decision budget is therefore
2 × `MAX_ATTEMPTS` × `CALL_TIMEOUT` = 6 calls and 30 minutes, down from 60
calls and roughly five hours.

Not done, and left for the next harness night: no board was built to drive
the validator to exhaustion, and no per-decision wall clock was measured. The
row that would need it is the menace board H11 describes, which is reachable
only through Terror of Kruin Pass.

## Method notes for the next harness night

- **The cheapest instrument for "is a legal option missing" is not a harvest,
  it is a key comparison.** Every collapse the seat does has a key, and every
  one of those keys has a counterpart in `invariants/legal.rs`'s
  `distinct_offers`. Where the seat's key is *coarser* than the engine's, an
  option is unreachable and no invariant can see it, because both of the
  engine's own lists still agree. Two such keys exist today; one is #589 and
  one (the alternative cost) has no card to exercise it.
- **A harvest tells you what is in the menu, never what is missing from it.**
  The board section of the prompt is what closes that gap: it prints an
  Aura's or Equipment's granted rules text verbatim, so "a granted ability
  named in the board and absent from the rows" is one greppable predicate
  over a whole game.
- **Replaying the same seed through `--p1 cli` is a free control.** The CLI
  renders `legal.actions` directly and so cannot lose a row; driving it to
  the same turn with `f` and a land each turn took about twenty keypresses
  and turned a code-reading claim into a side-by-side capture.
- **`--check-invariants` passing is not evidence that a seat was offered
  everything.** Every invariant here is engine-internal. The disagreement
  that matters is between the engine's key and the seat's, and nothing in
  the repo compares those.
