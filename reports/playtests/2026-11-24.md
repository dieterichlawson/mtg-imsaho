## L5: cost edges

Three boards placed on a turn-1 main phase (seed 55, a UR flashback deck on both sides; seed 67, a UGR Make a Wish deck), under `--check-invariants`.

Checked, correct:
- **Two flashback costs.** Snapcaster Mage on Think Twice gives one card both its printed `{2}{U}` and the granted `{1}{U}`, and both are offered. On Devil's Play, both `{X}{R}{R}{R}` and `{X}{R}` are offered, and the granted cost asks `choose X funding (0-7)` with 8 lands untapped, as it should.
- **Countered flashback spell.** Devil's Play cast with flashback and countered by Dissipate logs "was countered and exiled".
- **Past in Flames (CR 611.2c).** It grants "5 instant/sorcery cards", exactly the ones in the graveyard at resolution, with Dissipate's grant at `{1}{U}{U}` and Forbidden Alchemy's at `{2}{U}`. A Think Twice cast from hand afterwards reaches the graveyard with no grant (`until_end_of_turn` lists #11, not #12). A second Past in Flames is offered at `{3}{R}` (granted) and `{4}{R}` (printed).
- **Harvest Pyre.** With flashback granted, it offers 0-6 cards, exiles all six, deals 6, and exiles itself.
- **Cleanup.** The grants end at cleanup: `until_end_of_turn` is `[]` on turn 2.
- **Fizzling.** A flashback spell that fizzles is exiled: `move_spell_after_resolve` exiles on `cast_with_flashback`. I checked this by reading the code, not by playing it.

**Found:** granted flashback is keyed to the object id and is never ended when the card leaves the graveyard. I cast Snapcaster Mage on Think Twice, then Make a Wish returned Think Twice to hand. I cast it from hand, it resolved, and it was offered again for Snapcaster's `{1}{U}`. The cast was accepted, with no invariant violation (#1089, CR 400.7). It is on every surface, since they all read the one grant list.
