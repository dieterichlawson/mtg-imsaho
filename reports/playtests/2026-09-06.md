# Playtest night — 2026-09-06

**Crew**: 15 testers in parallel, three personas, one tmux socket each.
**Games**: 40 played to a real conclusion (10 Rules Lawyer, 18 Competitor,
12 Vandal), all hotseat `--p1 cli --p2 cli` with both seats played by hand,
plus ~9 scripted repro and probe runs. Nearly all under `--check-invariants`.
**Zero panics and zero genuine invariant failures in any game.**
**Issues**: 22 filed (#281–#302), ~22 comments across 15 existing issues.

Everything in the ledger was inside the two-week floor again, so the night was
the two ideas nobody had ever tried (L32 and L33) plus thirteen written for it:
L34–L36, C30–C34 and V31–V35.

## The night in one sentence

**The rules engine keeps holding; the layer between the player and it does
not.** Five of the ten game-subject missions came back with every CR claim on
their list verified correct and no issue to file, while all five CLI missions
found something — and the single worst finding of the night is that the CLI
cannot be trusted to show you what you are about to submit.

## What was verified correct

Whole areas passed under deliberate attack, and that is the more useful half of
the record:

- **L34 — "enters with counters" is a real replacement effect.** CR 614.1c,
  614.12, 616.1, 603.6b and 704.5f all held exactly. The sharpest case: with an
  Unbreathing Horde on the stack counting three Zombie cards in the graveyard,
  Purify the Grave exiled one in response and the Horde entered with **two**
  counters — the count is worked out as it enters, not at announcement. A
  Mikaeus for X=0 and a Horde with no other Zombies both arrived as genuine
  0/0s and died at the very next SBA check, with no window in which anything
  saw a base-P/T creature.
- **L35 — deathtouch, trample and lethal damage.** A Kessig-pumped 5/1 and then
  7/1 deathtouch trampler assigned exactly 1 to each blocker and trampled the
  remainder (CR 702.2c into 702.19b), triple block included. CR 510.1c counted
  damage already marked when computing lethal, 514.2 cleared it at cleanup, and
  a 0-power deathtoucher destroyed nothing (702.2b).
- **L36 — protection and the DEBT rules.** All four letters correct for Spare
  from Evil, including the subtlety its own source comment flags: protection
  from non-Human *creatures* correctly does **not** stop a Brimstone Volley.
  Non-Human blockers were absent from the legal set rather than
  offered-and-rejected, and Olivia's target menu collapsed the instant it
  resolved and was back the next turn.
- **C30 — tempo as pseudo-removal.** CR 508.1a, 508.1e/506.4, 509.1a,
  509.1h/506.4c, 704.5m, 400.7, 702.34a and 508.8 all held across four games
  and ~1500 actions. Bouncing a blocker mid-combat left the attacker blocked and
  dealing no damage, and the ability aimed at the bounced creature fizzled.
- **C31 — draw engines.** Every draw, look, mill and flashback reconciled
  exactly across library, hand, graveyard and exile over five games. Forbidden
  Alchemy moved exactly 4/1/3 and kept all four cards secret; Curiosity fired on
  damage to a player and stayed silent on damage to a creature.
- **C32 — lifelink**, which C22 recorded on 2026-09-02 as untestable. It is
  testable and it is right: part of the damage event with no stack object, gained
  even by a lifelinker dying to that same combat damage, and Butcher's Cleaver's
  Human-conditional grant verified both ways with one Equipment object and across
  a live Moonmist type change.
- **C33 — the graveyard as a resource.** Skaab Ruinator's exile-three additional
  cost is paid at announcement and the card is absent from the menu when it
  cannot be paid; graveyard-count P/T recomputes live, including mid-combat after
  blockers; and a deliberate mill to an empty library lost only on the DRAW two
  turns later (CR 104.3c / 704.5b).
- **V31 — auto-pass never takes a mandatory decision.** The auto-pass path is
  gated on the menu having a Pass option, and every mandatory choice in the
  program lacks one, so a forced attack, a forced sacrifice, a bottoming prompt
  and a mulligan all stopped and asked.
- **V34 — the save round-trips.** 15 probes, every ephemeral `GameState` field
  identical after kill-and-resume and, more importantly, still *applying*:
  marked damage still killed, the spent land drop stayed spent, the werewolf
  spell-count clock stayed on schedule.
- **V35 — nothing illegal is ever offered.** Hexproof targets, used loyalty
  abilities, summoning-sick attackers, defenders and unaffordable casts are all
  pre-filtered out of the menu. The offered-then-rejected failure did not happen
  once.

L33 also disproved its own premise: **Lost in the Mist can bounce a
planeswalker**, so the half of that mission the guide called unreachable was
reachable, and #246's fix holds on the first-strike path.

## The three engine defects

- **#285** — the serious one. Steal a creature with Olivia, take it back with
  Traitorous Blood, kill Olivia — and at your own cleanup the engine hands the
  creature to your *opponent*, permanently, with no control effect left in
  existence. `TemporaryEffect::ChangeControl` snapshots the momentary controller
  instead of deriving control from the effects still in force, so the revert
  names a player whose effect ended two spells ago (CR 110.2a). Reproduced twice,
  including from a fixed seed with a recorded keystroke sequence.
- **#298** — Heartless Summoning's `{2}` reduction is silently discarded on any
  `{X}` spell, because the reducer only subtracts from a `Generic` symbol and
  `{X}{W}` has none. Mikaeus for X=2 still costs three mana (CR 601.2f).
- **#302** — Tree of Redemption's exchange writes the object's **base** toughness
  instead of applying a layer-7b effect. The arithmetic is right in play — layer
  7b before 7d with counters, and marked damage correctly not laundered — but the
  copy path and `move_object` are patched by hand with comments naming this card,
  so the invariant is upheld by memory rather than by construction.

**#286** deserves separate mention: it is a regression left by #253's fix.
Olivia's `{3}{B}{B}` on a Vampire you already control now records a control
effect whose controller equals its original controller, which `check_settled`
rejects and which kills the run with exit 2. It reproduced from a plain
`random` vs `random` smoke test at `--seed 1`, so **nightly fuzz seeds over any
decklist containing Olivia can die on this false positive.**

## The CLI: three themes

**1. The screen does not always show what you are about to submit (#281).**
Both line readers keep an incremental echo model rather than repainting from the
buffer, and their per-`char` column count is 0 for combining marks and ZWJ and
knows nothing about grapheme clusters. Five keystrokes — `a`, one ZWJ emoji,
three Backspaces — leave the prompt line rendering **empty** over a non-empty
buffer. Enter then took a real, irreversible action at eight of eight prompt
types tried: it attacked with everything at a prompt reading `enter=none`, cast
Devil's Play for maximum X where a bare Enter is documented to mean X=0,
destroyed a creature, bottomed a card and picked a target. The two readers that
redraw from the string each keystroke — the `/` search box and the chooser
filter — are immune, which is both the contrast and the shape of the fix.
No backspace ever panicked, and #35's stale buffer is genuinely fixed.

**2. Prompts still commit things the player did not ask for.** V33 swept all 18
interactive prompts and found the three target choosers disagree about what a
bare Enter means — it cancels the cast at one and casts the spell at the other
two — while `prompt_target_optional` has no Cancel row at all (#288). An X-cost
*activated* ability pays its whole cost before the X prompt appears and offers
no cancel, where the spell path gets the same sequence right (#290). This is the
same class as #123, #249, #254 and #262; #123's own fix on the X-funding prompt
was verified holding.

**3. The log is silent where it matters.** Three separate missions independently
found a state change with no log line: Desperate Ravings' random discard (#301),
"enters with N counters" (#299), and a cast from the graveyard mislabelled as an
alternative cost with no zone named (#300). With #263 and #264 already open,
that is five instances of one shape.

Also filed: auto-pass's contract (#294–#296 — it silently skips the castable
spells its own guard exists to protect, its real break set is not the documented
one, and it cannot be cancelled), refusal messages that name the wrong object or
erase themselves on a 700–900 ms timer (#287, #289, #291), the action counter
restarting at 0 on `--resume` (#293), no pane showing a permanent's subtypes
(#297), and three more input-echo defects (#282–#284).

## What could not be reached

Worth recording so the next crew does not spend a night on it:

- Two durable control effects contesting one permanent (L32) — one Olivia is
  reachable, two is not.
- A blocked attacker aimed at a planeswalker on the first-strike path (L33), and
  Terror of Kruin Pass's menace grant.
- Two sources damaging an Unbreathing Horde simultaneously (L34, CR 614.5 — one
  counter or two?), and Ludevic's Test Subject's transform.
- `-X/-X` and a sweeper through protection (L36) — Dead Weight was never drawn,
  and a sorcery sweeper can never be cast while an until-EOT grant from the other
  seat is up.
- A lifelinker blocked by multiple creatures (C32) — every lifelinker in the pool
  has 1–3 toughness, so a rational defender never gang-blocks.
- A second P/T-setting effect on the Tree (C34) — ISD has none.
- Trigger-ordering as an editor target (V32), and an X-cost ability that also
  carries a sacrifice cost (V33).

One loose thread nobody chased: V33 saw two Falkenrath Nobles die with only one
dies-trigger reaching the stack. That is an engine question for a future
Rules Lawyer night, not a filed issue.
