memory. Deriving the per-subject last nights out of `reports/playtests/LEDGER.md`

`logs/playtest/l11-*` and `l15-*` prefixes and their own tmux sockets.

**Crew**: 3 probes. **L46** run here; **L11** and **L15** in parallel.

**Runs**: 13 games through the real `mtg-runner` as hotseat `cli`-vs-`cli`
self-play, plus 40 seeded `random`-vs-`random` games. Every game under
`--check-invariants`; **no invariant fired in any of them**. **No metered API
seat was spawned at any point** — every seat in the night was `cli` or `random`,
and no `claude`, `cc` or `gemini` seat was used at all.

**Issues**: 7 filed — #500–#506.

The night's shape: **the rules are right and the record of them is not.** Three
probes went at three unrelated parts of the engine — trigger gates, the layer
system, attachment SBAs — and all three found the CR behaviour correct on every
claim they could reach. All seven issues are about what the program *says*: a
resolution that does nothing and says nothing, a field the view type never had,
a bracket that does not name what is in it.

## The three that matter

**#500 — a transform trigger that cannot transform leaves no record at all.**
L46 asked for two things. The first was a sweep of every `should_trigger` in
`cards/isd/` for #469's conflation, a CR 603.4 gate that also tests a fact about
resolution. That sweep found nothing: all 38 implementations test the printed
condition and nothing else, and the three that look like conflations are not
(`thraben_sentry`'s face check is CR 712.8d, `civilized_scholar`'s is Homicidal
Brute's actual printed clause, and "another" is filtered by the collector for
the whole death-watch scan). #469 itself is fixed and the fix is right, verified
at runtime rather than read.

The second thing L46 asked for was the mirror — a resolution handler that
silently does nothing — and that is where the bug was. `apply_transform` has
three early returns *above* its log line: token (CR 111.7), single-faced clone
via `copy_grantor` (CR 701.28c), no back face. Each one is a trigger that
announced itself, took a full priority round, and then left the stack with no
line written. Over 40 random games, **380 announced transform triggers produced
221 `transforms into` lines**; the other 159 are indistinguishable from each
other and from a trigger that never resolved.

Three reproductions, escalating. A Cackling Counterpart token of Reckless Waif
triggering alone, which isolates the case with no other explanation. Two Evil
Twin clones of Merciless Predator triggering alongside the real card, where one
of three stack entries produces output. And the one that costs something: a
token copy of **Ludevic's Test Subject**, where ten mana and five activations log

```
Ludevic's Test Subject (#81) gets a hatchling counter (now 5)
Ludevic's Test Subject (#81) loses 5 hatchling counters (now 0)
Step: EndCombat
```

against the original's log in the same game state, byte-identical up to the line
that follows it:

```
Ludevic's Test Subject (#28) loses 5 hatchling counters (now 0)
Ludevic's Test Subject (#28) transforms into Ludevic's Abomination (#28)
```

`remove_counters` reports and `apply_transform` does not, in adjacent lines of
one printed sentence. That is a fairly direct argument that the missing line is
an oversight rather than a policy of staying quiet about no-ops — and
`Moonmist::on_resolve` already hand-rolls the distinction correctly, comparing
`is_transformed` before and after each call so it stays quiet only when the
count is really zero.

**#504 — a live "can't attack or block" is on no pane.** L11's layer arithmetic
held everywhere it could be reached, including a Wolf token reading 8/5 across a
7c anthem, a 7d counter, an equipment bonus and a second anthem, with every
removal recomputing rather than un-adding a stale number. What broke sits one
layer out. `PermanentView` in `mtg-engine/src/view.rs` has `keywords` and
`protections` and **no field at all** for `PreventAttack`, `PreventBlock`,
`ForceAttack`, `PreventUntap`, `CantBeBlocked`, `CanOnlyBeBlockedBy`,
`MinimumBlockers` or `PreventCombatDamage`. So a Bonds of Faith on a non-Human
silently stops the creature being a legal attacker, the declare-attackers step
is skipped entirely, and the log says `No attackers declared` — the same line a
player who *declined* to attack would get. The engine is right (the creature is
absent from the attacker set rather than offered and rejected, per CR 508.1a);
there is simply nowhere for a player to read why.

**#502 — the Equipment half of an SBA #358 already half-fixed.** L15 found every
CR claim holding, including the one worth keeping as a negative: hexproof granted
to an *already*-enchanted creature correctly does **not** detach the Aura, it only
removes the creature from the opponent's menus. An Aura goes to its owner's
graveyard on three separate routes even when the host belongs to the other
player, and the Equipment on the same creature merely unattaches — correct under
CR 704.5m/n, and correct again across a Traitorous Blood steal where CR 301.5e
keeps the Equipment attached and still granting +2/+0 to a creature its
controller no longer controls. But #358's own body says *"Same silence applies to
the equipment-detach branch a few lines up"*, and that half was never fixed: the
Aura arm got its line through `move_object`, and the Equipment arm has no zone
change to hang one on. Observed on three routes.

## The other four

**#501** — the `i` detail screen renders the *copied card's* printed text, so an
Evil Twin clone's granted `{U}{B}, {T}: Destroy target creature with the same
name` is offered in the action menu one keypress away and absent from its own
card text. Two permanents with the same name, the same P/T and different ability
sets present identically.

**#503** — the battlefield pane's attachment bracket names neither the
controller nor the kind of attachment.
`Doomed Traveler 6/3 [Spectral Flight,Silver-Inlaid Dagger]` is one player's Aura
and the other's Equipment rendered the same way, printed in the host's half of
the board. Sharpest after a Traitorous Blood steal, where p1's Dagger appears
inside p0's half. The `i` screen gets both directions right, and a Curse already
carries `[enchanting you]`/`[enchanting opponent]` on its own row (#81) — the
object-attached case never got the equivalent.

**#505** — Gutter Grime's Ooze is logged as `created 1 0/0 Ooze token` when the
slime counter is already on and CR 604.3 makes it a 1/1 from the instant it
exists. #329 fixed the count half of that line; this is the P/T half. Its `i`
page also shows no `Printed P/T: */*` and no ability text, because #267's fix
routes through `CardBehavior::prints_star_pt()` and a token has no registry
behaviour.

**#506** — the LLM seat's board never reads `PermanentView::protections`; every
non-test read of that field is in `cli.rs`. Filed as a **code-reading finding
that was not reproduced in play**, and says so in its own body — no metered seat
was available to confirm it, and it should be treated as a lead rather than an
observation.

## What the night says about method

Two of the three probes went looking for a structural failure where an idea
pointed, found the structure holding, and found the real defect one layer out in
what the program reports. That is the third night running with that shape (see
2026-09-14), and it is worth naming as a habit rather than a coincidence: when a
sweep comes back clean, the question to ask next is not "which card did I miss"
but "what does this code *say* on the paths where it does nothing".

L46 gives that a sharper form, now written into the guide as the reusable move:
**a fix that removed a false line may not have added the true one.** #469 and the
`apply_transform` doc comment both record deleting claims that cards were making
without checking. Nobody went back to ask what the code says *now* on those
paths, and the answer turned out to be "nothing" — 159 times in 40 games.

## Guide changes

Five commits, each on its own:

- **L46** rewritten with the gate sweep's negative result in full, so nobody
  pays for those 38 reads twice, plus the three pool facts that make either
  werewolf condition reachable on demand.
- **L11** and **L15** rewritten with what held and, more usefully, what bounds a
  repeat: ISD's exact layer inventory (one 7b effect, one token-borne 7a CDA,
  four card CDAs, one 7c self-mod), and two configurations that cannot be built
  in this pool at all.
- **L47** (new) — the permanent that is a copy, as a thing the player can see.
  A token is marked `[tok]` on the battlefield pane and on no other reader; a
  non-token clone is marked nowhere. Records the two surfaces already cleared
  while finding it, so the idea starts from the unexamined half.
- **L48** (new) — the attachment SBA that only knows one of its cases.
  `sba.rs` requires `attached_to.is_some()` directly beneath a comment describing
  the unattached case, and `invariants/permanents.rs::check_settled` tests two
  states the engine cannot produce. Records why no game in this pool reaches it
  and the three card-pool arrivals that would make it live.
- **L49** (new) — the characteristic-defining P/T as a value other cards *read*.
  L11 verified the display; Corpse Lunge is the one card that consumes an
  off-battlefield power, with the exile paid at announcement and the damage
  computed on resolution.
