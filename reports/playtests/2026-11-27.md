## D1: pack collation and conservation

I read `generate_foil` and `remove_from_run` against `docs/isd-booster-collation.md`. Then a scratch `mtg-draft/examples/` probe (deleted afterwards; kept in the session scratchpad) generated 400,000 packs. It classifies each pack's commons by run and keys them by the foil's rarity and `displaced_run`.

An uncommon foil should displace a B common. `remove_from_run` removes index 3, which is the first B card in three of the four common variants. In **4A+3B+2C2** it is the fourth A card, so those packs read `3A 3B 2C` while recording `displaced_run: "b"` (#1099). That is about 0.93% of packs, and the effect on any per-card rate is under 1%.
