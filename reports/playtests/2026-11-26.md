## G41: every way the page's runner can leave (first run)

| exit | runner | page |
|---|---|---|
| `--stop-after 6` | exit 0 | closed `over: "stopped"`, "the runner serving this page stopped: --stop-after 6", title "This game has stopped" |
| SIGTERM | 143 | the same, "stopped by SIGTERM" |
| SIGINT | 130 | the same, "stopped by Ctrl-C (SIGINT)" |
| SIGHUP | 129 | the same, "stopped by SIGHUP" |
| SIGKILL | killed | the mulligan modal is still live, `connected: false`, title unchanged |

Every exit the runner can announce holds. After SIGKILL the page keeps the question on screen as if it could still be answered. Clicking KEEP THIS HAND records the click in `mtgDebug.sent`, but `sendRaw` drops it because the socket is not open, and the question is cleared anyway. From then on the panel says `Connecting…` and nothing else, and still did 30 s later (#1093). The "can't reach the game" line #1033 added only appears after a game over.

## G4: the crowded board, with the attackers widget

The board: an Army of the Damned / Endless Ranks deck against 40 Plains, seed 11, stopped at decision 105 with `--save`. That is turn 36, with p0 holding 199 permanents and the game not over. I resumed it with p0 at the page.

The resting board holds: `ZT x45` and `ZT x129` stacks, and 0 hits off the pane.

Declaring attackers one click at a time does not hold (#1094). Each marked token becomes its own card, so the row grows by one sliver per click. At 42 marks it is full, and the 13 Zombies that can still attack are folded into `+142` together with the 129 summoning-sick ones. The `+142` slot cannot be clicked. The only way to attack with 43-54 is All, then withdraw.

One thing that looked like a second bug and was not: when the mouse rests on the row, the hovered card is drawn on top of the others. My driver aimed its clicks from a frame computed with no hover, so they landed on the raised marked card and withdrew it, and the count flipped between 14 and 15. That was the driver's mistake, not the page's: a person sees the raised card. The guide now says to move the mouse off the row between scripted clicks.
