## D45: standings by hand

- **A 7-seat best-of-3 stub run (seed 4545) holds.** It had three byes. I recomputed FINAL STANDINGS by hand and it agrees to 0.1% everywhere:
  - each opponent's percentage is floored at 33%;
  - a bye is a 2-0 win and no opponent;
  - the order is right, including two 1-2 seats both at OMW 66.7% that GW correctly split.
- **Float rounding decides real ties (#1032).** `sorted_standings` takes OMW as a float mean in the order opponents were met and sorts with `total_cmp`. Equal OMWs therefore differ by an ulp, and the rounding, not GW, decides the tie.
  - I drove 60,000 random 6/7/8-seat Swiss events through `generate_pairings` and `record_round`.
  - 1,129 adjacent pairs of seats were level on points with OMW differing only by rounding, and 420 of those pairs were ranked against GW.
  - The issue carries a deterministic 6-seat repro: "OMW 44.3%, GW 55.6%" ranked above "OMW 44.3%, GW 57.1%".

## D47: where everybody is

- **#1039:** the host is told "is back" but never that the person went away.
