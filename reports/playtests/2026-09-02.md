# Playtest night — 2026-09-02

Fifteen crews played the Competitor, Rules Lawyer and Vandal personas in
parallel, hotseat self-play through the real CLI: **30 games across 15
missions**, five per persona. Every mission on the menu had been played on
2026-08-29, 08-30, 08-31 or 09-01, so the entire menu was off-limits and
**all 15 missions tonight were new** — C19-C23, L21-L25, V21-V25. They are
now in `docs/plans/playtest-missions.md`; per-mission detail is in
`reports/playtests/LEDGER.md`.

Most games ran with an explicit `--seed` so the findings replay
deterministically; the seeds are in each issue's repro block.

## Issues filed

**42 new issues, #100-#141**, all `bug` + `phase:playtest`, plus 6 comments
on issues filed earlier tonight. Only three playtest issues were open when
the night started (#85, #65, #63) — the fixer had closed 53 of the previous
nights' 57.

## Headline

**#103 — a `cli` seat with no controlling terminal spins at 100% CPU
forever.** No output, empty stderr, no exit; reproduced seven times across
six command shapes (stdin at EOF, a pipe, an infinite feed, stdout to a
pipe, under `setsid`, and with only one `cli` seat). `grep -rn
"is_tty\|isatty\|atty"` over `mtg-runner` and `mtg-player` returns nothing,
so when crossterm cannot open a terminal the `Err(_) => continue` arm of the
input loop (cli.rs:1735, 1847) becomes a hot loop. The engine itself is
fine headless — `--p1 random --p2 random < /dev/null` exits 0 in 0.53s.

**#108 — duplicate indices at declare-attackers fire the attack trigger once
per repeat, and it won a game.** Typing `0 0` instead of `0` made Kessig
Cagebreakers create 10 Wolf tokens instead of 5 — 20 extra power attacking,
exactly lethal. Combat itself de-duplicates (the creature taps once, is
listed once to the defender, deals damage once), so only the log line and
the *triggered abilities* multiply. CR 508.1a/508.2 make the declaration a
choice of a set; CR 603.2 means the attack event happens once. Three layers
pass the duplicates through untouched, and `--check-invariants` never fired.

**#141 — Disciple of Griselbrand's sacrifice ability gains 0 life in a real
game.** The card reads the sacrificed creature's toughness out of
`state.events`, but `engine::submit_action` clears `events` at the top of
every action: the cost is paid during `ActivateAbility`, the ability
resolves during a later `PassPriority`, and by then the `CreatureDied` event
is gone. The three unit tests covering the card all resolve the stack
directly without going through `submit_action`, so they never clear the
events and pass regardless — a test-shape blind spot as much as a bug.

**#112 — turn order is never randomized.** `--deck1` is on the play in all
seven games checked, across five seeds and both slot assignments, and no
flag can change it (CR 103.1). Beyond rules parity this invalidates A/B deck
testing between the two slots, which is exactly what mission C23 was doing.

**#128 — Rooftop Storm's alternative cost is unreachable when the normal
cost is payable.** `legal/casting.rs` generates both cast actions, but the
CLI collapses every `CastSpell` for one object into a single menu row and
keeps the paid one; the mana really was spent (CR 601.2b/118.9).

## What came back clean

Three missions found no rules defect at all, and the negative results are
worth as much as the issues:

- **L21 (illegal targets on resolution) reached all five of its checks and
  every one was correct.** Full fizzle under CR 608.2b was produced four
  different ways — bounce, exile-as-an-activation-cost, killing the target
  of an *activated ability*, and sacrifice — each logged `fizzled (all
  targets illegal)` with zero effects applied. Partial resolution does as
  much as it can; targets are locked in at announcement (CR 601.2c/603.3d);
  legality is re-checked on resolution; hexproof is enforced in both
  directions.
- **L24 (turn structure) verified CR 502.3, 503.1, 504.1, 513.1, 500.4,
  514.1/2/3 and 603.4 across 44 turns**, including "doesn't untap", the
  intervening-if clause on werewolf transforms, mana emptying at step
  boundaries, and the *negative* case for the extra cleanup step.
- **L23 (regeneration)** confirmed shield creation and per-destruction
  consumption, regeneration replacing both first-strike lethal damage and
  Blasphemous Act's 13, unused shields expiring at end of turn, no save from
  sacrifice, "can't be regenerated" beating two shields at once, and
  Angelic Overseer's two-round SBA layering — proven by the death order not
  matching object-id order.

Also verified correct along the way: the deck-out ending fires on the draw
and not when the library empties (V22, at turn 74 in two games, exactly as
the arithmetic predicts); hand-size accounting stayed exact over 45 turns
with no hand-content leak at any prompt or seat swap (C20); every life
transition in two games reconciled against what resolved, with APNAP
ordering across two controllers and the exact-0 SBA (C22); and 16 of 24
hostile-environment probes were clean, including 1x1 panes at startup, 3000
SIGWINCHes in 52 ms, `TERM=dumb`, stdout redirection and POSIX-correct
backgrounding (V24).

## Cross-cutting themes

- **The disambiguation fix stopped at targeted abilities.** #36/#61/#80 are
  fixed and demonstrably working for target menus — and four other prompt
  kinds still show byte-identical options: land targets (#100), a dual
  land's two mana abilities (#118, seen four times; six identical rows on
  Shimmering Grotto), the trigger-*ordering* menu (#116), and the
  sacrifice-choice prompt with two identical tokens (#136). Two of these
  cost a real game action: a Ghost Quarter blew up its own controller's land,
  and a sacrifice picker gave no way to tell which token was a spell's
  target.
- **Prompts routed through `read_line` are second-class.** They advertise
  pane shortcuts that do nothing (#122), reject invalid input with no
  message (#122), print rejections with raw `println!` over the LOG panel
  (#110, three independent sightings), never clip their echo (#109), and at
  the combat prompts are missing the shortcut line entirely (#120). One rule
  would fix the set: every prompt gets the affordances `choose_action` has.
- **Bare Enter still means different things at different prompts.** #73 and
  #76 fixed two of them; the X-cost prompt is the remaining outlier and it
  is the worst one — Enter there *casts the spell for X=0* and there is no
  Cancel anywhere in that step (#123). Declining a concede with `n`+Enter
  passes priority with the trailing keystroke (#127).
- **The engine still knows things it never says**, now mostly outside
  combat: triggered abilities and ability targets are absent from the log
  and a partial fizzle is byte-identical to a full resolution (#135); the
  stack panel renders graveyard and spell targets as `?`, the same glyph it
  uses for a target that has become illegal (#134); non-combat life loss
  changes the total with no log line (#129); prevented combat damage logs
  nothing while prevented-by-protection does (#137); Nevermore never shows
  the name it named (#130); a hasted creature still renders `[S]` (#139).
- **Auto-tap strands legal casts in more than one way.** #84's tier
  tiebreak did not cover colour preservation: three separate boards across
  two missions lost a castable spell to it (#114 and its comment), once
  spending two of three black sources on a cost needing one, once
  over-tapping four mana for a three-mana spell. There is no undo — mana can
  be added but not un-added — so each instance is a lost turn.

## Gaps in the card pool, for future nights

Five missions could not reach part of their brief because no card in the
chosen decks produces the effect. These are deck-pairing problems, not
engine results, and the ledger records them as unreached rather than passed:

- **Lifelink (CR 702.15a) is untestable in wb-coverage vs ub-zombies** —
  neither deck has a lifelink card. C22 verified everything else about life
  transitions.
- **Life-payment costs (CR 118.4/118.6) do not exist in ub-coverage or
  br-coverage** — all 50 cards' oracle text was checked.
- **Neither coverage deck in C20's pairing contains a single discard
  spell**, so cleanup discard-to-hand-size was the only discard available;
  targeted, random and "discard from an empty hand" all went untested.
- **CR 513.2 (an end-step trigger created during the end step) and CR 514.3a
  (the extra cleanup step) cannot be produced by ug-coverage or
  wb-coverage.** L24 recommends a unit test for 514.3a rather than another
  playtest attempt.
- **Trample and deathtouch never appeared in a multi-block** (C19): the only
  trampler died to a double block before damage, and Festerhide Boar was
  never drawn.
- L23 also could not reach a two-destructions-in-one-turn kill, regeneration
  versus exile, or Grave Bramble's protection from Zombies — the last needs
  a different deck *pairing*, since only the BG deck makes Zombies and Grave
  Bramble is in it.

## Unconfirmed, deliberately not filed

- **A dropped Enter at combat prompts.** Twice in game 1 of C19, characters
  typed at a combat prompt accumulated in the buffer with the Enter lost
  (`Attack (numbers/all/none, enter=none)> none000`), and a later standalone
  `C-m` submitted the whole thing. It did not recur across the remaining
  ~250 inputs, and 70 deliberate reproduction attempts at two different send
  rates all submitted cleanly. #35 and #91 are the fixed neighbours; a tmux
  artifact cannot be ruled out. Recorded so a third sighting is recognised.
- **The V22 save-write pattern**: the save is rewritten whole at every
  decision point, so total write volume is O(n²) in game length — about
  180 MB over a 74-turn game. It never showed up in input latency (flat at
  ~24 ms median from turn 10 to turn 73), so it is an observation, not a bug.
- Two crews reported a main phase auto-passing with a land still in hand and
  both retracted it on single-stepping — their own queued keystrokes. This
  is the second night running that this false positive has appeared and been
  traced to the driver rather than the engine.
