## H37: what an AI seat is asked between games, with a real seat

`mtg-draft-runner --model cc:claude-haiku-5-5 --players 8 --best-of 3 --seed 20261123`. The stub answered every pick, deck and game call, and Haiku answered the 40 between-games calls.
- **Found:** 22 of 40 replies had the wrong number of `maindeck` counts. One more was a deck under 40 cards. 23 of 40 decisions were kept-the-deck fallbacks, and none was asked again. In 14 of the 22, the counts were exactly the current deck's with the trailing zero rows left off or short, and 12 of those 14 asked for different basic lands, which were dropped with the rest (#1086). The prompt never says how many rows there are. Keeping the spells costs a 35-41-integer array reproduced exactly, and the call is never retried.
- Holds: every sideboarding call wrote its cache at the hour TTL (40/40, no 5-minute writes), and 39/40 read from the cache. The opponent's shown cards are public cards only. `play_first` is asked of the chooser only.
- Caveat: the stub built the decks, so their mana bases were random, and a seat had more reason to change lands than the loop's seats do.
