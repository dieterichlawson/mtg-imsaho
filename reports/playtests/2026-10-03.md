Subject chosen from the ledger: **drafting** had gone longest without a
night. Its last night was 2026-09-28. The harness last had one on 09-29,
the game on 09-30, the CLI on 10-01 and the GUI on 10-02. Of

Every run used `--model cc` with `CLAUDE_CODE_BIN` set to an absolute

`claude -p` call was made.

## D24: does a seat play the match it is now told it is in?

**#609's `## This match` section is right everywhere a stub could reach
it.** The stub conceded on a plan keyed by the seat's decklist hash and
the game number in the system prompt. Eight runs covered best-of 1, 2,
3, 4 and 5. In every game:

- the game number matched the count of `GAME` blocks already logged;
- the two seats' scores were mirrors;
- the stake sentence was true of the position.

The case the idea singled out, a seat leading **2-1 into game 4 of a
best-of-4**, was reached twice and played to both endings: 3-1, and 2-2
recorded as a Draw in the `MATCH` line, the standings (`0-0-1`) and
`record_round`.

Three defects:

- **#649.** Once a game can be drawn (`GameResult::Draw`, or a game
  abandoned at the action cap, #630), a final game whose result is
  already settled is still described as deciding the match. At 2-0 going
  into game 4 of a best-of-4 the leader is told "winning this game wins
  you the match", and the trailer "losing this game loses you the match".
  Reproduced by calling `MatchFormat::match_section` directly; stub games
  never reach the cap.
- **#650.** The progress line prints `(winner: Seat draw)`.
- **#651.** A bye is credited `wins_needed(best_of)` game wins (1 at
  best-of-1, 3 at best-of-4 and 5). The comment beside it cites MTR's
  2-0, and game wins are the first tiebreaker.

## D21: every exit path

The fixes from #537 and #538 hold. SIGINT, SIGTERM and a seat-fatal `die`
swept every in-flight `claude -p` group, including all 8 at
`--players 8`. Every `die` from bad arguments happens before any seat
exists. Scratch dirs leak on every `process::exit` and signal path, but
the next cc run's `prepare_seat` sweep removes them (**checked,
correct**).

Defects:

- **#652, the night's sharpest.** Rust ignores SIGPIPE, so with stderr
  closed (`2>&1 | head`) a seat's `eprintln!` panics. `die`'s own first
  `eprintln!` then panics before the sweep or the exit. `REPORTED` then
  parks every other thread in `loop { sleep }`. The runner sits at 0 CPU
  for ever, and in-flight calls live until their own 300 s timeout.
- **#653.** SIGQUIT (Ctrl-\\) orphans every tree to init, because only
  INT, TERM and HUP have handlers.
- **#654.** SIGKILL orphans every tree, since there is no pdeathsig and
  no subreaper. This is filed as a design question.
- **#655 and #656.** `--resume` validates the card in a replayed pick but
  not the snapshot's shape. Two records for one seat give pools of 42
  and 41 and exit 0. A round missing one seat is silently re-asked live,
  and the summary still counts the dropped record as replayed.
- **#657.** `die`'s `Error:` is glued onto the pick progress line.

## D23: every record a worker writes

**#586 is fixed.** The pick loop now buffers too (`main.rs:1000/1027`),
and the eight pick-loop `SESSION` lines come back in seat order. Every
Info and Debug `game_log` write reachable from a `spawn_seat` worker was
enumerated, and each is inside one of the three buffered scopes.

Thirteen runs at `--players 8 --best-of 3` gave a **sorted diff of 0 on
every pair**. That included forced API_RETRY, API_ERROR, API_WARN,
NO_ANSWER, MALFORMED, FALLBACK and eight played-out STALLED forfeits, and
logs of 586k lines. STALLED and FALLBACK records are deterministic, and
`--resume` from a truncated save replays the same decks and tournament.

The **ordered** diff is never empty, though:

- **#658.** Error-level records are written through by design. The doc
  comment's premise is that a clean seeded run has none, and that is
  false: one rejected answer is enough. Those records are both
  reordered between runs and displaced from their match. A stall's 102
  explanatory `MALFORMED [Seat0]` lines sit 62,000 lines before the
  stall.
- **#659.** Game-side `API_RETRY` and `API_ERROR` name no seat. The
  thread is `seat A v B`, and the message has no `[SeatN]`.
- **#660.** The failure text reads `exit exit status: 1`.

## Method notes

- A stub that wants a specific match position reads the game number from
  `**This is game G of at most N.**` in the system prompt, and the seat
  from a hash of the system prompt with the `## This match` section cut
  out. That leaves the decklist, which is stable across the match.
- Launch a runner under test for signals from a launcher that resets
  SIGQUIT to `SIG_DFL`. A bash background job inherits it ignored, which
  hides #653.
