## L4: combat rules

**The read.** `queue_damage_step` walks the attackers.
- An attacker whose blockers are all gone (`live_blockers` empty) but which was blocked divides its power with `trample` deciding everything: all of it goes over with trample, none without.
- `with_lethal` gives 1 to a deathtouch source and otherwise toughness minus marked damage.
- `deals_in_step` keys the regular step on `dealt_first_strike`, which matches CR 702.7c and 702.4d.

Every one of these held on the board.

**Board A** (seed 412, p1 attacking):
- Typhoid Rats pumped by Kessig Wolf Run X=2 (3/1 trample deathtouch) was blocked by a Festerhide Boar. It assigned 1 lethal and 2 went to the player (CR 702.19c with 702.2c).
- p1's Festerhide Boar was double-blocked by two Manor Skeletons. Before damage, one Skeleton was regenerated in response to Geistflame: it was tapped, out of combat, `[BLK]` gone. The other was killed by a second Geistflame. The Boar dealt all 3 to the player (CR 702.19e).
- p0 went from 20 to 15, as the rules give.

**Board B:**
- Terror of Kruin Pass was placed transformed and pumped by Wolf Run X=1 (4/3 double strike, trample, menace). A single block was refused, citing CR 509.1b.
- It was then triple-blocked, ordered Ironsmith, Skeleton, Boar, with the Skeleton shielded.
- First-strike step: 1/1/2 assigned. Ironsmith's first strike hit the Terror. The Skeleton regenerated (out of combat) and Ironsmith died.
- Regular step: the Boar's lethal was recomputed as 1 (3 toughness, 2 marked), 1 was assigned to it and 3 trampled over. The Boar's 3 killed the Terror, which already had 1 marked.
- Ironsmith dealt in the first step only. p0 ended at 17. Correct throughout.

**Found: #1020.** The blocker prompt cut its refusal at the pane edge:

```
│  Terror of Kruin Pass 4/3 (opp) can't be blocked by fewer than 2 creatures (CR 509.1b) — add │
```

The message goes on "blockers or drop the block." Both combat prompts paint their refusal with a bare `clip_cols`, which drops the end and adds no marker. The end is where these messages say what to do. The LLM seat gets the whole text. The page draws its own notice.

## Notes for the next night

- **Placing a transformed DFC.** Set `is_transformed: true`, `transforms: 1`, and also the object's `name` and `power`/`toughness` to the back face's. The validator otherwise refuses: "name says Kruin Outlaw but the face is Terror of Kruin Pass".
- **The placer now clears `sba_check_starts` with `events`.** The validator refuses "sba_check_starts [2] not increasing within the 0 events that exist".

Twice tonight a loop of blind Enters skipped a sorcery-speed main phase. Read the header after every Enter, and stop the loop on the header you want.
