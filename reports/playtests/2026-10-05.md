## L53 — planner vs hand-tap parity

I read `compute_autotap` and its callers (`legal/casting.rs`,
`legal/abilities.rs`, `mana_sources.rs`, `funding.rs`, the auto-pass gate),
then ran 13 positions staged by editing saves.

**Checked, correct**:
- **Earlier fixes**: #615 (Grotto + Pilgrim offers Avacynian Priest), #616
  (the Darkthicket Wolf pump avoids the Mountain), #617 (a lone Grotto doesn't
  stop the seat) and #626 (Skirsdag Cultist funded by tapping the Pilgrim it
  then sacrifices; legal under CR 601.2g-h).
- **5fb646c**: a Grotto filter funded by floating `{W}` leaves `{R}` for
  Geistflame.
- **Plans**: Sol Ring's extra `{C}` floats and funds the next spell. Kessig
  Wolf Run's X maximum excludes Wolf Run itself and the sources in its own
  plan. Mikaeus's X is funded through the Grotto filter. Every cast row matched
  what could be cast by tapping by hand.
- **Brute force**: the real `compute_autotap`, linked into a scratch crate and
  run against every hand-tap over ~320k boards with pool-shaped costs, gave 0
  missing plans, 0 plans that fail to execute and 0 over-taps.

**Broke**: a plan can spend a source the next spell needed.
- **#674**: Phase 3's `is_redundant` uses `ability_producing`, which ignores a
  filter's cost. Shimmering Grotto therefore "covers" White even when nothing
  is left to fund it, so `{1}{G}` is paid with Forest + Plains and the `{W}`
  spell is stranded. Seen for spells and for the Disciple and Township ability
  plans.
- **#678**: a cast paid from floating mana pays generic in the fixed order
  C,W,U,B,R,G. With R G G floating, Grizzly Bears spends the `{R}` and
  Geistflame drops off the menu. With no other action on the board, the seat
  is then auto-passed into the opponent's turn.
- **#679**: `ManaSourceKind::Creature` outranks hand demand, so Chapel Geist
  taps the only red source to keep a Pilgrim untapped. Filed as possibly
  deliberate.

**Latent, not filed**: three-colour costs, two-colour costs with a repeated
pip, and `{C}` pips make the greedy scarcity sort miss real plans. No card in
today's pool has any of these costs, so nobody can meet it yet. It is recorded
as an idea instead.

## L54 — the response window that isn't there

I re-read `legal/abilities.rs`, `legal/casting.rs`, the auto-pass gate and
the CLI's `should_break_pass`. Every battlefield activated ability is now
funded by `compute_autotap`, including granted abilities, transformed faces,
X abilities and flashback.

**Checked, correct**: every case below had an empty pool and untapped lands,
and in each one the ability was offered and auto-pass stopped for it.
- **Disciple of Griselbrand**: offered in its own upkeep and main, and in
  response to Brimstone Volley on the opponent's turn. Sacrificing the

- **Own main phase**: Kessig Wolf Run (X 0–6, the correct maximum), Gavony
  Township, Moorland Haunt, Back from the Brink, Grimoire of the Dead, Skaab
  Ruinator's exile-3 cast and the flashbacks.
- **In response**: Selfless Cathar (White only through the Grotto filter),
  Skirsdag Cultist, Traveler's Amulet and the ability Blazing Torch grants.
- **After attacks**: the Feeling of Dread and Think Twice flashbacks.
- **Sorcery-speed items** were missing from every window on the opponent's
  turn, as they should be.
- **A summoning-sick Pilgrim** is never used as a mana source.
- **Liliana of the Veil** is offered in her own main and nowhere else.

The random, LLM and GUI seats read the same `LegalActions`, so they get the
same offers.

**Broke** (CLI only):

## L55 — nothing happens after "wins the game"

I read 31ab118, which snapshots the state at `end_game` and restores it at
every loop boundary. `end_game` has two callers: the SBA game-end check and
Laboratory Maniac.

**Checked, correct**: in each case below, nothing followed the win line.
- **Desperate Ravings with one card left**: no discard, and the RNG state was
  byte-identical before and after.
- **Civilized Scholar on an empty library**: no discard prompt on the CLI. The
  stubbed `cc` seat recorded only two calls, and the GUI seat received
  Decision, Decision, View, GameOver with no discard decision.
- **Bloodgift Demon's trigger** on the opponent: the Maniac won and the life
  loss never happened.
- **Murder of Crows**, and the **draw-step** win.
- **A first-strike kill**: there was no regular damage step, and Curiosity's
  trigger never went on the stack.
- **Two Falkenrath Noble triggers**: the second never resolved.
- **Runner**: exit 0, the summary printed once, RESULT is the last log line,
  and the final save was written.

Damage-then-X and life-loss-then-X cards can't end a game partway through,
because a player at 0 life loses only at the next SBA check (CR 704.3). So
those effects running to completion is correct.

**Broke**:
- **#675**: when a *spell* ends the game, the snapshot is taken with the spell
  popped off the stack and `resolving_spell` still set. `check_core` rejects
  that state, `--resume` calls the final save "an invalid game state" instead
  of giving the #316 "finished game" refusal, and the final view shows the
  spell in no zone. Abilities and triggers are clean.
- **#676**: Concede is offered only at a priority menu on the CLI and the
  page. The engine accepts it at every prompt (CR 104.3a), but the mulligan,
  combat declarations and resolution choices offer no way to send it.
- **#677**: the Debug line "First-strike combat damage step (CR 510.4);
  regular damage follows" is written even when first-strike damage ends the
  game. The tester saw it and left it unfiled; the lead filed it, per "observed
  is filed".

Not reached: a drawn game (both players losing at once) has no reachable
source in this pool.
