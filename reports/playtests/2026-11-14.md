## G12: auto-pass's silence (first run)

I played 10 whole games through the page against the random seat: seeds 1201-1208 over red-green/white-black, blue-white/black-aggro, innistrad-blue/green and the ub/rg coverage decks. A driver ran `autoplay.js`'s `step` and wrapped `window.mtgDebug.trace.push` to keep the fresh log slice of every frame that ended 8 or more unseen priorities.

- **`f` and `s` compose.**
  - `s` says "Stopping at every priority."
  - With it on, every pass-only priority is drawn as a menu.
  - `f` pressed with `s` on passes those priorities, logged as `auto-passed`, and stops at the promised Main Phase 1.
  - `s` off says "Passing automatically…".
  - Five of the `f` presses were refused with "Auto-pass would pass up your land drop", which is right.
  - The games produced no page errors and no invariant failures.
- **The longest runs** were 18-33 unseen priorities. The 33 covered T2 to T6, a whole turn of your own with no land and nothing castable.
- **What the band says after a run is often the wrong line.** `bandHeadline`'s second fallback, "a line the opponent wrote", tests `author(l) !== you`. An author-less line has `author` null, so turn separators and `X resolved` lines pass the test. They also come first in the interval, so they win the headline. Over the 94 frames that ended a long run:
  - 60 were headlined by such a line;
  - 34 of those had an opponent's cast or attack hidden behind "+N", with the second slot showing your own draw.

  This was checked against the real `dist/render.js`. Filed **#1026**.
