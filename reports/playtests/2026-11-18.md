## L68: where an entering copy waits

Board A: p0's Grimoire of the Dead (3 study counters) and Boneyard Wurm on the battlefield, p1's Doomed Traveler. In the graveyards: an Evil Twin each, p1's Festerhide Boar and p0's Splinterfright. Grimoire's resolution defers each Twin and asks them one at a time, and four rules go wrong in that gap:

- **#1064**: state-based actions run after the first Twin's answer. The Wurm and Splinterfright die mid-resolution, and the second Twin's copy of Festerhide Boar enters with morbid's counters as a 5/5 (CR 704.3).
- **#1065**: each Twin's menu offers the creatures returned with it, Splinterfright and the Boar, and for the second Twin the first Twin's copy too.
- **#1066**: p1's Twin, entering under p0's control, is asked of p1. The save's `pending_entry_controllers` holds the right player, but `awaiting_action` names the owner.
- **#1067**: the Twins never become black Zombies, since Grimoire's `continue` skips the stamp, and the count line counts them as returned.

Board B: Back from the Brink exiling an Evil Twin. **#1068**: the token is never asked what to copy and dies as a 0/0. A Twin cast from hand on the same board is asked, and at its prompt the Wurm reads 2/2 because the resolving Twin counts as a creature card in the graveyard. That is #1061's zone shown in a characteristic, so it went on #1061 as a comment.

Not reached: Unburial Rites on a Twin, a single card from the graveyard. The cast and Grimoire paths cover its mechanism, but it is not checked.
