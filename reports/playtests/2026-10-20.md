## L61: every planner that is not the menu's

**Method:**
- Listed every caller of `compute_autotap_around`, `gather_mana_sources` and `order_around_demands`, and compared what each passes.
- The menu (`engine.rs:333`), `payment_plan_with_sources`, X funding (`funding.rs`) and the auto-pass reach check (`cards_flow.rs`) all pass `prevents_artifact_abilities(state, registry)`.
- `plan_autotap_for_cost`, which only Screeching Bat calls, passes a literal `false`.

**Found, #866 (`sev:rules`).** Under Stony Silence the Bat's upkeep plan counts Sol Ring, and execution then skips it:

1. With Plains, 2 Swamps and Sol Ring, the cost is unpayable. The prompt still reads `pay {2}{B}{B} (tap 2x Swamp, Sol Ring) to transform?`, and a Yes taps both Swamps and logs `Could not pay {2}{B}{B} to transform`.
2. With Plains and **3** Swamps, the lands alone pay. The planner still picks Sol Ring, and the payment fails the same way.

Both reproduced in a CLI hotseat game (seed 6102, T10 and T12). Seed 6102 random vs random leaves 13 such lines, and seeds 6101-6140 leave 273. In the same position the menu correctly offers `Cast Screeching Bat (tap 2x Swamp (your))` with no Sol Ring. `--check-invariants` said nothing: no invariant compares a resolution-time prompt's plan against what execution can activate.

**Checked, correct (with the evidence):**
- **X funding and Mentor's {1} under Stony Silence:** 40 random games on Mountains, Kessig Wolf Run, Sol Ring, Stony Silence, Devil's Play and Mentor. There were 0 Sol Ring taps after a Stony Silence resolved and 0 `Could not pay` lines.
- **Artifact abilities under Stony Silence:** 40 random games with Blazing Torch, Butcher's Cleaver, Traveler's Amulet and Sol Ring on Doomed Travelers. Before Stony Silence there were 107 Equip and 37 Amulet activations, and after it resolved there were none.
- **Frightful Delusion's PayOrNot:** it names its plan ("(Pay: …)") and is gated on `payment_plan_with_sources`, which respects Stony Silence.
- **The planner's repair:** `Demand::payable` and `keep_the_hand_castable` read as described in L61. I did not stage a Phase 3 tie this time; that half of L61 is still open (see the guide).

**L57:** no card in `data/oracle_cache.json` has a three-colour, repeated-pip two-colour, `{C}` or hybrid cost the engine implements. Esper Sentinel, Asmoranomardicadaistinaculdacar and Tourach are in the cache but not in `cards/`, so nothing was filed.
