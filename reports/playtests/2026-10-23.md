**The ones to read first:**
- **#924.** #884's resume check covers only cards someone has already taken. A rebuild that moves an untaken card is accepted. The rare in the pack a person is holding changes under them, their review rewrites what they were passed, and packs 2-3 are another deal. This happens on the hosted table and in the batch runner.
- **#917.** The hosted table writes its snapshot from every seat and match thread through one shared `table.save.tmp`. That produces spurious "cannot be written" warnings at healthy tables; it was seen on three tables tonight. The same interleaving can rename an older state over a newer one.
- **#920.** A kept pick position does not record the seat's guide. The nightly loop drafts half its seats with a guide, so half of every night's "pick eval input" replays a different question from the one that was live. Under a stub that hashes the system prompt, only 6 of 39 guided picks replayed as live.
- **#921.** `mtg-draft-runner` with an unwritable `--save` or `MTG_DRAFT_POSITIONS` path runs the whole draft and tournament keeping nothing. It prints one warning per pick. #891 made the server refuse at start-up; the runner was not changed.

## D41: the pick nobody is asked

**Holds on every check.**
- Each seat is asked 39 picks and never a pick 14: 80 draft calls per 2-seat run, against 84 PICK lines.
- The 14th card lands in the pool, the snapshot, and both reviews.
- The notes from pick 13 open the next pack.
- `--resume` from either side of a 14th pick replays it without a call and ends on the uninterrupted run's picks.
- A person sees a one-card pack, on the client and on the page, and the timer takes it.

**Found:**
- **#923.** `ledger.py` counts the 14th pick as taken and never as seen, so taken/seen ratios are overstated.

## D42: kept picks

**Holds on every listed check.**
- Each asked pick is kept once. None is kept for a one-card pack or a replayed pick.
- `substituted` is right.
- The notes match what the seat was shown, byte for byte.
- Replay chose `live` on every unguided line.

**Found:**
- **#920.** A position does not record the seat's guide (see above).
- **#921.** The runner does not refuse an unwritable path (see above).
- **#922.** `mtg-draft-replay` calls a typo like `CC` "a metered seat; pass --metered".

**Method note:** `scripts/llm-prompt-stub.py` hashes only stdin. A "replay matches live under the stub" check is blind to anything that changes the system prompt.

## D53: a drawn game in a match

The ISD pool cannot stage a draw. No implemented card kills or decks both players at the same check, and the 50,000-action cap has no knob. So the probe used three methods:
- a scratch crate calling the crate's public functions;
- a hosted table resumed from a doctored save holding drawn games;
- a code read of every surface.

**Holds:**
- After a draw, the same seat chooses again (MTR 2.2).
- GW%, OMW and OGW with draws are right on the terminal, `/api/view` and the page. I worked them by hand.

**Found:**
- **#918.** The game page, draft page and client call a game abandoned at the action budget "a draw". #743 says it is not one.
- **#919.** A drawn match shows as a bare "0-0" in the pairings.
- **#917.** The snapshot race, found by accident.
- Live prompt text added to #913.
