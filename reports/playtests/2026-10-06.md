## Method notes

- A sweep tester also has to check that **two handler sets compose**: #686 exists because the #653 fix was tested with no `cli` seat.
- Stderr has two failure shapes. A closed stderr (`2>&-`) is EBADF and harmless. A pipe whose reader has exited is EPIPE, and that one panics.
- V53's boards are a reusable asset. The staging script in #701 builds them from any base save in about 30 lines.
