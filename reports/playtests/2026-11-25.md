## L72: CR 400.7 off the battlefield

Code read: `GameObject`'s fields beside `move_object_inner`, and every `GameState` list keyed by `ObjectId`.

Checked, correct:
- **Marks a cast leaves.** `x_value` goes off the stack and the battlefield. `cast_with_flashback` and `cast_from_zone` are cleared on a move to hand or library, and both are rewritten at every cast. `targets` and `chosen_mode` are written fresh on every cast, so a later cast cannot inherit an earlier one.
- **`card_state`.** It is cleared on entering the battlefield. On a spell it holds only cost records (Harvest Pyre's `EXILE_COUNT`, Corpse Lunge's exiled power), and those are re-inserted on every cast.
- **Activated-ability counts.** `abilities_activated_this_turn` is cleared on leaving the battlefield. No card in the pool activates from a graveyard or a hand, so the other zones never need it.
- **A cancelled cast.** `cancel_stashed_cast` cancels before the card moves, so hand → stack → hand never happens.
- **Fiend Hunter.** Its ETB trigger resolving after the Hunter has left and been re-cast stores nothing, through `still_on_battlefield`'s `is_new_source`.
- **`ModifyPTWhileSourceInPlay`.** It would revive if its source came back, but no card creates it.

**Found:** a source's id in another creature's `damaged_by` is never removed when that source leaves the battlefield. The board was Abattoir Ghoul (p0) against Fortress Crab (p1), seed 72, a UB placed board. The Ghoul dealt 3 first-strike damage to a blocking Crab. In main phase 2, p0 bounced the Ghoul with Silent Departure, re-cast it, and cast Victim of Night on the Crab. The new Ghoul triggered and p0 gained 6 (#1090, CR 400.7). It is the source-side twin of what #1089 fixed for granted flashback, and Abattoir Ghoul is the only card that reads `damaged_by` against `self_id`.

Not reached: the graveyard → library → graveyard board with Memory's Journey and Forbidden Alchemy. The code read found nothing that survives that move, so I did not build it.
