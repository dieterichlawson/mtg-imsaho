### A probe from the sweep: what the CLI reads that the page is not sent

#785 is a case of a general shape: the CLI holds a `CardRegistry` and the
page only has `GameView`, so anything the CLI looks up there the page cannot
show. Walking those call sites, and then the places the engine puts
information in `legal.context` rather than in the prompt payload, found
**#786**: the page titles a resolution prompt from `rp.description`, but
#362's "which of my two Thraben Sentries" tag is appended only to
`legal.context`. Two Sentries' transform prompts are byte-identical on the
page, nothing on the board marks the source, and #116's ordering rows come
out identical once `withoutIds` strips `#12`/`#13` (and are clipped before the
tag anyway). Staged with the exact shapes the engine's #362 test produces.
