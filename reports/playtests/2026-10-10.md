## L60 — costs that ask a question halfway through paying (first run)

The code read narrowed the idea. Spells never prompt after paying: X funding, "up to N" targets and exile costs all ask with nothing paid yet, and sacrifice choices are made in the client before anything is sent. Only two prompts come after the tap plan has run: Grimoire of the Dead's discard and Moorland Haunt's exile with two or more creature cards. Neither offers a cancel, which is legal because the cost is always payable at that point. Both log activated, then the taps, then the cost, which fits CR 601.2h.

Every cancel was tried on the CLI, the page and the random seat: Wolf Run's X, Skirsdag High Priest's tap-two, Stitched Drake's exile, Devil's Play's flashback X, Feeling of Dread's targets, and the Disciple and Altar's Reap sacrifice screens. Each one untapped the lands, put the card back, and logged no cast or activated line. 20 random games (seeds 6301-6320) cancelled 13 times with 0 violations.

What is wrong is the log:
- **#760:** Back from the Brink's exile cost is paid at activation with no line. The only mention comes at resolution, after the token line.
- **#761:** an X-spell cancel is logged at Debug only, and the target-set cancel doesn't name the spell.
- **#762:** Grimoire's single study counter is logged twice (#706's shape).
- **#763:** Blazing Torch's sacrifice is logged "to pay for its own ability", but the ability belongs to the equipped creature.

## L58 — the final position is a real position (first run)

About 24 endings were played and each final save was checked three ways:
- through `--resume`;
- through a scratch crate running `check_core`/`check_settled` and building `GameView` for both seats;
- through the page's `game_over` over a WebSocket seat.

**#675's fix holds on every ending inside the rules.** These all gave a clean `check_core`, the #316 refusal on resume and nothing logged after the win line, with the reason named on the CLI, in RESULT and on the page:
- Laboratory Maniac wins from a spell, an ability (Civilized Scholar), a trigger (Bloodgift Demon) and the draw step;
- a first-strike kill;
- a Falkenrath Noble drain;
- a decking loss.

Two gaps:
- **#765:** a concede or forfeit at the mulligan or bottom prompt leaves a save `check_core` rejects ("mulligan phase with … a result"), so `--resume` calls it an invalid state instead of giving the finished-game refusal.
- **#766:** a resolving spell is in no zone of `GameView`. The STACK reads "(empty)" while Night Terrors asks its question, and after a Desperate Ravings Maniac win the spell is nowhere on the page's final board.

## L59 — CR 104.3a at every prompt kind (first run)

Concede is offered on every CLI screen reached (#676 holds). A concede at priority, declare attackers, declare blockers and cleanup discard ends the game cleanly.

**#764 (sev:rules).** A concede at a resolution choice (Night Terrors, Liliana of the Veil +1) or at a trigger-target prompt (Bloodgift Demon) does not end the game. `lose_to_act` leaves the loss to the next state-based-action check, so:
- the loser is asked the same question again, and the effect finishes after "p0 conceded";
- after Liliana +1, the winner is then asked to discard too;
- under `--check-invariants` the run exits 2;
- a `cc` seat forfeiting at that prompt loops 100 times until the watchdog stops the run with exit 1 and no RESULT.

It behaves the same on the CLI and the page. CR 104.3a says a player who concedes leaves the game immediately.
