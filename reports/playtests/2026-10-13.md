## D33 — the build clock (new)

**When the clock runs out, `auto_build` ignores the deck the seat sent** (#810). The only deck that survives the clock is one marked Ready. These were all replaced by `fallback_deck`, in different colours:
- a legal 40 sent but not Ready;
- a 40 edited card by card;
- the 40 of a seat whose tab was then closed.

The log says "no deck was submitted", which is false for all of them. The page had just told the person they had "Ns left to send a deck, or the table builds one for you".

Other findings:
- **#811:** a seat that finishes drafting first is on the clock while the view's phase is still `drafting`. Its page says "Waiting for a pack" with no deck screen. On one table, 5 of 20 s were gone before the build screen appeared.
- **#812:** both clocks accept any u64. At u64::MAX the first deadline panics with "overflow when adding duration to instant" and wedges the table: `/ws` and `/api/view` stop answering. A clock of 0 is accepted.
- **#813:** the client never shows `build_deadline_ms`.
- **#815:** the client prints the person's own deck after the table replaced it.
- **#817:** the page's "The table built your deck" notice is cleared within about 1 ms by the playing view.

**Checked, correct:**
- Ready before the deadline keeps the person's deck.
- All eight `decks/seat-N.txt` files are legal against their POOL blocks.
- The log names each expiry with a reason.
- The page's countdown tracks `build_deadline_ms` within one second over 45 s.
- A short deck is kept with its `problem`.
- The flag parser refuses `-1`, `abc` and 2^64.

## D34 — the bye with a person at it (new)

**The tournament half is right.** Over 3- and 5-seat hosted tables (5 byes) and a 3-seat runner on the same seed:
- The bye goes to the lowest-ranked seat on the fewest byes, without rematches, and the runner's pairings are identical.
- A bye scores 3 points plus `wins_needed` game wins, with `[1 bye]` on every surface.
- The tiebreak agrees with the runner.
- Packs are 14 cards, numbered 1..14.
- Pass direction wraps correctly over 3 and 5 seats. 234 pack chains were checked: each pack is the upstream pack minus its pick.

**What a person is told is not right:**
- **#814:** the seat with the bye sees "Your matches: No pairings yet". A round-2 bye never appears in their list, and the client prints nothing for it.
- **#816:** the host terminal and the client use names during the draft and seat numbers from the tournament on.
- **#818:** a bye reads "done" all round, while the standings beside it give that seat 0 points until the round ends.
- **#819:** "has drafted all 42 cards" and "the draft is over" print before that seat's last "picked" line.
- **#820:** the hosted log files the next round's BYE and FINAL STANDINGS under the thread of the match that just ended.

## D35 — a person leaves mid-match (new)

**Both D28 gaps from 2026-10-08 still reproduced. They had been written into the plan, not the tracker, and are now filed.**
- **#821:** `kick` mid-match prints "its games are forfeit", but the match goes on and the kicked seat's page keeps answering. When the opponent conceded, the kicked seat was credited with game 1, and then game 2 waited on that page for ever. The round never ends and round 2 never pairs. The same happens with `human,ai`. `docs/draft-with-friends.md` lists mid-match kicks under Limitations, which the issue notes, but the host's own message promises a forfeit and the table can never finish.
- **#823:** SIGINT exits 130 with no line on the terminal or in the log, while `quit` writes a NOTE.

Also new:
- **#822:** a closed game page leaves the opponent at "OPPONENT IS THINKING" with both draft pages showing "here". The here/away column follows only the draft page's socket.
- **#824:** `quit` resets the sockets, so the client prints a protocol error and the page retries "Your match is on" for ever.
- **#825:** a kick is never logged, and its forfeits read "the seat is away".
- **#826:** "1 rounds", and one seat appears as "Lea", "seat 0" and "Seat 0".

**Checked, correct:**
- Closing only the draft tab leaves the game alone.
- Reopening the game URL re-asks the pending decision.
- A kick before the round forfeits the match 2-0, standings agree on all surfaces, and round 2 pairs.
- No orphaned ports, threads or stub children after SIGINT or `quit`.
