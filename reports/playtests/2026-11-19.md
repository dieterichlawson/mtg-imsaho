## L69: the cast-time position

Sturmgeist on p0's battlefield and in its graveyard, and a hand of 4 including Smite the Monstrous and Corpse Lunge.

- The engine is right. Smite is offered only p1's 4/4 Sturmgeist, because p0's is 3/3 once Smite is on the stack. The Lunge exiling the graveyard Sturmgeist deals 3.
- The screens are not. The board and the Lunge's mark screen both count the spell in hand (`[x] 0: Sturmgeist {3}{U}{U} 4/4`), so the number the player picks by is one more than the damage (#1069).
- Checked, correct: a spell cast from its graveyard cannot target itself. `o.id != spell_id` is on every zone arm of `valid_targets_for_req`.
