## V58 — the tap a row or a prompt promises (new)

Setup:
- Two one-off decks: a Grixis pile (Drownyard, Bloodhall, Think Twice, Moan, Bump in the Night, Devil's Play, Volley, Ravings) and a G/W pile (Township, Mentor, Doomed Traveler, Viper, Travel Preparations, Midnight Haunting).
- One hotseat CLI game (seed 5801) under `--check-invariants`, plus five positions staged by save edit.
- For every row chosen, I diffed its "(tap …)" against the "tapped X" lines in `--log`, then checked that the next menu still offered everything that had been payable both ways.

**The rows tell the truth.** Each of these tapped exactly what its row said:
- Brimstone Volley.
- Desperate Ravings with a floating {U}, which was correctly kept back for Think Twice's flashback.
- Drownyard's mill.
- Midnight Haunting.
- Doomed Traveler.
- Mentor's heading "pay {1} (tap Gavony Township) to draw a card?", which also fits at 80 and 100 columns.

**X funding does not protect anything (#843).** Devil's Play with X = 3 tapped Mountain, then Island, Island, Swamp. That left no blue, and Think Twice's flashback dropped off the menu. Bloodhall, Drownyard and a Swamp could have paid X instead. The cause is in `order_around_demands`:
- it sorts by category first, so basics are spent before `{C}`;
- it ranks by the coloured pips of every demand, including ones that cannot be paid (Bloodhall's {B}{R} once the Mountain is gone);
- it never checks payability the way the cast planner does.

The X prompt takes only a number, so the only way to avoid this is to hand-tap first.

30 random-vs-random games on the pair (seeds 5810-5839, 27 X fundings): 0 invariant violations.

Checked, correct, not filed:
- For an activated ability the log line comes before the taps; for a cast it comes after. Both orders follow CR 601.2/602.2.
- At 100 columns the battlefield land row elides Gavony Township as "+1 more" while the prompt names it. That is a deliberate elision at the pane edge.

## V3 — save/reload abuse (re-run)

Method: copy the live `--save` at each pending decision, resume the copy with the same seed, then:
- diff the captured screens;
- type the same keys into both processes;
- compare the normalised logs and deep-diff the two saves.

**Every pending decision resumes identically.** Positions covered: the mulligan, the bottom, declare attackers and blockers, Mentor's pay, Caravan Vigil's search, the X-funding prompt (including `c` to cancel after the resume), the cleanup discard, Traveler's Amulet's search, damage assignment order, and Liliana's +1 with one seat's discard held.

Also checked, correct:
- A marked but unsubmitted bottom is not saved.
- The same file resumed twice, in sequence or in parallel, gives two independent games.
- Chained `--resume X --save X` logs one RESUMED line per process.
- A finished save is refused.
- `rr` keeps `--save`, `--log` and `--seed`, and drops keys typed while it rebuilds.

**A resume is not a continuation (#844).** The random seat's RNG is not in the save. `make_player` re-seeds the seat from `--seed`, so its sequence restarts at the resume point. Seed 404 played straight through ends at 713 actions; resumed mid-game from snapshot s37 it ends at 716, diverging 14 log lines after the resume point. Another snapshot gave 892 actions. The consequence: a fuzz failure at action N cannot be reproduced by resuming a save from before N.

Two side observations from the V3 subagent, not filed:
- Caravan Vigil's search is a numbered menu, while Traveler's Amulet's is the arrow-key search widget. Both resumed correctly, and nothing shows the difference to be a defect.
- Some tmux captures taken 1-3 s after a decision had row ends missing, and were complete when captured later. This was paint latency in the capture and never reproduced as a stuck frame.
