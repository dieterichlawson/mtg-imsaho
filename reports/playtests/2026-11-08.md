## H40: the guide-replay instrument against a seat that does not answer

I used 40 kept positions from tonight's draft loop and replayed each three
times (none-a, none-b, current) under a wrapper of the prompt stub that
answers wrongly one call in four:

- Prose with no object: each such pick retried with the same prompt,
  which the deterministic stub answers the same way, until its budget ran
  out. `mtg-draft-replay` then panicked with a backtrace (exit 101): it has
  no `install_panic_hook`, which #218 gave the runner and the server
  already has. `draft-replay.py` keeps the last stderr line as the reason,
  and for a panic that line is the backtrace footer, so all four dead rows
  say "note: Some details are omitted…". **#1009** (presentation).
- An index past the pack: after the re-ask, 3 of 120 picks were
  substituted (pack[0], `fallback: true`). `guide-effect.py` counted two of
  them as the model disagreeing with itself and one as a settled pick the
  guide left alone. `judge-picks.py` and the replay's resume read `action`
  the same way. **#1010** (game-affecting; unsure against presentation, so
  I took the higher).

The published 2026-11-07 guide-replay results contain 0 fallback rows, so
their numbers are not affected.
