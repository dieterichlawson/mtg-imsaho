## G39: every strip of the page at its largest

  - Strips: hand, either battlefield, graveyards, exile, `turn_effects`, the band's flags, the mana pool, all the strip's counts at once, and an inspected creature's facts.
  - For each frame I read `mtg.hits` for any rectangle past the board (x+w > 480) or off the canvas. A `fillText` wrapper caught text that has no hit: the band's flag row and the strip's parts. Every frame was screenshotted.
