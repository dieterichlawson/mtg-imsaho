## V36: the trigger-ordering prompt as a reader

- **Setup:** Rolling Temblor into p1's two Unruly Mobs, Delver and Doomed Traveler gives p1 a 7-trigger ordering screen. p0's lone Traveler trigger goes first and needs no prompt.
- **What the screen shows:** each row has its source id and cause, and the screen shows the stack beneath and the sources' text.
- **Hostile inputs:** a repeat, a gap, an out-of-range 9, `1a`, `-1` and `s` mid-list are each refused with a named reason. A comma-separated list is accepted.
- **A stray order typed at the next priority prompt** is refused as "Invalid input".
- **The log:** "ordered 7 triggers" prints each row without its cause, so the six Mob triggers are two repeated strings. They are interchangeable (same source, same ability), so nothing is lost.
- **A hotseat trap:** after the sweeper, p1 had no response, so the engine skipped p1's priority window. A second Enter therefore lands on the ordering screen and keeps the order shown. This is by design, and it's noted in the guide.
- Holds.
