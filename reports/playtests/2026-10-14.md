## H25 — every zone through every label helper (first run)

**Labels hold in every zone. The gaps are where nothing is shown at all.**

The probe built a board where both players hold a same-named object in each zone: land, creature, graveyard, exile, hand and stack. It rendered that board through the seat's `target_labels`, through the CLI's label helpers, and through the page's `beginDecision` under node.
- Every LLM and CLI label carries an owner, or an id that a pane on that surface also prints.
- Exile's `(exiled)` has an id but no owner. That cannot be reached today: `ExileCard` offers only the caster's own exile.

A delegating stub ran the real stub and then overrode `action` with the row matching `$PICK`. That steered a free game into one card.

- **#827** (game-affecting): a cast with one legal target is aimed without a prompt, and the row never names the target. GAME_RULES promises a `<card>: select a target:` follow-up. Twenty Brimstone Volleys against Witchbane Orb hit the seat 7 times and killed it. This is #254 on its fourth surface: the CLI and the page name the forced target, the seat does not.
- **#828** (game-affecting, picked over presentation): the prompt prints exile counts only. A seat deciding on Runic Repetition cannot see what it would return.
- **#829** (presentation): the page's forced verb reads "Cast → Invisible Stalker" with one Stalker on each side. The CLI says `(your)`.

Part (b) swept `activated_abilities` and `legal_actions` over boards of 0-20 creatures a side, with the morbid and transformed gates open.
- Skirsdag High Priest is 1 action at every size, so #670 holds.
- Skirsdag Cultist (861 actions at 20) and Demonmail Hauberk (400) remain target × sacrifice products in the engine. Every surface asks them one slot at a time.
- At 20v20 the page receives about 175 KB of `LegalActions` per offer. That cost is not person-facing, so it was not filed.

## What the night taught

- **The harness's open gaps are now absences, not mislabels.** The label helpers held everywhere H25 looked. The bugs were in what is never shown: a forced target, exile, turn-scoped facts, and prompts built outside `build_prompt`.
- **Grep the `len() == 1` shortcuts.** Every place a seat is answered on the model's behalf must be stated in GAME_RULES and named in the row. #827 is the second time this class has reached three surfaces and missed the fourth.
- **A delegating `$PICK` stub** steers a free game into one card cheaply (its source is in #827's repro).
- **Against `random`, games end before plans span turns.** The mulligan's keep → bottom pair is the one reliable two-decision plan, and it was where the seat forgot.
- **`pkill -f "seed N"` matches your own shell command.** It killed a Bash call in two probes tonight. Use `pgrep -f "mtg-runner.*--seed N"` and kill by PID.
