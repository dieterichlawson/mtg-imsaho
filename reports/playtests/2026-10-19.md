## H31: the turn that is one answer

**Ran:**
- 94 logs, with 1,519 PLAN and 1,283 ATTACK_PLAN lines scanned.
- Custom stubs:
  - an *order* stub (a spell before its land in `then`);
  - a *flash* stub (a second cc seat holding up Ambush Viper, Village Bell-Ringer or Midnight Haunting);
  - a *repair* stub (a flashback row copied into `then`).
- 9 stub drafts.

**What held:**
- Every plan step played the card it named (437 checked).
- Duplicates in `then` work.
- No step crossed a turn or a main phase.
- A dead, tapped or summoning-sick planned attacker is dropped.
- The draft runner's games plan through the same `LlmPlayer`.

**Found:** the plan's checks run only when the seat is called.
- **#860.** A pass answered with a plan whose first card can't be cast carries out the pass anyway. The land later in `then` is lost and the seat is not asked. Repo-stub seed 4, turn 27, reproduced by me independently.
- **#861.** The engine's auto-pass never calls a seat with nothing to respond with. So an opponent's flash creature cast after the plan is never seen:
  - the attack was declared into it 10 times in 26 planned attacks over 8 games;
  - the next `then` card was cast after a response.
- **#862.** The seat's own cast-time prompt (X funding, an ETB target, Mentor's pay) drops the attack plan, and the same attack is asked again at combat. This may be deliberate, but no plan description says it.
- **#863.** `repair_enum_strings` reads "Flashback Think Twice (tap Island, Island, Island)" as "Island" and plays the land.

**Tool:** to test "nothing happened since", use a second stub cc seat that casts flash creatures. A `random` opponent holds up mana on your turn too rarely: 40 games, 0 cases.

## H33 + H29: what the prompt no longer says; every caller of `send_message_structured`

**H29: clean.**
- Every caller goes through `build_prompt` / `build_prompt_with_header`.
- Mulligan and bottom carry the notes without a board, by design.
- A checker over 50 stub games found every prompt's notes equal to the previous THOUGHT, for every kind reached. That includes the new concede count-out (31bc97d), X, search, name-a-card, discard, bottom, damage order and the legend rule.
- Not reached in play, but going through `build_prompt` by code read: piles, combat damage assignment, trigger order and damage division.

**H33: two regressions from 52eac98/8d3e283**, shown byte for byte against a 6c437b9 build on the same saved position.
- **#864.** An opponent's graveyard card loses its text when the pending choice or a stack object targets it:
  - Memory's Journey's targets, including a textless "Unbreathing Horde 0/0";
  - Nevermore's obvious names;
  - Falkenrath Noble under the opponent's Ghoulcaller's Chant.
  - Meanwhile, cards that merely say "from your graveyard" stay described.
- **#865.** The opponent's floating mana appears in no prompt now that tap events are dropped. `format_state_body` renders only the seat's own pool. The page draws the opponent's pool; the CLI reads only `your_mana_pool`.

**Checked, correct:**
- `(×N)` merges only identical stack and event lines, and a dead source's trigger keeps its effect text.
- Stripped reminder text is never a card's only rules definition. Morbid, transform and back faces stay defined.
- The seat's own `Mana pool:` still shows.

**Tool:** `--save X --stop-after N`, then `--resume X --stop-after 2` on each of two builds, gives the same position's prompt byte for byte. It works between 6c437b9 and master.
