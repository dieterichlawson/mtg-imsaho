## R1: propagation sweep (issues closed 2026-11-06: #963, #964, #998)

- **#998 (play/draw after ready).** The fix is in `Lobby::play_draw` and the terminal client.
  - The page disables Play first / Draw first once `ready || readySent`, and `actions.playDraw` returns early on the same condition.
  - `open_between` marks an AI or resumed seat ready only together with its `play_first`, and the clock's `take_between` never calls `play_draw`. So no other path writes the choice after ready.
  - The game page's `between_line` ("…choose play or draw where you drafted") is set once, when the between opens. It points the person at the draft page; it does not claim the choice is open.
  - Holds.
- **#963/#964 (narrow screens, the input echo).** Both fixes are CLI-only. The terminal client is line-based on cooked stdin, with no framed pane, pager or echo. The page and the LLM and random seats draw none of these screens. Holds.

## L8: SBA order (re-run, aimed by a code read)

**The read.** `check_state_based_actions` repeats its sweep until nothing happens (CR 704.3), and every pass's deaths land in the same `state.events`. `collect_triggers` then hands every collector the *whole* action's event list. `was_on_the_battlefield(state, events, id)` answers "yes" for any object with a `LeftBattlefield`/`CreatureDied` anywhere in that list. That makes a watcher that died in pass 1 a witness to a death in pass 2. CR 603.10a's look-back reaches only the event the permanent left in.

**The board.** Seed 8802, p1 on the play, placed on the turn-1 save:
- Falkenrath Noble with 1 damage, for p0.
- Mayor of Avabruck, and Village Cannibals wearing Dead Weight (a 1/1 only through Mayor's +1/+1), for p1.

Mayor attacked and Noble blocked.

**What happened.**
- Pass 1 put Noble and Mayor in the graveyard. Pass 2 put Cannibals there.
- Noble triggered three times: itself, Mayor, and Cannibals. The rules allow two.
- p1 ended at 17 and p0 at 23, where the rules give 18 and 22.
- `--check-invariants` was silent. Filed **#1000** (`sev:rules`).
- The damage collectors use the same helper. That half is a code read only.

**Checked, correct (code):** two players at 0 life together draw. The 704.5a sweep marks every player before `end_game_if_decided` counts the living, so the result is a draw.

## C7: curses (re-run)

**The board.** Seven curses on p1: two Pierced Heart, Bloody Tome, Oblivion, Nightly Hunt, Death's Hold, Stalked Prey. Against them were two Village Cannibals, Grizzly Bears and Doomed Traveler, and three graveyard cards. The game was played to turn 5.

**Holds:**
- Four upkeep triggers, ordered by their controller and in two different orders over two upkeeps (CR 603.3b).
- Bloody Tome's mill resolved before Oblivion's choice in one upkeep and after it in the other. The choice offered the right count each time.
- Death's Hold killed Doomed Traveler on placement, and its Spirit token as it was created (0/0, CR 704.5f, then 704.5d).
- Nightly Hunt marked every able creature `[MUST ATTACK]` and refused a smaller attack, citing CR 508.1d.
- Stalked Prey put a counter on the Falkenrath Noble that hit p1.

**Found:**
- The log never names milled cards, or the cards Curse of Oblivion exiles: "p1 milled 2 cards", "exiled a card from p1's graveyard" (**#1001**). Graveyard Shovel, Purify the Grave and Cellar Door do name theirs.
- The set screen still offers `[n=none]` on "Mark 2 of the 6" and "Mark 1 of the 8" (**#1002**, the other half of #997).
