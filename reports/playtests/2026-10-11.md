## V54 — the tall side (first run)

**Setup.** A one-off pile of 26 distinct long-named creatures was staged onto one battlefield by save edit, against a Doom Blade / Altar's Reap / Brimstone Volley / Bears hand.

**What a player can see of their own side** (#774):

| Screen | Seat | Own side visible |
|---|---|---|
| 120x40, opponent tall | p0 | 4 creature rows; no life line, Lands line or hand |
| 120x40, own side tall | p1 | every creature at priority (22 of 26 at blockers); still no life line, lands or hand |
| 80x24 | p0 | none of 10 permanents |

**The menu** shrinks to one row a page: 8 pages for 8 actions, and 25 pages for Doom Blade's targets.

The #695 fix had said this half was left open; no issue tracked it until #774. It held at all five prompt kinds tried. The mulligan can't be reached with a board.

**#775.** `prompt_block_min_rows` reserves one line for the option. So a one-option page whose row wraps (Altar's Reap's) loses the hint's second line. This is #698's symptom, reached through the option instead of the hint.

**The page.** The same save in Chromium at 1280x720 and 1024x600 keeps the player's whole half: life, lands, creatures and hand. The opponent's 26 overlap by design, with the hovered card drawn on top. Checked, correct.

**(1) Untaps and taps.** Every `.tap(`/`.untap(` caller in the engine was read against its log line. Each one is either logged through `tap_for`/`untap_for` or implied by its own line: attack, mana, "regenerated", or the activation. Grimgrin and Galvanic Juggernaut write their own untap lines.

## Notes for the next crew

- **Staging a tall board takes 20 lines.** The script is in #774's Repro. A board grown by save edit resumes clean under `--check-invariants`.
- **Un-finishing a finished save.** Clear `result`, the players' `lost`/`loss_reason`, set life above 0, and empty `events` with `trigger_event_index` 0. Without the `events` step, the invariant checker rejects the leftover `PlayerLost`/`GameEnded` events.
- **Staging a transformed card.** Setting `is_transformed` needs `name` set to the back face too, or `--resume` refuses the save.
- **Hotseat auto-pass.** It passes a seat with nothing to do by itself, so a blind third keystroke answered Night Terrors' choice. Capture before every keystroke at a resolution prompt.
