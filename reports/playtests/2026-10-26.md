## R1: propagation sweep (issues closed 2026-10-24 to 10-26)

Closed since the last sweep: #878, #935, #936, #941, #942 (#930-#933 were
swept on 2026-10-25).

- **#878** (a person's sideboard and play/draw were logged nowhere).
  `deck::log_between` now writes the AI seats' `SIDEBOARD before game N`
  line for every seat no model answered. Read beside the AI path
  (`log_sideboard`) and the batch runner's `before_game`. One difference:
  the AI line ends "; plays first" when the answer has no `play_first`,
  where the person's says "(no choice was made)". To see whether a seat
  can reach that, the batch runner was run under a stub whose sideboard
  calls fail (`--players 2 --best-of 3 --seed 31`). The failure is fatal
  to the run (14 attempts in 545 s, exit 1, usage printed), which is the
  batch runner's design. So only a parsed answer missing the field could
  reach the AI wording, and schema and text checks both require it.
  Checked, not filed.
- **#935, #936.** One reader (`read_request_head`) serves both the game
  page and the lobby, so there is no second copy to miss.
- **#941, #942.** These are drivers only. Each now takes `gui:0` and
  reads the URL its runner prints.

## L62: every legal division of combat damage

Setup: a 4/5 Hollowhenge Scavenger triple-blocked by Grizzly Bears, in a
hotseat game (seed 6201).

- The CLI asked "how much of the 4 left goes to Grizzly Bears (#62)? At
  least 2 (lethal), at most 4".
- 3 was entered. The second blocker, with 1 left (below its lethal), was
  not asked and took 1. The third took 0.
- The log agrees: `p0 assigned 3 of Hollowhenge Scavenger (#21)'s combat
  damage to Grizzly Bears (#62) (CR 510.1c)`.
- The damage-order prompt fits on the full screen.

The engine keeps the damage-assignment order of the rules it was written
to (CR 509.2). That is deliberate and is noted in the idea.
