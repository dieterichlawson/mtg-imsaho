## C8: werewolf spell counts

The board: Terror of Kruin Pass (transformed Kruin Outlaw) on one side, Gatstaf Shepherd on the other, and Geistflame and Moonmist in both hands.

Checked, correct:
- one spell from each player on a turn flips neither face, because the back face needs two spells from *one* player;
- a turn with no spells flips the Shepherd at the next upkeep;
- Geistflame plus its flashback is two spells from one player, and flips both back faces;
- the two triggers go on in APNAP order and resolve top-down (CR 603.3b).

Found: the stack and log label for this trigger differs by card (**#1063**). The 12 front faces and Terror of Kruin Pass say only `(transform)`. Ten back faces say `(transform back if 2+ spells cast)` and Howlpack Alpha says `(transform if a player cast 2+ spells last turn)`.

## L6: copies of transformed DFCs

**Board one: a transformed Mayor of Avabruck (Howlpack Alpha).** Cackling Counterpart made a token copy of it and Evil Twin copied it, so p0 had three Alphas, each 5/5 from the other two. An end-step Wolf entered 5/5.

At the next upkeep, after p0 cast two spells, the real Mayor flipped back. The Twin, correctly, did not (CR 701.28c, a single-faced card). Neither did the token, which logged `does not transform — a token copy of a double-faced card is not itself double-faced (CR 111.7)`.

Since the 2021 rewrite, CR 707.8a makes a token copy of a transforming permanent a *transforming token* with both faces. 701.28c names "a transforming token" as something that can transform. The engine and its comments (#469, #500) follow the 2011 rule. Filed as **#1060**, `sev:rules`, with a note that it may be deliberate.

**Board two: Bloodline Keeper and four Vampires, an Evil Twin on each side.** Checked, correct:
- p1's Twin copied the Keeper and may activate `{B}: Transform`, which does nothing (701.28c);
- the real Keeper transformed, and Lord of Lineage gave +2/+2 to every other Vampire, the Twin's copy included;
- p0's Twin then copied the Lord as a 5/5. Its "destroy target creature with the same name" offered exactly the two Lords and killed p1's;
- the anthem fell away, and Falkenrath Noble triggered on the Lord's death.

Found along the way:
- **#1061**: while Evil Twin's "enter as a copy" choice is pending, the resolving Twin is in its owner's graveyard. The board says `2gy`, `g` lists it, the save has `zone: Graveyard`, and the events record `ObjectMoved Stack → Graveyard`. Nothing in the pool reads a card going to a graveyard, so it is filed as presentation. L68 is added to probe every way an entering copy arrives.
- A comment on **#1059**: at the copy chooser the footer's `g` (not just `/`) types into the line and needs Enter.
