# Playtest night — 2026-09-08

**Crew**: 9 testers in parallel, three personas, one tmux socket each.
**Games**: 23 driven through the real CLI (7 Competitor, 9 Rules Lawyer, 7
Vandal), all hotseat `--p1 cli --p2 cli` with both seats played by hand, plus
14 resume/cold-start renders across 12 terminal sizes, 2 minimal-repro replays
and 2 resumes. All under `--check-invariants`.
**Zero panics, zero hangs and zero invariant failures in any game.**
**Issues**: 18 filed (#350–#367), 5 comments on 4 existing issues (#318, #326
twice, #356, #362).

Every mission id in the guides — C1–C37, L1–L39, V1–V38, M1–M5, H1–H5, D1–D7 —
had been played inside the two-week floor, so all nine of tonight's missions are
new ideas written for it: C38–C40, L40–L42 and V39–V41. Six of the nine were
chosen from mechanics the ledger had never touched at all (vigilance, haste,
intimidate, fight, the untap step, the intervening "if"); the three Vandal
probes were chosen from the *shape* of the open issue list, which was three
clipping bugs found by accident at three different terminal sizes.

**The rules engine had a very good night.** Six missions went looking for a
CR violation and none found one. Fight, intimidate, vigilance, haste, the untap
step, APNAP upkeep ordering and both halves of CR 603.4 all held on every claim
that could be reached. Seventeen of the eighteen issues filed are in the
machine — what the program *shows* and what it *reads* — and the one arguable
exception (#357) is an information gap, not a wrong resolution.

## The three that matter

**#360 — a keystroke typed at one seat's declare-blockers prompt is executed by
the other seat's next priority menu.** `LAST_DECISION_IDENTITY` is written only
by `choose_action`, so `choose_attackers`/`choose_blockers` never register with
it; the post-combat menu compares itself against the *pre*-combat menu, sees no
identity change, and skips the type-ahead drain that #71 added. In a hotseat the
intervening prompt belongs to the opponent. p1 sent `0:0␍4␍` in one burst at
DECLARE BLOCKERS and the `4` landed on p0's MAIN PHASE 2 menu, where line 4 is
Concede — so p0 got the concede dialog. Reproduced twice more, including in the
same-seat direction from DECLARE ATTACKERS.

**#361 — the concede confirmation accepts type-ahead.** `confirm_yn` reads
without draining, so `4␍y␍` in one burst ends the game with "Are you sure you
want to concede?" never rendered. It composes with #360: the defending seat's
single burst `0:0␍4␍y␍` concedes the *attacking* seat's game, with neither
player having seen a prompt. Both are cheap fixes — register the two combat
prompts in the identity, and add the three-line drain `read_line_redrawing`
already runs — and neither is covered by `cli_pty.rs`.

**#352 — below 70 columns the combat prompts show no creature list at all.**
Declare-blockers at 70x20 draws neither the attackers nor your blockers, prints
no paging marker, and still accepts and commits `blocker:attacker` pairs;
declare-attackers prints `… showing 0-0 of 0-1` with no row beneath it. This
came out of the first systematic geometry sweep the project has run, which also
established that the program's worst width is one nobody would have guessed:
`has_right = w >= 100` switches the CARDS gutter on at exactly 100 columns and
the middle panel drops from 63 (at 80) to 58, so 100 is *narrower for content*
than 99. #318, #350 and #351 all live at that width. 200x50 and 120x45 were
clean on every screen tested.

## By mission

**C38 the upkeep-trigger engine as a clock** — clean. A four-trigger upkeep
(Endless Ranks + Bloodgift Demon against two Curse of the Bloody Tome) bucketed
exactly as CR 603.3b requires, each player ordering only their own group.
Bloodgift Demon picks its target on the way onto the stack, offers you yourself,
and still draws and drains after Altar's Reap sacrifices the Demon in response
to its own trigger. Two corrections for the guide: Endless Ranks of the Dead's
current Oracle text has **no** "if you control two or more Zombies" clause, and
the ISD curses read "enchanted player's upkeep", which makes them the cheapest
way to hand the non-active player a trigger on the active player's upkeep.

**C39 vigilance and haste** — clean on every claim. Both keywords go through
single central gates, so there is no per-card drift to find. The sharpest state
reached: a Thraben Sentry that attacked with vigilance and then transformed into
Thraben Militia *mid-combat*, losing vigilance — it stayed untapped and stayed an
attacker, rendered one line above the sibling that attacked already transformed
and was tapped.

**C40 intimidate** — every CR 702.13a / 105.2c / 204.2 / 509.1b claim held
across four attacker colour classes, including a colourless artifact attacker
blockable only by artifacts and a multicoloured Geist of Saint Traft. The defect
is downstream: colour decides an intimidate combat and no pane ever prints it,
which for a Gatstaf Howler — green by colour indicator, with no cost to infer it
from — leaves the defender reading back which of their own creatures the engine
allowed (#357).

**L40 the untap step** — clean. The restriction is genuinely evaluated *at* the
untap step rather than latched when the permanent was tapped (an Avacynian
Priest tapping an untapped Grimgrin at end of turn leaves it stuck the following
untap step), and removing a Claustrophobia after its controller's untap step
leaves the creature tapped for a full turn cycle. No priority in ~50 untap steps.
Both defects are in the log (#358, #359).

**L41 the fight event** — clean. `combat::fight` reads both powers before
dealing either and routes everything through the one damage pipeline, so CR
701.12d falls out correctly rather than needing per-card patches: fight damage
survived both Moonmist and Ghostly Possession. Correction for the guide: **Garruk
Relentless never reaches the fight event** — his 0 ability is two damage
instructions, correctly implemented as such — so only Prey Upon and Nightfall
Predator do.

**L42 the intervening "if"** — clean, and it produced the night's most useful
scoping fact: **no implemented card has an intervening-"if" condition that can
change between trigger and resolution.** Every one is "last turn's spell count"
or "a creature died this turn", both frozen once the trigger fires, so the
classic "kill the Zombie in response" demonstration is unbuildable from this
pool. The first check held on six upkeeps where the condition was false, and
Moonmist cast in response to Gatstaf Shepherd's own trigger flipped it forward
and let the trigger flip it back (CR 603.4 + 712.8). Two follow-on ideas are
proposed in the guide.

**V39 the terminal geometry matrix** — see #352 above; also #350, #351, #353,
#354. Running each position twice, once cold-started at the size and once
resized into it, is what separated the three static overruns from the one
resize-only defect (#353: both combat prompts measure the terminal once, outside
the closure that repaints them).

**V40 the hotseat hidden-information boundary** — the first night to look, and
the rendering came back clean: `GameView::for_player` is genuinely per-seat,
`render_paged` opens with `Clear(All)` so no stale frame survives a switch, and
a capture at 0.1s after the mulligan hand-off already shows the new seat's hand,
gutter and header. `--log` is honest too — the Delver look-at and the mulligan
bottoming are both unnamed in the file. The boundary that failed is made of
keystrokes, not pixels (#360, #361).

**V41 the read-only viewers** — the viewers pass the hard half of the contract
and fail the easy half. 60+ opens across nine prompt kinds moved not one byte of
`--save` or `--log`, answered no decision, and returned the prompt byte-identical
every time; escape is uniform and nothing nests or sticks; `d` never reveals
library order and `/` only searches zones the seat can see. What broke is
presentation: `i` has no pager at all (#364), both pagers size pages in entries
while printing wrapped rows (#365), `i` is accepted-but-unadvertised while `/`
is silently dead below 100 columns (#366), and `g` orders its blocks by seat
where `e` anchors them to "you" (#367).
