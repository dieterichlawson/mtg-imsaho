# `was_on_the_battlefield(..) && is_creature(..)` guarding a combat-damage

# combat damage (CR 510.1), and a source that dealt it and then died has its
# `CreatureDied` in the same event window, so `was_on_the_battlefield` finds

# defensive, and the half that carries the rule (CR 603.10, an ability
