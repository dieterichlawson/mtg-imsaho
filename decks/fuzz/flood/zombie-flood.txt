# Fuzz flood deck: a token engine that grows the board past what the coverage
# decks can reach. Endless Ranks of the Dead and Parallel Lives compound each
# upkeep; Army of the Damned and Moan of the Unhallowed seed them. The
# coverage fuzz's slowest game is under 2 s, so the walls found by hand at
# 500-2,000 permanents (#565, #640, #641, #642) never showed up in it.
# scripts/fuzz.sh plays this deck as a mirror, a few seeds per night, under
# the per-game timeout: a game that cannot finish inside it is the finding.
